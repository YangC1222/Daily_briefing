中文摘要

HTGR型SMR研发增多，其高燃耗TRISO燃料及石墨结构使乏燃料特性不同于大型PWR。棱柱燃料块是主要配置之一，但综合后端燃料循环评价仍有限。本文以SCALE 6.3.1建立基于设计的替代燃料柱模型，评价乏燃料质量、体积、衰变热、活度和放射毒性，并与参考PWR比较，考察燃耗BU、初始富集度IE和冷却时间CT。所采用假设下，大多数燃耗条件中HTGR型SMR乏燃料质量较低，但因石墨块及TRISO结构，其体积显著较大。后端指标对燃耗比对初始富集度更敏感。较高燃耗提高短期衰变热和放射毒性，但随着锕系核素贡献增大，可降低部分长期放射性指标。相对差异指标表明，在可行的燃耗和富集度组合及本文假设下，120–150 GWd/MTU和15–17 wt%可作为兼顾短期管理与长期处置的指示范围。研究可为HTGR型SMR的贮存、运输、后处理或循环利用及处置策略提供定量基础。

English abstract

With increasing interest in the development and deployment of high-temperature gas-cooled reactor (HTGR)- based small modular reactors (SMRs), their spent nuclear fuel (SNF) may exhibit characteristics different from those of conventional large PWRs because of high burnup (BU) TRISO fuel and graphite structures. Although prismatic block fuel is a major HTGR-based SMR fuel configuration, integrated backend nuclear fuel cycle (BNFC) assessments remain limited. In this study, BNFC metrics, including SNF mass, volume, decay heat, radioactivity, and radiotoxicity, were evaluated for an HTGR-based SMR using prismatic block fuel and compared with those of a reference PWR (Ref.PWR). A design-based surrogate fuel column model was developed using SCALE 6.3.1, and the effects of BU, initial enrichment (IE), and cooling time (CT) were examined. Under the adopted assumptions, the HTGR-based SMR tended to show lower SNF mass than the Ref.PWR under most BU conditions, whereas its SNF volume was substantially larger because of the graphite block and TRISO fuel structure. The BNFC metrics appeared more sensitive to BU than to IE. Higher BU tended to increase short-term decay heat and radiotoxicity, while reducing several long-term radiological metrics as actinide contributions became more important. The relative difference index suggested that 120–150 GWd/MTU and 15–17 wt% may represent an indicative range under the adopted assumptions for balancing short-term management requirements and long-term disposal implications when feasible BU and IE combinations are considered. This study can provide a quantitative basis for establishing SNF storage, transportation, reprocessing or recycling, and disposal strategies for HTGR-based SMRs.

中文摘要

最佳估计加不确定性（BEPU）分析需要同时处理与固有参数变异性相关的随机不确定性和因模型及系统运行信息不完整而产生的认知不确定性。直接嵌套随机抽样对于系统热工水力分析而言计算量巨大。本研究提出了一种简化的双层不确定性分析策略，该策略结合了确定性抽样和代理辅助的补充案例选择。该方法以经济简化型沸水反应堆（ESBWR）主蒸汽管破裂（MSLB）事故为例进行了验证，该事故使用RELAP5程序进行模拟。三个安全系统动作参数被视为外层认知变量，而衰变功率和给水流量被视为内层随机变量。首先，分别使用精细计算来验证物理子空间和逻辑子空间中的不利区域，然后将保留的变量联合传播，并使用RELAP5重新计算经代理筛选出的最不利组合。对于物理不确定性，所有四个由代理模型辅助的案例均位于最低的五个最低折算水位（MCL）响应值之间，将最低验证MCL值从5.792米降低至5.618米。对于逻辑不确定性，两个由代理模型选择的案例得到的MCL值分别为8.216米和8.226米，均低于153个样本集的四阶威尔克斯准则对应的第四小值8.303米，尽管原始样本中仍存在绝对最小值8.111米。联合传播进一步得到的MCL值低于单独进行物理或逻辑分析所得的值，表明执行场景与物理变异性之间存在条件耦合。所提出的策略在保持反应堆安全分析高保真验证和可追溯性的同时，提高了不利案例的识别率。

English abstract

Best-estimate plus uncertainty (BEPU) analysis requires the combined treatment of aleatory uncertainty associated with inherent parameter variability and epistemic uncertainty arising from incomplete knowledge of models and system actuation. Direct nested random sampling is computationally expensive for system thermal-hydraulic analysis. This study develops a reduced double-layer uncertainty-analysis strategy combining deterministic sampling with surrogate-assisted supplementary case selection. The method is demonstrated for an Economic Simplified Boiling Water Reactor (ESBWR) hypothetical Main Steam Line Break (MSLB) accident simulated using RELAP5 code. Three safety-system actuation parameters are treated as outer-layer epistemic variables, while decay power and feedwater flow rate are treated as inner-layer aleatory variables. Separate refined calculations are first used to verify the adverse regions in the physical and logical subspaces, while the retained variables are then propagated jointly, and the most adverse surrogate-screened combinations are recalculated using RELAP5. For the physical uncertainties, all four surrogate-assisted cases were located among the five lowest minimum collapsed water level (MCL) responses, reducing the lowest verified MCL from 5.792 m to 5.618 m. For the logical uncertainties, the two surrogate-selected cases yielded MCL values of 8.216 and 8.226 m, both below the fourth-smallest value of 8.303 m associated with the fourth-order Wilks criterion for the 153-sample set, although the absolute minimum of 8.111 m remained in the original samples. Joint propagation further produced MCL values lower than those obtained from either isolated physical or logical analyses, indicating a conditional coupling between actuation scenarios and physical variability. The proposed strategy improves adverse-case identification while retaining high-fidelity verification and traceability for reactor safety analysis.

中文摘要

小型模块堆是大型核电站的替代选项，其设计和安全需要优化。本文用MARS-KS研究整体式PWR型SMR的容器容积及堆芯功率对小破口失冷事故的影响。在非能动安全系统条件受控的情况下，改变容积（原摘要比例为1:1、1:2、1:4/3）及功率80、160和240 MWt，形成3×3参数矩阵。瞬态分为喷放主导期与长期冷却期。喷放期排出冷却剂质量随容积缩放，分别为基准排出质量的51–52%及75–76%；几何缩比使水位下降近似随容积立方根变化，分别为基准降幅81%及91–92%。长期冷却期，折算水位变化率与功率体积比q/V呈线性关系。因此紧凑化通过两条路径影响SBLOCA：容积决定喷放后库存，q/V决定长期冷却。本文量化了SMR后续非能动安全系统尺寸设计需要补偿的紧凑化代价。

English abstract

Small-modular reactors (SMRs) are alternatives to conventional large nuclear power plants. Consequently, their design characteristics and safety must be optimized. Herein, the effects of reactor vessel volume and core power on small-break loss-of-coolant accident (SBLOCA) in integral pressurized water reactor (iPWR)-type SMRs were investigated using the MARS-KS system code. A 3×3 parametric matrix was developed by varying the vessel volume (1:1, 1:2, and 1:4/3 scale) and core power (80, 160, and 240 MWt) under controlled passive safety system conditions. Governing mechanisms divided the transient into discharge-dominant and long-term cooling phases. During the discharge-dominant phase, the discharged coolant mass scales with the vessel volume (51–52 % and 75–76 % of the baseline discharged mass), whereas the resulting water-level reduction follows a cube-root dependence on the volume due to geometric scaling effects (81 % and 91–92 % of the baseline level drop). During the long-term cooling phase, the collapsed water-level change rate exhibits a linear correlation with the power-to-volume ratio (q/V). Therefore, the reactor compactness influences SBLOCA performance through two mechanisms: the volume determines the coolant inventory remaining after blowdown, whereas q/V governs the long-term cooling behavior. This work quantifies the compactness penalty that a later PSS sizing must compensate for SMRs.

中文摘要

MCNP 到 Geant4 的几何重用十分困难，因为 MCNP 通过定向曲面的布尔组合来定义区域，而 GDML 则要求在体积层次结构中使用有限实体。本文提出了一种名为 MCGeoBridge 的方法，该方法在输出标准 Geant4 GDML 的同时，保留了 MCNP 单元表达式在声明的有限域内的成员关系。它采用抽象语法树解析、对支持的补集进行保守归一化、对可约化的立方体、圆柱体和球体进行精确的图元恢复，以及对其他由曲面定义的叶节点进行有界布尔形式转换。验证过程结合了语言覆盖率统计、源表达式、转换后的实体以及独立的 Geant4 点分类检查。在九个回归模型和 420 个已解析的单元中，193,318 个全局点、单元局部点和边界指向点在内部评估器和 Geant4 中均与源分类结果一致；在既定协议下，420 个单元中有 390 个单元具有区分性样本。一个包含 70 个中等复杂度卡组的几何去重语料库生成了格式良好的 GDML 代码，其中包含有效的内部引用；近似状态、警告和源材料完整性分别报告。目标加载和导航检查评估了扩展晶格的情况。这些结果支持在声明的有限转换域内，所测试的支持案例的单元几何一致性。

English abstract

MCNP-to-Geant4 geometry reuse is difficult because MCNP defines regions by Boolean combinations of oriented surfaces, whereas GDML requires finite solids in a volume hierarchy. This work presents MCGeoBridge, a method that preserves MCNP cell-expression membership within a declared finite domain while emitting standard Geant4 GDML. It uses abstract-syntax-tree parsing, conservative normalization of supported complements, exact primitive recovery for reducible boxes, cylinders, and spheres, and bounded Boolean lowering of other surface-defined leaves. Validation combines language-coverage accounting with source-expression, converted-solid, and independent Geant4 point-classification checks. Across nine regression models and 420 resolved cells, 193,318 global, cell-local, and boundary-directed points produced no disagreement with source classification in either the internal evaluator or Geant4; 390 of 420 cells had discriminating samples under the stated protocol. A geometry-deduplicated corpus of 70 medium-complexity decks generated well-formed GDML with valid internal references; approximation status, warnings, and source-material completeness are reported separately. Destination loading and navigation checks assess expanded lattice cases. These results support per-cell geometry agreement for tested supported cases within the declared finite conversion domain.
