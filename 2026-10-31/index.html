中文摘要

最近开发了一种单向流固耦合框架[1]，用于评估螺旋盘绕式直流蒸汽发生器（H-OTSG）管在密度波振荡（DWO）下的结构完整性和微动磨损。本文针对不同反应堆功率、运行工况和流量振荡幅度，针对各种管几何形状和DWO工况，给出了案例研究结果。将基于局部三维实体管支撑模型获得的等效支撑刚度应用于梁模型。结构完整性评估包括塑性坍塌、局部失效和疲劳，而微动磨损则以微动功率评价。所有低功率工况均满足结构完整性准则，且微动功率均低于设定的限值。反应堆功率、运行工况和管几何形状对结构完整性和微动磨损的影响各不相同，而流量振荡幅度对微动磨损的影响大于对结构完整性的影响。在 100% 功率运行下，某些情况下微动功率超过了所采用的指导方针，尽管在这些情况下 DWO 的发生预计会受到限制。

English abstract

A one-way fluid-structure interaction framework was recently developed [1] to evaluate the structural integrity and fretting wear of helically coiled once-through steam generator (H-OTSG) tubes under density wave oscillation (DWO). This article presents case-study results for various tube geometries and DWO scenarios under varying reactor powers, operating conditions, and flow-rate oscillation amplitudes. An equivalent support stiffness obtained from a local three-dimensional solid tube-support model was applied to the beam model. Structural integrity was evaluated for plastic collapse, local failure, and fatigue, while fretting wear was evaluated using the fretting work rate. All low-power cases satisfied the structural integrity criteria, and the fretting work rates remained below the adopted guideline. Reactor power, operating condition, and tube geometry affected structural integrity and fretting wear differently, while flow-rate oscillation amplitude had a greater effect on fretting wear than on structural integrity. Under 100% power operation, the fretting work rate exceeded the adopted guideline in some scenarios, although the occurrence of DWO is expected to be limited in these cases.

中文摘要

核数据同化把先验核数据、协方差、灵敏度和积分实验纳入统一统计框架。传统实现多基于多群截面，增加对群结构和群平均方式的依赖。本文利用能量相关协方差和连续能灵敏度，在NECP-UNICORN中开发连续能核数据同化。先用Jezebel基准对照既有多群实现，再用于中国实验快堆CEFR，并把连续能调整因子传播到ACE格式核库以直接进行连续能蒙卡计算。调整库重算响应与相应线性响应预测之差不超过12 pcm。另一次样本外检验把CEFR实验排除在同化数据之外，CEFR keff偏差仍从583 pcm降至30 pcm。这支持该流程用于大尺度反应堆计算，并在本文调整范围内支持线性响应近似。

English abstract

Nuclear data assimilation combines prior nuclear data, covariance information, sensitivity coefficients, and integral experimental results within a unified statistical framework. Conventional implementations are generally based on multigroup cross sections, which introduce additional dependence on the adopted group structure and group-averaging procedure. In this work, a continuous-energy (CE) nuclear data assimilation capability was developed in NECP-UNICORN using energy-dependent covariance information and CE sensitivity coefficients. The implementation was first verified with the Jezebel benchmark by comparison with the established multigroup implementation. The method was then applied to the China Experimental Fast Reactor (CEFR), and the CE assimilation adjustment factors were further propagated to ACE-format libraries for direct continuous-energy Monte Carlo calculations. The recalculated responses obtained with the adjusted ACE libraries differ from the corresponding linear-response predictions by no more than 12 pcm. In an additional out-of-sample test in which the CEFR experiment was excluded from the assimilation dataset, the CEFR keff deviation was reduced from 583 pcm to 30 pcm. These results demonstrate the applicability of the CE assimilation workflow to large-scale reactor calculations and support the linear-response approximation over the adjustment range considered in this work.

中文摘要

铅铋共晶（LBE）冷却燃料组件对铅冷快堆很重要。本文研究带圆形、三角形及梯形绕丝的7棒束中LBE流动与换热。三维CFD采用SST k–ω湍流模型、温度相关LBE物性及湍流Pr数处理。入口温度673.15 K、速度0.36 m/s，棒表面均匀热流50,000 W/m²。通过竖直加热管内LBE向上流动实验评价模型，较好重现Nu–Pe趋势。考察绕丝几何、400与600 mm配置、湍流Pr处理及部分堵塞。400 mm螺距下，三角丝产生最强动量再分配和横向二次流，中心子通道TSEF峰值0.25–0.27，下游换热系数维持8000–9000 W/(m²·K)，但水力代价较高。圆丝速度和温度场较平滑，梯形丝阻力较小但横向混合弱、热积聚大。400 mm方案比600 mm换热更强，但比较同时受丝尺寸和水力直径差别影响。湍流Pr处理改变Nu大小而不改变轴向趋势。30%外围径向堵塞比15%中心轴向堵塞造成更强热不对称及水力扰动，下游部分恢复。结果有助于在强化换热、阻力与堵塞容忍度之间选择绕丝设计。

English abstract

Lead-bismuth eutectic (LBE)-cooled fuel assemblies are important for lead-cooled fast reactors. This study investigates LBE flow and heat transfer in a 7-rod bundle with circular, triangular, and trapezoidal spacer wires. A three-dimensional CFD model used the SST (k)-(omega) turbulence model, temperature-dependent LBE properties, and turbulent Prandtl-number treatments. Simulations used an inlet temperature of 673.15 K, an inlet velocity of 0.36 m s−1, and a uniform rod-surface heat flux of 50,000 W m−2. The model was assessed against experimental data for upward LBE flow in a heated vertical tube and reproduced the Nusselt–Peclet trend satisfactorily. The effects of wire geometry, 400- and 600-mm configurations, turbulent Prandtl-number treatment, and partial blockage were examined. At 400 mm pitch, the triangular wire generated the strongest momentum redistribution and transverse secondary flow, with central-subchannel TSEF peaks of 0.25–0.27. It maintained a downstream heat-transfer coefficient of 8000–9000 W m m-2K−1, although with a higher hydraulic penalty. The circular wire produced smoother velocity and temperature fields, while the trapezoidal wire offered lower hydraulic resistance but weaker lateral mixing and greater thermal accumulation. The 400 mm configurations showed stronger heat-transfer performance than the 600 mm cases, although the comparison also reflects differences in wire dimensions and hydraulic diameter. The turbulent Prandtl-number treatment changed the predicted Nusselt-number magnitude without altering the axial trend. A 30% peripheral radial blockage caused stronger thermal asymmetry and hydraulic disturbance than a 15% central axial blockage, with partial downstream recovery. These findings support spacer-wire designs that balance heat-transfer enhancement, hydraulic resistance, and blockage tolerance.

中文摘要

微型气冷快堆（MGCFR）凭借其体积小、质量轻、热电转换效率高等优势，在深空探测等领域具有广阔的应用前景。因此，MGCFR的设计稳定性和安全性面临着更高的要求。稳定性分析是MGCFR设计和研究的基础，它决定了反应堆系统能否安全稳定运行。本文以普罗米修斯计划的开放格架反应堆设计为例，利用Matlab®构建了MGCFR的一阶线性系统模型。基于该系统模型，推导出了状态空间表示的稳定性分析模型。采用根轨迹法，定量分析了关键参数（包括三个反应性温度反馈系数和冷却剂流量）对系统稳定性的影响，并获得了这些关键参数的稳定区间。然后，利用系统模型验证了稳定性分析结果。分析和验证结果表明，反应性温度反馈能够使系统模型保持稳定状态。此外，所有关键参数的设计值都具有一定的安全裕度。然而，当关键参数值超出其稳定区间时，系统无法维持稳定运行。本研究为微型气冷快堆（MGCFR）的稳定性分析方法提供了参考。

English abstract

The Micro Gas-Cooled Fast Reactor (MGCFR), by advantages of their small size and mass and high thermal-to-electric conversion efficiency, has broad potential application in scenarios such as deep space exploration. Therefore, the MGCFR is imposed higher requirements on its design stability and safety. Stability analysis is a fundamental aspect in the design and research of MGCFR, determining whether the reactor system can operate safely and stably. Referring to the open-lattice reactor design of Prometheus Project, this paper constructs a first-order linear system model of a MGCFR using Matlab®. A state-space representation is derived as the stability analysis model from the system model. Based on the root locus method, the effects of key parameters (including three reactivity temperature feedback coefficients and the coolant flow rate) on system stability are quantitatively analyzed, and the stable intervals of these key parameters are obtained. The stability analysis results are then verified using the system model. The analysis and verification results prove that the reactivity temperature feedback effects can maintain the system model in a stable state. Furthermore, the design values of all key parameters possess a certain safety margin. However, when the values of the key parameters lie outside their stable intervals, the system cannot maintain stable operation. This research provides a reference for stability analysis methods for MGCFRs.

中文摘要

深穿透、强角各向异性及复杂三维几何条件下的中子输运,是核工程辐射屏蔽计算长期面临的关键难题。传统离散纵标方法具有较高的全局计算效率,但在强角各向异性问题中易产生射线效应;蒙特卡罗方法能够准确描述复杂几何和粒子输运过程,但在深穿透低通量区域存在严重的欠采样和统计效率下降问题。随机特征线方法(the random ray method,TRRM)在特征线方法框架中引入随机相空间求积,通过随机采样特征线的空间起点和飞行方向,并沿特征线执行确定性输运扫描,为兼顾上述两类方法的优势提供了新的技术途径。近年来,浙江大学围绕TRRM开展了系统研究,形成了从强角各向异性输运、深穿透计算、随机特征线生成、线性源近似,到三维输运程序THETA研发及工程验证的完整技术路线。研究表明,TRRM可有效消除固定离散角度导致的射线效应;在通量衰减6~7个数量级的深穿透区域仍能维持稳定统计精度,其相对于标准多群蒙特卡罗方法的计算品质因子提升可超过4个数量级。基于TRRM研发的THETA程序已完成由理论基准、VENUS-3和PCA-Replica实验基准到H.B.Robinson Unit 2(HBR-2)商用压水堆的逐级验证;在HBR-2问题中,压力容器内监督盒和压力容器外腔室剂量计的计算值与实验值的相对偏差平均值分别为5.4%和13.3%,同时在超过10个数量级的全局通量衰减范围内保持较均匀的统计不确定度。进一步引入线性源近似后,TRRM对细空间网格的依赖显著降低,可在保持计算精度的同时大幅减少存储和计算开销。在上述验证工作的基础上,THETA正进一步向复杂工程输运平台发展,已开展CAD-非结构网格随机特征线输运、History-based/Event-based GPU并行、首次碰撞源以及正向/共轭随机特征线与FW-CADIS权窗生成等功能研发。相关工作初步拓展了TRRM在复杂几何、异构高性能计算、数值稳定性及混合确定性-蒙特卡罗减方差等方向的应用能力。上述研究表明,TRRM正在由一种新型输运理论逐步发展为面向复杂三维核工程问题的高保真粒子输运计算技术体系。
