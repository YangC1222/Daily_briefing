中文摘要

钠冷快堆设计阶段目前已考虑堆芯熔毁严重事故的潜在后果。这类事件形成熔融物，即熔化核燃料与结构材料组成的放射性混合物。先进钠冷快堆可采用两类保护装置：排出管将熔融物转移到压力容器下部，以防止再临界；堆芯捕集器接收熔融物以利长期冷却。然而，这些缓解设计可能使熔融物以约3000 K高温液体射流冲击捕集器，造成局部热烧蚀。部分捕集器设计设置倾斜挡板，以耗散射流动能。必须理解倾斜表面的烧蚀，才能确保熔融物迁移阶段捕集器的结构完整性。

English abstract

In sodium-cooled fast reactors (SFRs), the potential consequences of a severe accident with core meltdown are now considered during the reactor design phase. Such events lead to the formation of a corium, a radioactive mixture of molten nuclear fuel and structural materials. Two protective devices can be implemented in advanced SFR designs: discharge tubes, used to transfer the corium to the lower part of the reactor vessel to prevent recriticality, and a core catcher, designed to receive the corium to facilitate its long-term cooling. However, with such new mitigation designs, the corium may impinge on the core catcher as a high-temperature liquid jet (∼3000K), which can lead to localized thermal ablation of the core catcher. Some design proposals for the core catcher include angled baffles intended to dissipate the jet’s kinetic energy. The ablation of such inclined surfaces must be understood to ensure the structural integrity of the core catcher during the corium relocation phase.

English abstract

This work presents a modal reduced-order modelling approach to predict the stability of xenon oscillations in large Pressurised Water Reactors (PWRs). The model, labelled 2G-HET, is developed and compared to a simplified model (2G-HOM). The 2G-HET model takes the two-energy group and three-dimensional macroscopic cross sections, homogenised at the assembly or sub-assembly level, as well as the reactor geometry, as input. It calculates spatial eigenmodes and their coupled time-dependent amplitudes for neutron flux, iodine, and xenon concentrations. In contrast, the 2G-HOM model uses cross sections homogenised over the entire core-reflector region and analytically calculated eigenmodes.

For reference and verification, analyses were also performed for a conventional low-enriched uranium pressurized water reactor (PWR) and a recycled-fuel SFR.

English abstract

This study proposes a partially closed nuclear fuel cycle that integrates a NuScale-like reactor with an adapted Accelerator-Driven System (ADS) through a three-stage approach. Initially, the ADS-SMR arrangement contains reprocessed fuel in the seed region and thorium dioxide in the blanket region, aiming at fuel regeneration. This thorium-after-breeding fuel is then progressively loaded back into the three batch positions of the NuScale core designed for fresh fuel insertion during reshuffling, thereby resulting in three models with distinct fuel loading patterns. Results showed that the effective multiplication factor from the ADS-SMR dropped slightly over ten years, even without additional fissile input during the operation time. It also generated significant 233U, reaching about 5.82 wt% fissile content. Back to the NuScale reactor, the three models exhibited early criticality increases due to in-situ fissile breeding, with one of the models nearly doubling energy output per kgHM compared to the reference case. Overall, the study highlights a feasible path for a partially closed nuclear fuel cycle strategy integrating ADS and SMR technologies. The Serpent code version 2.1.32, developed by VTT, and the ENDF/B-VII.0 nuclear data library have been used to perform the simulations.

中文摘要

点堆动力学中的中子噪声分析传统上在小幅正弦扰动下使用线性响应理论。这种方法成功为中子噪声测量和诊断建立概念基础，但忽略了可能改变反应堆定常状态的非线性效应。特别是，非线性相互作用可以产生有效反应性偏移，标准线性化处理无法捕捉这些效应。然而在确定性驱动下，通常假定这些效应可以忽略。存在随机涨落时，这一假定可能失效。乘性噪声确实可以在有效动力学中产生系统漂移项，使平均中子数偏离定常状态。

English abstract

Neutron noise analysis within the point-kinetics model is traditionally formulated using linear response theory under small-amplitude sinusoidal perturbations. Although this approach has proven successful in providing a conceptual foundation for neutron noise measurement and diagnostics, it neglects potential non-linear effects that may, for example, modify the stationary state of the reactor. In particular, effective reactivity shifts can be generated by non-linear interactions, which are not captured by the standard linearized treatment. These effects are nevertheless generally assumed to remain negligible under deterministic forcing. This assumption may break down in the presence of stochastic fluctuations. Multiplicative noise can indeed induce systematic drift terms in the effective dynamics, leading to noise-induced reactivity shifts that drive the mean neutron population away from stationarity.

These results validate the robustness and practical feasibility of the proposed TD3-PID architecture, offering a viable pathway for the deployment of intelligent control systems in advanced nuclear energy applications.

English abstract

Dry storage is a key technology of spent nuclear fuel (SNF) management. However, no previous studies have examined the application of air-conditioning (AC) systems to indoor dry storage facilities. The results show that installing two AC units is sufficient to reduce temperatures to below 33°C at a height of 2 m. This study provides practical design guidance to improve the thermal safety and working environment in indoor SNF dry storage facilities.

English abstract

The corrosion behavior of Inconel 600, SS316H, and Hastelloy C276 was investigated in molten NaCl-MgCl2 eutectic salt by 500 h static immersion tests at 550 °C and 650 °C. Mass change measurements, SEM-EDS, and XRD were combined to relate alloy degradation to surface and subsurface microstructural evolution. At 550 °C, all three alloys showed relatively shallow, surface-confined attack without detectable crystalline corrosion products, indicating dissolution-dominated behavior. At 650 °C, corrosion became strongly alloy-dependent: Inconel 600 developed a continuous MgO-rich surface deposit and limited subsurface damage with net mass gain, SS316H experienced dissolution-dominated attack with deep void formation and no protective deposit, and Hastelloy C276 formed a thick but non-uniform spinel layer coupled with significant internal degradation. These results demonstrate a temperature-driven transition from simple dissolution to mixed dissolution-precipitation and show that nominal Cr content alone is insufficient to predict corrosion resistance in molten chlorides, underscoring the need to consider full alloy chemistry and salt impurity effects in materials selection for chloride-based molten salt systems.
