中文摘要

随着海洋核电产业的快速发展，核安全保障被视为核心要务。海上核电站受制于有限的外部能源供应，其安全稳定运行高度依赖于安全壳非能动冷却系统（PCCS），该系统无需外部动力输入即可运行。动态海洋环境，特别是垂荡运动，会显著影响PCCS的传热效率和响应特性。为了研究这些影响，我们设计了一个大型实验平台，系统地研究了垂荡条件下PCCS的瞬态传热现象。引入非平衡分数（NEF）来定量表征冷凝过程的非稳态特性。提出了一种简化的瞬态传热模型来估算瞬态冷凝传热系数（HTC），该模型能够捕捉传热强度的时间演变。此外，还提出了平均HTC的预测关联式。对垂荡周期、振幅、初始安全壳压力和不凝性气体比例等关键变量进行了参数研究。结果表明，垂荡运动显著提高了系统的平均传热系数，而峰值传热速率主要取决于初始热状态，对外部扰动基本不敏感。此外，在测试范围内，冷却水温度对传热性能的影响可以忽略不计。本研究为评估动态海洋环境中安全壳非能动冷却系统（PCCS）的热工水力性能和安全鲁棒性提供了实验证据和理论见解。

English abstract

In tandem with the rapid progress of the ocean-based nuclear power industry, nuclear safety assurance is regarded as a core priority. Offshore-deployed FNPPs are constrained by limited external energy supply, and their safe and stable operation is highly dependent on the PCCS—a passive system operable without external power input. The dynamic marine environment particularly heaving motion can significantly influence the heat transfer efficiency and response characteristics of PCCS. To investigate these effects, a large-scale experimental platform was designed to systematically study the transient heat transfer phenomena of PCCS under heaving conditions. A Non-Equilibrium Fraction (NEF) was introduced to quantitatively characterize the non-steady nature of the condensation process. A simplified transient heat transfer model was proposed to estimate the transient condensation heat transfer coefficient (HTC), capturing the temporal evolution of heat transfer intensity. In addition, a predictive correlation for the average HTC is proposed. Parametric studies were conducted on key variables including heaving period, amplitude, initial containment pressure, and non-condensable gas fraction. Results indicate that heaving motion significantly enhances the system's average HTC, whereas the peak heat transfer rate is primarily determined by the initial thermal state and is largely insensitive to external disturbances. Moreover, within the tested range, the effect of cooling water temperature on heat transfer performance was found to be negligible. This study provides experimental evidence and theoretical insight for evaluating the thermal-hydraulic performance and safety robustness of PCCS in dynamic marine environments.

中文摘要

铅铋冷却堆是第四代系统候选。降低节径比P/D可提高体积功率密度，但P/D约1.1的紧凑绕丝组件LBE实验数据稀缺。本文在LECOTH装置测试19棒电加热模拟器，P/D=1.1、H/D约14.2；水力阻力实验Re范围2700–75000，换热实验Pe范围196–1234。实测显示中心子通道温度显著高于边、角及次中心通道。绕丝强烈促进通道间混合，次中心通道LBE温度甚至可超过加热棒壁温。中心通道受外壁影响最小，最能代表堆芯热工行为。棒束摩擦系数几乎不受温度影响，与Novendstern及Cheng–Todreas关联式符合良好。低流速截面温度高度不均匀，增速后趋于均匀。摘要另报告Pe从170增至1200时Nu由4.2升至11，实验最符合El-Genk关联式。整个实验范围内可忽略浮力，换热以强制对流为主。本文为紧凑铅铋堆设计与安全分析提供实验支持。

English abstract

Lead‑bismuth-cooled reactors (LBRs) are promising candidates for Generation IV nuclear systems. Compact core design with a reduced pitch-to-diameter ratio (P/D) can significantly improve volumetric power density, but experimental data for liquid lead‑bismuth eutectic (LBE) in compact wire-wrapped fuel assemblies (P/D ≈ 1.1) remain scarce. To fill this gap, this study investigated the flow and heat transfer characteristics of LBE in a highly compact wire-wrapped rod bundle. A 19-pin electrically heated fuel bundle simulator with P/D = 1.1 and H/D ≈ 14.2 was tested in the LECOTH test facility. Hydraulic resistance experiments were conducted over a Reynolds number (Re) range of 2700–75,000, and heat transfer experiments were performed within a Peclet number (Pe) range of 196–1234. Based on the measured temperature distributions in different subchannels, the central subchannel exhibits obviously higher temperatures than the edge, corner, and sub-central subchannels. Owing to the strong inter-subchannel mixing induced by the wire wrap, the LBE temperature in the sub-central subchannel can even exceed the wall temperature of the heating rods. The central subchannel is least affected by the outer wall effects and thus shows the most representative thermal-hydraulic behavior for reactor core applications. The friction factor of the rod bundle is barely affected by temperature and agrees well with the Novendstern and Cheng-Todreas correlations. The cross-sectional temperature distribution is highly non-uniform at low flow rates and becomes more homogeneous with increasing flow velocity. The Nusselt number increases from 4.2 to 11 as Pe rises from 170 to 1200, and the experimental heat transfer data conform best to the El-Genk correlation. Throughout the entire experimental operating range, buoyancy effects can be neglected, and the heat transfer is dominated by forced convection. This work provides reliable experimental support for the thermal-hydraulic design and safety analysis of compact LBR cores.

中文摘要

本技术说明评估了利用罐体表面热特征作为乏核燃料干式储存容器间接核保障指标的可行性。文中采用水平式NUHOMS型干式储存模块的计算热模型，研究了与转移相关的场景，其中选定的燃料组件被移除或替换为低功率的模拟组件。利用分布式线探针数据（旨在模拟光纤温度测量），将罐体表面的温度分布与正常装载条件下的温度分布进行了比较。结果表明，组件产热量的变化会在罐体表面产生局部且空间结构化的温度偏差，其大小和模式取决于组件位置和转移场景。这些结果表明，分布式热监测可以提供一种补充性的、非侵入式的特征，用于验证干式储存容器内的燃料情况，并在长期储存期间保持信息的连续性。该方法并非作为一种独立的保障技术提出，而是作为一种实用的补充特征，可以增强现有的基于辐射、视觉和封隔/监视的方法。

English abstract

This technical note evaluates the feasibility of using canister-surface thermal signatures as an indirect safeguards indicator for spent nuclear fuel dry storage casks. A computational thermal model of a horizontal NUHOMS-type dry storage module was used to examine diversion-relevant scenarios in which selected fuel assemblies were either removed or replaced by lower-power dummy assemblies. Temperature distributions on the canister surface were compared against the normal loading condition using distributed line-probe data intended to represent fiber-optic temperature measurements. The results show that changes in assembly heat generation produce localized and spatially structured temperature deviations on the canister surface, with the magnitude and pattern depending on the assembly location and diversion scenario. These results suggest that distributed thermal monitoring could provide a complementary, non-intrusive signature for verifying dry cask contents and maintaining continuity of knowledge during extended storage. The method is not proposed as a standalone safeguards technology, but as a practical supplemental signature that may enhance existing radiation-based, visual, and containment/surveillance approaches.

中文摘要

为支持压水堆（PWR）常规碳-14（14C）释放的剂量评价，本研究建立了连接14C生成、化学形态及释放途径的设计阶段一体化框架。首先，利用多群中子通量和微观截面，计算主要的17O(n,α)14C与14N(n,p)14C反应产生的14C，再通过有效可迁移份额，将其转换为注入可交换碳库的有效源项。其次，建立五区室质量平衡模型，描述一回路中水相溶解无机碳/有机碳（DIC/DOC）、气态14CO2和烃类之间的相互转化、捕集及释放，以及在废离子交换树脂上的累积。通过蒙特卡罗不确定性传播，给出各释放途径及化学形态份额的设计分位值。结果显示稳定的归趋层级：气态释放占主导，树脂固体废物居次，液态流出物最少。化学形态明显依赖释放途径：气态释放以烃类为主，而液态废物处理和贮存中的形态转变，使液体排放点通常以无机DIC为主。这强调源项核算和剂量评价需要区分化学形态的监测。该框架无需电厂特定运行历史，即可形成可追溯的设计包络源项，并为监测优先级、气态释放管理及树脂废物负荷评价提供定量指导。

English abstract

To support dose assessment of routine carbon-14 (14C) releases from pressurized water reactors (PWRs), this study develops a design-stage integrated framework linking 14C production, chemical speciation, and release pathways. First, 14C production from the dominant 17O(n, α)14C and 14N(n,p)14C reactions is calculated using multigroup neutron fluxes and microscopic cross sections, and converted to an effective source term injected into an exchangeable carbon pool through an effective mobilizable fraction. Second, a five-compartment mass-balance model is formulated to describe interconversion, capture, and releases among aqueous-phase dissolved inorganic/organic carbon (DIC/DOC), gaseous 14CO2 and hydrocarbons, and accumulation on spent ion-exchange resins in the primary circuit. Monte Carlo uncertainty propagation provides design quantiles for pathway-specific releases and speciation fractions. Results show a stable fate hierarchy: gaseous releases dominate, resin solid waste is secondary, and liquid effluents are minimal. Speciation is strongly pathway dependent, with hydrocarbons dominating the gaseous releases, whereas inorganic DIC tends to dominate at the liquid discharge point due to speciation shift during liquid-waste treatment and storage. These results highlight the need for speciation-resolved monitoring for source term accounting and dose assessment. The framework produces a traceable design-envelope source term without plant-specific operating history and provides quantitative guidance for monitoring priorities, gaseous release management, and resin waste-load evaluation.
