中文摘要

重大核事故暴露了应急响应中快速预测源项的关键局限性。传统的高保真度模拟方法计算成本高昂，不适用于近实时决策。本研究开发了一种机器学习代理框架，用于预测CAP1400反应堆在假设的安全壳旁路场景下的放射性核素源项组合——惰性气体、碘和颗粒物。使用3000个RASCAL生成的模拟样本进行训练和测试，评估了三种集成模型：LightGBM、Stacking和CatBoost。LightGBM在未见过的测试数据上取得了最佳的整体性能（R²=0.05）。2 = 0.714），且推理速度接近瞬时（约 0.001 秒）。惰性气体表现出最高的预测准确率（测试 R²）。2 = 0.762），而颗粒物仍然是最具挑战性的目标（R）。2 = 0.58–0.74）。SHApley 加性解释 (SHAP) 分析确定质量数、堆芯暴露时间和颗粒物浓度是主要的预测特征。结果表明，机器学习代理模型具有为近实时核应急响应提供快速可靠源项估计的潜力。

English abstract

Major nuclear accidents reveal critical limitations in rapid source term prediction during emergency response. Conventional high-fidelity simulation approaches are computationally expensive and unsuitable for near-real-time decision-making. This study develops a machine learning surrogate framework to predict grouped radionuclide source terms-noble gases, iodine, and particulates-under a hypothetical containment-bypass scenario for the CAP1400 reactor. Using 3000 RASCAL-generated simulation samples for training and testing, three ensemble models were evaluated: LightGBM, Stacking, and CatBoost. LightGBM achieved the best overall performance on unseen test data (R2 = 0.714) with near-instantaneous inference (∼ 0.001 s). Noble gases showed the highest predictive accuracy (test R2 = 0.762), whereas particulates remained the most challenging target (R2 = 0.58–0.74). SHapley Additive exPlanations (SHAP) analysis identified mass number, core uncovered time and particulate concentration as dominant predictive features. The results demonstrate the potential of machine learning surrogate models to provide rapid and reliable source term estimates for near-real-time nuclear emergency response.
