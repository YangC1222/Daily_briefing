中文摘要

反应堆数字孪生等应用需要近实时精细三维全芯中子学/热工水力耦合计算，而直接输运因自由度巨大而昂贵。降阶模型虽能加速，但全局低维空间难表示强局部组件变化，离线又常需多次全芯高保真计算及庞大快照存储。本文提出低成本训练的可扩展组件式降阶模型AB-ROM。离线只需一个收敛全芯高保真耦合参考解，从中按组件类型提取边界入射角通量、热工反馈参数及总源分布，构造局部工况快照；再按组件类型、能群和角方向建立POD基库，在降阶系数空间以拉丁超立方采样扩充训练。在线每个组件在自身低维子空间求解参数化输运方程，通过间断Galerkin框架的迎风数值通量与相邻组件耦合。降阶输运与无Jacobian Newton–Krylov及子通道热工程序集成求稳态核热耦合。三维PWR MOX/UO₂基准、未见装载及2×2小堆测试中，keff误差在10 pcm内，最大棒功率误差小于2.62%；全芯自由度约减少4700倍，运行加速超过三个数量级。

English abstract

Advanced applications such as reactor digital twins require near-real-time, detailed 3D full-core coupled neutronic/thermal–hydraulic (N/TH) simulation, yet direct transport calculations remain prohibitively expensive because of their enormous discrete degrees of freedom. Although reduced-order models (ROMs) offer a promising route to acceleration, traditional global ROMs still face two major obstacles in 3D full-core coupled problems with distributed thermal–hydraulic feedback: a single global reduced space often requires many modes to represent strongly localized assembly-wise variations, while offline training usually depends on repeated full-core high-fidelity simulations and the storage of extremely large snapshot datasets. To address these challenges, this work proposes a scalable assembly-based reduced-order model (AB-ROM) with low-cost training for 3D full-core coupled N/TH simulation. In the offline stage, only one converged full-core high-fidelity coupled reference solution is required. From this solution, the boundary incident angular fluxes, assembly-wise thermal–hydraulic feedback parameters, and total source distributions are extracted by assembly type to construct local operating-condition snapshots. Proper orthogonal decomposition basis libraries are then generated by assembly type, energy group, and angular direction, and Latin hypercube sampling in the reduced coefficient space is adopted to efficiently enrich the training set. In the online stage, each assembly solves the parameterized transport equation in its own low-dimensional subspace and is coupled to neighboring assemblies through upwind numerical fluxes within a discontinuous Galerkin framework. The reduced transport system is integrated with a Jacobian-free Newton-Krylov solver and a subchannel TH code for steady-state coupled N/TH analysis. Numerical tests on the 3D PWR MOX/UO2 benchmark, unseen core loading patterns, and a 2 × 2 mini-core benchmark show strong accuracy and generalization capability, with keff errors within 10 pcm and a maximum pin-power error below 2.62%, while reducing full-core degrees of freedom by about 4700 times and achieving speedups of over three orders of magnitude.

中文摘要

螺旋十字形燃料（HCF）能够增强传热、提高冷却剂混合效果，并提升先进水冷反应堆的安全性，但其复杂的几何形状使得高保真度中子学/热工水力学（N/TH）耦合分析极具挑战性。本研究通过将三维特征线法（3D Method of Characteristics）程序OpenMOC与改进的子通道程序RESTAR-HF相结合，开发了一种适用于HCF堆芯的耦合框架。首先，将四分之一堆芯组件反馈计算结果与OpenMC/RESTAR-HF的计算结果进行比较。在此基础上，进行了全堆芯逐棒反馈耦合计算，并将该方法进一步扩展到从热满功率状态开始的弹棒事故的瞬态分析。对于该热满功率工况，结果表明，堆芯峰值功率在0.04秒时达到108.451%，同时燃料中心线温度、包壳表面温度以及最小偏离核态沸腾比（MDNBR）均满足所分析瞬态过程的预设准则。

English abstract

Helical cruciform fuel (HCF) offers enhanced heat transfer, stronger coolant mixing, and improved safety potential for advanced water-cooled reactors, but its complex geometry makes high-fidelity neutronics/thermal-hydraulic (N/TH) coupling analysis challenging. In this study, a coupled framework for HCF cores is developed by integrating the 3D Method of Characteristics code OpenMOC with the refined subchannel code RESTAR-HF. Quarter-core assembly-feedback calculations are first compared with OpenMC/RESTAR-HF results. On this basis, full-core pin-by-pin feedback coupling is performed, and the method is further extended to transient analysis of a rod ejection accident starting from hot full power. For this HFP case, results show that the peak core power reaches 108.451% at 0.04 s, while fuel centerline temperature, clad surface temperature, and minimum departure from nucleate boiling ratio satisfy the predefined criteria for the analyzed transient.

展开中英文摘要

中文摘要

热管微型反应堆（HPMR）结构紧凑、可运输，具有固有安全特征，适合交通受限且依赖高成本化石燃料的偏远地区。前期工作通过代理模型和强化学习优化，把技术经济因素纳入设计，以自下而上的成本估算最小化平准化电力成本（LCOE）。本文采用强化学习增强Pareto包络算法（PEARL），将该框架拓展为多目标优化，在安全与运行约束下同时最小化棒积分峰因子FΔh和LCOE。评价三种成本情景：轴向及控制鼓反射层均高成本、轴向反射层低成本、轴向及控制鼓反射层均低成本。结果表明，减小固体慢化剂半径、棒节距及控制鼓涂覆角，同时增大燃料高度，可有效降低FΔh。三种情景下，优化LCOE的一致策略为：在轴向反射层昂贵时减小其成本贡献、减少对控制鼓的依赖、以价格与石墨相当的轴向反射材料替代昂贵TRISO燃料，以及提高燃耗。PEARL能够探索多种情景的权衡，但代理预测与全阶模拟的偏差会导致部分方案不可行，尤其涉及燃料寿期和停堆裕量。因此本文应视为代理引导的权衡探索，而不是精确Pareto最优解的确定。放宽约束及改进代理模型可能进一步改善方法，这仍是持续研究方向。

English abstract

Heat-pipe microreactors (HPMRs) are compact and transportable nuclear power systems exhibiting inherent safety, well-suited for deployment in remote regions where access is limited and reliance on costly fossil fuels is prevalent. In prior work, we developed a design optimization framework that incorporates techno-economic considerations through surrogate modeling and reinforcement learning (RL)-based optimization, focusing solely on minimizing the levelized cost of electricity (LCOE) by using a bottom-up cost estimation approach. In this study, we extend that framework to a multi-objective optimization that uses the Pareto Envelope Augmented with Reinforcement Learning (PEARL) algorithm. The objectives include minimizing both the rod-integrated peaking factor (FΔh) and LCOE—subject to safety and operational constraints. We evaluate three cost scenarios: (1) a high-cost axial and drum reflectors, (2) a low-cost axial reflector, and (3) low-cost axial and drum reflectors. Our findings indicate that reducing the solid moderator radius, pin pitch, and drum coating angle—all while increasing the fuel height—effectively lowers FΔh. Across all three scenarios, four key strategies consistently emerged for optimizing LCOE: (1) minimizing the axial reflector contribution when costly, (2) reducing control drum reliance, (3) substituting expensive tri-structural isotropic (TRISO) fuel with axial reflector material priced at the level of graphite, and (4) maximizing fuel burnup. While PEARL demonstrates promise in navigating trade-offs across diverse design scenarios, discrepancies between surrogate model predictions and full-order simulations can lead to infeasibility in some cases, particularly for fuel lifetime and shutdown margin. Therefore, this study should be understood as a surrogate-guided trade-off exploration study rather than an effort to identify exact Pareto-optimal solutions. Further improvements are anticipated through constraint relaxation and surrogate development, constituting an ongoing area of investigation.

Dry storage systems are considered worldwide as the main solution to store spent nuclear fuel outside the spent fuel pools of nuclear power plants.
