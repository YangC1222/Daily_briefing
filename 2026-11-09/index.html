中文摘要

本文提出新实验方法，在折射率匹配的6×6燃料棒束中测量三维速度场，取得所展示方法中的最高分辨率；该棒束代表压水堆常见几何。作为方法开发的第一步，实验采用等温、单相条件，雷诺数低于典型压水堆运行值，不声称具有反应堆级动力相似性。测量采用新的单相机配置——plenoptic 3.0成像，在14×10×12 mm³区域内实现拉格朗日粒子追踪，即用单相机进行时间分辨的示踪粒子体积追踪。速度数据通过约束径向基函数后处理，保证流场平滑、物理一致，并满足不可压缩性和边界条件。该方法能够计算壁面剪切应力和近棒壁边界层剖面等流动统计量。与先前实验数据比较显示，其空间分辨率、统计收敛和物理一致性均得到改善。该高保真数据集可支持热工水力模型验证和对燃料组件内流动的认识。

English abstract

This study presents a novel experimental approach to measure three dimensional velocity fields with the highest demonstrated resolution in a refractive index matched 6 × 6 fuel rod bundle which is typical of pressurized water reactors rod bundles. As a first step in the development of this measurement methodology, the experiments are conducted under isothermal, single-phase conditions and at Reynolds numbers below prototypical pressurized water reactor operating conditions; dynamic similarity with reactor-grade conditions is not claimed. Measurements rely on a novel single camera configuration, plenoptic 3.0 imaging which enables Lagrangian particle tracking – i.e. time-resolved, volumetric tracking of tracer particles with a single camera – in a 14×10×12mm3 domain. Velocity data are post-processed with constrained radial basis function to ensure smooth and physically consistent velocity fields that satisfy incompressibility and boundary conditions. This methodology allows computation of accurate flow statistics, including wall shear stress and boundary layer profiles near rod surfaces. The results are compared to prior experimental datasets and demonstrate improved spatial resolution, statistical convergence, and enhanced physical consistency. This high-fidelity dataset supports the validation of thermal–hydraulic models and improves the understanding of flow behavior in fuel assembly geometries.

中文摘要

在压水反应堆（PWR）的再注水过程中，尤其是在冷却剂丧失事故（LOCA）或更严重的事故条件下，锆-4氧化、瞬态热工水力行为和包壳变形之间会形成强烈的热化学反馈。快速注入冷却剂会产生复杂的临界后热通量（post-CHF）沸腾状态和淬火前沿的传播，这些因素控制着包壳冷却、氧化动力学和瞬态氢气生成。本文综述了目前对这些耦合现象的理解，重点关注与状态相关的氧化动力学、脱离行为、预先存在的腐蚀层的保护作用、再润湿动力学以及临界后热通量传热。本文从设计基准、设计扩展和严重事故等不同工况下对证据进行了解释，以区分原型LOCA行为与机理或边界观测结果。本文对现有热工水力系统代码和计算流体动力学 (CFD) 方法的能力和局限性进行了深入评估，尤其关注高温氧化、氢气生成、多相流耦合以及再注水过程中的热力机械效应。综述指出，在低于 1000°C 的氧化动力学、氧化皮完整性、局部蒸汽可用性、膨胀和破裂几何形状以及模型间耦合等方面仍存在诸多不确定性，并强调了在早期再注水过程中改进瞬态氢气生成和包壳响应预测方面的研究需求。

English abstract

During reflood in a Pressurized Water Reactor (PWR), particularly following a Loss-of-Coolant Accident (LOCA) or under more severe accident conditions, strong thermo-chemical feedback can develop between Zircaloy-4 oxidation, transient thermal-hydraulic behaviour, and cladding deformation. Rapid coolant injection generates complex post-critical heat flux (post-CHF) boiling regimes and quench front propagation, which govern cladding cooling, oxidation kinetics, and transient hydrogen generation. This review synthesizes current understanding of these coupled phenomena, with emphasis on regime-dependent oxidation kinetics, breakaway behaviour, the protective influence of pre-existing corrosion layers, rewetting dynamics, and post-CHF heat transfer. Evidence is interpreted across design-basis, design-extension, and severe-accident conditions to distinguish prototypic LOCA behaviour from mechanistic or bounding observations. The capabilities and limitations of existing thermal-hydraulic system codes and Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) approaches are critically evaluated, particularly with respect to high-temperature oxidation, hydrogen generation, multiphase-flow coupling, and thermo-mechanical effects during reflood. The review identifies persistent uncertainties in sub-1000°C oxidation kinetics, oxide-scale integrity, local steam availability, ballooned and burst geometries, and model-to-model coupling, and highlights research needs for improved prediction of transient hydrogen generation and cladding response during early reflood.

中文摘要

超临界自然循环回路（SCNCL）拟临界区热物性强非线性变化，使常规亚临界关联式难以预测稳态流动。本文基于基本物理考虑提出稳态质量流量关联式，并用超临界CO₂和水的实验数据校核，在一定工况和工质范围内表现出可靠预测能力。另外用水进行Ledinegg流量逸走分析，评价稳态稳定性及回路布置影响。竖直加热器—水平冷却器（VHHC）布置具有唯一稳态，而水平加热器—水平冷却器（HHHC）可能存在多个稳态，说明稳定性对几何布置敏感。参数研究考察压力、直径和高度对临界稳定边界（MSB）的作用，再进行瞬态计算，研究密度波振荡（DWO）风险及其边界。最后比较两种边界以识别安全运行区域。

English abstract

The prediction of steady state flow behavior in supercritical natural circulation loops (SCNCLs) is challenging due to strong nonlinear variations of thermophysical properties in the pseudo-critical region, which limit the applicability of conventional subcritical correlations. In this work, a correlation for predicting steady state mass flow rate in SCNCLs is proposed based on fundamental physical considerations and validated against experimental data for supercritical carbon dioxide (SCO2) and water (SH2O). The proposed correlation demonstrates reliable predictive capability over a range of operating conditions and working fluids. In addition, a Ledinegg excursion analysis is performed with H2O to assess steady state stability and the influence of loop orientation. The results show that a vertical heater horizontal cooler (VHHC) configuration exhibits a unique steady state, whereas a horizontal heater horizontal cooler (HHHC) configuration may lead to multiple steady states, highlighting the strong sensitivity of SCNCL stability to geometric configuration. A comprehensive parametric study is performed to explore the influence of different parameters like loop pressure, diameter and height on the related marginal stability boundary (MSB). Next, transient simulations are carried out to investigate the risk of density wave oscillations (DWOs) and to determine the related MSB, i.e., from DWOs. Finally, both the MSBs are compared with an effort to identify the safe operating regions of the SCNCL.

中文摘要

本文评价低浓缩铀燃料大叻核研究堆（DNRR）的有效缓发中子份额βeff、中子代时间Λ及瞬发中子寿命lp，并量化βeff的核数据不确定性。采用MCNP6.3与ENDF/B-VIII.0、ENDF/B-VIII.1、JEFF-3.3、JENDL-5及CENDL-3.2核库开展高保真计算。参数通过伴随加权法求取，另以prompt k-ratio法独立比较βeff。不同核库所得βeff、Λ和lp分别为731–769 pcm、80.31–80.91 μs及80.45–81.38 μs。基准ENDF/B-VIII.0伴随加权结果分别为739 pcm、80.31 μs和80.45 μs。灵敏度/不确定性分析表明，βeff总不确定性主要受Al-27、B-10、Be-9和H-1弹性散射截面影响，其中Al-27贡献显著。结果为DNRR动力学分析提供定量基础，并强调安全和许可评价需采用完整协方差数据。

English abstract

This study evaluates the kinetic parameters, including the effective delayed neutron fraction (βeff), neutron generation time (Λ), and prompt neutron lifetime (lp), for the Dalat Nuclear Research Reactor (DNRR) with low enriched uranium fuel. Additionally, the nuclear data-induced uncertainty of βeff was quantified. High fidelity simulations were performed using the MCNP6.3 code in conjunction with multiple latest evaluated nuclear data libraries, including ENDF/B-VIII.0, ENDF/B-VIII.1, JEFF-3.3, JENDL-5, and CENDL-3.2. The kinetic parameters (βeff, Λ, lp) were evaluated using the adjoint weighted method, and the prompt k-ratio method was applied to provide an independent comparison of βeff. Depending on the data library, the calculated values for βeff, Λ, and lp ranged from 731 to 769 pcm, 80.31 to 80.91μs, and 80.45 to 81.38μs, respectively. For the baseline ENDF/B-VIII.0 library, the adjoint weighted calculations yielded the values of 739 pcm for βeff, 80.31μs for Λ, and 80.45μs for lp. Sensitivity and uncertainty analysis revealed that the aggregate βeff, uncertainty is primarily driven by the elastic scattering cross sections of Al-27, B-10, Be-9, and H-1, with Al-27 contributing significantly to the total uncertainty budget. These results provide a quantitative basis for the DNRR’s kinetics analysis and underscore the necessity of employing comprehensive covariance data for reactor safety and licensing assessments.
