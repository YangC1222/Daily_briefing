English abstract

A loss-of-coolant accident (LOCA) initiated from a shutdown-cooling condition (shutdown-cooling LOCA) was investigated in the ATLAS integral effect test facility as the C4.2 test of the OECD-ATLAS3 joint project. The test simulated a 50 % direct vessel injection (DVI) line break of APR1400, with two operator actions: PAFS (passive auxiliary feedwater system) actuation on one steam generator after a 30-minute grace delay, and manual safety injection on a surrogate heater-temperature trigger. Under these low-power, low-pressure conditions, loop seal clearing and reformation developed asymmetrically: after both intermediate legs cleared, only the PAFS-cooled loop reformed its loop seal; the other remained cleared, holding the hot leg and downcomer at nearly equal pressure. This asymmetric state governs the inter-loop pressure distribution, yet the core inventory continued to deplete - PAFS removes heat but cannot make up lost inventory - and active injection was required. A limited MARS-KS assessment reproduces the pre-injection transient (heat-up onset within 1.3 %), and a PAFS-configuration sensitivity study indicates that passive cooling delays but does not prevent the excursion. The C4.2 dataset provides a unique integral characterization of a shutdown-cooling LOCA with a passive secondary heat sink and a code-assessment benchmark in a largely unvalidated low-pressure regime.

English abstract

Despite its widespread adoption in Industrial Control Systems (ICS), the Controller Area Network (CAN) lacks built-in security, rendering it vulnerable to cyber threats. Existing anomaly detection methods for CAN are hindered by the lack of labeled attack data, diverse attack patterns, and stringent resource constraints, particularly in nuclear applications, where KB-level memory, stringent real-time latency, and complex physical transients pose unique challenges. To address these challenges, we present CANCLLA, an unsupervised autoencoder framework with parallel CNN and BiLSTM encoders to jointly capture spatial payload patterns and temporal dependencies, enhanced by attention and feature-wise threshold selection.
