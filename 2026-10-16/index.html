中文摘要

为节能和电网调峰，大型压水堆需要参与负荷调节。模型预测控制（MPC）比传统PID更适于多变量控制，但宽工况下的非线性限制固定模型MPC的表现。本文利用线性变参数（LPV）模型的非线性近似能力和较低计算成本，提出用于非线性华龙一号模型的LPV-MPC。先以渐近方法辨识局部模型，再用最小二乘估计权函数，最后以全数据迭代优化。局部模型通过时域与频域验证，优化后LPV模型在高低负荷阶跃响应上均与对象接近。与原控制系统和固定模型MPC比较：100%满功率下性能相近；50%满功率下调节更快，蒸汽发生器水位波动抑制更强。相对固定模型MPC表现出更好的适应性和鲁棒性，大范围过渡工况也显示更好的负荷跟踪性能。

English abstract

For energy conservation, large-scale Pressurized Water Reactors (PWRs) are required to participate in grid peak shaving. Compared to traditional PID control, Model Predictive Control (MPC) algorithm offers advantages in handling multivariable control problems. However, PWR systems exhibit strong nonlinearity under wide-range operation, limiting conventional MPC performance. Therefore, Linear Parameter Varying (LPV) models, valued for their nonlinear approximation capability and low computational cost, are employed in nonlinear MPC. This paper proposes an LPV model-based MPC algorithm to enhance the control of a nonlinear HPR1000 model. An iterative optimization strategy is adopted for LPV model identification: first, initial local models are identified using the asymptotic method; then, parameters of the weighting functions are estimated via least squares; finally, the LPV model is optimized iteratively based on the total data. The local models demonstrate good performance in both time-domain and frequency-domain validation. The optimized LPV model shows high similarity to the plant’s step responses at both high and low loads, confirming its suitability for MPC controller. The LPV-MPC is compared with both the original control system and a fixed-model MPC (F-MPC) through test scenarios at 100 % and 50 % FP. The results indicate that the LPV-MPC controller offers comparable performance at 100 % FP, but exhibits faster regulation speed and stronger capability in suppressing SG water level fluctuations at 50 % FP. The comparison with F-MPC demonstrates that the LPV-MPC possesses stronger adaptability and robustness. Meanwhile, a large-scale transition scenario was implemented. The comparative analysis confirms that the LPV-MPC controller possesses superior load-following performance.

中文摘要

自然循环广泛用于核反应堆非能动余热排出。微胶囊相变材料（mPCM）浆液可将潜热储存与相变密度变化结合，但高浓度引起的黏度增大可能抵消换热收益。本文将计入温度与浓度影响、经标定的平台黏度关联式加入一维稳态自然循环程序NCLS1，并以纯水实验及Vijayan关联式验证单相求解器。研究浓度、加热功率、回路高度及管径的影响。结果表明，mPCM并不总能改善自然循环；只有形成在加热器熔化、冷却器凝固的相变循环时才有冷却收益。研究范围内，有利区间为10–20 W、质量浓度5%–10%，此时潜热强化与黏性阻力达到平衡。增大高度可拓宽有利区，管径则需避免沸腾和低剪切状态。研究揭示潜热、浮升力和黏度的耦合作用，为核非能动排热候选工质评价提供参考。

English abstract

Natural-circulation is widely used in passive heat-removal systems for nuclear reactors. Microencapsulated phase change material (mPCM) slurry is a potential working fluid because it can couple latent heat storage with phase-change-induced density variation. However, the viscosity increase at higher mPCM concentrations may raise flow resistance and offset the heat-transfer benefit. To evaluate this trade-off, a calibrated plateau-viscosity correlation accounting for temperature and concentration effects was incorporated into a one-dimensional steady-state natural-circulation loop (NCL) program, termed NCLS1. The single-phase natural-circulation solver was verified against pure-water experimental data and the Vijayan correlation. The effects of mPCM concentration, heating power, loop height, and pipe diameter were evaluated using NCLS1. The results show that mPCM slurry does not always improve natural-circulation heat transfer. Cooling benefit appears only when a phase-change circulation is established, allowing the slurry to melt in the heater and solidify in the cooler. Under the investigated conditions, the favorable operating region occurred at heating powers of 10–20 W and mPCM mass concentrations of 5–10 wt%, where latent heat enhancement balanced viscosity-induced flow resistance. Increasing loop height broadened this region, whereas pipe diameter required careful selection to avoid boiling and low-shear conditions. These findings clarify the coupled effects of latent heat, buoyancy, and viscosity in mPCM slurry loops and provide guidance for evaluating candidate working fluids in passive nuclear heat-removal systems.

中文摘要

本文用粒子图像测速（PIV）研究窄矩形通道中中心方形及菱形堵塞的流动特性，堵塞比α=30%，雷诺数1268–4438。通过时均流动、湍流统计和本征正交分解（POD）考察堵塞几何对涡演化及湍动能耗散的影响。菱形尖角诱发局部高涡量区，涡核更强；方形直角导致流动滞止，湍动能耗散分布更宽，对尾迹扰动更强。POD分析与流场重构确认两种堵塞均有周期性涡脱落。结果可为板状燃料组件堵塞事故模拟提供实验数据。

English abstract

This study investigates the flow characteristics in a rectangular channel with center square and diamond blockages (α =30%) at varying Reynolds numbers (Re = 1268 – 4438) using particle image velocimetry (PIV). Time-averaged analysis, turbulence statistics, and proper orthogonal decomposition (POD) were employed to examine the effects of blockage geometry on vortex evolution and turbulent kinetic energy dissipation. Key findings demonstrate that the diamond blockage induces localized high-vorticity zones due to its sharp vertices, resulting in stronger vortex cores. In contrast, the square blockage triggers flow stagnation due to its right-angled geometry, exhibiting broader turbulent kinetic energy dissipation and imposing stronger disturbances on the wake fluid. POD analysis and flow field reconstruction confirm the periodic vortex shedding in both blockage conditions. The present results can provide effective experimental data for blockage accident simulations of plate-type fuel assemblies.

蒙卡多群截面生成常用Legendre展开描述各向异性散射，但截断可能产生负散射截面。本文在RMC中提出角度离散的宏观散射矩阵，通过连续能量蒙卡直接径迹长度计数生成，并用于完整两步蒙卡流程。该表示在所有角区间中按构造保持非负，避免Legendre截断的负值。还给出全堆计数方案，减小单组件反射边界引起的环境效应误差，并实现适于该流程的SPH迭代。在压水堆组件、控制棒插入/退出的MOX组件、四组件小堆芯和C5G7上验证；参考为连续能量RMC，并与OpenMC的Legendre结果比较非负性。主要结果包括：小堆芯采用反射边界群常数时约600 pcm的特征值偏差，使用全堆群常数后降至6 pcm。

English abstract

The Legendre expansion, widely adopted for representing anisotropic scattering in Monte Carlo multi-group cross section generation, suffers from truncation artifacts that can lead to negative scattering cross sections. This paper develops an angle-discretized macroscopic scattering matrix representation, generated by direct track-length tallying during continuous-energy Monte Carlo transport and used as the working representation throughout a complete Monte Carlo two-step workflow in the Reactor Monte Carlo code RMC. The representation is non-negative by construction across all angular bins, avoiding the negative scattering cross sections that arise from Legendre truncation. Supporting engineering elements required for the complete workflow—including a full-core tallying scheme that mitigates environmental-effect errors associated with reflective single-assembly boundary conditions, and a Monte Carlo–adapted SPH iteration for use within the workflow—are also described. The methodology is validated on a PWR assembly, a MOX fuel assembly with control-rod insertion and withdrawal, a four-assembly mini-core, and the C5G7 benchmark. Reference solutions are obtained from continuous-energy RMC simulations, and the angle-discretized scattering matrices are compared against OpenMC Legendre-expansion results to illustrate the non-negativity property of the proposed representation. Key results include a reduction of the mini-core eigenvalue deviation from approximately 600 pcm under reflective-boundary group constants to 6 pcm under full-core group constants.

钍基熔盐堆芯的高温燃料熔盐既是载热剂,又是核反应的热源,是完全不同于其它固体燃料的一种全新核反应堆燃料利用技术。石墨材料具有高纯度,耐高温、耐腐蚀、高导电、高导热等优异性能,是液态熔盐反应堆慢化中子的首选材料。尽管石墨材料具有诸多优异特性,但由于堆芯慢化剂石墨和液态高温熔盐直接接触,熔盐将在石墨孔隙中扩散和渗透,使熔盐在石墨孔隙区域富集,导致局部中子通量较高;另外,中子慢化过程中,将引发石墨晶格畸变,形成大量的间隙原子-空位缺陷,辐照损伤较多,导致石墨材料热电性能降低,几何尺寸变化;且间隙原子处在石墨的层与层之间,容易团聚并储能,储能瞬间释放出大量热,使石墨慢化剂局部区域温度陡升,对反应堆造成危害。此外,渗透到石墨慢化剂孔洞中的熔盐经中子反应后形成Xe、Kr等裂变产物不易被去除,不利于反应堆的稳定高效运行。

中文摘要

热化学碘硫/混合硫循环是最有前景的利用高温气冷堆工艺热实现大规模制氢的技术。硫酸分解器作为实现反应堆与制氢工艺的关键设备,其性能对核能制氢的安全性和制氢效率具有重要影响。刺刀管式碳化硅反应器在耐高温、耐硫酸腐蚀以及热量回收等方面具有优势,对其进行结构优化能够提升反应性能。在前期研究基础之上,本文研制了新型结构尺寸的刺刀管式碳化硅反应器,并开展了热工水力学和硫酸分解实验研究。研究过程中监测了反应器进出口流体温度、外壁温度、压降以及产氧速率等关键参数,根据这些参数分析了水和硫酸在碳化硅反应器内部的状态变化;同时采用模拟软件Aspen Plus搭建了热量计算模型,借助模型计算了硫酸分解过程所需热量以及反应器在进水和进硫酸过程中的热负荷,分析了反应器的换热性能。研究结果表明:新型结构碳化硅反应器能够降低组装难度并提高反应通量,产氧速率最高达到154 L·h-1,相比于原始结构反应器,反应通量提高了50%;此外,碳化硅反应器能够实现产物热量回收,降低热量损耗,碳化硅反应器内物料升温过程约有30%的热量来自反应产物。该研究为列管换热式硫酸分解器的研制奠定了基础。
