中文摘要

单金属中间层可保护标准锆合金管，是事故容错燃料包壳候选，但界面反应及共析出可能消耗保护层，降低长期有效性。本文研究CrFeNiMoAl多金属高熵合金涂层，重点考察难熔金属Mo在高温条件下的作用。用磁控溅射在Zircaloy-4上制备Mo含量0–30%的四种涂层HEAMo0–3，分别进行LiOH/H3BO3介质电化学试验、常压1200℃保温5 min的加速退火，以及360℃、18.6 MPa蒸汽腐蚀试验。适宜Mo含量有利于以体心立方纳米晶多相组织为主的结构，改善强度和屏障性能。摘要所列屏障性能由优到劣为HEAMo1（10.1% Mo）、HEAMo2（20.7%）、HEAMo3（30%）、HEAMo0（0%）。过高Mo使组织过饱和、涂层退化、Mo离子释放增加，并形成腐蚀离子通向锆基体的额外路径。本文不研究界面元素扩散，重点在冷却剂化学与涂层成分对下一代事故容错包壳耐久性的影响。

English abstract

Single-metal interlayer coating systems reliably protect standard Zircaloy tubes, making them suitable candidates for accident-tolerant fuel (ATF) cladding in nuclear service environments. However, they also deplete during interfacial metallic interactions (e.g., during Nb-Zr interdiffusion) and co-precipitate (e.g., when Mo interlays between Cr and Zr), thereby losing their availability as protective coatings for long-term use. In the quest to address these shortcomings, the present study explores a multiple-metal-engineered high-entropy alloy (HEA) coating (CrFeNiMoAl), proposed as a reliable alternative, particularly based on Mo as a refractory metal, for testing under high-thermal conditions. Four coating variants with Mo content ranging from 0 to 30 % (HEAMo0-3) were deposited on Zircaloy-4 via magnetron sputtering. To examine their corrosion behavior, these coatings were comparatively tested under conditions simulating Pressurized Water Reactor (PWR) cooling systems via electrochemistry in LiOH/H3BO3, by accelerated annealing (1200 °C at atmospheric pressure for 5 min), and subcritical corrosion tests at 360 °C in 18.6 MPa steam. In addition to material strengthening, improved barrier performance is attributed to the optimal Mo content, which supports the presence of predominantly body-centered cubic (BCC) nanocrystalline multiphase structure. Barrier performance depends largely on Mo content in the following order of increasing strengths: HEAMo1 (with 10.1 % Mo) ˃ HEAMo2 (with 20.7 % Mo) ˃ HEAMo3 (with 30 % Mo) ˃ HEAMo0 (with 0 % Mo). Higher Mo content oversaturated the internal coating structure, leading to coating degradation, increased Mo-ion release, and the formation of additional pathways for corrosive ions to reach the Zr surface. This work does not aim to investigate interfacial elemental diffusion, as ample research already addresses this topic. The essential insight from the present study concerns the impact of coolant chemistry and coating content on the performance and durability of ATF-cladding materials for next-generation nuclear reactor fuel systems.

在印度压水重水堆中，严重失水事故伴应急堆芯冷却失效及慢化剂完全排空可能导致压力管失效。本文用ANSYS 2023 R1研究220 MWe机组单燃料通道的热行为、换热与熔化进程。稳态计算热流密度900–4950 W/m²，用于确定温度分布和熔化起始热流；瞬态相变计算取4400–4950 W/m²以确定熔化速率。压力管—排管组件存在明显温度不对称，压力管峰值温度从396.25℃升至2080.60℃，预测熔化起始热流约4400 W/m²。随热流增大，完全熔化时间从80 s降至8 s，熔化速率指数约增大一个数量级，表现出强非线性。提出压力管—排管热耦合指数TCI、归一化温度比NTR和熔化速率指数MRI，定量表征热脆弱性及熔化进程，为印度重水堆严重事故分析提供工程分析方法。

English abstract

Failure of the Pressure tube under severe Loss of Coolant Accident (LOCA) with Emergency Core Cooling System (ECCS) failure and complete moderator voiding is a serious issue in Indian Pressurized Heavy Water Reactors (IPHWRs). The present study investigates the thermal behaviour, heat transfer characteristics, and melting progression of a single fuel channel of the 220 MWe IPHWR using ANSYS 2023 R1. The steady-state simulations were performed for heat fluxes ranging from 900 to 4950 W/m2 to determine the temperature distributions and to identify the melting initiation heat flux for the onset of melting, while the heat flux ranges 4400–4950 W/m2 were used for the transient phase change simulations to determine the melting rate. The results show that there is a significant temperature asymmetry within the pressure tube-calandria tube assembly. The peak temperature of the pressure tube was raised from 396.25°C to 2080.60°C and the melting initiation heat flux was predicted to be approximately 4400 W/m2. With increasing heat flux, the complete melting time decreased from 80 s to 8 s, with an increase of about ten in the Melting Rate Index (MRI), thus representing very non-linear melting behaviour. The proposed PT-CT Thermal Coupling Index (TCI), Normalized Temperature Ratio (NTR) and Melting Rate Index (MRI) are quantitative assessments of the thermal vulnerability and melting progress. The proposed approach will be useful for severe accident analysis and safety assessment of Indian PHWR systems for engineering applications.

中文摘要

本文在ALEPH燃耗程序中评估（α,n）中子源项，对比重锕系氧化物基体的乏燃料与轻低原子序数基体的镅铍源。分别用解析厚靶产额及直接α粒子输运计算源强和α能谱。乏燃料中，ALEPH输运求解器MCNP给出的产额比厚靶法低16%，而镅铍源两者相差约1%以内。差异来自MCNP内部阻止本领表：在重基体中偏离已验证的SRIM与NIST ASTAR数据，在轻基体中则没有此问题。因此目前ALEPH中可靠的（α,n）源项应采用厚靶产额；重基体不宜用该MCNP α输运设置，若需显式输运，应选阻止本领经验证的程序，如PHITS。另外发现与基体无关的MCNP α注量能量分箱伪差：在相邻能箱间重新分配注量，不偏置积分量，仅影响微分能谱计数。利用PHITS屏蔽标定实验室模型，评估JENDL-5、JEFF-4.0、TENDL-2025中子发射谱对镅铍源周围剂量当量率H*(10)的影响，并与ISO 8529-1参考谱比较，结果支持JENDL-5作为ALEPH默认库。

English abstract

The modeling of (α,n) neutron source terms in the ALEPH depletion code is assessed for two contrasting systems: spent nuclear fuel, a heavy actinide-oxide matrix, and an americium-beryllium (AmBe) source, a light low-Z matrix. In each, the source intensity and alpha spectrum are computed both by the analytic thick target yield and by direct alpha transport. For the spent fuel the yield from MCNP, ALEPH’s transport solver, falls 16% below the thick target yield, whereas for AmBe the two agree to within about 1%. The discrepancy is traced to MCNP’s internal stopping power table, which deviates from the validated SRIM and NIST ASTAR data in the heavy matrix but not the light one. The thick target yield is therefore at present the only reliable route to (α,n) source terms within ALEPH: MCNP alpha transport should not be used in heavy matrices, and an explicit transport estimate should come from a code with validated stopping powers, such as PHITS. A separate, matrix-independent energy-binning artifact in the MCNP alpha fluence spectrum is also identified; it redistributes fluence between neighboring bins without biasing integral quantities, and matters only when a differential spectrum is tallied. The neutron emission spectra from JENDL-5, JEFF-4.0 and TENDL-2025 are assessed through their effect on the ambient dose equivalent rate H*(10), computed for the AmBe source in a PHITS model of a shielded calibration laboratory and benchmarked against the reference ISO 8529-1 spectrum. The comparison supports JENDL-5 as the ALEPH default.

本文在OpenModelica中建立可复现的熔盐研究堆降阶动态模型，用于启动至临界、非线性阶跃瞬态及随功率变化的小信号响应。模型在统一框架内结合考虑循环燃料输运的修正点动力学、线性温度反应性反馈、分组衰变热和集总热工水力节点，可选单区或九区堆芯。启动分析采用分阶段外源辅助临界方案，逐步增加一次流量并量化缓发中子先驱核漂移补偿，最终排热爬坡阶段达到约1 MW。动态分析包括反应性阶跃、流量降低和失去排热，以及10⁻³–10¹ rad/s的多功率正弦扫频；每个频点检查稳定过程，在低功率下用锁相估计分离受迫响应与缓慢的功率—温度振荡。结果表明存在随工作点变化的谐振带通响应，功率升高使峰值进入扫频区。堆芯分区带来适度阻尼：额定功率下九区峰值增益比单区约低2.4 dB。开源实现把已有熔盐堆降阶动力学方法连接到透明、具有代码仓库支持的流程，适于设计阶段运行与控制研究。

English abstract

This paper presents a reproducible reduced-order dynamic model of the Molten Salt Research Reactor (MSRR) in OpenModelica for three analyses: startup to criticality, nonlinear step transients, and power-dependent small-signal response. The model combines transport-aware modified point kinetics for circulating fuel, linear temperature-reactivity feedback, grouped decay-heat dynamics, and lumped thermal-hydraulic nodes in a shared framework with selectable one-region and nine-region core representations. The startup analysis examines a staged, source-assisted approach to criticality with progressive primary-flow increases, quantifying the associated delayed-neutron precursor drift compensation; the complete sequence reaches ∼1MW during the final heat-removal ramp. Dynamic simulations evaluate large step disturbances (reactivity insertions, flow reductions, and loss of heat rejection) alongside multi-power sinusoidal frequency sweeps from 10−3 to 101rad/s, supported by per-point settling checks and, at low power, a lock-in estimator that separates the forced response from a slow power–temperature oscillation. Results demonstrate an operating-point-dependent resonant (band-pass) response whose peak migrates into the sweep band with increasing power. Core segmentation introduces a modest damping effect, with the nine-region model exhibiting about 2.4dB lower peak gain than the one-region model at nominal power. The resulting open-source OpenModelica implementation links established reduced-order MSR dynamics methodology to a transparent, repository-backed workflow suitable for design-stage operational and control studies.

English abstract

Reliable reconstruction of spatial source-term distributions is important for the radiological characterization of activated bioshield concrete during nuclear power plant decommissioning. Although full-field neutron transport calculations remain necessary, performing mesh-wise activation calculations or radiochemical analyses over an entire structure is computationally and practically demanding. This study developed a Gaussian Process Regression (GPR) approach to reconstruct spatial source-term distributions from limited data. The OPR-1000 bioshield concrete region was discretized into 7500 mesh cells, and reference specific-activity fields for Co-60, Eu-152, and U-234 were generated using MCNP6.3 and SCALE6.3/ORIGEN. Reconstruction performance was compared among a coordinate-only GPR (CO-GPR) using spatial coordinates, a flux-only log-linear regression (FO-LR) baseline, and a flux-informed GPR (FI-GPR) incorporating total neutron flux. CO-GPR reproduced the overall spatial trends but retained localized residual errors as the number of training points increased. FI-GPR reproduced the reference fields with 10–20 training points and reduced both average and maximum errors. It achieved lower errors than FO-LR for Eu-152 and U-234, while maintaining high accuracy for Co-60, demonstrating consistent reconstruction performance across the three radionuclides. It also yielded narrower GPR posterior prediction intervals associated with sparse-data reconstruction, although empirical coverage differed among the radionuclides. The proposed method therefore reduces the number of required mesh-wise ORIGEN calculations while retaining the full-field MCNP flux calculation.

中文摘要

谱移式反应堆通过改变中子慢化性能来控制反应性,通过谱移材料的动作,实现反应堆中子能谱的调节。本文通过理论分析谱移式反应性控制机理,并提出一种谱移材料中子学性能评价方法,通过能谱软化因子、谱移因子以及谱移贡献因子,量化表征谱移材料对于能谱移动以及堆芯反应性的影响程度。选取典型谱移材料,对不同谱移材料的中子学性能进行了分析论证,确定ZrH1.6是优选的谱移慢化材料。在此基础上,开展了ZrH1.6谱移慢化材料对堆芯反应性控制能力及温度反馈效应的影响分析,研究表明,较粗的谱移慢化棒会因过慢化使中子能谱调节能力下降,同时ZrH1.6的“栅元效应”会使堆芯反应性反馈趋于正反馈效应。上述问题可以通过调整慢化棒材料密度、设置中心气腔等方式加以解决,其中气腔中填充BeO作为反射材料可以带来更好的能谱调节能力,同时减小固体慢化材料对温度反馈效应的影响。
