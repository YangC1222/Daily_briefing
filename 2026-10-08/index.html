中文摘要

随着研究堆提高功率以增加医用同位素生产和材料试验能力，较高的燃耗及衰变热会显著压缩假设事故条件下乏燃料贮存设施的热工裕量，因此需要系统重新评估其安全限值。本研究对高功率研究堆乏燃料贮存系统开展失水事故安全分析。建立事故模型，计算水位下降、燃料组件温度及相关安全响应，并进一步分析屏蔽水层厚度对人员剂量的影响。结果显示，在本文所研究的池体几何和辐射源项条件下，为使人员剂量率低于采用的10 μSv/h准则，燃料组件架顶部上方约需300 cm水柱。这些结果为高功率研究堆乏燃料贮存系统的安全评价和运行规划提供定量参考。研究动机还包括高功率运行导致燃料辐射源项和余热负荷上升，事故分析因而同时涉及热工安全与辐射防护。阅读时需注意，摘要所述失水事故模型针对其特定贮存池配置；300 cm是满足该研究剂量限值的计算结果，并非通用设计标准。用于工程判断前还需核实燃料装载、池水位变化过程、人员位置及剂量计算模型。

English abstract

As research reactors undergo power uprates to enhance medical isotope production and materials testing, higher fuel burnup and elevated decay-heat levels significantly reduce the thermal margins of spent fuel storage facilities under postulated accident conditions, necessitating a systematic reevaluation of their safety limits. This study presents a safety analysis of the spent fuel storage system of a 10 MW research reactor under a postulated loss-of-pool-water accident. A multiphysics framework combining neutronic analysis, transient thermal–hydraulic modeling, and radiation transport calculations is employed to evaluate the safety limits of a high-density two-tier rack configuration. The results show that the effective multiplication factor remains well below unity for all investigated configurations, indicating preserved subcriticality. However, thermal–hydraulic margins are significantly reduced for fuel with only 24 h of post-irradiation cooling, resulting in cladding blistering after approximately 5 h of air exposure. In contrast, a decay period of 144 h provides sufficient passive cooling to prevent cladding degradation under complete loss of pool water. Radiation shielding analysis further indicates that a water column of approximately 300 cm above the top of the spent fuel assembly rack is required to reduce the personnel dose rate below the adopted criterion of 10 μSv/h for the pool geometry and radiation source considered in this study. These findings provide quantitative guidance for safety evaluation and operational planning in high-power research reactor spent fuel storage systems.

中文摘要

超临界二氧化碳（S-CO₂）直接循环核能系统的设计与改进，对于推进绿色能源应用具有重要意义，其关键在于掌握S-CO₂流体的传热特性。既有研究主要关注近临界点附近的传热，而对高温高压条件下的传热行为关注不足。本研究在竖直管中开展高温高压S-CO₂传热实验，考察不同热工条件下的换热响应及其变化规律。结果表明，传热系数受热流密度影响。基于这些发现，研究提出新的传热关联式；该关联式在本研究实验数据和外部数据上均表现出较好的准确性与泛化能力，可用于高温高压条件下的传热预测，并为S-CO₂反应堆燃料组件设计提供参考和数据支持。作者还将多种既有传热关联式与当前高温高压实验及外部实验数据作比较。比较结果指出，Krasnoshchekov关联式在所用数据范围内预测精度最高；Chen–Fang关联式表现出较稳定的适用性。研究观察到热流密度以及浮升相关参数、流体密度和黏度修正会影响传热预测。该结果强调，在高温高压工况应用时，应将物性变化和自然对流影响纳入关联式评估，避免直接沿用近临界区结论。

English abstract

The design and refinement of the supercritical carbon dioxide (S-CO2) direct cycle nuclear energy system are crucial for advancing the application of green energy and mitigating the energy crisis. The key to this lies in the research on the heat transfer characteristics of S-CO2 fluid. Previous studies primarily focus on the heat transfer behavior of S-CO2 near the critical point, leaving a significant gap in understanding its characteristics under high-temperature and high-pressure conditions. To address this gap, this paper investigates the heat transfer characteristics of S-CO2 fluid in a vertical tube at pressure and fluid temperature up to 14 MPa and 451℃, respectively. By evaluating the classical heat transfer correlations, it was found that the Krasnoshchekov et al. (1964) correlation provides the most accurate predictions, while the Chen and Fang (2014) correlation is the most stable. The study found that the corrections of Gr number, density, and viscosity have a significant impact on the prediction results. The magnitude of these correction effects is influenced by heat flux. Based on these findings, a new heat transfer correlation was proposed, which demonstrates excellent accuracy and generalization capability for both the experimental data in this study and external data, providing a reliable tool for heat transfer prediction under high-temperature and high-pressure conditions. This paper can serve as a reference and provide data support for the design of S-CO2 reactor fuel assembly.

中文摘要

熔盐堆（MSR）相较传统轻水堆具有若干优势，但核材料保障仍是其部署面临的障碍。本文所研究MSR设计采用非传统液态燃料，并存在额外物料流，这给核保障带来挑战；这些特征使传统的实物计数保障方式难以直接适用，也产生了新的材料监测需求。本研究应用机器学习方法识别MSR中的钚转移。研究利用反应堆运行及核材料流相关信息建立识别模型，并评价其区分正常运行与转移情景的能力。结果显示，数据驱动方法可用于识别所考察的钚转移行为，为MSR核材料保障提供分析工具。摘要未给出可据以补写的完整性能指标，因此不推断其普遍检出率或实际监管效能。研究使用Serpent和SCALE等工具生成核材料演化与监测响应数据，并考虑每种情景下大量运行样本以及核数据不确定性。模型利用伽马、中子和阿尔法能谱等信息识别异常，摘要报告在多数受测情景中的分类性能超过90%。所讨论的核素包括242Cm、239/240Pu、239Np、133Xe和132I。作者进一步提出对疑似异常采取隔离与控制的保障处置思路。上述性能是模拟情景结果，不能直接解释为真实设施的监管检出率；训练和测试的情景独立性尤其需要全文核查。

English abstract

Molten salt reactors (MSRs) have advantages over traditional light water reactors; however, nuclear material safeguards remain an obstacle to their deployment. The nontraditional liquid fuel of the MSR designs examined in this work and additional material flow streams create challenges for safeguards. These properties not only preclude traditional item-counting safeguards but also create new material diversion pathways. The approach proposed here involves modeling a reactor similar to currently proposed molten salt reactors using Serpent and SCALE, simulating its operation under normal conditions and under various scenarios of plutonium removal. SCALE Sampler is used to introduce isotope-specific nuclear data uncertainty perturbations across 10,000 reactor operations for each scenario. Gamma-ray, neutron and alpha spectra measurement signatures are then collected from each perturbed reactor simulation to identify differences caused by plutonium removal. These signatures are then passed to a machine learning model that classifies each dataset as originating from either normal reactor operation or reactor operation during which plutonium diversion occurred. The highest performing developed machine learning model is capable of correctly classifying over 90% of all reactor simulations for most examined diversion scenarios. Alpha spectra features created by 242Cm, 239Pu, 240Pu, along with gamma-ray spectra features created by 239Np, 133Xe, 132I and other isotopes, proved instrumental for the classification process. The results indicate that, given appropriate measurement signatures, plutonium diversion can be recognized with confidence, complementing the proposed isolate-and-contain safeguards approach for MSRs. Accordingly, MSRs would particularly benefit from safeguards by design that enable collection of these signatures.

中文摘要

控制棒尖峰效应是移动控制棒反应堆堆芯二维/一维输运计算中的关键数值问题。研究提出两种改进的尖峰效应修正方法。第一种方法采用基于材料区域的二维/一维输运处理。第二种为仅轴向均匀化（AOH）方法，在基于材料区域的二维/一维输运方法基础上，利用材料区域的高精度轴向细网格通量进行通量—体积加权轴向均匀化。该方法先以相邻层的径向泄漏项近似子平面泄漏项，再通过预先计算的径向泄漏修正因子进一步改进这些泄漏项。研究通过基准计算比较方法表现，以降低控制棒轴向位置变化产生的输运误差。第一种修正方案把轴向非均匀材料区域显式用于二维/一维求解；第二种AOH方案进一步把精细轴向网格的通量按材料体积加权平均，构造用于均匀化的通量信息。对局部子平面泄漏项，作者利用相邻轴向层的径向泄漏近似，再通过预先计算的泄漏修正因子补偿误差。此方法主要处理输运离散和轴向均匀化带来的控制棒尖峰，不应与机械结构中的接触或应力集中混为一谈。

English abstract

The cusping effect is one of the critical challenges that must be addressed in the reactor core physics simulations involving control rod movement. This study proposes two improved methods for correcting the control rod cusping effect in the 2D/1D transport calculations. First, the conventional Both Axial and Radial Homogenization (BARH) method in NECP-X is enhanced by subdividing partially rodded axial planes into sub-planes, each undergoing independent radial homogenization. The radial leakage term of each sub-plane was then approximated using the leakage terms of its adjacent layers. Second, an Axial-Only Homogenization (AOH) method was developed, building on the material-region-based 2D/1D transport method. This method leverages high-precision axial fine-mesh fluxes from material regions to perform flux-volume-weighted axial homogenization. It initially approximates the radial leakage terms of sub-planes using those of adjacent layers, and further refines these terms via pre-calculated radial leakage correction factors.

English abstract

Position signals of control rods are important parameters that should be measured during the operation of nuclear reactors. The performance of capacitive detectors for measuring control rod positions in a double-layer medium is numerically studied in this paper. According to the experimental results of concave electrodes capacitive detectors, the numerical model is verified. The mathematical expression of the detector signal is derived as a function of rod position and liquid level. The error characteristics of the capacitive detectors for measuring the eccentric metal rod are revealed. The method for evaluating measuring errors of capacitive detectors in double-layer medium is constructed. The relation between flare angles of electrodes and measuring errors is studied, and the optimization method of the flare angles is discussed. The design of the integrated capacitive metal rod position detector (CMRPD) is proposed. Measuring errors and performance of the detector are evaluated.

English abstract · 本报告依据完整中文摘要翻译，不是来源英文摘要

The study examines debris-bed coolability under several water-injection strategies, including a water pool, natural circulation with bottom injection, and top injection. It evaluates dryout heat flux and the location of dryout. The results show that injection mode changes the dryout pattern and coolability; top injection can leave an upper region insufficiently cooled, while other arrangements shift the dryout location. The findings emphasize the role of boundary conditions in coolability experiments.

English abstract · 本报告依据完整中文摘要翻译，不是来源英文摘要

A one-dimensional porous-medium model is extended to predict debris-bed coolability in pressurized-water-reactor severe accidents. The numerical method solves coupled conservation equations and is checked against TUTU, COOLOCE and STYX experiments. Model predictions are compared with existing correlations. The study applies the code to a high-power case and reports that predicted coolable bed height depends on the injection strategy, reaching different values for bottom injection and top flooding.
