中文摘要

钴自给能中子探测器（SPND）因响应迅速，广泛用于压水堆保护系统。但堆内强伽马辐射会产生干扰电流，影响中子通量测量。本文提出高保真蒙特卡罗方法，采用反应堆—探测器两步耦合评估伽马本底电流：首先从典型压水堆精细模型获得探测位置的中子和伽马能谱，再作为精细SPND模型的源项，其中绝缘层分为50个径向层。模型显式区分正电子与电子贡献，并在电子输运中计入空间电荷效应。对ZTCo121和ZTCo122两种原型，伽马本底电流分别占总信号的2.56%和4.19%，均低于5%，对灵敏度的影响处于可接受工程不确定度内。与游泳池反应堆辐照实验的验证支持在初始灵敏度标定中忽略伽马干扰。该方法可拓展到其他瞬发响应SPND。

English abstract

Cobalt self-powered neutron detectors (SPNDs) are widely used in pressurized water reactor (PWR) protection systems because of their prompt response characteristics. However, in-core cobalt SPNDs are exposed to intense gamma radiation that generates interfering currents, compromising the accuracy of neutron flux measurements. This paper presents a high-fidelity Monte Carlo (MC) simulation method that uses a two-step reactor–detector coupling strategy to evaluate the gamma-induced background current in cobalt SPNDs. The strategy first obtains the neutron and gamma energy spectra at the detector location from a detailed MC model of a typical PWR core, and then uses these spectra as the source term for a fine-scale SPND model with the insulator divided into 50 radial layers. The contributions of positrons and electrons are explicitly distinguished, and the space charge effect is incorporated into the electron transport calculation. Applied to two prototype cobalt SPNDs (ZTCo121 and ZTCo122), the method yields gamma-induced background current fractions of 2.56% and 4.19% of the total signal, both of which are below 5%. The resulting impact on detector sensitivity remains within the acceptable engineering uncertainty. Results validated against irradiation experiments performed in a swimming-pool reactor confirm that neglecting gamma interference in initial sensitivity calibration is justifiable. The method is general and extendable to other prompt-response SPNDs.

中文摘要

铅冷快堆中，不同燃料组件流出的冷热铅铋共晶流体温差可超过100 K；它们在上腔室相互作用形成复杂射流，可能造成构件热条纹和热疲劳。常规RANS难以可靠捕获瞬态温度脉动，纯LES对多工况研究的成本较高。分离涡模拟（DES）结合近壁RANS与分离区LES，在分辨涡结构和效率之间折中。本文用DES研究平行三射流构型的冷热铅铋混合，先以液钠三射流实验验证，再考察入口流速和温差。入口流速升高增强射流惯性、推迟入口附近混合并扩展温度脉动频带；温差增大主要增强温度梯度和脉动强度，对总体流动结构及主频范围影响较小。与LES参考数据比较，DES能够重现温度脉动强度随工况变化的总体趋势和相近振幅范围，估算计算成本约低94%。

English abstract

In lead-cooled fast reactors (LFRs), the temperature difference between hot and cold lead–bismuth eutectic (LBE) streams from different fuel assemblies can exceed 100 K. Their interaction in the upper plenum generates complex turbulent jets, which may cause thermal striping and thermal fatigue in structural components, making thermal mixing critical to reactor safety. Conventional RANS methods struggle to reliably capture transient temperature fluctuations, while pure LES incurs prohibitively high computational cost for multi-condition engineering studies. Detached eddy simulation (DES), which combines near-wall Reynolds-averaged Navier–Stokes modeling with large eddy simulation in separated regions, strikes a practical balance between eddy-resolving capability and computational efficiency, providing an efficient approach for resolving large-scale turbulent structures and temperature fluctuations. In this study, DES was used to investigate the thermal mixing of hot and cold LBE coolant jets in a parallel triple-jet configuration. The method was validated against liquid-sodium triple-jet experimental data and then applied to examine the effects of inlet velocity and temperature difference. The results show that higher inlet velocity enhances jet inertia, delays near-inlet mixing, and broadens the frequency range of temperature fluctuations, whereas a larger temperature difference mainly increases temperature gradients and fluctuation intensity, with little influence on the overall flow structure and dominant frequency range. A reference comparison with LES data further demonstrates that DES can reproduce the overall dependence of temperature fluctuation intensity on the operating parameters with comparable fluctuation amplitude ranges, while requiring approximately 94 % lower estimated computational cost.

核电站熔融物堆内滞留—压力容器外部冷却（IVR-ERVC）策略中，临界热流密度（CHF）限制压力容器完整性。理想平面安全评估常忽略长期腐蚀和制造缺陷造成的宏观不规则性。本文以自主饱和池沸腾系统和高速摄影研究凸起、凹陷的影响。接近沸腾危机时，凸缺陷和凹缺陷使平均CHF分别降低约27.4%和16.7%。凹陷形成滞汽区，主要由加工锐边对三相接触线的钉扎导致气泡滞留和局部干涸；凸起则机械阻碍气泡脱离，使沸腾危机提前。在更能代表IVR-ERVC的条件下进一步验证后，可考虑把表面退化惩罚系数作为核安全分析的保守选项。

English abstract

In the “Melted Reactor Retention and External Pressure Vessel Cooling (IVR-ERVC)” strategy for nuclear power plants, the Critical Heat Flux (CHF) serves as the critical limit for ensuring pressure vessel integrity. Traditional safety assessment procedures rely on an ideal flat surface assumption, neglecting macroscopic irregularities caused by corrosion and manufacturing defects during long-term operation. To address this issue, this study systematically investigated the impact mechanism of macroscopic irregularities on CHF using a self-developed saturated pool boiling experimental system and high-speed imaging technology. Results demonstrate that as the system approaches boiling crisis, macroscopic convex and concave defects reduce average CHF by approximately 27.4% and 16.7%, respectively. Mechanism analysis demonstrates that concave defects form a “steam trap, ”the vapor retention is attributed primarily to triple-phase contact line (TPL) pinning at the sharp machined edges, leading to bubble retention and localized drying; whereas convex defects impede bubble detachment through “mechanical obstruction,” resulting in premature CHF. Once validated under conditions more representative of IVR-ERVC, a “surface degradation penalty coefficient” may be considered in future nuclear safety analyses as a possible conservative option.

中文摘要

CHF预测具有强非线性。概率神经网络虽能给不确定度，但校准和与数据分布的一致性仍不清楚。本文以24579组CHF数据比较贝叶斯神经网络、MC Dropout及深度集成，以AUSE、CE、NLL评估校准，用认知不确定度与数据稀疏度的Spearman相关评价一致性。发现各模型认知不确定度更受预测值幅度而非稀疏程度影响，反映一致性有限。提出剪枝多样化深度集成PDiv-DE，在保留预测性能的同时改善校准并加强不确定度与稀疏程度的联系，为安全相关热工数据驱动预测提供评价框架。

English abstract

Critical heat flux (CHF) is a key thermal-hydraulic phenomenon in reactor safety; however, its accurate prediction remains challenging due to highly nonlinear behavior. Accordingly, data-driven models have been developed to overcome limitations of conventional correlations. Although probabilistic neural networks provide uncertainty estimates alongside predictions, the reliability of their calibration and consistency with the underlying data distribution remain unclear. A unified framework is proposed to evaluate uncertainty reliability using 24,579 CHF data points and three probabilistic approaches: Bayesian neural networks, Monte Carlo Dropout, and Deep Ensembles. Calibration is assessed using AUSE, CE, and NLL, while uncertainty consistency is evaluated through Spearman correlation between epistemic uncertainty and data sparsity measures. The results reveal that epistemic uncertainty across models is more strongly influenced by prediction magnitude than data sparsity, indicating limited consistency with the underlying data distribution. To improve the uncertainty calibration and consistency of the probabilistic framework, a pruned diversified deep ensemble (PDiv-DE) is proposed. The PDiv-DE preserves predictive performance while improving calibration accuracy and strengthening the consistency between epistemic uncertainty and data sparsity. Therefore, the proposed framework provides a practical step toward more reliable uncertainty quantification for CHF prediction and supports the development of data-driven models for safety-relevant thermal-hydraulic applications.

核电厂大修期间一回路冷却剂温度下降以及氧含量增加会导致活化腐蚀产物释放,为降低各系统辐射水平,可采用氧化净化的方式,通过注入双氧水使得活化腐蚀产物快速集中释放并通过化学容积与控制系统(RCV)净化系统去除。“华龙一号”机组作为三代核电机组与CPR1000机组存在较大差异,对于提前停运1~2台主泵对辐射源项变化的影响,停运最后一台主泵的时间以及连续监测点位选择等问题,相关标准仍有待进一步探索。大修期间氧化净化均作为关键路径控制,以便节省大修工期,同时,若氧化净化时间太短会导致大修机组辐射水平增加,不利于个人剂量控制。通过分析“华龙一号”机组在首次大修氧化净化期间反应堆冷却剂(RCP)、RCV、安全注入(RIS)系统的辐射源项变化趋势,探讨连续监测点位的选择、提前停运2台主泵的影响以及停运最后一台主泵的标准。

中文摘要

核电厂在运行过程中会产生一系列的辐射源项,主要有裂变产物、活化腐蚀产物等,对核电厂辐射场有着显著的影响,随着对58Co和60Co进行控制之后,一些核电厂停堆后辐射场的主要贡献者转变为110mAg,由于110mAg是一种强辐射核素,对辐射场的影响有着重要的贡献,占集体剂量的10%~15%。本文通过分析110mAg辐射影响及形态变化,结合水-水高能反应堆(VVER型)机组停堆期间的110mAg变化趋势,总结出在一回路氮气吹扫和解密封后,110mAg形态开始转变为离子态后大量释放规律,提出并实施了在此阶段不依靠主泵运行,而是通过多个辅助工艺系统组合运行新的净化工艺,实现了110mAg辐射源项的控制目的,有效降低了110mAg的辐射影响,其中一回路110mAg净化效率达到91.6%,反应堆厂房辐射指数下降40%~60%,大修集体剂量降低约50人∙mSv,对同类型机组具有较好的借鉴意义。
