在压水堆（PWR）发生失水事故（LOCA）后的长期冷却过程中，压力冷却系统（PCS）引起的冷凝会显著影响安全壳内的自然循环和蒸汽分层。然而，互连开口几何形状对多舱室安全壳中PCS驱动的流动路径和蒸汽分布的影响尚不明确。本研究基于PANGU试验装置，建立了一个三维CFD模型，该模型包含了典型压水堆安全壳的主要舱室特性。结果表明，相似的整体压力衰减和散热性能可能对应于不同的局部循环路径和气体分布。将侧开口向上移动会削弱来自环形区域的冷却气体供应，使主要流入路径向上方开口移动，并将蒸汽体积分数差异降低约69%。增大底部开口面积会增强冷却气体流入和上方排放，并将该差异降低约41%。采用完整的环向开口布置会重新分配蒸汽发生器（SG）舱室之间的冷却气体流入，并将该差异降低约24%。

English abstract

During long-term cooling following a LOCA in PWR, PCS-induced condensation strongly influences natural circulation and steam stratification in the containment. However, the effects of interconnecting opening geometry on PCS-driven flow paths and steam distribution in multi-compartment containments remain unclear. In this study, a three-dimensional CFD model based on the PANGU facility was developed by incorporating the main compartment characteristics of a typical PWR containment. The results show that similar global pressure decay and heat-removal performance may correspond to different local circulation paths and gas distributions. Moving the side opening upward weakens the cooled-gas supply from the annular region, shifts the dominant inflow path toward the upper opening, and reduces the difference in steam volume fraction by approximately 69%. Increasing the bottom opening area strengthens cooled-gas inflow and upper discharge, reducing this difference by approximately 41%. A complete circumferential opening arrangement redistributes cooled-gas inflow among SG compartments and reduces this difference by approximately 24%.

English abstract

This study developed a SLAM-based radiation measurement system using an autonomous mobile robot and a LiDAR sensor to improve the quantitative accuracy of surface contamination assessments. Conventional fixed or manually operated radiation detection systems have limitations in performing precise evaluations over wide areas. To resolve these issues, the proposed system integrates autonomous navigation, real-time localization, and dynamic measurement correction. Correction factors derived from static experiments are applied in real time to compensate for CPS errors caused by variations in detector height and speed, enabling accurate measurements under various conditions. Scanning experiments were conducted using Co-60 radiation sources. After applying the correction factors, the average deviation was reduced to within 10%, with lower scanning speeds resulting in further error reduction. This system, equipped with a real-time correction algorithm, serves as a foundational technology for dynamic radiation mapping and has potential applications in facility decommissioning and precision measurement of radioactive contamination areas.

The main goal of this study is to estimate the most unfavorable stability conditions for the purpose of transport and dispersion simulations of radioactive nuclides from hypothetical accidental releases at a planned nuclear power plant location. Based on these weather sequences, it is possible to determine, with a high degree of confidence, the maximum extent of Emergency Planning Zones (EPZs) around nuclear power plants, thereby enabling effective preparedness planning and optimized emergency response strategies. Regulatory inspection programs should ensure safety during dry storage operation and future transport after long-term storage. Therefore, inspection programs should support differentiated inspection based on safety performance and risk significance, beyond simple compliance with procedures and acceptance criteria. This study develops an Inspection Maturity Level (IML) model based on international recommendations and a case study of the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) Independent Spent Fuel Storage Installation (ISFSI) inspection program. Using this model, the U.S. and Korean inspection frameworks for spent fuel dry storage operations were compared. The results show that South Korea's inspection framework for loading and unloading activities remains at IML-1 because dedicated inspection programs and detailed procedures have not yet been established. A screening-level relative risk assessment and an IML evaluation matrix were then applied to derive key elements of an inspection program. The results suggest that South Korea should develop structured inspection contents integrating detailed procedures, performance verification, and risk-informed prioritization.

English abstract

Once the off-site nuclear emergency alarm is triggered, the government will organize the affected residents to evacuate to specific decontamination sites based on the emergency plan. Considering the complexity of the evacuation process and the potential panic among evacuees, this paper posits that public preferences or autonomous behaviors should be considered. For instance, some evacuees may choose not to follow government instructions and instead opt for an alternative decontamination site. This study develops a framework for a nuclear emergency evacuation and decontamination analysis system by combining integrated programming with simulation analysis. Within this framework, the study analyzes the comprehensive effects of three choice behaviors: following government instructions, heading to the nearest decontamination site, and choosing a preferred decontamination site. Using a case study from a nuclear power plant in China, this paper employs simulation analysis to explore the impact of evacuees' intentions on the outcomes of the evacuation process. The research results indicate a significant influence of evacuee behavior on decontamination progress. The key findings are as follows: disregarding government instructions leads to a substantial increase in decontamination time. Additionally, the proportion of evacuees not adhering to government instruction has a significant impact on the overall decontamination time. Different emergency scenarios exhibit varying advantages in terms of secondary evacuation and the reallocation of decontamination resources. Through this research, in-depth analysis and scientific decision support are provided for future off-site nuclear emergency management.
