中文摘要

由于压水堆控制棒驱动机构（PWRCRDM）结构复杂且运行环境恶劣，其振动信号具有高度随机性且易受噪声污染，导致现有故障诊断方法的性能受限。为解决这一问题，本文提出了一种基于多尺度特征提取和轻量级注意力机制的PWRCRDM故障诊断方法。首先，设计了一种多尺度双分支异构残差网络，用于从PWRCRDM时频谱中提取全局时域趋势特征和局部频域细节特征，从而获得故障特征的互补表示。其次，引入了一种基于残差低秩投影和残差瓶颈净化的交叉注意力融合机制，用于特征降维并实现特征互补融合，从而减少模型参数数量并提高诊断性能。最后，搭建了PWRCRDM热态实验平台，用于采集热态运行条件下的振动信号，以进行实验验证。结果表明，所提出的 PWRCRDM 诊断方法达到了 98.5% 的诊断准确率，优于现有的最先进方法。

English abstract

Due to the complex structure and operating environment of the pressurized water reactor control rod drive mechanism (PWRCRDM), its vibration signals are highly random and strongly contaminated by noise, leading to limited performance of existing fault-diagnosis methods. To address this issue, a fault-diagnosis method for the PWRCRDM based on multi-scale feature extraction and a lightweight attention mechanism is proposed. Firstly, a multi-scale dual-branch heterogeneous residual network is designed to extract global time-domain trend features and local frequency-domain detailed features from PWRCRDM time-frequency spectra. This extraction yields a complementary representation of fault features. Secondly, a cross-attention fusion mechanism based on residual low-rank projection and residual bottleneck purification is introduced to perform feature dimensionality reduction and achieve feature complementarity and fusion, thereby reducing the number of model parameters and improving diagnostic performance. Finally, a PWRCRDM hot-state experimental platform is established to collect the vibration signals under hot-state operating conditions for experimental verification. The results show that the proposed PWRCRDM diagnosis method achieves a diagnostic accuracy of 98.5%, outperforming existing state-of-the-art methods.

中文摘要

氧核受中子照射会使水活化，生成短寿命氧和氮同位素。其中17O(n,p)17N生成的17N因β衰变伴随缓发中子发射而受到关注，这些次级中子影响核电站一回路附近的辐射场。本文在LVR-15开展明确条件的辐照实验，测量冷却水活度。在距堆芯约10 m的出口管上布置金箔，以其中子活化率量化堆芯产生的17N；精心布置箔片以消除泵房本底。利用已知堆芯能谱，得到谱平均积分截面0.79 μb，不确定度18%。考虑LVR-15能谱与235U热中子诱发裂变谱之间的差异后，对应后者的谱平均截面为3.4 μb。作者报告，现有核数据评价均显著高估该值，测量与评价的17O(n,p)17N截面仍存在较大差异。

English abstract

Water activation occurs when oxygen nuclei are exposed to neutrons, producing short–lived radioactive isotopes of oxygen and nitrogen. Among these, 17N—formed via the 17O(n,p)17N reaction—is of particular interest because its β–decay is accompanied by the emission of a delayed neutron. These secondary neutrons influence the radiation field in the vicinity of primary cooling circuits in nuclear power plants. This study investigates the activity induced in cooling water during a well–defined irradiation experiment in the LVR–15 reactor. The production of 17N in the core was quantified by measuring the neutron activation rate of a gold foil attached to the outlet tube located ∼ 10 m from the core. Owing to a carefully selected foil arrangement, background contributions from the pump room were effectively eliminated. Using the known neutron spectrum of the LVR–15 core, the spectrum–averaged integral cross section was determined to be 0.79 µb, with an associated uncertainty of 18 %. After accounting for the spectral differences between the LVR–15 neutron field and the 235U(nth,f) spectrum, the corresponding Spectrum–Averaged Cross Section (SACS) for a 235U thermal neutron induced fission spectrum was found to be 3.4 µb. All current nuclear data evaluations significantly overpredict this value, and substantial discrepancies persist between measured and evaluated 17O(n,p)17N cross sections.

中文摘要

在反应堆一回路热段的超声波流场测量中，温度场和速度场的不均匀性会使声射线偏转并改变接收到的声压，从而影响超声波渡越时间的测量。为了应对华龙一号（HPR1000）热段复杂的热工水力条件，本研究开发了一种数值框架，该框架结合了流场校正三角前向传播、背景场径向基函数重构以及改进的高斯波束模型，以表征热流不均匀性对声射线轨迹和接收器压力的影响。结果表明，在满功率和超功率工况下，整体流场的平均绝对重构误差分别为0.3965 m/s和0.4017 m/s，相应的相对均方根误差分别为4.0565%和4.3999%。使用相同的参考扩展指数m=-18在两种工况下，背景场重构将接收器压力差相对于 COMSOL 数值参考值分别从约 37% 和 83% 降低至 2.1% 和 7.2%。三维参考场与映射后的二维场之间的映射级流量积分差为 +2.12%，表明降维映射能够较好地保持整体流量。这些结果为复杂热流条件下反应堆一回路超声波流量测量的声传播分析和后续工程验证提供了理论和数值基础。

English abstract

In ultrasonic flow measurement in the reactor primary-loop hot leg, nonuniform temperature and velocity fields can deflect acoustic rays and alter the received acoustic pressure, thereby affecting ultrasonic transit-time measurements. To address the complex thermal–hydraulic conditions in the Hualong One (HPR1000) hot leg, this study develops a numerical framework that combines flow-corrected triangular forward propagation, radial-basis-function reconstruction of the background fields, and an improved Gaussian-beam model to characterize the effects of thermoflow nonuniformity on acoustic-ray trajectories and receiver pressure. The results show that the mean absolute reconstruction errors of the overall flow field are 0.3965 m/s at full power and 0.4017 m/s at overpower, with corresponding relative RMS errors of 4.0565 % and 4.3999 %. Using the same reference spreading exponent of m=-18 for both operating conditions, background-field reconstruction reduces the receiver-pressure differences relative to the COMSOL numerical reference from approximately 37 % and 83 % to 2.1 % and 7.2 %, respectively. The mapping-level flow-integral difference between the 3D reference field and the mapped 2D field is + 2.12 %, indicating that the dimensional-reduction mapping preserves the overall flow rate reasonably well. These results provide a theoretical and numerical basis for acoustic-propagation analysis and subsequent engineering validation of ultrasonic flow measurement in reactor primary loops under complex thermoflow conditions.

中文摘要

本研究开发了一种用于研究反应堆冷却剂泵（RCP）空化的实验可视化系统，旨在阐明由蒸汽发生器下封头复杂几何形状引起的非均匀流入的影响。该系统采用具有折射补偿功能的高速成像技术，消除了光学畸变的影响，从而能够在均匀和非均匀流入条件下获取瞬态空化演化模式。同时，系统地测量了泵入口处的压力脉动信号，以表征不同空化阶段的动态特征。结果表明，在非均匀流入条件下，空化演化会产生明显的低频宽带分量（0–4 Fn，Fn的定义以原文为准），在入口压力谱中，这些低频分量源于流入畸变引起的局部攻角空间波动与非对称空化区域体积振荡之间的耦合。这种耦合机制被认为是加剧反应堆冷却剂系统（RCP）运行不稳定性的一个关键因素。本研究为核反应堆冷却剂系统中流入畸变与空化引起的不稳定性之间的关系提供了新的见解。

English abstract

This study develops an experimental visualization system for investigating cavitation in the reactor coolant pump (RCP) and aims to elucidate the influence of non-uniform inflow generated by the complex geometry of the steam generator lower head. A high-speed imaging system with refraction-compensation capability was developed to eliminate optical distortion effects, thereby enabling the acquisition of transient cavitation evolution patterns under both uniform and non-uniform inflow conditions. Meanwhile, pressure pulsation signals at the pump inlet were systematically measured to characterize the dynamic features associated with the different cavitation stages. The results indicate that, under non-uniform inflow conditions, cavitation evolution induces pronounced low-frequency broadband components (0-4 Fn) in the inlet pressure spectrum. These low-frequency components arise from the coupling between the spatial fluctuations of the local angle of attack caused by inflow distortion and the volumetric oscillations of the asymmetric cavitation regions. This coupling mechanism is identified as a key factor exacerbating the operational instability of the RCP. This study provides new insights into the relationship between inflow distortion and cavitation-induced instability in nuclear reactor coolant systems.

中文摘要

结构件流致振动影响核反应堆系统安全，圆柱和方柱均有广泛应用。本文比较两种截面并排布置时的涡激振动，采用完全双向流固耦合，研究间距8D、不同折合速度的双自由度响应。流体采用非定常雷诺平均Navier–Stokes方程，通过CFD与结构动力学耦合求解；结构方程使用UDF实现的四阶Runge–Kutta法积分。结果显示几何形状造成根本差异：圆柱具有明显锁定区、振幅饱和及对称2P涡脱落；方柱则表现为驰振型响应，振幅随折合速度持续增大，没有锁定，振荡不规则且非周期，并伴随显著耦合的非定常反相尾流。该框架和比较结果为反应堆工程中并排结构的几何相关振动提供参考。

English abstract

Flow-induced vibration (FIV) of structural components critically affects the safety of nuclear reactor systems, where circular and square cylinders are widely used. This study conducts a comparative analysis of the vortex-induced vibration (VIV) characteristics of these two typical geometries in side-by-side arrangements. A fully two-way coupled fluid-structure interaction (FSI) approach is employed to investigate the VIV of side-by-side cylinders at a spacing of 8D under various reduced velocities. The numerical model is based on a two-degree-of-freedom (2-DOF) system, in which the Unsteady Reynolds-Averaged Navier-Stokes (URANS) equations are solved through a coupled Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) and Computational Structural Dynamics (CSD) approach, while structural equations are integrated using a fourth-order Runge-Kutta method implemented through User-Defined Functions (UDFs), ensuring high computational accuracy and reliability. The results reveal fundamental differences in VIV behavior governed by geometry. Circular cylinders exhibit a distinct lock-in region with predictable amplitude saturation and symmetric 2P vortex shedding. In contrast, the square cylinders exhibit a galloping-type response, characterized by amplitudes that grow continuously with reduced velocity without lock-in, irregular and aperiodic oscillations, and appreciably coupled, unsteady anti-phase wakes. The numerical framework and comparative findings provide a useful reference for understanding geometry-dependent VIV in side-by-side arrangements relevant to reactor engineering.

中文摘要

跨循环优化燃料装载需同时改善堆芯性能、满足安全约束和燃料经济性。本文提出代理辅助的逐循环优化框架，数据来自韩光3号机组第1–2循环核设计报告的参考装载及平衡堆芯研究。用STREAM/RAST-K为数据标注循环长度和功率峰因子，训练逐棒视觉Transformer，将装载表示为棒级图像状张量并预测堆芯参数。模型嵌入模拟退火搜索的筛选步骤，减少RAST-K计算。采用外部验证数据和测试子集评价，模型在这些数据上呈一致预测趋势。连续十个OPR-1000循环的实验中，平均筛选效率99.8%；在单核CPU配合GPU筛选下，各优化运行十小时内收敛。优化后的十循环累计长度增加，功率峰因子保持与参考装载相当，说明框架具有可行性。

English abstract

Optimizing fuel loading patterns (LPs) across reactor cycles is challenging because it requires improving core performance while satisfying safety constraints and fuel economy requirements. This study proposes a surrogate-assisted cycle-by-cycle optimization framework using datasets derived from reference LPs in the Hanbit Unit 3 Cycle 1–2 nuclear design reports and an equilibrium-core design study. Using the STREAM/RAST-K code system, the datasets were labeled with cycle length and power peaking factor and used to train a pin-wise Vision Transformer (pin-wise ViT) that predicts core design parameters from LPs represented as pin-level image-like tensors. The trained model is embedded in a screening step during the simulated annealing (SA) search to filter LPs and reduce reliance on RAST-K calculations. Model validation was performed using an external validation dataset and a testing subset, and the pin-wise ViT showed consistent predictive trends across these datasets. Experiments across ten consecutive OPR-1000 cycles showed 99.8% average screening efficiency, with optimization runs converging within ten hours on a single CPU core with GPU-assisted screening. The resulting optimized LPs achieved a longer cumulative cycle length over the ten-cycle sequence while maintaining power peaking factors comparable to those of the reference LPs, demonstrating the feasibility of the proposed framework.

中文摘要

再生式换热器由再生段和冷却段共同构成,凭借再生段的加入,再生式换热器有助于减小换热器承受的热应力以及回收部分热量,而广泛应用于研究型反应堆的各试验回路中。在再生式换热器设计中,通常会对换热面积保留一定的裕量以应对换热管破损、阻塞以及工况波动等情况发生。结合再生式换热器的结构特点,在不利因素未发生时,将设计裕量效果计入实际的换热中,分析不同的设计裕量对再生式换热器理论运行功率、流质流量以及出口温度等的影响。分析结果表明,再生式换热器的理论运行功率随着冷却段设计裕量的增加而逐渐增加,而随着再生段设计裕量的增加而逐渐减小。再生段及冷却段设计裕量对再生式换热器的功率调节跨度影响不大,但再生式换热器的理论功率调节区间在再生段设计裕量增加时会逐渐下移,而在冷却段设计裕量增加时会逐渐上移。要克服再生段裕量对运行功率带来的不利影响,可以选择增加相应的冷却端设计裕量以及加大二次水流量。再生式换热器运行于功率调节下边界以下时,再生段保持一定设计裕量有利于一次水的流量更为接近设计流量。鉴于分析的结果,对于处于稳态试验回路中的再生式换热器,建议再生段裕量小于10%以满足设计功率,而对需要具备降功率运行的再生式换热器,建议可以取较大的再生段裕量以减小一次水流量的波动。
