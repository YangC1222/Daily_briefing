中文摘要

在深穿透蒙特卡罗模拟中，权窗全局方差减小（WWGVR）方法的效率关键取决于权窗下界的准确性。自耦合蒙卡方法初始迭代中的统计波动常导致有偏的权窗估计，从而引发长历史问题。传统做法依赖经验参数施加强制约束。然而，这些参数在合理范围内对计算效率指标（FOM）的影响有限，说明效率瓶颈在于权窗分布是否合理，而非参数微调。为此，本文提出自适应空间卷积权窗平滑（ASCWWS）方法，使用误差自适应的邻域卷积核，对权窗场进行低通滤波和正则化，将点估计转为场估计，以抑制高频噪声并恢复物理一致的重要性梯度。在HBR-2和EURACOS基准上的验证表明，相对于传统MAGIC方法，ASCWWS分别使FOM提高到1.86倍和4.06倍，重要性反转的发生率降低超过31%。通过改善权窗分布质量，ASCWWS降低了对经验参数的依赖，并可作为后处理工具直接集成到现有自耦合蒙卡框架中，为高效模拟深穿透问题的WWGVR提供新途径。

English abstract

In deep-penetration Monte Carlo simulations, the efficiency of the weight window global variance reduction (WWGVR) method critically depends on the accuracy of the lower weight window bounds. Statistical fluctuations during the initial iterations of self-coupled Monte Carlo method often lead to biased weight window estimates, triggering long-history problems. Conventional practice relies on empirical parameters as mandatory constraints. However, these parameters exert limited influence on Figure-of-Merit (FOM) within reasonable ranges, indicating that the efficiency bottleneck lies not in parameter fine-tuning but in the rationality of the weight window distribution. To address this, an Adaptive Spatial Convolution Weight Window Smoothing (ASCWWS) method is proposed, which employs an error-adaptive neighbourhood convolution kernel to achieve low-pass filtering and regularization of the weight window field, transforming point estimation into field estimation to suppress high-frequency noise and restore physically consistent importance gradients. Validation on the HBR-2 and EURACOS benchmarks shows that ASCWWS improves the FOM by factors of 1.86 and 4.06 compared to the conventional MAGIC method, respectively, with the incidence of importance inversion reduced by over 31%. By enhancing weight window distribution quality, ASCWWS reduces reliance on empirical parameters and can be directly integrated into existing self-coupled Monte Carlo method frameworks as a post-processing tool, offering a new pathway for efficient WWGVR simulation of deep-penetration problems.

English abstract

To address the significant energy dependence of long neutron counter responses in wide-energy-range fluence measurements, this paper proposes a differentiable-optimization-based method for flattening the energy response of a multi-detector long neutron counter. A Geant4 model of the multi-detector long counter was developed, and the energy–position response matrix of this detector was constructed for the energy range of 1 eV to 20 MeV. The relative standard deviation (RSD) of the synthesized response was used as the flatness metric, and two optimization approaches—discrete combinatorial search and differentiable continuous optimization—were compared. The results show that multi-position response synthesis effectively improves the response flatness of the long counter across a broad energy range. For a detector array with K = 8, the sampled combinatorial-search benchmark identified a configuration with an RSD of 3.79%, whereas differentiable optimization yielded 2.98%, indicating a lower RSD under the present calculation settings. Additionally, embedding tungsten rods in the moderator of a long neutron counter was found to improve the overall flatness of its response. Independent verification simulations were performed by placing 6Li micro-detectors at the eight optimized axial positions, yielding a verified RSD of 2.84%. This study demonstrates that the proposed method effectively enhances the wide-energy-range response consistency of the multi-detector long counter and provides a reproducible computational framework for position optimization in such detector systems.

准确评估时变本底，对中子共振分析（NRA）中可靠的中子飞行时间（TOF）透射测量至关重要。本文在京都大学复合原子力科学研究所直线加速器（KURNS-LINAC），使用⁶Li玻璃闪烁探测器，实验研究中子透射测量中的时变本底。在相同测量条件下，比较黑共振、中子吸收体和脉冲高度（PH）门三种本底评估技术。结果表明，插入材料会衰减伽马射线，使共振滤片或中子吸收体技术得到的本底水平偏低。相反，PH门技术可能因散射热中子俘获产生的伽马射线，而在热能区高估本底。

English abstract

Accurate evaluation of time-dependent background is essential for reliable neutron time-of-flight (TOF) transmission measurements in neutron resonance analysis (NRA). In this study, we experimentally investigated the time-dependent background in neutron transmission measurements utilizing a 6Li-glass scintillation detector at the Kyoto University, Institute for Integrated Radiation and Nuclear Science - Linear Accelerator (KURNS-LINAC). A comparative analysis was conducted on three background evaluation techniques, namely the black resonance, neutron absorber, and pulse-height (PH) gate techniques under identical measurement conditions. The results demonstrated that background levels obtained with resonance filters or neutron absorbers can be underestimated due to gamma-ray attenuation by the inserted materials. Conversely, the PH gate technique can overestimate the background in the thermal energy range due to gamma-rays produced by the capture of scattered thermal neutrons.
