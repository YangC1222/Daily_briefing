核心观点

  • 同一压降关联式在独立计算中准确，但采用沿程局部物性嵌入 SPACE 后误差明显增大，说明实现方式与物性更新会改变系统级预测。

中文摘要

准确预测两相摩擦压降对于小型模块化反应堆螺旋盘管蒸汽发生器的热工水力分析十分重要。现有压降关联式通常基于代表性运行工况下的流体物性建立；将它们用于系统程序时，由于沿流动路径的局部压力和流体物性持续变化，预测精度可能降低。本研究首先采用独立计算和 SPACE 系统程序仿真，评估现有两相压降关联式对系统程序计算的适用性。结果表明，现有关联式在独立计算条件下与实验数据符合良好，但在 SPACE 程序中实现后，预测误差显著增大。

English abstract

Accurate prediction of two-phase frictional pressure drops in HCSGs is important for the thermal-hydraulic analysis of SMRs. Existing correlations are generally developed using fluid properties evaluated at representative operating conditions and may exhibit reduced accuracy when implemented in system codes, where local pressure and fluid properties continuously vary along the flow path. In this study, the applicability of existing two-phase pressure-drop correlations to system-code calculations was first assessed using both standalone calculations and SPACE code simulations. Although the existing correlations showed good agreement with experimental data under standalone conditions, their prediction errors increased significantly after implementation in the SPACE code.

中文摘要

为加速铅铋共晶回路的计算流体力学模拟，本研究采用近壁处理开发了高雷诺数 k–ω–kθ–εθ 湍流模型。该模型通过五个基准算例验证，在典型流动构型中表现出令人满意的精度；但由于壁面函数在分离流中的效率不足，它在复杂几何中仍有局限。为解决这一问题，作者在统一框架中提出混合分辨率策略：在加热段等关键区域保持高精度，同时在直管和弯管等简单区域降低计算成本。该方法应用于 KYLIN-II 和 TALL-3D 两个代表性铅铋回路，并比较高分辨率、混合分辨率和低分辨率模拟。结果表明，高分辨率模型能够可靠预测铅铋回路；高雷诺数模型在粗网格上显著降低计算成本，同时保持可接受精度；混合分辨率方法则获得接近高分辨率模型的精度，并降低计算成本。研究显示该湍流模型和混合分辨率策略可用于快速且准确地模拟快堆液态金属回路。

English abstract

To accelerate computational fluid dynamics (CFD) simulations of lead–bismuth-eutectic (LBE) loops, this study develops a high-Reynolds k–ω–kθ–ϵθ turbulence model using near-wall treatments. The model is validated against five benchmark cases to demonstrate satisfactory accuracy in typical flow configurations. However, limitations are observed in complex geometries due to the inefficiency of wall functions in separated flows. To overcome this drawback, a hybrid-resolution strategy is proposed within a unified framework. This method effectively maintains high accuracy in key areas such as heated sections while reducing computational costs in simple areas such as straight pipes and bends. This method is applied to two representative LBE loops, KYLIN-II and TALL-3D, with high-resolution, hybrid-resolution, and low-resolution simulations for comparison. Results indicate that (1) the high-resolution model provides reliable predictions for LBE loops, (2) the high-Reynolds k–ω–kθ–ϵθ turbulence model achieves acceptable accuracy with significantly reduced computational costs on a coarse mesh, and (3) the hybrid-resolution method achieves high accuracy comparable to the high-resolution model while lowering computational costs. Overall, this study demonstrates the potential of the high-Reynolds k–ω–kθ–ϵθ turbulence model and hybrid-resolution strategy for efficient and accurate simulation of liquid metal loops in fast reactors.

Migration of pores and grain boundaries in uranium dioxide driven by temperature gradient: A phase-field simulation

核心观点

  • UO₂ 定向晶粒粗化取决于晶界能否克服颗粒钉扎阻力；孔径控制迁移速率，热迁移还会拖曳晶界。

展开中英文摘要

中文摘要

在大温度梯度下，多孔二氧化铀燃料同时发生孔隙迁移、晶界运动和第二相颗粒钉扎，它们的相互作用控制微观组织演化。本研究建立相场模型，考察温度梯度驱动下孔隙迁移、颗粒钉扎与晶界迁移之间的作用。模拟表明，孔隙迁移速率与孔径密切相关；热迁移不仅改变孔隙形貌，还会拖曳晶界运动。与此同时，静止颗粒的钉扎作用显著抑制晶界迁移。在同时含有高压气孔和第二相颗粒的多晶体中，沿温度梯度方向的定向晶粒粗化由晶界克服钉扎阻力并在热梯度下有效迁移的能力控制，并非仅由温度梯度或孔隙迁移决定。该研究揭示了温度梯度下 UO₂ 燃料中孔隙迁移、颗粒钉扎和晶界迁移之间的动力学竞争，为理解含气孔条件下由晶界迁移驱动的柱状晶重构提供了机理认识。

English abstract

Under large temperature gradients, porous uranium dioxide (UO2) fuel exhibits the concurrent action of pore migration, grain boundary (GB) movement, and second-phase particle pinning, whose interplay governs microstructural evolution. In the present work, a phase-field model was developed to examine the interaction between pore migration, particle pinning, and GB migration driven by temperature gradients. The simulated results demonstrate that the pore migration rate is closely related to pore size and that thermal migration not only affects pore morphology but also exerts a dragging effect on GB movement. Meanwhile, the pinning effect of stationary particles significantly inhibits GB migration. In polycrystals containing high-pressure gas pores and second-phase particles, directional grain coarsening along the temperature gradient is governed by the ability of GBs to overcome pinning resistance and migrate effectively under thermal gradients, rather than by the temperature gradient or pore migration alone. This study reveals the kinetic competition between porosity migration, particle pinning, and GB migration in UO2 fuel under temperature gradients. It provides mechanistic insights into columnar crystal reconstruction driven by GB migration under gas-filled pore conditions.

专有名词解释

概率安全评价Probabilistic Safety Assessment, PSA

以事件频率、设备失效概率和后果模型量化核设施风险的方法。

本文语境 本文用 PSA 信息识别非能动安全系统中的关键失效路径。

非能动安全系统Passive Safety System

依靠重力、自然循环、压差或储能等自然机制完成安全功能，尽量减少外部动力和主动控制依赖。

本文语境 本文关注阀门等部件失效后非能动系统如何维持安全。

展开中英文摘要

中文摘要

先进反应堆越来越多地采用具备失效安全驱动的非能动安全系统，其中关键阀门通过持续通电保持，并在失电时移动到预定位置。然而，保持电源丧失也会导致误动作，因此常增加防误动措施。对大型核电厂和小堆设计的回顾表明，此类措施只出现在误动作会排放一回路冷却剂的场景，说明失效安全概念是否合适取决于其承担的功能。本研究对示例电厂开展概率安全评价，分析相关风险如何重新分配。包括失效安全属性在内的部件级特征不能只在部件层面验证，因为抑制误动作会增加驱动条件、失效模式和操作员动作，从而提高需求时失效概率。一个改进案例量化了权衡：防误动措施使误动作贡献下降约73%，但增加需求失效贡献，因此堆芯损坏频率净下降仅8%；后续简化设计则实现24%的下降。作者据此提出 PSA 支持的设计决策框架，要求迭代评价两方面风险，只有候选方案的全厂总风险最低时才定型，并概括了六种代表性设计案例。

English abstract

Advanced reactors increasingly employ passive safety systems (PSSs) with fail-safe actuation, in which key valves are held by continuous energization and move to predefined positions upon de-energization. Loss of the holding power, however, can also cause spurious actuation, so prevention features are often added. A review of large-plant and SMR designs shows such features appear only where unintended actuation discharges primary coolant, indicating that the suitability of a fail-safe concept depends on the function it serves. This study applies probabilistic safety assessment (PSA) to an example plant to examine how the resulting risk is redistributed. Component-level attributes, including the fail-safe designation itself, cannot be verified at the component level alone, since suppressing spurious operation adds actuation conditions, failure modes, and operator actions that raise failure on demand. A refinement example quantifies the trade-off: a prevention feature cut the spurious-operation contribution by about 73% but raised the demand-failure contribution, giving only an 8% net reduction in core damage frequency, whereas later simplification achieved 24%. A PSA-informed design decision framework is therefore proposed, in which both perspectives are evaluated iteratively and a design is finalized only when total plant-level risk is lowest among candidates. Six representative design cases are also generalized.

A three-dimensional boiling-flow simulation method was developed within the Eulerian two-fluid computational fluid dynamics framework for boiling water reactor (BWR) fuel assemblies. The method extends the previously developed two-dimensional axisymmetric PSI-CTU boiling model, originally formulated for the DEBORA experiment, to fully three-dimensional geometries and water boiling conditions at 72 bar. A key feature of the model is the coupling of a void-fraction-dependent bubble lift-force model with a population balance model (PBM) employing twenty bubble-size bins and tuned coalescence and breakup models. Different combinations of nucleation site density and bubble departure diameter models were employed for the DEBORA and SIRIUS-3D simulations because of the different working fluids. The model was implemented in Ansys Fluent through user-defined functions.

  2. 通过均匀化或代表体积单元得到有效导热率和热膨胀参数，并给出不确定区间。
  3. 在轴对称燃料棒 APDL 模型中加入间隙导热、接触和温度相关材料，把 LPD 历程转化为 PCI 载荷。
  4. 用燃料性能程序、公开辐照后数据或独立试验校核中心温度、间隙闭合和包壳应力。
