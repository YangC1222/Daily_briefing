中文摘要

在大多数熔盐反应堆（MSR）设计中，反应堆堆芯内的石墨慢化剂元件与熔融燃料盐直接接触，熔盐渗入石墨已成为一个主要问题。由于大多数现有的核石墨等级难以有效防止熔盐渗入，人们探索了各种后处理技术来提高其阻隔性能。本文提出了一种通过碳气凝胶填充孔隙来提高熔盐反应堆中石墨不渗透性的方法。该方法利用低粘度稀释的间苯二酚-糠醛溶液对核石墨进行真空压力渗透，然后进行一系列处理，将石墨孔隙中的溶液转化为碳气凝胶。研究结果表明，填充孔隙的石墨的孔径可调，并且熔盐渗入阈值压力可以显著提高，这已通过压汞实验和熔盐渗入实验得到证实。此外，经低模量碳气凝胶处理后，石墨样品的应力-应变曲线和热膨胀行为基本保持不变。填充碳气凝胶的低模量可能有助于适应快中子辐照过程中石墨的尺寸变化。

English abstract

In most molten salt reactor (MSR) designs, the graphite moderator elements within the reactor core come into direct contact with the molten fuel salt, and molten salt infiltration in graphite has become a major concern. Since most existing nuclear graphite grades struggle to prevent molten salt infiltration, various post-treatment technologies have been explored to enhance their barrier performance. This work presents an approach to enhance the impermeability of graphite in MSRs through pore-filling with carbon aerogel. This method utilizes vacuum-pressure infiltration of nuclear graphite with a low-viscosity diluted resorcinol-furfural solution, followed by a series of treatments to convert the solution in the graphite pores into carbon aerogel. The findings indicate that the pore diameter of pore-filled graphite is tunable, and the threshold infiltration pressure of the FLiBe salt can be significantly increased, as demonstrated by the mercury intrusion and molten salt infiltration experiments. Furthermore, when treated with low-modulus carbon aerogel, the stress-strain curve and thermal expansion behavior of the graphite sample remained largely unchanged. The low modulus of the filled carbon aerogel may facilitate the accommodation of dimensional changes in the graphite during fast neutron irradiation.

中文摘要

本研究定量评估了压水堆（PLUS7）燃料组件排水后残留的初始水量，这是决定乏核燃料干式储存真空干燥工艺效率和成功率的关键边界条件。为了研究流体动力学捕集机制，我们对系统模块化的组件（顶部和底部喷嘴、间隔栅格和活性燃料棒单元段）进行了多模式排水试验，包括重力排水、2 bar压缩空气吹扫、2 bar氦气吹扫以及氦气与水泵混合吹扫。结果表明，简单的重力排水会在全尺寸组件中残留高达8460.42 ± 20.30 g的水，因此必须采用主动加压排水。采用压缩空气吹扫后，总残留水量降至 3957.00 ± 20.30 g；而氦气吹扫由于其优异的扩散性和微流道渗透性，进一步将残留水量降至 3550.28 ± 20.30 g。值得注意的是，结合下游水泵和氦气的混合方法记录到最低的总残留水量，为 3495.08 ± 20.30 g。虽然水泵的液压阻力略微降低了小型单个部件的排水效率，但它显著延长了流体在占残留水量 96% 以上的大块活性燃料棒段中的停留时间，从而最大限度地延长了氦气气动剪切力的作用时间，有效去除粘性液膜。所建立的定量数据库提供了一种数据驱动的操作策略，可最大限度地减少残留水量，为长期干式储存许可的安全评估和工艺可靠性提供支持。

English abstract

This study quantitatively evaluates the initial residual water remaining after drainage in a pressurized water reactor (PLUS7) fuel assembly, a critical boundary condition determining the efficiency and success of the vacuum drying process in spent nuclear fuel dry storage. To investigate the hydrodynamic trapping mechanisms, multi-mode drainage tests—including gravity drainage, 2 bar compressed air purge, 2 bar helium gas purge, and a hybrid helium purge with a water pump—were conducted on systematically modularized assembly components (top and bottom nozzles, spacer grids, and active fuel rod unit sections). Results demonstrated that simple gravity drainage would leave a massive 8460.42 ± 20.30 g of water across a full-scale assembly, necessitating active pressurized drainage. The application of a compressed air purge reduced the total residual water to 3957.00 ± 20.30 g, whereas helium gas purging further decreased it to 3550.28 ± 20.30 g due to its superior diffusivity and micro-flow-channel penetrability. Notably, the hybrid method combining a downstream water pump and helium gas recorded the lowest total residual water of 3495.08 ± 20.30 g. Although the pump's hydraulic resistance slightly degraded drainage efficiency in small single components, it significantly extended the flow residence time in the vast active fuel rod sections—which account for over 96% of the trapped water—thereby maximizing the duration of the helium gas's aerodynamic shear force to effectively remove the viscous liquid film. The established quantitative database provides a data-driven operational strategy to minimize residual water, supporting safety evaluations and process reliability for long-term dry storage licensing.

中文摘要

人们对熔盐反应堆的兴趣重燃，促使人们研究泄漏事件中熔盐的铺展和凝固过程。本研究提出了一种基于GPU加速的全显式移动粒子方法（E-MPS），该方法考虑了表面张力、润湿性和相变模型。该模型应用于三维平面不锈钢板上的FLiNaK熔盐泄漏模拟，并获得了稳定的结果。在等温情况下，最终熔池半径与毛细—重力平衡的解析解吻合。考虑传热和凝固后，预测的前缘半径小于一维MELTSPREAD和二维ANSYS Fluent参考模拟的结果。在参考代码中，熔体惯性导致铺展超出静态平衡，而在E-MPS中，基于势的表面张力模型抑制了这种效应。灵敏度分析表明，密度、表面张力系数和接触角控制着铺展，而粘度和热导率的影响可以忽略不计。基底处的凝固使最终半径减小了约6%。 GPU 实现的计算成本与粒子数量呈线性关系，并且比多核 OpenMP CPU 实现快好几倍。

English abstract

Renewed interest in Molten Salt Reactors motivates the study of molten salt spreading and solidification during leakage events. This study presents a GPU-accelerated fully explicit moving particle method (E-MPS) with surface tension, wettability and phase-change models. The model was applied to a FLiNaK spill onto a flat stainless-steel plate in full 3D and produced stable results. For the isothermal case, the final puddle radius agreed with the analytical capillary–gravity equilibrium. With heat transfer and solidification included, the predicted leading-edge radius was lower than in the 1D MELTSPREAD and 2D ANSYS Fluent reference simulations. In the reference codes, melt inertia carries the spreading beyond the static equilibrium, whereas in E-MPS this effect is damped by the potential-based surface tension model. A sensitivity study showed that density, surface tension coefficient and contact angle govern the spreading, while viscosity and thermal conductivity have a negligible effect. Solidification at the substrate reduces the final radius by about 6 %. The computational cost of the GPU implementation scales linearly with the number of particles, and it is several times faster than a multi-core OpenMP CPU implementation.

中文摘要

液态铅铋合金中氧化铅颗粒的溶解特性是固态氧控制的关键因素之一。本研究采用粉末冶金法制备了高强度“球-柱”形氧化铅颗粒，并开发了一种迷宫式“气动驱动”传质器。通过数值模拟和实验分析，研究了低雷诺数下氧化铅颗粒的溶解和传质行为，并建立了低雷诺数范围内氧化铅颗粒的溶解模型。结果表明，该传质器的迷宫式流道设计能够稳定控制不同温度下的氧浓度，有效避免氧化铅颗粒的“中毒”现象。低雷诺数范围内“球-柱”形氧化铅颗粒的溶解模型与实验结果的偏差为15.5%，且氧化铅颗粒的溶解速率随温度升高而显著增加，其次是流速的影响。这项研究为后续铅铋快堆固态氧控制装置的设计奠定了基础。

English abstract

Solubility properties of lead oxide particles in liquid lead-bismuth alloys are one of the keys to solid-state oxygen control. In this study, high-strength “spherical-cylindrical” lead oxide particles were prepared by powder metallurgy, and a labyrinth-type “pneumatic pressure-driven” mass exchanger was developed. Through numerical simulations and experimental analyses, the dissolution and mass transfer of lead oxide particles at low Reynolds numbers were investigated, and the dissolution model of lead oxide particles in the range of low Reynolds number was developed. The results show that the labyrinth flow channel design of the mass exchanger can stably control the oxygen concentration at different temperatures, which can effectively avoid the “poisoning” phenomenon of lead oxide particles. The deviation of the dissolution model of “spherical-cylindrical” lead oxide particles in the low Reynolds number interval from the experimental results is only 15.5%, and the dissolution rate of lead oxide particles increases significantly with the increase of temperature, followed by the effect of flow velocity. This study lays the foundation for the design of solid-state oxygen control device for the subsequent lead-bismuth fast reactor.
