物理信息神经网络（PINN）已成为求解中子扩散方程等偏微分方程（PDE）系统的一种有前景的替代方案。传统PINN假定PDE参数固定，但实际反应堆堆芯内部的材料参数持续变化。这种变化使固定参数的传统PINN不再适用，因为模型需要重新训练。为解决这一问题，构建参数化PINN，以便在不同材料性质下进行推断，而无需重新训练。本文首先展示PINN在一维和二维几何中求解固定参数中子扩散方程的能力，并与参考有限差分求解器比较。然后，使用OpenMC生成的、反映反应堆工况真实物理变化的一系列材料参数训练网络，拓展PINN的功能。在OpenMC生成的参数范围内，取一百组随机材料性质，与参考求解器对比验证，显示与参考解良好吻合，同时具有大幅加快的计算流程。

English abstract

Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) have emerged as a promising alternative for solving systems governed by partial differential equations (PDEs), such as the neutron diffusion equation. Here, conventional PINNs assume fixed PDE parameters, however, in reality, the material parameters inside a nuclear reactor core vary continuously. The variation renders the conventional fixed parameters PINNs unsuitable as the models must be retrained. To address this challenge, a parametric PINNs model is built so inferences can be made on various material properties without the need for retraining. This article aims to demonstrates the capabilities of PINNs in resolving the neutron diffusion equation with fixed parameters in both one-dimensional and two-dimensional geometries and compared to the reference finite difference solver. Then, the functionality of PINNs is extended by training the network across a range of material parameters pertaining to real physical changes in reactor conditions generated through OpenMC. The model is then verified against a reference solver on one-hundred random material properties within the OpenMC generated range, showing good agreement with the reference solution but with a vastly accelerated calculation routine.

中文摘要

中子扩散计算是反应堆物理分析的重要组成部分。在多材料域反应堆模型中，扩散系数和宏观截面通常分段定义。它们在材料界面的不连续会导致中子通量梯度不连续，给基于深度学习的求解器带来挑战。本文将变域积分弱解（VIWS）理论应用于多材料域中子扩散问题，推导了适用于复杂几何和多群条件的VIWS形式。在该形式基础上，开发了用于固定源和本征值问题的无网格深度学习方法。使用单个神经网络表示全域中子通量，并在整个计算域施加VIWS残差约束，避免对材料界面采取专门数值处理。还引入原函数变换，将含连续核函数的弱形式积分项转换为微分约束。以有限元（FEM）解为参考的数值结果显示，VIWS比标准PINN具有更高准确性，并可求解多材料域中的二维和三维中子扩散问题。本工作为多材料域中子扩散提供了一种新的深度学习方法，并显示其拓展到大规模复杂反应堆物理计算的潜力。

English abstract

Neutron diffusion calculations are an important part of reactor physics analysis. In reactor models involving multi-material domains, diffusion coefficients and macroscopic cross sections are typically defined piecewise. Their discontinuities at material interfaces result in discontinuous neutron flux gradients, posing challenges for deep learning-based solvers. In this work, the variable-domain integral weak solution (VIWS) theory is applied to neutron diffusion problems in multi-material domains. A VIWS formulation is derived for complex geometries and multigroup conditions. Based on this formulation, a meshless deep learning method is developed for both fixed-source and eigenvalue problems. A single neural network is used to represent the global neutron flux, while VIWS residual constraints are imposed over the entire computational domain, avoiding special numerical treatments at material interfaces. An antiderivative transformation is also introduced to convert weak-form integral terms with continuous kernels into differential constraints. Numerical results using finite element method (FEM) solutions as references show that VIWS achieves higher accuracy than the standard physics-informed neural network (PINN) and can solve two-dimensional and three-dimensional neutron diffusion problems in multi-material domains. This work provides a new deep learning approach for neutron diffusion problems in multi-material domains and shows its potential for extension to large-scale, complex reactor physics calculations.

English abstract

An elitist strategy was implemented within both GA and DE frameworks to preserve high-fidelity solutions throughout the evolutionary process. The ICFM problem for the DNRR entails the strategic arrangement of 100 fuel bundles with diverse burnup levels, aiming to maximize fuel utilization while enhancing safety margins. Comprehensive computations were performed to evaluate the sensitivity of the algorithms to the ratio between the elitist archive size (A) and population size (NP). Furthermore, the integration of elitism enables DE to utilize larger population sizes effectively, overcoming the inherent limitations of its basic scheme and yielding superior solution quality. Statistical analysis reveals that the DE variant outperforms GA in terms of both convergence rate and solution diversity for DNRR fuel management. Notably, the optimal core configurations identified by both metaheuristics exceeded the performance of the reference core across all primary objectives, extending reactor operation by approximately 1700 h and reducing the power peaking factor by nearly 4.0%.
