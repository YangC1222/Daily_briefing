中文摘要

液态铅铋合金对X射线具有很强的衰减性，毫米级气泡仅产生较小的局部X射线透射率差异。因此，其成像质量和尺寸测量精度极易受到气泡几何特征和射线照相参数的影响。为了研究强衰减条件下毫米级气泡的X射线测量性能，我们根据液态铅铋合金中气泡的典型尺寸和形状，设计了球形、椭球形和球冠形三种模拟气泡。使用铅层来提供不同的X射线穿透厚度，并在不同的气泡等效直径、铅层厚度、管电压和曝光量下进行了数字射线照相实验。采用对比噪声比（CNR）评价气泡的可检测性，并通过测量值与设计值之间的偏差来评估尺寸测量精度。结果表明，三种模拟气泡形状的CNR均随等效直径的增大而增大，随铅层厚度的增大而显著减小。管电压和曝光量的影响相对较弱，且对成像条件有一定的依赖性。增加铅层厚度和减小靶材尺寸是导致测量误差增大的主要因素。提高管电压可以在一定程度上提高测量精度，而增加曝光量带来的改善则相对有限。在450 kV和15 mAs的条件下，当铅层厚度不超过10 mm时，等效直径为2～6 mm的球形和椭球形靶材的绝对尺寸误差小于0.50 mm，而2～5 mm靶材的绝对尺寸误差小于0.20 mm。对于球冠形靶材，在大多数情况下，测量误差也保持在0.50 mm以内。这些结果可为后续对液态铅铋中毫米级气泡进行X射线测量的实验测试段设计、成像参数选择以及动态测量条件评估提供参考。

English abstract

Liquid lead–bismuth exhibits strong X-ray attenuation, and millimeter-scale bubbles produce only small local differences in X-ray transmission. Consequently, their imaging quality and dimensional measurement accuracy are readily affected by both bubble geometric characteristics and radiographic parameters. To investigate the X-ray measurement performance of millimeter-scale bubbles under strong attenuation conditions, simulated bubbles with spherical, ellipsoidal, and spherical-cap geometries were designed according to the typical sizes and shapes of bubbles in liquid lead–bismuth. Lead was used to provide different X-ray penetration thicknesses, and digital radiography experiments were conducted under different bubble equivalent diameters, lead thicknesses, tube voltages, and exposure levels. The contrast-to-noise ratio (CNR) was used to evaluate bubble detectability, while dimensional measurement accuracy was assessed from the deviation between the measured and designed equivalent diameters. The results show that the CNR of all three simulated bubble shapes generally increases with increasing equivalent diameter and decreases markedly with increasing lead thickness. The effects of tube voltage and exposure are relatively weak and exhibit some dependence on the imaging conditions. Increasing lead thickness and decreasing target size are the primary factors responsible for increased measurement errors. Increasing the tube voltage can improve measurement accuracy to some extent, whereas increasing the exposure provides relatively limited improvement. At 450 kV and 15 mAs, when the lead thickness does not exceed 10 mm, the absolute dimensional errors of spherical and ellipsoidal targets with equivalent diameters of 2–6 mm are below 0.50 mm, while those of 2–5 mm targets are below 0.20 mm. For spherical-cap targets, the measurement errors are also maintained within 0.50 mm under most conditions. These results provide a reference for the design of experimental test sections, selection of imaging parameters, and evaluation of dynamic measurement conditions for subsequent X-ray measurement of millimeter-scale bubbles in liquid lead–bismuth.

中文摘要

液态燃料熔盐反应堆停堆后，衰变热会在整个循环燃料盐中产生，这与实验中常用的外壁加热方式不同。本研究采用计算流体动力学方法，在同一个自然循环回路中比较了集中式和分布式热源以及壁面加热和体积加热两种加热方式。除了FLiBe外，还加入了水和THERMINOL VP-1流体，以考察流体相关的响应。对于功率为1–5 kW的FLiBe，与集中式加热相比，分布式体积加热和壁面加热分别使循环质量流量降低了35.0%–48.9%和17.2%–22.4%。高度相关的密度差表明，分布式热源会削弱有利的浮力作用，并在上部垂直段引入相反的作用，从而降低循环驱动水头。在集中加热条件下，体积加热会增加三种流体的流量，但它们的功率响应不同：相对于壁面加热，FLiBe 的流量增幅从 26.3% 上升到 58.1%，而水和 VP-1 的流量增幅则随功率增加而下降。截面温度-质量通量相关性和壁面剪切结果表明，这种差异与低雷诺数、相对高普朗特数的 FLiBe 中更显著的温度和速度重新分布有关。在分布式加热条件下，两种加热模式下 FLiBe 的流量差异缩小至 -0.8% 至 4.1%，而局部温度和剪切分布仍然明显不同。这些结果表明，空间热分配是自然循环中的一个重要因素，并表明在使用分布式壁面加热来模拟内部热源时，应同时考虑循环流量和局部传热。

English abstract

Following the shutdown of a liquid-fueled molten salt reactor, decay heat is generated throughout the circulating fuel salt, unlike the external wall heating commonly used in experiments. This study uses computational fluid dynamics to compare concentrated and distributed heat sources and wall and volumetric heating in a common natural-circulation loop. Water and THERMINOL VP-1 are included alongside FLiBe to examine fluid-dependent responses. For FLiBe at 1–5 kW, distributed volumetric and wall heating reduce the circulation mass flow rate by 35.0%–48.9% and 17.2%–22.4%, respectively, relative to their concentrated counterparts. The height-dependent density differences show that distributed generation weakens favorable buoyancy contributions and introduces an opposing contribution in the upper vertical legs, reducing the circulation driving head. Under concentrated heating, volumetric heating increases the flow rate for all three fluids, but their power responses differ: the FLiBe increase relative to wall heating rises from 26.3% to 58.1%, whereas the increases for water and VP-1 decline with power. Sectional temperature–mass-flux correlations and wall-shear results associate this contrast with the more pronounced temperature and velocity redistribution in low-Re, relatively high-Pr FLiBe. Under distributed heating, the FLiBe flow-rate difference between the two heating modes narrows to −0.8%–4.1%, while local temperature and shear distributions remain distinct. These results identify spatial heat allocation as an important factor in natural circulation and show that both circulation flow rate and local heat transfer should be considered when using distributed wall heating to represent internal heat generation.

屏蔽设计是核反应堆辐射安全的基础；屏蔽验证作为闭环设计周期中的关键环节，通常通过基准屏蔽实验进行。屏蔽基准实验数据可用于验证、确认和改进屏蔽计算软件和屏蔽截面数据库。因此，独立开展深穿透屏蔽基准实验并获取可靠的基准实验数据具有重要意义。本文基于Cf-252中子源，介绍了一系列板状和球状屏蔽基准实验的设计研究。从中子源、屏蔽样品、散射扣除装置和具体实验内容等方面介绍了实验设计思路和结果。采用蒙特卡罗方法验证了散射扣除装置的有效性，并基于实验设计进行了测量不确定度的预评估。验证结果表明，所设计的阴影锥系统和准直器具有良好的散射扣除效果。球形屏蔽实验中散射中子干扰引起的最大相对偏差为5.21%，60厘米铁板屏蔽实验中为5.51%。不确定度预评估结果表明，球形屏蔽实验的测量相对不确定度不超过6.19%，97%的铁板屏蔽实验的测量相对不确定度低于20%。实验设计基本满足预期要求。

English abstract

Shielding design is fundamental to radiation safety in nuclear reactors; shielding validation, as a key component of the closed-loop design cycle, is typically carried out through benchmark shielding experiments. Shielding benchmark experiment data can be used for verification, validation and improvement of shielding calculation software and shielding cross-section databases. Therefore, it is of great significance to independently carry out deep-penetration shielding benchmark experiments and obtain reliable benchmark experimental data. This paper presents the design research of a series of plate and spherical shielding benchmark experiments based on a Cf-252 neutron source. The overall experimental design ideas and results are introduced from aspects such as neutron source, shielding samples, scattering subtraction device, and specific experimental contents. The Monte Carlo method is used to verify the effectiveness of the scattering subtraction device, and a pre-evaluation of measurement uncertainty is carried out based on the experimental design. The verification results show that the designed shadow cone system and collimator have good scattering subtraction effects. The maximum relative deviation caused by scattered neutron interference in spherical shielding experiments is 5.21%, and in 60 cm iron plate shielding experiments it is 5.51%. The uncertainty pre-evaluation results show that the relative uncertainty of measurements in spherical shielding experiments does not exceed 6.19%, and the relative uncertainty of measurements in 97% of plate shielding experiments is below 20%. The experimental design basically meets the expected requirements.

先进核电站安全壳内，蒸汽在换热管束表面凝结时，多组分混合气的输运特性对严重事故分析十分重要，而相关传热传质过程仍未充分阐明。本文针对3×3管束，研究氢气相对不凝气浓度Xh2/Xnc为30%–70%、管距为1.5d–5d的情况，开展机理数值分析。研究阐明了多组分不凝气叠加效应对流型和凝结的影响，分离流状态下凝结换热最弱。与单管相比，管束外凝结显著增强气体轴向和径向传质。在管束近壁区，不凝气叠加使氢浓度峰值高于单管近壁区，并抑制凝结，使总体传热系数低于单管。增大管距既能抑制管束区氢积聚，也可增强凝结换热。

English abstract

In advanced nuclear power plant containments, the transport characteristics of multicomponent mixture gas during steam condensation on the surface of heat transfer tube bundles are crucial for severe accidents. This complex heat and mass transfer phenomenon has not been clarified. Therefore, mechanism numerical simulation analysis was conducted on 3×3 tube bundle under the conditions of relative hydrogen concentration range of Xh2/Xnc = 30%–70% and tube bundle structure with tube pitch of 1.5d–5d in this paper. The mechanism underlying how the superposition effect of multicomponent non-condensable gases impacts the gas flow pattern and condensation was clarified, and the condensation heat transfer performance under the separated flow regime reaches its minimum. Moreover, condensation outside the tube bundle significantly promotes the axial and radial mass transfer of gas in severe accidents inside the containment compared to single tube conditions. In the tube bundle's near-wall region, the superposition effect of non-condensable gases leads to a higher peak hydrogen concentration than that in the wall-adjacent region of a single tube. This effect also suppresses condensation outside tube bundles, which consequently leads to lower overall heat transfer coefficients relative to single tubes. By increasing the tube pitch, not only can hydrogen accumulation in the bundle region be suppressed, but condensation heat transfer can also be enhanced.

中文摘要

本文基于LIVE(Late In-Vessel Phase Experiments)三维压水堆熔融池模拟实验,采用COMSOL Multiphysics软件建立了适用于熔融池的仿真计算模型。通过非等温流动模块耦合流体传热和湍流流动过程,使用相变材料模型模拟实验中的非共晶摩尔比例20%NaNO3-80%KNO3熔盐,湍流模型设置为低雷诺数k-ε模型。仿真结果表明,稳态下熔融池内存在较强的沿冷却壁面向下的自然对流,同时还伴随着大量的涡流,在涡流搅拌作用下温度场形成了明显的热分层结构。仿真得到的熔融池中部和顶部区域温度分布以及冷却壁面上的结壳厚度与实验值十分接近,说明COMSOL软件适用于熔融池仿真工作。
