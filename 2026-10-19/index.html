English abstract

This research work accurately simulates the response function of a liquid organic scintillator NE213 (BC501A) detector to fast neutrons in the energy range from 15 MeV to 800 MeV, taking into account the light transport within the detector-lightguide assembly. In contrast to previous studies, which mainly focused on the energy deposited by charged particles and neglected the light transport; this investigation uses the new GEANT4.11 code, which has integrated optical physics functions. This allows an investigation of the effects of the geometry, length, and type of polished and painted lightguide layers on the detection efficiency. The results are compared with experimental data and the output of the multipurpose code MCNPX-PHOTRACK. The analysis shows that the GEANT4 code has a high ability to reproduce optical features and neutron interactions accurately and shows improved agreement with experimental data in the investigated configuration and energy range, particularly when optical photon transport is explicitly modeled.

English abstract

Accurate prediction of subchannel temperatures in lead–bismuth eutectic reactors requires distinguishing directed crossflow from stochastic turbulent mixing. A Reynolds stress model, assessed against experimental temperatures and pressure-drop correlations, was applied to a 19-rod wire-wrapped bundle. The complete Reynolds stress tensor was projected onto the local normal direction of each gap, and quantitative statistics were evaluated downstream of the first wire-wrap pitch. For the 328 mm-pitch bundle, gap-normal velocity fluctuations exhibit approximately periodic variations downstream of the entrance region. The ratios of the pitch-averaged gap-normal fluctuating velocity to the pitch-averaged crossflow velocity are approximately 0.50 for Gap 1, 0.40 for Gap 3, and 0.12 for Gaps 5 and 7, with only minor variation across the tested mass flow rates. The CFD-derived internal mixing parameters are 8.48%-10.43% higher than the Cheng-Todreas predictions, whereas the peripheral sweeping parameters are 18.31%-18.38% lower. The proposed method provides a quantitative link between gap-resolved CFD results and subchannel mixing closures.

中文摘要

在不凝性气体存在时，管外蒸汽凝结是核电站性能与安全中的重要传热传质过程。已有实验主要研究单管的孤立几何参数，对低过冷度关注较少。本文在受控条件下，系统改变单根水平或倾斜管的长度、直径和倾角，研究蒸汽—空气混合物凝结。压力为0.2–0.6 MPa，空气质量分数0.13–0.89，壁面过冷度4.9–87.5 K。凝结换热系数随压力升高而增加，随空气浓度升高而单调下降；随管长增加先降后升，在3 m处最小；管径减小、姿态从竖直转向水平时换热系数单调增加。在低压、高空气份额且低过冷度条件下，换热系数与过冷度正相关，对较大管径或倾角的3 m管尤其明显。建立了适用于多种几何和较宽过冷度范围的经验关联式，与实验数据的偏差在±25%以内。

English abstract

Steam condensation on the external surface of tubes in the presence of non-condensable gases is a critical heat and mass transfer process, playing a vital role in nuclear power plant performance and safety assurance. Although prior experimental work has largely centered on isolated geometric parameters using single-tube configurations, limited attention has been paid to low subcooling conditions. To address this gap, the present study conducts systematic experiments on steam–air mixture condensation over a single horizontal or inclined tube, systematically varying tube geometry including length, diameter, and inclination under controlled operating conditions. Experiments span a pressure range of 0.2 ∼ 0.6 MPa, air mass fraction from 0.13 to 0.89, and wall subcooling between 4.9 ∼ 87.5 K. Key findings reveal that the condensation heat transfer coefficient (CHTC) exhibits a positive correlation with system pressure but declines monotonically as the air concentration increases. With increasing tube length, the CHTC initially decreases and then recovers, reaches a minimum at 3 m. It increases monotonically with decreasing tube diameter and with a change in inclination from vertical to horizontal. Under low pressure and high air fraction, a positive correlation between the CHTC and subcooling is observed in the low subcooling regime, notably for the 3 m tube with larger diameters or inclinations. Moreover, an empirical correlation applicable to various geometric conditions and a wider subcooling range was developed. Comparisons against existing correlations show that its prediction error falls within ± 25% relative to the experimental data.

中文摘要

GAINS是一款面向全堆芯临界性与中子学性能优化、基于构造实体几何（CSG）的GPU连续能量蒙特卡罗中子程序。它采用事件—历史混合流程抑制GPU线程束分歧，以预计算累积分布函数替代概率密度函数，采用直接索引数据布局，并将递归CSG遍历展开为基于栈的球面射线步进过程，还引入可定制的局部距离缓存。精度与性能在Pu-239 Jezebel、带可移动铪吸收体的圆柱燃料元件及全堆芯晶格工业基准上评估。相对参考结果的有效增殖因子偏差分别为21 pcm、各棒位平均40 pcm和28 pcm，主要归因于截面重采样误差使能谱偏硬。在RTX 4090上，1.36亿粒子历史在燃料元件及全堆芯基准中分别耗时23.26 s和271.48 s；相对双路Xeon、160线程的Serpent 2，加速比分别为47.2和6.1。开启缓存后分别达到57.6和8.2。该方法在与CPU CSG程序保持一致的同时，支持通用硬件上的快速优化迭代。

English abstract

GAINS is a GPU-accelerated Constructive Solid Geometry (CSG)-based continuous energy Monte Carlo neutron simulation code, designed for full core optimization, with regard to criticality and neutronics reactor performance. GAINS combines a hybrid event-history workflow to curb warp divergence, replaces Probability Density Functions (PDFs) with precomputed Cumulative Distribution Functions (CDFs), direct-indexed data layouts, and unrolls recursive CSG traversal into a stack-based scheme, which is evaluated using a spherical ray-march algorithm. Additionally, GAINS incorporates a localized and customizable distance cache. Accuracy and performance are assessed on three problems: the PU-239 Jezebel critical benchmark, a cylindrical fuel-element case (Base) with a movable hafnium absorber, and a full-core lattice industry benchmark. GAINS reproduces reference trends with small, traceable biases keff of 21 pcm (Jezebel), 40 pcm average across control-rod positions (Base), and 28 pcm (Benchmark), primarily attributable to resampling inaccuracies in cross-section data leading to a faster spectrum. On an RTX 4090, GAINS completes 136 million histories in 23.26 s (Base) and 271.48 s (Benchmark), achieving 47.2× and 6.1× speed-ups over Serpent 2 on a dual-Xeon (160-thread) node, with significantly better cost-to-performance. Enabling the cache increases these speed-ups to an overall speed-up of 57.6x - 8.2x, respectively. The resulting capability enables fast, accurate neutronics calculations suitable for optimization loops and rapid design iteration on commodity hardware, while maintaining agreement with CSG-based CPU codes.

English abstract

This study presents experimental results on large-scale containment thermal-hydraulics driven by the combined effects of steam injection and spray activation under a postulated design basis accident scenario, such as a main steam line break. The experimental campaign consisted of two experiments, identified as P1A1_5 and P1A1_6, which form part of the P1A1 series conducted within the OECD/NEA PANDA project to investigate PWR containment spray systems. The experiments were conducted in the facility, which consists of a large-scale cylindrical vessel. Initial conditions involved pressurization with air and steam up to 2.5 bar under superheated conditions. The spray system was activated using a single nozzle. The experiments were divided into three phases: steam injection only, combined steam and spray injection, and spray only injection. The results show that during containment depressurization, steam remains superheated above the spray nozzle, whereas below the nozzle the measured fluid temperature closely follows the saturation temperature. This behavior may be associated with enhanced condensation near the vessel wall and possible spray-induced flow recirculation. Upon spray activation, pressure and temperature gradients below the spray nozzle decrease more rapidly over time than during Phase 2, possibly reflecting spray-induced mixing and droplet dynamics.

中文摘要

聚变堆因其几何复杂、中子能量高、堆芯空间大,对蒙特卡罗粒子输运模拟带来一定挑战。本文采用蒙特卡罗程序cosRMC建立了基于中国聚变工程试验堆(China Fusion Engineering Test Reactor,CFETR)超临界二氧化碳(sCO2)液态锂铅包层(COOL)的22.5°扇段模型(CFETR-COOL)及水冷陶瓷增殖剂包层(water-cooled ceramic breeder,WCCB)的360°全堆模型(CFETRWHOLE),计算了CFETR-COOL包层模块的中子注量、氚增殖比和核热沉积,并采用自动迭代权窗减方差方法计算了CFETR-WHOLE模型的中子注量空间分布。结果表明,cosRMC计算的包层模块的中子学参数与MCNP符合良好,大部分相对偏差在±3σ以内。通过权窗自动迭代减方差方法,CFETR-WHOLE模型的中子注量计数网格占比从1.29%提高到94.32%,得到了全堆的中子注量空间分布。本文工作充分证明了cosRMC具备开展大型聚变反应堆中子学模拟的能力。
