堆芯装载方案（LP）设计是压水堆燃料管理的核心任务之一。工程中，方案构建高度依赖专家经验，并需在庞大的组合空间反复开展昂贵的堆芯物理计算，耗费人力且周期较长。本文提出LPGPT（装载方案生成预训练Transformer），在反应堆专属的条件—装载数据上从随机初始化开展自回归预训练，用于生成候选方案。以具有1/8堆芯对称性的代表性小型压水堆演示该方法。数据集包含PCM生成并评价的20,043组条件—装载对，词元级交叉熵训练和验证损失分别收敛至约0.24和0.23。LPGPT以焓升热通道因子FΔH、有效增殖因子、燃耗、最大和最小轴向偏移为条件，直接生成128词元的候选装载序列。PCM对代表性候选的评价显示，四项所要求的方向得到改善，但最小轴向偏移存在权衡。公开的三维扩散测试支持可复现的条件生成；MLP+GA与目标的符合程度更高。LPGPT支持先生成候选、再用PCM验证的流程。

English abstract

Core loading pattern (LP) design is one of the central tasks in Pressurized Water Reactor (PWR) fuel management. In engineering practice, LP construction relies heavily on expert heuristics and repeated computationally expensive core physics calculations over a vast combinatorial search space, resulting in substantial manual effort and long turnaround times. This paper proposes LPGPT (Loading Pattern Generative Pretrained Transformer), a domain-specific Transformer autoregressively pretrained from random initialization on reactor-specific condition–LP data for candidate generation. The approach is demonstrated on a representative small PWR with 1/8 core symmetry. The dataset contains 20,043 condition–LP pairs generated and evaluated with PCM; the token-level cross-entropy training and validation losses converged to approximately 0.24 and 0.23, respectively. LPGPT directly generates 128-token candidate LP sequences conditioned on the enthalpy-rise hot-channel factor (FΔH), effective multiplication factor, burnup, maximum axial offset, and minimum axial offset. PCM evaluation of a representative candidate shows improvement in four requested directions, with a trade-off in minimum axial offset. A public three-dimensional diffusion test supports reproducible conditional generation; MLP+GA achieves closer target agreement. LPGPT supports candidate generation with subsequent PCM verification.

中文摘要

蒙特卡罗方法是目前用于精确中子模拟的最先进方法，但这些高保真计算的计算成本对于多查询应用（例如设计、优化和灵敏度分析）而言可能难以承受。模拟的精度取决于粒子数量：对于低统计量（粒子数量少）的情况，计算成本通常较低，但结果会受到显著统计噪声的影响，这可能会损害结果的准确性；另一方面，对于高统计量的情况，结果更准确，但计算成本更高。为了将大量廉价的低统计量数据与稀缺的高统计量数据结合起来，本文提出了一种基于浅层解码器网络的多保真代理模型。浅层解码器网络是一类前馈神经网络，最初用于从稀疏传感器数据中重建流体，其架构简单，优先考虑快速训练、易于超参数调优以及学习基的物理可解释性。本文重点在于构建代理模型，利用输入参数（例如控制棒位置）或稀疏的中子通量测量数据，以及二者的结合，重建TRIGA Mark II反应堆堆芯中的中子通量场。研究表明，此类代理模型能够显著提高低保真度蒙特卡罗模拟的精度。通过从低统计量数据中提取主要特征，并利用少量高统计量模拟结果修正高保真度解，最终重建的中子通量场相对误差小于2%。

English abstract

Monte Carlo methods are the state-of-the-art for accurate neutronic simulations, but the computational costs associated with these high-fidelity calculations can be prohibitive for multi-query applications, such as design, optimization, and sensitivity analysis. The accuracy of the simulations depends on the number of particles: for low-statistics (few particles), the computational costs are generally lower, but the outcome is affected by a significant statistical noise, which can be detrimental for the accuracy of the results; on the other hand, for high-statistics, the results are more accurate, but the computational cost is higher. To merge the abundance of cheap low-statistics data with the scarcity of expensive high-statistics data, this work proposes a multi-fidelity surrogate model based on Shallow Decoder Networks, a class of feed-forward neural networks, originally proposed for fluid reconstruction from sparse sensor data, with a simple architecture that prioritizes fast training, easy hyperparameter tuning, and physical interpretability of the learned basis. In particular, this work focuses on generating surrogate models to reconstruct the neutron flux field in a TRIGA Mark II reactor core, using either input parameters (e.g., control rod positions) or sparse measurements of the neutron flux, as well as a combination of both. This work demonstrates that a surrogate model of this kind can be very useful in improving the accuracy of low-fidelity Monte Carlo simulations. By extracting dominant features from low-statistics data and correcting high-fidelity solutions with a small set of high-statistics simulations, a relative error of less than 2% is achieved in the reconstructed neutron flux field.

English abstract

The high density of reinforcement in nuclear containment structures often leads to unintentional internal voids due to construction difficulties. This study evaluates the cyclic performance of reinforced concrete shear walls with high reinforcement ratios and low aspect ratios, specifically addressing these construction-induced defects. Through a comprehensive experimental and numerical program, six specimens were tested under cyclic loading, with artificial voids introduced as primary variables. Results indicate that global responses remain similar, however voids significantly accelerate post-peak strength degradation and localize damage by disrupting internal shear transfer mechanisms. The developed finite element model, validated within a 5% error margin, was utilized for an extensive parametric study. The analysis identifies the void area ratio as the governing factor, showing a linear strength reduction that becomes more pronounced beyond a 20% ratio. Based on these findings, a practical evaluation-oriented approach is proposed to quantify shear strength reduction as a function of the void area ratio. This research provides essential guidance for the structural assessment and maintenance of heavily reinforced nuclear facilities containing internal defects.
