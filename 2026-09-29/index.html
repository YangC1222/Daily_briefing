中文摘要

鼓泡流在与核反应堆装置相关的多种过程中发挥重要作用，其中准确预测气体分布和相含率至关重要。在此背景下，本研究选取中试规模的鼓泡塔作为代表性算例，对单分散和多分散的均匀鼓泡流态开展CFD模拟。采用基于OpenFOAM Foundation软件的多相HZDR程序进行瞬态三维模拟，重点预测整体气相含率及径向气体体积分数分布。HZDR汇集的相间动量交换闭式关系作为本研究的起点。整体气相含率的计算结果与实验数据吻合较好。然而，径向气体体积分数分布的数值结果与实验数据存在偏差，在多分散均匀流态下尤其明显。具体而言，数值计算的局部气体体积分数在壁面附近出现峰值，与实验分布相反。研究讨论了这种偏差的可能原因，重点关注横向升力。为检验气泡群效应影响升力的假设，额外改变升力系数开展模拟，以评估其对气泡分布的影响。数值结果支持该假设，因为计算与实验的吻合有所改善。不过，靠近竖直壁面处仍存在偏差。这些结果为进一步改进鼓泡流数值建模提供了基础。

English abstract

Bubbly flows play a key role in several processes relevant to nuclear reactor plants, where the accurate prediction of gas distribution and phase holdup is of primary importance. In this context, this work presents CFD simulations of mono-dispersed and poly-dispersed homogeneous bubbly flow regimes in a pilot-scale bubble column selected as a representative case study. Transient three-dimensional simulations were performed using the multiphase HZDR code based on the OpenFOAM Foundation software, focusing on the prediction of global gas holdup and radial gas volume fraction profiles. The set of closure relations for the momentum exchange between the phases collected at HZDR serves as a starting point for the present investigation. Good agreement with the experimental data is obtained for the global gas holdup. Discrepancies between the numerical results and the experimental data are encountered in the prediction of the radial gas volume fraction profiles, especially in the poly-dispersed homogeneous flow regime. In particular, the numerical local gas volume fraction profiles are wall-peaked, in contrast to the experimental ones. Potential reasons for this deviation are discussed, with particular focus on the lateral lift force. To test the hypothesis of a swarm effect on the lift force, additional simulations were performed by varying the lift coefficient to assess its influence on bubble distribution. The numerical results support this hypothesis, as a better agreement with the experimental data is obtained. However, discrepancies remain in the proximity of the vertical walls. The results provide the basis for future improvements in the numerical modelling of bubbly flows.

中文摘要

核电厂退役过程中，活化结构材料的放射性表征是一项关键任务。强烈的空间非均匀性及受限的采样可达性，使核素存量估计和废物分类存在显著的认知不确定度。本研究提出一种考虑不确定度的代表性采样框架，将基于超像素的空间分割、高斯过程（GP）代理建模和贝叶斯优化结合起来，在采样条件受限时尽量降低不确定度。利用OPR-1000反应堆三维蒙特卡罗中子输运与活化分析获得的高分辨率活化场，通过超像素分割在保留空间非均匀性的同时降低维数。敏感性分析表明，将活化场离散为25层，可在不确定度表征与计算效率之间取得有效平衡。利用分割后的表示构建GP代理模型，显式量化预测不确定度，并通过贝叶斯优化指导序贯采样。该框架应用于Ag-110m和C-14的代表性活化算例。结果显示，不确定度迅速收敛，约5个经过优化的采样位置便足以达到接近最小的后验方差。所选采样点始终位于活化梯度较大的过渡区域，表明由不确定度驱动的采集函数能够有效优先选择信息增益最大的区域。该方法在实际退役条件限制下，减少所需样本数量并保持代表性。超像素分割与贝叶斯优化的结合，为采样设计提供了透明且可论证的策略，有助于可靠估计核素存量、降低分析成本，并改善OPR-1000退役中废物分类的决策。

English abstract

Radiological characterization of activated structural materials is a critical task in nuclear power plant decommissioning, where strong spatial heterogeneity and limited sampling accessibility introduce significant epistemic uncertainty in radionuclide inventory estimation and waste classification. This study proposes an uncertainty-aware representative sampling framework that integrates superpixel-based spatial segmentation, Gaussian process (GP) surrogate modeling, and Bayesian optimization to minimize uncertainty under constrained sampling conditions. High-resolution activation fields derived from three-dimensional Monte Carlo neutron transport and activation analyses for the OPR-1000 reactor were processed using superpixel segmentation to reduce dimensionality while preserving spatial heterogeneity. Sensitivity analysis indicated that discretizing the activation field into 25 layers provides an effective balance between uncertainty representation and computational efficiency. The segmented representation was used to construct GP surrogate models that explicitly quantify predictive uncertainty and guide sequential sampling through Bayesian optimization. The proposed framework was applied to representative activation cases for Ag-110 m and C-14. Results show that uncertainty converges rapidly, with approximately five optimized sampling locations sufficient to achieve near-minimal posterior variance. The selected sampling points were consistently located in transition zones with strong activation gradients, indicating that the uncertainty-driven acquisition function effectively prioritizes regions with the highest information gain. The proposed approach reduces the number of required samples while maintaining representativeness under practical decommissioning constraints. The integration of superpixel segmentation and Bayesian optimization provides a transparent and defensible strategy for sampling design, supporting reliable radionuclide inventory estimation, reduced analytical costs, and improved decision-making for waste classification in OPR-1000 decommissioning.

中文摘要

我们提出一种用于湍流中拉格朗日轨迹的模态表示，称为拉格朗日本征正交分解。利用一组粒子轨迹构建速度时间序列，对每条轨迹分别归一化，以分离速度脉动。随后对所得数据集进行主成分分析，并以各时间采样点构成特征空间。该方法在均匀各向同性湍流的直接数值模拟轨迹和三维粒子追踪实验轨迹上进行检验，两类数据的主要模态表现出相似结构与能量分布。通过组合模态和系数、恢复脉动尺度并进行时间积分，可以得到截断重构。对于持续时间与积分时间尺度同量级的轨迹，仅用有限数量的模态（约O(10)个）便能准确重现单粒子扩散及轨迹曲率统计；但要捕捉加速度分布的尾部及高阶拉格朗日结构函数，则需要更多模态（约40–80个）。更长轨迹的准确重构需要逐渐增加模态数。这些结果提示，可以通过对模态拉格朗日动力学进行随机采样，探索数据驱动的合成粒子轨迹生成方法。

English abstract

We introduce a modal representation for Lagrangian trajectories in turbulence, termed Lagrangian proper orthogonal decomposition. An ensemble of particle trajectories is used to construct velocity time series, which are normalized independently for each trajectory to isolate fluctuations. Principal component analysis is then applied to the resulting dataset, with temporal instances defining the feature space. The method is tested on trajectories from both direct numerical simulations of homogeneous isotropic turbulence and a three-dimensional particle-tracking experiment, showing that the leading modes exhibit similar structures and energy distributions in both cases. Truncated reconstructions are obtained by combining modes and coefficients, rescaling the fluctuations and integrating in time. For trajectories of the order of the integral time scale, single-particle dispersion and curvature statistics are accurately reproduced using a limited number of modes ( ∼O(10) ), whereas capturing the tails of acceleration distributions and higher-order Lagrangian structure functions requires a larger set ( ≈40–80). Longer trajectories require progressively more modes for accurate reconstruction. These results suggest a possible route to data-driven generation of synthetic particle trajectories via stochastic sampling of the modal Lagrangian dynamics.
