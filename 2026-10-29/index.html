中文摘要

反应堆实时状态估计须满足严格固定步长时限，高保真多物理场模型通常难以在线运行。本文在固定步长、多速率数字孪生原型中，将POD—算子推断降阶模型与集合卡尔曼滤波器结合。可复现全阶模型及可追溯日志生成可重放参考数据，用于训练验证，并记录能量项以检查能量核算和日志流程。在线时ROM按固定步长推进，EnKF同化稀疏间歇测量以修正漂移。部署前，用全阶参考流能量项构造能量一致性残差，统计其滑动窗口90%分位数P90-ECA。将离线NRMSE与P90-ECA，以及每步p99.9延迟、每小时超时次数和实时因子组成部署检查。典型瞬态、稳定化消融及步长扫描中，ROM+EnKF满足精度标准并保有实时裕量。计时指标开销很小，可用于运行监控；依赖参考值的ECA适于可重放数据回归测试。无参考值的代理监测留待后续研究。

English abstract

Real-time reactor state estimation must meet strict fixed-step deadlines, while high-fidelity multiphysics models are often too expensive for online use. We therefore couple a POD–OpInf reduced-order model (ROM) with an ensemble Kalman filter (EnKF) within a fixed-step, multi-rate digital twin prototype. A reproducible full-order model (FOM) and a traceable logging protocol generate replayable truth data for ROM training and validation, together with energy-term traces used to verify the energy bookkeeping and logging pipeline. During online execution, the ROM advances on the fixed-step grid and the EnKF assimilates sparse, intermittent measurements to correct drift. For pre-deployment qualification, we assess physics consistency with a truth-stream energy-consistency residual derived from FOM energy terms and summarize it by a sliding-window 90th percentile (P90-ECA). We combine offline accuracy and consistency metrics (NRMSE and P90-ECA) with runtime timing metrics (per-step p99.9 latency, Miss/h, and real-time factor, RTF) into a practical deployment checklist. Across representative transients, stabilization ablations, and step-size sweeps, ROM+EnKF meets the accuracy criterion while preserving real-time margin. The timing metrics add negligible overhead and support runtime monitoring, whereas truth-ECA provides a lightweight regression-test signal for replayable datasets. Proxy monitoring without truth access is left for future work.

中文摘要

分离—嬗变可减少次锕系处置负担，分离残留的稀土、铀和钚又改变燃料性能。本文以OpenMC建立500 MWth铅冷快堆，研究237Np、241Am、243Am燃料中三类残余的影响。在0–50 wt%范围，有效缓发中子份额非单调变化，未见明显下降。RE2O3和UO2使谱软化，PuO2使谱硬化。随着前两者增加，三种核素嬗变率及俘获/裂变比近似线性增加；随PuO2增加则下降。研究阐明不同残余的作用，为不纯次锕系燃料嬗变策略提供基础。

English abstract

Partitioning and Transmutation (P&T) is recognized as an effective strategy for mitigating the burden associated with the geological disposal of minor actinide (MA) nuclides. This study investigates the impact of three types of residual nuclides, namely, rare earth elements (REs), uranium, and plutonium, that remain in MA fuels during the partitioning process. Based on a 500 MWth lead-cooled fast reactor (LFR) developed using OpenMC, the effects of these residuals on the transmutation performance of 237Np, 241Am and 243Am are systematically analyzed. The results reveal that the effective delayed neutron fraction varies non-monotonically within the 0-50 wt.% residual nuclide content range, and no significant decrease is observed. Spectral analysis demonstrates that RE2O3 (rare earth oxide) and UO2 induce spectrum softening, whereas PuO2 hardens the spectrum. Transmutation calculations further show that for all three MAs, both the transmutation rates and capture-to-fission ratios increase approximately linearly with an increasing RE2O3 and UO2 content, whereas they decrease with an increase in PuO2 content. This research elucidates the distinct mechanisms of these residuals in LFRs, providing a foundational basis for developing transmutation strategies for impure MA fuels.

起始核态沸腾ONB和显著含汽起点OSV划分过冷沸腾状态，影响核反应堆及蒸汽发生器设计。本文汇集圆管、矩形及棒束低压ONB实验，发现经典模型离散较大，基于活化成核点密度与无量纲参数建立新关联，使ONB壁面过热预测平均绝对相对偏差降至19.7%。对OSV采用漂移流模型，分析截面空泡份额和轴向增长率，得到与修正无量纲Sauter直径的指数关系。结合气泡层厚度模型，分别表征外加热通道和内加热环隙的OSV局部参数。外加热通道气泡层平均空泡份额约0.1、近似不变；内加热环隙则随截面空泡份额指数增加。所建关系补充基于聚并OSV模型的局部闭合，为过冷沸腾建模提供支持。

English abstract

The onset of nucleate boiling (ONB) and the onset of significant void (OSV) are critical transition points that demarcate heat transfer regimes and govern the development of two-phase flow in subcooled boiling flows. Accurate prediction of these two characteristic points is of great significance for the thermal-hydraulic design and safety analysis of industrial heat exchange equipment, such as nuclear reactors and steam generators. This study focused on the local characteristics at the ONB and OSV points in vertical upward flow channels. Firstly, a low-pressure ONB experimental database covering various channel geometries (including circular tubes, rectangular channels, and rod bundles) was compiled, and the existing classical ONB prediction models were systematically evaluated. The results indicated that the existing models exhibited considerable scatter and significant deviations. To address this issue, a new ONB correlation was developed based on a correlation analysis of the active nucleation site density with dimensionless parameters. The developed correlation reduced the mean absolute relative deviation for wall superheat prediction at ONB to 19.7%, outperforming existing models. Secondly, for the OSV point, a comprehensive drift-flux model was employed to analyze the cross-sectional void fraction and its axial development rate, revealing an exponential correlation between these parameters and the modified dimensionless Sauter mean diameter. Furthermore, by incorporating a bubble layer thickness model, the local parameters of the bubble layer (i.e., bubble layer thickness and void fraction) at the OSV point were quantitatively characterized for both externally heated channels and internally heated annular channels, and corresponding predictive correlations were established. The results demonstrated that the average void fraction within the bubble layer remains essentially constant (approximately 0.1) in externally heated channels, whereas it increased exponentially with the cross-sectional void fraction in internally heated annular channels. The quantitative relationships established in this study addressed the deficiency of existing bubble coalescence-based OSV models in terms of closure relations for local parameters, thereby providing crucial theoretical support for the accurate modeling and engineering applications of subcooled boiling two-phase flows.

中文摘要

在核反应堆运行过程中,因一回路系统中的冷却剂大量、快速流失而引发的失水事故(loss of coolant accident,LOCA)是燃料包壳设计所参考的基准事故之一,同时也是影响包壳结构完整性与安全裕度的关键工况.为了分析事故发生后极端环境下涂层包覆事故容错燃料(accident tolerant fuel,ATF)包壳的力学行为,本文首先确定了LOCA发生后初期及中期的温度变化情况,在此基础上结合力-热-辐照多场耦合效应,模拟了两个完整燃料循环周期(0~48个月)内反应堆运行至不同时刻发生事故后包壳的力学响应特征.研究明确了两种涂层包覆ATF包壳的塑性应变分布规律及事故不同时期的塑性应变累积特性,结果表明:循环周期内塑性应变随时间逐步递增,且不同涂层表现出明显差异.同时,获取了不同时期总应变的极值范围,并识别出包壳内外侧及涂层交界处的关键响应区域.通过对比Cr与FeCrAl涂层在不同运行月份发生事故后初期与中期的力学响应差异,结合环向应变标准差变异系数反映的变形非均匀性特征,系统分析了应变能及塑性能量耗散的演化规律,进一步揭示了涂层类型、运行时间与包壳结构安全性之间的关联机制.本文的研究将为燃料包壳的设计优化及工程应用提供参考与借鉴.

压水堆组件入堆后,锆合金包壳表面会迅速形成氧化层,随着燃耗加深,氧化层厚度不断增加,并伴随污垢沉积现象。目前压水堆包壳和芯块相互作用(PCI)二类瞬态分析的研究中暂未考虑包壳氧化层演化对典型PCI二类瞬态影响。采用三维物理热工耦合的方法,对比分析了不同污垢和氧化层厚度下的典型PCI二类瞬态(URWP)热工瞬态响应。结果表明,随着氧化层和污垢厚度增加,降低燃料组件换热性能,增加燃料峰值温度,降低瞬态核功率增长速率,增大瞬态最小偏离泡核沸腾比(DNBR),延迟停堆时间约0.2 s。为后续的压水反应堆PCI二类瞬态分析方法的持续优化提供了借鉴。

中文摘要

上海交通大学数字核能研究所基于COMSOL平台开发了环形U-10Zr燃料多物理场耦合程序ANTHEM 1.0(Annular Nuclear Thermo-Mechanical Multiphysics 1.0),实现了传热、热膨胀、燃耗、蠕变、辐照肿胀、裂变气体释放及气室压力的耦合计算。通过与美国BISON基准算例进行代码对比,验证了ANTHEM 1.0在燃料温度、力学及气室压力等燃料行为预测上与BISON的一致性。计算结果显示,燃料温度随燃耗呈初期下降、裂变气泡连通前升高、裂变气泡连通后趋稳三阶段特征;燃料内径变化分为初期快速向内应变和裂变气泡连通后减缓两个阶段;气室压力表现为初期缓慢上升、裂变气泡连通时急剧增加及后期持续升高。上述规律与裂变气泡连通、燃料-包壳间隙闭合及孔隙率演化密切相关。研究揭示了环形U-10Zr燃料向内变形过程中温度、力学及气室压力的演化规律与机制。
