中文摘要

燃料成本是球床反应堆（PBR）运行费用的重要组成部分，而燃料球运动轨迹的不确定性可能造成辐照不足或过度辐照，从而降低燃料利用率，并可能使反应堆性能下降。因此，更准确地预测燃料球路径能够改善燃料管理的经济性，尤其是在负荷跟踪运行期间。球床反应堆中的颗粒流模拟是一项具有挑战性的任务，因为颗粒流受到材料属性和接触力学的影响，而这些因素会随着运行过程和燃料位置发生显著的时间变化。上述不确定性会直接传递到燃料球轨迹和停留时间的预测中，而这两个量是燃料管理的核心参数。本文报告了 PebbleTRACK 的开发与验证。PebbleTRACK 是一种由离散元法（DEM）提供信息、用于快速评估燃料球轨迹的统计框架。该方法利用无网格 DEM 模拟生成高分辨率轨迹数据和接触相互作用数据，其中包括动态表面相互作用。PebbleTRACK 对轨迹和停留时间的预测与实验数据符合良好，而所需计算时间约为完整 DEM 模拟的 5.52%。尽管该框架是针对球床反应堆应用开发的，但它可以扩展到任意几何结构的颗粒流预测。这些组成和性能为实时预测燃料球轨迹提供了一条计算效率较高的路径，可服务于先进燃料管理策略；未来还将把轨迹与中子通量历史结合，以帮助确定燃料球的燃耗。

English abstract

Fuel costs are a major component of pebble bed reactor (PBR) operating expenses, and uncertainty in pebble trajectories can lead to under- or over-irradiation, reducing fuel utilization and potentially degrading performance. More accurate pebble-path prediction can therefore improve fuel-management economics, particularly during load-following operation. Simulating granular flow in PBRs is a challenging task because it is affected by material properties and contact mechanics, which exhibit a large degree of time variability because of operation and fuel position. These uncertainties propagate directly into predictions of pebble trajectories and residence times, which are central quantities for fuel management. This paper reports the development and validation of PebbleTRACK: a discrete element method (DEM)-informed statistical framework for rapid pebble trajectory assessment. The PebbleTRACK approach uses mesh-free DEM simulations to generate high-resolution trajectory and contact-interaction data, including dynamic surface interactions. PebbleTRACK-predicted trajectories and residence times agree well with experimental data, requiring approximately 5.52% of the computational time as compared to a full DEM simulation. While developed for PBR applications, PebbleTRACK can be extended to produce granular flow predictions for an arbitrary geometry. These components and performances provide a computationally efficient path to predicting real-time pebble trajectories suitable for advanced fuel management strategies that will be combined with the neutron flux history to help determine a pebble’s burnup in the future.

中文摘要

本研究基于 MATLAB/Simulink 平台建立了敏捷微型反应堆系统（AMS）的动态模型，其中热管反应堆与超临界二氧化碳（sCO₂）布雷顿循环相结合。在此基础上，研究考虑了两种控制策略：一种保持透平入口质量流量恒定，另一种保持透平入口温度恒定。为评价两种策略的性能，作者对系统功率由 100% 满功率（FP）降至 90% 满功率的降载过程进行了瞬态模拟。结果表明，当透平入口质量流量保持恒定时，透平入口温度由 873.3 K 降至 823.8 K，循环效率降至 27.05%，系统稳定所需时间较长，约为 1900 s。相比之下，在透平入口温度恒定的策略下，质量流量由 16.5 kg/s 降至 16.02 kg/s，循环效率达到 27.13%，系统可在约 700 s 内稳定。总体而言，保持透平入口温度恒定的控制策略使关键系统参数响应更快、稳定时间更短，因此更适合用于先进核能系统的控制系统开发。相关结果可为敏捷微型反应堆系统未来的工程应用提供技术支持。

English abstract

This work establishes a dynamic model of an agile microreactor system (AMS), in which a heat pipe reactor is integrated with a SCO2 Brayton cycle, based on the MATLAB/Simulink platform. On this basis, two control strategies are considered, with one maintaining a constant turbine inlet mass flow rate and the other maintaining a constant turbine inlet temperature. To evaluate their performance, transient simulations are carried out for a load reduction from 100%FP to 90%FP. The results indicate that, when the turbine inlet mass flow rate is maintained constant, the turbine inlet temperature decreases from 873.3 K to 823.8 K, and the cycle efficiency is reduced to 27.05%, with a relatively long stabilization time of about 1900 s. In contrast, under the constant turbine inlet temperature strategy, the mass flow rate decreases from 16.5 kg/s to 16.02 kg/s, while the cycle efficiency reaches 27.13%, and the system can be stabilized within approximately 700 s. Overall, the control strategy with constant turbine inlet temperature demonstrates a faster response of key system parameters and a shorter stabilization time. This advantage makes it a more favorable option for control system development in advanced nuclear energy systems. These could provide technical support for the engineering application of AMS in the future.

中文摘要

不凝性气体（NCG）会削弱热管的传热性能，进而影响热管反应堆系统的运行安全。为揭示不凝性气体对热管冷却反应堆加热单元的影响，本研究采用三级嵌套迭代过程，将钠热管分析程序与 COMSOL Multiphysics 中的三维加热单元模型进行耦合，并据此数值模拟加热单元的稳态热工水力耦合特性。结果表明，提高不凝性气体的初始体积分数会使热管和加热单元的运行温度升高。当不凝性气体初始体积分数由 0% 增至 6% 时，最大蒸汽速度和最大压降分别下降约 33.7% 和 35.1%；与此同时，平均蒸汽温度由 1159.0 K 升至 1208.1 K，加热单元最高温度升高约 3.8%。提高燃料棒平均功率会减小不凝性气体所占体积。当功率由 1.25 kW 增至 2 kW 时，加热单元最高温度升高约 13.5%，但不凝性气体引起的温升幅度降低了 46.3%。本文结果可为考虑不凝性气体效应的热管反应堆热分析和安全评价提供参考。

English abstract

Non‑condensable gas (NCG) deteriorates the heat transfer performance of heat pipes, thereby affecting the operational safety of heat pipe reactor systems. To reveal the influence of NCG on the heating unit of a heat pipe cooled reactor, this study couples a sodium heat pipe analysis program with a 3D heating unit model in COMSOL Multiphysics via a three‑level nested iterative procedure. The steady‑state thermal‑hydraulic coupling characteristics of the heating unit are then numerically simulated. The results reveal that increasing the initial NCG volume fraction raises the operating temperature of the heat pipe and the heating unit. When the initial NCG volume fraction increases from 0% to 6%, the maximum vapor velocity and the maximum pressure drop decrease by approximately 33.7% and 35.1%, respectively, while the average vapor temperature rises from 1159.0 K to 1208.1 K, and the maximum temperature of the heating unit increases by about 3.8%. Increasing the average fuel rod power reduces the volume occupied by NCG. When the power increases from 1.25 kW to 2 kW, the maximum temperature of the heating unit increases by about 13.5%, but the temperature rise amplitude induced by NCG decreases by 46.3%. The results presented in this paper provide a reference for thermal analysis and safety assessment of heat pipe reactors considering non‑condensable gas effects.

研究开发了一套高度可扩展的安全分析程序 SCENES，用于先进反应堆安全设计和多物理场耦合分析。该程序采用两流体六方程模型，使用交错网格上的有限体积法进行离散，并通过 Newton–Raphson 方法求解。作者采用含汽率平滑方法改进了 Henry–Fauske 临界流模型。为提高可扩展性，程序实现了两项关键功能：一是能够自动求解所有控制体坐标的递归坐标模型，二是采用命名管道与外部程序集成的通用耦合框架。作者利用现象学算例、分离效应实验和扩展性测试开展验证。结果表明，改进后的临界流模型消除了两相过渡区中的质量流量不连续问题；计算得到的热工水力参数与解析解、实验数据和参考结果符合良好；坐标模型能够准确确定一维和二维坐标；数据也能够在耦合接口之间准确传递。SCENES 因此为核电安全分析提供了一套集成化且高度可扩展的工具。

English abstract

A highly extensible safety analysis code, SCENES, was developed for advanced reactor safety design and multi‑physics coupling analysis. The code adopted a two‑fluid six‑equation model, discretized by the finite volume method on a staggered mesh and solved with the Newton–Raphson method. The Henry–Fauske critical flow model was improved using a quality smoothing method. Two key features were implemented to enhance extensibility: a recursive coordinate model that automatically solves the coordinates of all control volumes, and a generic coupling framework using named pipes for integration with external codes. Phenomenologic cases, separate effects experiments and extension tests were used for validation. The results show that the modified critical flow model resolves the mass flow rate discontinuity in the two‑phase transition region, the calculated thermal‑hydraulic parameters agree well with analytical solutions, experimental data and reference results, the coordinate model accurately determines one and two dimensional coordinates, and data can be accurately transferred between the coupling interfaces. SCENES provides an integrated, highly extensible tool for nuclear power safety analysis.

本研究对 DFRm 反应堆的运行反应性控制策略进行了中子学评估。作者建立了包含燃料燃耗的完整蒙特卡罗模型，用于评价三种控制方式：控制棒、控制鼓和燃料液位调节。在每个燃耗步，研究均使用自动搜索脚本确定维持近临界状态所需的控制位置。改进后的控制棒构型具有最高的寿期初（BOL）反应性价值，达到 13262 pcm，停堆裕量为 11625 pcm；基于标准反射层的控制棒系统则实现了最长的运行周期，约为 3960 d。控制鼓系统在功率分布方面较为均衡，并提供 6189 pcm 的停堆裕量，但它明显更早达到次临界极限，约在 1530 d 后即达到该状态。燃料液位控制呈现平滑的反应性响应，并可维持临界状态最长约 3640 d。补充开展的非对称停堆计算表明，即使一个控制棒组或一个控制鼓停留在反应性最高的位置，基于吸收体的系统仍能保持次临界。对四种吸收材料的比较显示，在所研究的构型中，B₄C 的反应性抑制效果最好。核数据敏感性计算表明，与 JEFF-4.0 相比，ENDF/B-VIII.1 会得到更偏次临界的预测结果。研究结果说明，专门设置的控制系统能够为双流体反应堆技术提供有效的运行和停堆能力，使反应性管理不再仅依赖固有温度反馈。

English abstract

This study presents a neutronic assessment of operational reactivity control strategies in a DFRm reactor. A full Monte Carlo model with fuel depletion was developed to evaluate three control approaches: control rods, control drums, and fuel level adjustment. An automated search script was applied at each burnup step to determine the control position required to maintain near-critical conditions. The modified control rod configuration provided the highest BOL reactivity worth, reaching 13262 pcm with a shutdown margin of 11625 pcm, while the standard reflector-based control rod system achieved the longest campaign, about 3960 days. Although the control drum system was well balanced in terms of power distribution and provided a shutdown margin of 6189 pcm, it reached the subcritical limit noticeably earlier, after about 1530 days. Fuel level control showed a smooth reactivity response and maintained criticality for up to 3640 days. Additional asymmetric shutdown calculations showed that the absorber-based systems remain subcritical even when one control rod group or one control drum remains in its most reactive position. The comparison of four absorber materials showed that B4C provides the most effective reactivity suppression in the considered configurations. Nuclear data sensitivity calculations showed that ENDF/B-VIII.1 leads to more subcritical predictions than JEFF-4.0. Obtained results demonstrate that dedicated control systems can provide meaningful operational and shutdown capabilities in DFR technology, extending reactivity management beyond inherent temperature feedback alone.

中文摘要

由于循环流动的燃料盐会携带缓发中子先驱核（DNP），熔盐反应堆（MSR）的无保护失流（ULOF）瞬态受热工水力与中子学之间独特耦合作用的支配。本研究使用 SIMMER-III 多物理场程序，分析了一座与 LOTUS 设计相似的通用快谱氯盐反应堆在无保护失流事故中的响应。研究考察了此前观察到的功率演化双峰现象，并分析该行为出现的条件。随着流量下降，缓发中子先驱核滞留在堆芯中，这是第一功率峰形成的主要原因；第二功率峰则由温度较低的燃料盐从换热器返回堆芯引起，该过程会引入正反应性。在两个功率峰之间，当负温度反馈暂时占主导时，会出现功率低谷。作者通过全面的参数研究考察关键运行参数对这些特征的幅值、出现时间及是否出现的影响。结果表明，较高的质量流量和较长的惰走时间通常会提高第一功率峰的幅值，而较低的换热器温度和较低的初始功率会增强两个功率峰并延长振荡。在高功率运行及特定流动条件下，双峰行为可能受到明显抑制，甚至完全消失；低功率运行的熔盐反应堆则更容易出现显著功率振荡。例如，在参考条件下，初始功率为 0.8 MW 时，第一峰约为初始功率的 104%，随后功率降至 55%，第二峰又升至 108%。在其他条件相同的情况下，将初始功率提高至 3.2 MW 会使第一功率峰消失，功率直接下降至初始值的 24%，随后恢复至 40%。分析显示，熔盐反应堆的瞬态行为对稳态设计参数高度敏感，并强调在液态燃料反应堆安全评价中必须准确模拟缓发中子先驱核的平流输运和换热器性能。

English abstract

Unprotected loss-of-flow (ULOF) transients in molten salt reactors (MSRs) are governed by a unique interplay between thermal-hydraulics and neutronics due to the circulation of the fuel salt, which carries the delayed neutron precursors (DNPs). This study analyzes a generic fast-spectrum molten chloride reactor, similar to the LOTUS design, during a ULOF accident using the SIMMER-III multi-physics code. The work investigates the previously observed double-peak in the power evolution and examines the conditions under which this behaviour occurs. The first power peak arises primarily from the retention of DNPs in the core as flow decreases, while the second peak is driven by the return of colder fuel salt from the heat exchanger, inserting positive reactivity. Between these peaks, a power valley occurs when negative temperature feedback temporarily dominates. A comprehensive parametric study examines the influence of key operational parameters on the magnitude, timing, and presence of these characteristic features. Results demonstrate that higher mass flow rates and longer coast-down times generally increase the amplitude of the first power peak, while lower heat exchanger temperatures and lower initial powers enhance both peaks and prolong oscillations. For high-power operation​, and certain flow conditions, the double-peak behavior can be significantly damped or even eliminated. For MSRs operating at low power, the risk of significant power oscillations increases. For example, for the reference conditions, an initial power of 0.8 MW results in a first peak of approximately 104%, a subsequent valley of 55%, and a second peak of 108% of the initial power. Increasing the initial power to 3.2 MW under the same conditions eliminates the first peak, while the power decreases directly to 24% before recovering to 40% of the initial value. The analysis highlights the strong sensitivity of MSR transient behavior to steady-state design parameters and underscores the importance of accurately modelling DNP advection and heat exchanger performance in liquid-fuel reactor safety assessments.

中文摘要

随着对长续航海洋能源系统的关注不断增加，铅基反应堆因具有固有安全特性和低压运行能力而受到海洋核能应用领域的重视，但仍面临若干技术挑战。为揭示典型平移工况下水平布置铅铋堆芯的流动与传热特性，本研究采用数值模拟方法，建立了能够模拟平移运动条件下棒束通道内铅铋共晶（LBE）流动与传热特性的数值模型。结果表明，在横荡工况下，缩短平移周期会增强附加惯性力场，使二次流结构及其对应的局部传热响应发生更加明显的变化。相比之下，在所研究工况范围内，出口平均温度、轴向质量流量以及组件平均对流换热系数几乎保持不变。不同平移方向下的响应机制存在显著差异。纵荡主要扰动轴向动量平衡，使进出口压降波动最高达到 225.95%，但对横向流动结构和全局平均传热参数的影响有限。横荡对二次流结构和子通道间混合的改变更加明显。升沉对全局热工水力状态的影响较弱，不过部分窄间隙区域对等效重力场变化表现出相对较高的局部敏感性。本研究结果可为海洋铅基反应堆的热工水力评价提供参考。

English abstract

With the growing interest in long-endurance marine energy systems, lead-based reactors have attracted attention for marine nuclear applications due to their inherent safety characteristics and low-pressure operation capability, though technical challenges remain. To reveal the flow and heat transfer characteristics of the horizontal lead-bismuth core under typical translational conditions, based on the numerical simulation method, a numerical model capable of accurately simulating the flow and heat transfer characteristics of lead-bismuth eutectic (LBE) in rod bundle channels under translational conditions is established in this study. The results show that, under sway conditions, shortening the translational period strengthens the additional inertial-force field and produces more evident changes in the secondary-flow structure and relative local heat-transfer response. In contrast, the outlet-averaged temperature, axial mass flow rate, and assembly-averaged convective heat-transfer coefficient remain nearly unchanged under the investigated conditions. There are significant differences in response mechanisms under different translational directions. Surge mainly perturbs the axial momentum balance, resulting in inlet–outlet pressure-drop fluctuations of up to 225.95%, while its influence on the transverse-flow structure and global averaged heat-transfer parameters is limited. Sway produces a more evident modification of the secondary-flow structure and inter-subchannel mixing. Heave has only a weak influence on the global thermal-hydraulic state, although selected narrow-gap regions exhibit comparatively higher local sensitivity to variations in the equivalent gravitational field. The results of this study provide a reference for the thermo-hydraulic assessment of marine lead-based reactors.
