中文摘要

先导式泄压隔离阀（PORIV）是维持核电站安全稳定运行的关键设备。然而，由于阀门内部存在显著的多物理场耦合效应以及流体引起的不稳定性，目前对其动态性能特征的全面了解尚不充分。本研究开发了一种高效的混合计算框架，该框架融合了计算流体动力学（CFD）和代理模型技术，用于研究PORIV的非线性动态行为。利用CFD模型，分析了阀门运动过程中流体受力的变化、内部流动特性以及涡旋位置。采用数据驱动的平滑策略，揭示了由流动不稳定性以及流固耦合作用引起的流体受力波动。基于响应数据，构建了高精度代理模型，用于预测关键动态响应，包括运动时间、流体受力均方根值和峰峰值。利用代理模型，揭示了关键结构参数对阀门动态性能的影响模式。结果表明，每个结构参数对不同的动态特性具有不同程度的影响。即使结构参数的微小变化也会导致阀门性能的显著改变。此外，结构参数之间复杂的耦合效应使得难以独立表征每个参数对阀门性能的影响。本研究加深了对PORIV动态特性的理解，并为其结构设计和性能优化提供了参考。

English abstract

The pilot operated release isolation valve (PORIV) is critical equipment for maintaining the safe and stable operation of nuclear power plants. However, due to significant multi-physics coupling effects within the valve and fluid-induced instabilities, there is currently a lack of comprehensive understanding of its dynamic performance characteristics. In this study, an efficient hybrid computational framework integrating computational fluid dynamics (CFD) and surrogate modeling techniques is developed to investigate the nonlinear dynamic behavior of the PORIV. Using the CFD model, the variation of fluid forces during valve motion, internal flow characteristics, and vortex locations were analyzed. A data-driven smoothing strategy was employed to reveal fluid force fluctuations induced by flow instabilities and fluid–structure interactions. Based on the response data, high-accuracy surrogate models are constructed to predict key dynamic responses, including motion time, root mean square of fluid forces, and peak-to-peak values. Using the surrogate model, the influence patterns of key structural parameters on valve dynamic performance were revealed. The results indicate that each structural parameter exerts varying degrees of impact on different dynamic characteristics. Even minor changes in structural parameters can lead to significant alterations in valve performance. Furthermore, the complex coupling effects among structural parameters make it difficult to independently characterize the influence of each parameter on valve performance. This study deepens the understanding of the dynamic characteristics of the PORIV and provides a reference for their structural design and performance optimization.

中文摘要

生物屏蔽混凝土的长期力学性能是老旧研究堆退役评估中的重要考量因素。本研究针对已退役的重水研究堆（HWRR）的生物屏蔽墙，建立了一套针对该反应堆的评估框架。首先利用MCNP计算了累积快中子注量的空间分布，并以此表征屏蔽墙内的暴露场。然后从屏蔽墙的代表性位置提取混凝土芯样，测量其抗压强度，并与细观模拟结果进行比较。HWRR混凝土包含含石英骨料、铸铁块、铁珠和砂浆，这些成分根据已有的工程资料在细观模型中进行了表征。通过模拟应力-应变响应曲线上升段的斜率，获得了各暴露水平下的弹性模量，并建立了残余强度和刚度关系，用于后续的结构评估。然后利用残余刚度关系构建空间刚度场，并通过USDFLD子程序将其映射到宏观HWRR模型上，以评估位移、应力重分布和结构安全裕度。分析表明，空间变化的材料属性场比均匀属性模型产生更大的局部拉应力集中。计算得到的位移和拉应力均低于既定的工程限值。所提出的框架将中子场计算、混凝土取芯试验、细观力学分析、空间属性映射和老化核混凝土结构的结构安全评估联系起来。

English abstract

The long-term mechanical performance of biological shielding concrete is an important consideration in the decommissioning assessment of aging research reactors. This study develops a reactor-specific assessment framework for the biological shielding wall of a decommissioned heavy water research reactor (HWRR). The spatial distribution of cumulative fast-neutron fluence was first calculated using MCNP and used to characterize the exposure field within the shielding wall. Concrete cores were extracted from representative locations of the shielding wall, and their compressive strengths were measured and compared with the mesoscopic simulation results. The HWRR concrete contains quartz-bearing aggregates, cast-iron blocks, iron beads, and mortar, and these constituents were represented in the mesoscopic model according to the available engineering information. The elastic modulus at each exposure level was obtained from the slope of the ascending portion of the simulated stress–strain response, and residual strength and stiffness relationships were established for the subsequent structural assessment. The residual stiffness relationship was then used to construct the spatial stiffness field, which was mapped onto the macroscopic HWRR model through the USDFLD subroutine to evaluate displacement, stress redistribution, and structural safety margins. The analysis shows that the spatially varying material-property field produces greater local tensile-stress concentration than the uniform-property model. The calculated displacement and tensile stress remain below the adopted engineering limits. The proposed framework links neutron-field calculation, core testing, mesoscopic mechanical analysis, spatial property mapping, and structural safety assessment for aging nuclear concrete structures.

中文摘要

球床高温气冷堆中球形燃料元件随机堆积。水或空气侵入事故时，氧化性气体与球形元件基体石墨反应，可能导致燃料颗粒暴露并威胁固有安全。因此随机堆积石墨氧化特性对安全评价很重要。本文基于清华大学IG-110核石墨氧化实验开展CFD模拟，研究6 mm随机石墨球在870–1366 K、流量200 mL/min、20% O₂和80% He气氛中的氧化。结果表明流动、传质和氧化均高度不均匀；因沿程氧气消耗，氧化率随床层深度逐层降低。单个球的氧化不均匀性与其氧化率不存在简单正相关。1366 K时，前六层石墨球的不均匀性因子范围4–15，平均值5–12；870 K时，大多为2–10，平均约4–6。这些结果为球床高温气冷堆局部氧化与燃料颗粒暴露风险评价提供定量依据。

English abstract

In pebble-bed high temperature gas-cooled reactor (HTGR), spherical fuel elements are randomly packed in reactor core. Under water or air ingress accidents, oxidizing gases enter the core and react with matrix graphite of spherical fuel elements. Oxidation of the matrix graphite may lead to fuel particles exposure, thereby threatening the inherent safety of nuclear reactor. Therefore, research on the oxidation characteristics of randomly packed spherical nuclear graphite is important for safety assessment of pebble-bed HTGRs. In this study, computational fluid dynamics simulations were performed based on oxidation experiments of IG-110 nuclear graphite conducted at Tsinghua University. The oxidation behavior of randomly packed 6 mm graphite spheres were simulated under conditions of 870–1366 K and a flow rate of 200 mL/min (20% O2 and 80% He). The results show that the randomly packed graphite spheres exhibits pronounced non-uniformity in flow, mass transfer, and oxidation. The oxidation rate of graphite spheres decreases layer by layer with increasing bed depth due to oxygen consumption along the flow. In addition, no simple positive correlation is observed between oxidation non-uniformity of an individual graphite sphere and its oxidation rate. At 1366 K, the oxidation non-uniformity factors of graphite spheres in first six layers range from 4 to 15, with average values between 5 and 12. At 870 K, the non-uniformity factors in the first six layers mostly range from 2 and 10, with average values of approximately 4–6. These findings provide quantitative support for evaluating local graphite oxidation and fuel-particle exposure risk in pebble-bed HTGRs.

中文摘要

在极端环境下，移动电源的需求迫切。开式布雷顿循环气冷微反应堆是理想的解决方案。这种直接循环装置无需单独的二次传热回路即可在反应堆内加热涡轮机的工作空气。然而，目前缺乏针对这类强耦合多物理场系统的高效分析工具。本研究开发了移动式气冷微反应堆涡轮系统瞬态分析程序（MGTS-TAP）。该程序集成了中子动力学、热工水力学和涡轮机械动力学。模型已通过HTRE-3实验数据验证。关键运行参数的平均相对误差为1.98%，而涡轮机入口压力的最大相对误差为5.50%。传统的阶跃控制会在负载变化期间导致严重的电力过冲和欠冲。这是由于中子动力学和热惯性之间的响应速率不匹配造成的。本文提出了一种分级反应性控制策略。该策略利用温度反馈动态匹配反应性插入速率。在26秒内实现了平稳的功率过渡。功率偏差和温度回弹均有所降低。负荷跟踪控制应优先考虑电力稳定性。本研究为变负荷移动式核系统的安全运行和控制优化提供了理论基础。

English abstract

Mobile power supply is urgently needed in extreme environments. Open Brayton cycle gas-cooled microreactors are ideal solutions. The direct-cycle arrangement heats the turbine working air in the reactor without a separate secondary heat-transfer loop. Efficient analysis tools for these strongly coupled multiphysics systems are lacking. This study developed the Mobile Gas-cooled Microreactor Turbine System–Transient Analysis Program (MGTS-TAP). The program integrates neutron kinetics, thermal-hydraulics, and turbomachinery dynamics. The model was validated against HTRE-3 experimental data. The average relative error for key operating parameters is 1.98%, while the maximum relative error is 5.50% for the turbine inlet pressure. Conventional step control causes severe electrical power overshoot and undershoot during load changes. This results from mismatched response rates between neutron kinetics and thermal inertia. A staged reactivity control strategy is proposed. The strategy dynamically matches the reactivity insertion rate with temperature feedback. A smooth power transition was achieved within 26 s. Power deviations and temperature rebounds were reduced. Load-following control should prioritize electrical power stability. This research provides theoretical foundations for the safe operation and control optimization of variable-load mobile nuclear systems.
