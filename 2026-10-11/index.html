氦冷陶瓷球床（HCCP）试验包层模块（TBM）屏蔽体是安全重要部件（SIC-1），充当真空边界和第一道包容屏障；为改善可制造性，研究提出了内部结构简化的设计。本研究依据RCC-MRx 2018，对简化后的HCCP TBM屏蔽体进行了结构完整性评估，考虑15种单一载荷和17种载荷组合。评估覆盖所有运行状态，即正常运行状态（NOS）、烘烤状态及氚释出状态（TOS），并考虑电磁和地震载荷。事故载荷下确认有足够裕量，但由烘烤状态压力主导的载荷组合出现负裕量，说明烘烤状态是屏蔽体的设计控制工况。原因被分析为：240 ℃高温使许用应力强度降低，同时内部板间距增大使壁板弯曲应力增加。在恢复裕量的设计改进中，可制造性始终作为决策准则。针对加宽含管套弯头屏蔽块的改进几何，研究对侧壁厚度、前板厚度及管套布局进行了参数分析，确认40 mm侧壁与不小于60 mm前板的组合可使所有屏蔽块恢复结构完整性。此外，研究定量表明，管套发挥结构加强件的作用，其加强效果随管套布局对称性的提高而增强。这些结果给出了不重新引入内部板、同时兼顾结构完整性和可制造性的设计方案，并为最终设计阶段的几何优化提供了定量依据。

English abstract

The Helium Cooled Ceramic Pebble (HCCP) TBM shield is a safety-important component (SIC-1) serving as a vacuum boundary and the first confinement barrier, and a design with a simplified internal structure has been proposed to improve its manufacturability. In this study, a structural integrity assessment of the simplified HCCP TBM shield was performed in accordance with RCC-MRx 2018, considering 15 single loads and 17 load combinations. The assessment covers all operating states, namely the normal operation state (NOS), the baking state, and the tritium outgassing state (TOS), as well as the electromagnetic and seismic loads. While sufficient margins were confirmed for the accidental loads, negative margins were found in the load combinations governed by the pressure in the baking state, identifying the baking state as the design driver of the TBM shield. The cause was analyzed to be the combination of the reduction in the allowable stress intensity at the elevated temperature of 240 °C and the increased bending stresses of the walls caused by the enlarged spacing between the internal plates. In the design improvement for the margin recovery, manufacturability was consistently applied as a decision criterion. Parameter studies on the side wall thickness, the front plate thickness, and the pipe casing layout were performed for an improved geometry in which the width of the shield block containing the pipe casing bends was increased, and it was confirmed that the combination of a 40 mm side wall and a front plate of 60 mm or thicker recovers the structural integrity in all shield blocks. In addition, it was quantitatively shown that the pipe casings act as structural stiffeners and that their reinforcing effect increases with the symmetry of the casing layout. These results provide a design solution reconciling the structural integrity with the manufacturability without reintroducing the internal plates, and a quantitative basis for the geometry optimization in the final design phase.

English abstract

Fine debris can migrate through connected pores in ceramic breeder beds before bed-scale structural degradation becomes measurable. We combine particle-resolved DEM tracking with DEM-derived pore reconstruction in a stiff Li4SiO4 bed of 10,000 1mm pebbles and evaluate outlet breakthrough, local deposition, connected-pore loss and pressure response as distinct observables. Across the terminal drive, inventory and internal-release cases, the migration and deposition channels responded differently, while the largest resolved connectivity loss was 5.3×10−4. Localized release separated a retained bulk from a sparse leading population, showing why outlet arrival is not a hydraulic-failure measure by itself. Same-mesh OpenFOAM calculations provide an independent debris-free pressure reference and a prescribed resistance-loading response. The resulting staged workflow can select cases for finer pore reconstruction, coupled flow analysis or pressure measurement. Its interpretation is limited to the studied parameter space, fixed-bed one-way DEM assumptions and finite voxel resolution; predicting a clogging transition requires resolved gas–solid feedback and hydraulic validation.

中文摘要

严重事故期间，大量放射性气溶胶释放到安全壳中。非能动安全壳冷却系统启动后，这些气溶胶受扩散泳作用驱动，向发生凝结的换热表面运动，从而改变安全壳内放射性物质存量的分布。本研究通过实验考察单管和管束凝结条件下的气溶胶扩散泳。在系统改变蒸汽摩尔分数、凝结速率和系统压力的条件下开展一系列实验，并实时监测气溶胶空间浓度。结果表明，颗粒尺寸对扩散泳速度的影响可忽略；蒸汽凝结速率增加会增强沉积，蒸汽摩尔分数降低则抑制沉积。较高蒸汽密度会抑制扩散泳速度。研究还比较了单管与管束凝结条件，并提出与凝结速率和蒸汽摩尔分数相关的有效沉积面积系数α，用以修正经典模型，提高预测准确性。研究提出的有效沉积面积系数α同时与蒸汽凝结速率和蒸汽摩尔分数有关，用于修正经典扩散泳模型。该修正有效减小实验数据与模型预测的偏差，显著提高强热阱管束凝结条件下扩散泳衰减常数的预测准确性。研究为评估事故后安全壳放射性物质分布提供了重要实验数据和改进模型。

English abstract

During a severe accident, substantial amounts of radioactive aerosols are released into the containment. Upon activation of the Passive Containment Cooling System, these aerosols are driven by diffusiophoresis toward condensing heat exchange surfaces, thereby altering the distribution of radioactive inventory within the containment. This study experimentally investigates aerosol diffusiophoresis under single-tube and tube-bundle condensation conditions. A series of experiments on aerosol diffusiophoresis were conducted under systematically varied steam mole fractions, condensation rates, and system pressures, with real-time monitoring of the spatial aerosol concentration. The results indicate that particle size has a negligible effect on the diffusiophoresis velocity, an increased steam condensation rate enhances the deposition, while a reduction in steam mole fraction inhibits it. A higher steam density inhibits the diffusiophoresis velocity. Furthermore, the study investigates diffusiophoresis under both single-tube and tube-bundle condensation conditions. The effective deposition area coefficient (α), correlated with condensation rate and steam mole fraction, is proposed to modify the classical model, thereby improving its predictive accuracy. The study proposed the effective deposition area coefficient α, related to both the steam condensation rate and steam mole fraction, to modify the classical diffusiophoresis model. This correction effectively reduces the deviation between experimental data and model predictions, significantly improving the accuracy of diffusiophoresis decay constant forecasts under strong heat-sink tube-bundle condensation conditions. The findings provide essential experimental data and a refined model for assessing post-accident radioactivity distribution within the containment.

MOC/蒙卡—热工结构反馈：分开误差，再讨论POD加速

研究空白

待检验的缺口是几何映射、输运离散、蒙卡统计和降阶截断分别怎样改变局部功率与热工裕量，而非首次实现核热力耦合。今天的弹簧映射与RMC—ANSYS摘要均未充分给出这种误差预算。

为什么值得开展

若能在同等精度下保留最差温度、间隙和应力裕量，并对外推工况拒绝不可靠结果，可形成可复现的误差控制贡献。只报告快或负反应性反馈不足以证明安全与价值。

建议工作

  1. 先复现一个公开堆芯或燃料组件：分别固定几何、开启膨胀、开启密度反馈，核对质量守恒。

  3. POD快照覆盖整组独立温度/功率工况；按局部功率、温升、间隙裕量选择截断，避免只按全场能量。
  4. 按完整工况留出验证，统计离线求解、截面处理、映射、在线迭代和回退成本；结果超出设定误差预算时回退高保真模型。
