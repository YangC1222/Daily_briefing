钠冷快堆双层管道的分布式温度泄漏检测

Advanced leak detection technique using a distributed temperature sensing for a double-walled pipe for a sodium-cooled fast reactor

中文摘要

早期检测和定位钠泄漏对于确保钠冷快堆（SFR）管道系统的安全至关重要。双层管道可以减轻外部钠泄漏的影响；然而，传统的线式泄漏检测器提供的空间信息有限，并且可能受到泄漏流体与传感电极接触不完全的影响。本研究实验开发并验证了一种基于拉曼散射分布式温度传感（DTS）的双层管道定量泄漏检测和定位方法。我们搭建了一个水模拟试验装置WALSUM，用于模拟双层管道环形空间内的泄漏，并在0.1–7.0 mL/min的泄漏流量和35–90 °C的流体温度范围内评估了该方法的检测性能。为了提高泄漏识别对测量噪声和热瞬变的鲁棒性，我们将三点空间温度比值法与基于比值差标准偏差的统计检测准则相结合。基于无泄漏基线三个标准差的裕度，确定了0.03的临界阈值。实验结果表明，DTS系统能够检测到0.1 mL/min的最小测试泄漏速率，响应时间根据泄漏流量和流体温度的不同，范围为195至2579 s。随着泄漏流量和流体温度的增加，响应速度加快，泄漏引起的热信号也随之增强。在包含反复加热和冷却循环的33小时无泄漏瞬态测试中，未观察到误报。对比测试表明，在电极接触不良导致线型检测器失效的情况下，DTS系统仍能成功检测到泄漏事件。此外，基于DTS 0.5 m的空间分辨率，其分布式传感能力实现了泄漏定位，理论位置不确定度为±0.25 m。这些结果证明了拉曼-DTS技术在双壁SFR管道中定量分布式泄漏监测和定位方面的可行性，并为进一步开发应用于实际反应堆提供了基于实验的性能指标。

English abstract

Early detection and localization of sodium leakage are important for ensuring the safety of sodium-cooled fast reactor (SFR) piping systems. Double-walled piping can mitigate the consequences of external sodium leakage; however, conventional wire-type leak detectors provide limited spatial information and may be affected by incomplete contact between the leaked fluid and sensing electrodes. This study experimentally develops and validates a quantitative leakage detection and localization methodology based on Raman-scattering distributed temperature sensing (DTS) for double-walled piping. A water-mockup test facility, WALSUM, was constructed to simulate leakage in the annular space of a double-walled pipe, and the detection performance was evaluated over leak flow rates of 0.1–7.0 mL/min and fluid temperatures of 35–90 °C. To improve the robustness of leakage identification against measurement noise and thermal transients, a three-point spatial temperature-ratio method was combined with a statistical detection criterion based on the standard deviation of the ratio difference. A critical threshold of 0.03 was established from a three-standard-deviation margin of the non-leakage baseline. The experimental results demonstrated that the DTS system detected the minimum tested leak rate of 0.1 mL/min, with response times ranging from 195 to 2,579 s depending on the leak flow rate and fluid temperature. The response became faster and the leakage-induced thermal signal increased with increasing leak flow rate and fluid temperature. During a 33 h non-leakage transient test involving repeated heating and cooling cycles, no false alarms were observed. In comparative tests, the DTS system successfully detected a leakage event under conditions where the wire-type detector was affected by incomplete electrode contact. In addition, the distributed sensing capability enabled leak localization with a theoretical positional uncertainty of ±0.25 m based on the 0.5 m spatial resolution of the DTS. These results demonstrate the feasibility of Raman-DTS for quantitative, distributed leakage monitoring and localization in double-walled SFR piping and provide experimentally based performance metrics for further development toward practical reactor applications.

中文摘要

微型反应堆通常具有紧凑的堆芯结构，因此只能使用堆芯外探测器进行在线功率监测。有效的监测依赖于精确的堆芯功率分布重建。在此背景下，本文针对采用堆外探测器进行堆芯功率重建，提出了一种定制的算法框架。为验证该框架的有效性，本文以简化的TOPAZ II空间反应堆堆芯为例，利用堆外探测器的响应函数进行了可行性分析。基于分析结果，根据在线监测的特点和要求，提出了一种堆外探测器布置方案。后续在各种运行条件下的验证表明，重建结果的均方根误差低于2%，最大相对误差低于4.5%。这些结果表明，利用堆外探测器进行微型反应堆堆芯功率在线监测是可行的，可为未来反应堆堆芯监测系统的开发提供参考。

English abstract

Micro-reactors typically feature compact core structures, so only ex-core detectors can be used for online power monitoring. The effective monitoring relies on accurate core power distribution reconstruction. In this context, this paper develops a tailored algorithm framework for the core power reconstruction with ex-core detectors. To illustrate this, the paper conducts a feasibility analysis utilizing the simplified TOPAZ II space reactor core, employing the detector response function of ex-core detectors. Building on the results of this analysis, an ex-core detector arrangement scheme was proposed according to the characteristics and requirements of online monitoring. Subsequent verification under various operating conditions demonstrated that the reconstruction achieved root mean square errors below 2% and maximum relative errors under 4.5%. These results show the viability of using ex-core detectors for online core power monitoring of micro-reactors, thereby providing a reference for the development of future reactor core monitoring systems.

中文摘要

SIMP钢是含约9–12 wt% Cr的低活化铁素体/马氏体合金，被关注用于铅冷快堆堆芯构件，但缺少专用低活化焊丝限制工程应用。本文按低活化设计原则制备三种不同铬当量Creq焊丝，系统研究其对δ铁素体残留、显微组织与焊缝力学性能的影响。降低Creq可抑制δ铁素体；Creq降至8.276时，焊缝近乎全马氏体，多层多道焊亦促进δ铁素体消除。δ铁素体增多使硬度、强度和高温延伸率下降，但室温延伸率无明显变化。W3焊丝Creq=8.276，不仅满足低活化要求，而且冲击能68 J、室温抗拉强度790 MPa、550℃抗拉强度430 MPa。结果为低活化铁素体/马氏体钢专用焊材开发提供指导。

English abstract

SIMP steel is a low-activation ferritic/martensitic alloy containing approximately 9–12 wt% Cr, which has attracted increasing interest for core components in lead-cooled fast reactors. However, its engineering application is limited by the lack of specialized low-activation welding wires. In this study, three low-activation welding wires with different chromium equivalents (Creq) were designed and fabricated based on low-activation alloy design principles. The effects of Creq variation on δ-ferrite retention, microstructural characteristics, and mechanical behavior of the weld metals were systematically investigated. The results indicate that reducing Creq effectively suppresses δ-ferrite formation. When Creq was decreased to 8.276, the weld metal exhibited an almost fully martensitic microstructure. In addition, the multilayer multi-pass welding process promoted the elimination of δ-ferrite in the weld metal. Increasing δ-ferrite content led to reductions in hardness, strength, and high-temperature elongation, whereas no clear change in room-temperature elongation was observed. Among the welding wires studied, the W3 welding wire with Creq of 8.276 not only meets the low activation requirement but also has an impact energy of 68 J, a room-temperature tensile strength of 790 MPa, and a tensile strength of 430 MPa at 550 °C. These findings provide valuable guidance for developing dedicated welding consumables for low-activation ferritic/martensitic steels.

螺旋肋燃料棒比传统圆柱棒具有更大的表面积体积比，并可通过自支承接触取消定位格架。本文用OpenMC建立AP1000基础的17×17螺旋肋燃料组件，装入(²³⁸U+²³⁹Pu)O₂+AlSi、(²³²Th+²³³U)O₂+AlSi、(²³²Th+²³⁵U)O₂+AlSi、(²³⁵U+²³⁸U)O₂+AlSi及U-50Zr五种燃料。与传统圆柱组件比较，螺旋肋几何降低k∞并使能谱软化。采用LMDI-CF方法量化四因子贡献。其中(²³²Th+²³³U)O₂+AlSi的k∞最高，为1.83362，寿期780有效满功率日，反映钍—²³³U循环较好的中子经济性。U-50Zr能谱最硬，寿期最长为1920有效满功率日，比前者长146.15%；其排出燃耗143,914 MWd/tHM略低于前者147,153 MWd/tHM。结果为无格架螺旋肋组件燃料选择及中子学优化提供定量支持。

English abstract

Spiral-ribbed fuel rods provide a larger surface area-to-volume ratio than conventional cylindrical rods and can eliminate spacer grids through self-supporting contact. In this study, an AP1000-based 17×17 spiral-ribbed fuel assembly is modeled using OpenMC and loaded with five fuels: (238U+239Pu)O2+AlSi, (232Th+233U)O2+AlSi, (232Th+235U)O2+AlSi, (235U+238U)O2+AlSi, and U-50Zr. Comparison with a conventional cylindrical assembly shows that the spiral-ribbed geometry reduces k∞ and softens the neutron spectrum. The LMDI-CF method is applied to quantify four-factor contributions. Among the fuels, (232Th+233U)O2+AlSi achieves the highest k∞ of 1.83362 and a lifetime of 780 EFPD, reflecting the favorable neutron economy of the thorium-233U cycle. U-50Zr exhibits the hardest neutron spectrum and the longest lifetime of 1920 EFPD, 146.15% longer than that of (232Th+233U)O2+AlSi. Its discharge burnup is 143,914 MWd/tHM, slightly lower than the 147,153 MWd/tHM of (232Th+233U)O2+AlSi. These results provide quantitative support for fuel selection and neutronic optimization of spacer-grid-free spiral-ribbed assemblies.
