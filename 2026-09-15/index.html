02 热管堆热管反应堆斯特林发动机多物理场耦合KRUSTY

斯特林型热管反应堆系统的多物理场耦合热电性能分析

Multiphysics coupled thermal-to-electric performance analysis of a Stirling-type heat pipe reactor system

Annals of Nuclear Energy · 2026-09-06 · Aoguang Wu, Weixiang Wang, Kefan Zhang, Xiao Luo, Hongli Chen

核心观点

  • 把堆芯三维传热、点堆动力学、热管网络、斯特林发动机和辐射排热串成完整热—电链，并分析稳态、反应性扰动与单热管失效。
  • 模型显示扰动后系统依靠负温度反应性反馈趋于新平衡，同时反应堆瞬态会传递至发电端并改变输出功率。

创新点

  • 相较只处理堆芯或只处理转换系统的模型，在同一 COMSOL 系统框架中保留堆芯三维传热并显式计算反应堆扰动到斯特林输出的传播。

不足

  • 摘要未说明与 KRUSTY 全系统瞬态实验的定量误差、热管启动/极限模型和结构应力，因此自稳定结论仅适用于所建模型与所考察工况。
  • 点堆动力学和热阻网络可能弱化空间功率重分布及热管两相极限，需全文核对耦合步长、网格和材料参数。

未来方向

  • 将该系统级方法用于未来深空热管反应堆的性能与安全裕量设计。
  • 把热管退化、接触热阻和 U-Mo 温度依赖本构的不确定性传播到堆芯峰值应力与斯特林净功率，形成概率安全裕量。
  • 用 KRUSTY 试验时序或硬件在环数据校准热管—转换器动态，而非只比较最终稳态。
⚑ 全文应提取 KRUSTY 三维几何、材料热物性、热管热阻、斯特林参数、反应性系数、辐射边界、耦合时间步及单热管失效边界，供 ANSYS 热—结构模型校核。

中文摘要

随着空间探索对可靠、长寿命电源的需求不断增长，斯特林型热管反应堆因结构紧凑、固有安全性和较高热电转换效率而受到关注。然而，传统系统级分析常过度简化系统内多物理场耦合，尤其忽略堆芯三维传热和温度引起的反应性反馈，从而削弱瞬态性能预测的准确性。为弥补这一缺口，研究基于 COMSOL 建立了增强的斯特林型热管堆系统级多物理场框架，将高分辨率三维固体传热模块嵌入系统，并与点堆动力学、热管热阻网络、改进二阶斯特林发动机模型和辐射排热模块集成。该框架应用于代表性的 KRUSTY 构型，分析稳态运行、反应性引入瞬态和单根热管失效。结果表明，框架能够捕捉关键系统耦合响应。反应性扰动和热管失效会引起暂时功率波动，但在模型设定下，系统依靠负温度反应性系数转入新的稳态，呈现整体自稳定趋势。反应堆侧扰动沿传热链传播并改变斯特林发动机输出功率，说明堆芯热瞬态会直接影响下游能量转换性能。该方法为评估斯特林型热管堆的性能与安全裕量提供系统级数值工具，并可支持未来深空电源设计。

English abstract

Driven by the escalating demand for reliable, long-duration power sources in space exploration, Stirling-type heat pipe reactor (HPR) systems have garnered significant attention due to their compact configuration, inherent safety, and high thermal-to-electric conversion efficiency. However, conventional system-level analyses frequently oversimplify the multiphysics coupling behavior within these systems, largely neglecting the three-dimensional (3D) heat transfer characteristics of the reactor core or temperature-induced reactivity feedback, thereby compromising the accuracy of transient performance predictions. To bridge this gap, this study develops an enhanced system-level multiphysics coupled analysis framework for Stirling-type HPR systems based on the COMSOL Multiphysics platform. The proposed framework seamlessly embeds a high-resolution 3D solid heat transfer module into the system architecture, integrating it with a point kinetics model, a heat pipe thermal resistance network, an improved second-order Stirling engine model, and a radiative heat rejection module. To demonstrate and evaluate the integrated framework, it is applied to the representative KRUSTY reactor configuration under steady-state operation, reactivity insertion transients, and single heat pipe failure scenarios. The results demonstrate the framework’s capability to capture the key coupled system-level responses. Specifically, transient analyses reveal that while reactivity perturbations and heat pipe failures induce temporary power fluctuations, the system consistently transitions to a new steady-state equilibrium driven by its intrinsic negative temperature reactivity coefficient, thereby indicating a global self-stabilizing tendency under the modeled conditions. Meanwhile, these reactor-side perturbations propagate through the heat transport chain and lead to corresponding variations in the Stirling engine output power, demonstrating the direct influence of reactor thermal transients on downstream power-conversion performance. Ultimately, this methodology offers a comprehensive system-level numerical tool for evaluating the performance and safety margins of Stirling-type HPRs, providing crucial insights for the design of future deep-space power systems.

中文摘要

燃料棒包壳与定位格架弹簧之间的微动磨损威胁压水堆燃料组件结构安全。本文建立了耦合 Archard 磨损模型、任意拉格朗日—欧拉自适应网格和用户子程序的数值框架，并基于试验数据提出时间缩放加速算法，以高效模拟长期磨损。圆柱—圆柱试验用于验证累积磨损体积和最大磨损深度。磨损体积误差均低于 8%，但最大磨损深度偏差较明显，因为这一局部极值对表面粗糙度、磨屑和不规则接触几何十分敏感。验证后的模型用于实际格架弹簧—包壳接触，并以总磨损体积和最大磨损深度为指标分析三个运行因素。磨损体积随循环数、位移幅值和法向载荷单调增加；最大磨损深度也随位移幅值和法向载荷增加。研究还完成了代表性反应堆工况下 18 个月全寿期累积磨损体积模拟。该方法可同时评价磨损体积与深度，并为核燃料部件结构优化提供高效工具。

English abstract

Fretting wear between fuel rod cladding and spacer grid springs threatens the structural safety of Pressurized Water Reactor (PWR) fuel assemblies. This work develops an integrated numerical framework coupling the Archard wear model, Arbitrary Lagrangian-Eulerian (ALE) adaptive meshing and user subroutine. A time-scaling acceleration algorithm is proposed based on test data to enable efficient long-term wear simulation. The framework is validated by cylinder-on-cylinder tests, producing results of cumulative wear volume and maximum wear depth. Wear volume errors are all below 8%, whereas maximum wear depth shows obvious deviations, since this local extreme value is sensitive to surface roughness, debris and irregular contact geometry. Validated simulations are conducted on practical spring-cladding contact. Parametric studies analyze three operating factors against two damage indicators (total wear volume and maximum wear depth). Wear volume rises monotonically with cycles, amplitude and normal load, and maximum wear depth also increases with larger displacement amplitude and higher normal load. An 18-month full-lifecycle simulation is carried out to acquire the cumulative wear volume of cladding under representative reactor operating conditions. This method supports comprehensive wear evaluation combining volume and depth metrics, offering an efficient tool for the structural optimization of nuclear fuel components.

中文摘要

格架—燃料棒微动是压水堆燃料组件的主要失效模式。研究在标准硼锂水化学的模拟一回路冷却剂中，让锆合金包壳与锆质凸点接触，在 25–315 ℃ 范围开展微动磨损试验。结果显示，摩擦响应与磨损响应对温度的变化趋势明显不同：磨损体积和 Archard 磨损系数均具有显著温度依赖，并在 190 ℃ 达到峰值；能量等效摩擦系数在全部工况下基本不变。磨损演化由热诱导的摩擦氧化层控制，可分为三个阶段：低温下以机械剥层为主；中温下疏松氧化磨屑引起严重三体磨粒磨损；高温下形成连续富氧化物表层，减弱对基体的犁削破坏。该研究揭示了摩擦氧化产物沿多温度梯度对微动损伤的双重调控作用，为简化实验室条件下锆合金燃料包壳微动风险评价提供实验和理论依据。

English abstract

Grid-to-rod fretting (GTRF) is a primary failure mode of pressurized water reactor (PWR) fuel assemblies. In this study, fretting wear tests were conducted on zirconium alloy cladding against zirconium dimples under simulated primary coolant with standardized boron‑lithium (BLi) water chemistry, at temperatures ranging from 25 °C to 315 °C. The results reveal a clear divergence between friction and wear responses as a function of temperature. Specifically, the wear volume and Archard wear coefficient exhibit a pronounced temperature dependence, both peaking at 190 °C, whereas the energy-equivalent friction coefficient remains approximately constant across all tested conditions. The wear evolution is governed by three distinct stages controlled by thermally induced tribo-oxide layers: at low temperatures, mechanical delamination dominates; at intermediate temperatures, loose oxide debris promotes severe three-body abrasion; and at high temperatures, the formation of continuous oxide-rich surface layers mitigates substrate ploughing damage. This work elucidates the dual regulatory role of tribo-oxide products on fretting damage across a multi-temperature gradient, providing a fundamental experimental and theoretical reference for assessing the fretting risk of zirconium fuel cladding under simplified laboratory conditions.

中文摘要

球床高温气冷堆存在周向功率不均和复杂内部结构，需要三维热工水力分析来解析局部温度分布。本文开发了球床堆三维多孔介质热工水力程序，模型包括固体导热、氦气流动与换热，以及燃料球和 TRISO 颗粒的子网格温度计算。程序用 SANA 对称与非对称加热实验验证，多数温度偏差在 15% 以内；对 HTR-PM 模型与 THERMIX 进行程序间比较，温度和压力分布偏差在 10% 以内，入口—出口温升和堆芯压降等整体参数偏差在 3% 以内。程序随后用于 HN-750。总功率不变、周向功率峰值高出 20% 时，最大局部温度约升高 130 ℃。PARDISO 求解器使压力场求解时间减少 87.2%，总计算时间减少 14.4%。结果说明该程序可用于评估球床高温气冷堆堆芯三维温度不均匀性和局部热点。

English abstract

The pebble-bed high-temperature gas-cooled reactor (HTGR) requires three-dimensional thermal–hydraulic analysis because circumferential power non-uniformity and complex internal structures may affect local temperature distributions. In this study, a three-dimensional porous-medium thermal–hydraulic code was developed for pebble-bed HTGR analysis. The model includes solid heat conduction, helium flow and heat transfer, and a sub-grid temperature calculation for fuel pebbles and TRISO particles. The code was validated using SANA experimental data under symmetric and asymmetric heating conditions, with most temperature deviations within 15%. Code-to-code comparison with THERMIX for the HTR-PM model showed deviations within 10% for temperature and pressure distributions, and within 3% for integral parameters such as inlet-to-outlet temperature rise and core pressure drop. The code was then applied to the HN-750 reactor. Under a 20% circumferential power peaking condition with unchanged total power, the maximum local temperature increased by approximately 130 °C. The use of the PARDISO solver reduced the pressure-field solution time by 87.2% and the total computation time by 14.4%. These results indicate that the developed code can be used to evaluate three-dimensional temperature non-uniformity and local hot-spot behavior in pebble-bed HTGR cores.
