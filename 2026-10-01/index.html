中文摘要

热管冷却反应堆因结构紧凑及安全特性，被认为适合向偏远电网供电。在运行和事故工况下，热膨胀对产生足够的负反应性十分重要，因此必须纳入此类反应堆的中子学分析。本文在SARAX程序的堆芯计算模块中建立了中子学—热—热膨胀耦合计算框架，并以Megapower反应堆为算例。该框架包括三个主要求解器：中子学求解器在任意三棱柱几何上求解中子输运方程，得到中子通量和功率分布；包含热管模拟的热求解器在六边形几何上求解导热，提供六边形及三棱柱区域的温度分布；热膨胀求解器计算轴向和径向膨胀率，重新定位网格点并更新几何。每一层内的轴向膨胀假定均匀，径向膨胀则由网格点的非均匀移动表示，其位置采用弹簧法确定。三个求解器在统一网格体系中通过Picard迭代耦合。耦合结果给出的温度分布反应性反馈为−714 pcm，热膨胀反馈为−640 pcm，总负反应性为−1354 pcm。燃料和基体在堆芯内部的温度分布较均匀，对功率分布的影响较小。假定底层固定时，顶部最大轴向膨胀达到2 cm；燃料区角部最大径向位置偏移约为5.3 mm。这些结果表明该耦合方法能够捕捉多物理场反馈效应，为热管冷却反应堆中子学分析提供工具。

English abstract

Heat pipe cooled reactors are perceived as promising for providing power supply in remote grids owing to their compactness and safety properties. In this context, thermal expansion plays an important role in inducing sufficient negative reactivity during operation and accident conditions, and thus must be considered in neutronics analysis of reactors of this type. In this paper, a coupled neutronics–thermal–thermal expansion calculation framework is developed within the core calculation module of the SARAX code for heat pipe reactor analysis, with the Megapower reactor as a case study. The coupled framework consists of three main solvers, as follows. The neutronics solver solves the neutron transport equation on arbitrary triangular prism geometry, providing the neutron flux and power distribution. The thermal solver, which includes heat pipe simulation, solves heat conduction in hexagonal geometry and provides temperature distributions for both hexagonal and triangular prism regions. The thermal expansion solver computes axial and radial expansion ratios, repositions mesh points, and updates the geometry accordingly. The axial expansion is assumed to be uniform within each layer, whereas radial expansion is represented by the non-uniform repositioning of mesh points, with their positions determined using the spring method. The three solvers are coupled through a Picard iteration scheme on a unified mesh system. Based on the coupled solution, the reactivity feedback from the temperature distribution is calculated to be −714 pcm, while that from thermal expansion is −640 pcm, yielding a total negative reactivity of −1354 pcm. The temperature distributions of the fuel and matrix were relatively uniform inside the core region, with only a minor impact on the power distribution. The maximum axial expansion reached 2 cm at the top, assuming the bottom layer remains fixed. The maximum radial shift of position in the fuel region was around 5.3 mm at the corners. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of the coupled method in capturing multiphysics feedback effects and thus provide a reliable tool for the neutronics analysis of heat pipe cooled reactors.

English abstract

Flow-induced vibration (FIV) of fuel rods in axial coolant flow can drive grid-to-rod fretting and, in severe cases, lead to cladding failure, motivating high-fidelity fluid–structure interaction (FSI) analysis with improved computational efficiency. This study develops a partitioned framework that couples large-eddy simulation (LES) of the axial flow with a transfer-matrix and modal-superposition structural solver, where the fuel rod is modelled as an Euler–Bernoulli beam. The framework is verified on a clamped–clamped rod by comparison with an in-house finite element method (FEM) beam model, the Ansys APDL BEAM4 element, and the SOLID187 solid element. The first three natural frequencies and the root-mean-square (RMS) displacements at L/4, L/2, and 3 L/4 agree to within approximately 1% across all comparisons. The predicted displacement spectra accurately reproduce the first three structural modes and their orbital trajectories. Compared with the in-house FEM, the beam-based TMM structural solver reduces the structural computation time by about a factor of four at comparable accuracy for the present single-rod case, and its advantage is expected to grow for multi-span, grid-supported rod configurations, since the size of the transfer-matrix system is independent of the number of spans and lumped supports. Validation against the axial-flow-induced vibration experiment of Cioncolini et al. yields predictions of the first-mode frequency and RMS free-end displacement within 2.4% and 3% of the measurements, respectively.

中文摘要

裂变产物、辐解、氦气鼓泡和外部污染产生的不凝气体气泡会显著影响熔盐反应堆的热工水力性能。尽管现代系统分析程序包含气体输运功能，许多换热和压降闭合关系仍来自常规流体，对熔盐—气体体系的验证有限。本文采用CFD评估泡状熔盐流动的现有两相换热与摩擦压降关联式。在ANSYS Fluent中建立二维轴对称模型，采用代数滑移混合模型和SST湍流模型。模拟FLiNaK—氦、FLiBe—氦及KCl-MgCl₂—氦流动，液相速度为3–5 m/s，气相空隙率为0–10%。首先用空气—水两相关联式评估数值框架，再利用单相熔盐换热关联式进一步评价。在固定体相速度下，空隙率上升因混合物热物性和雷诺数变化而降低对流换热系数；增大速度则通过增强湍流输运提高换热。固定速度时，空隙率增大使混合物密度、黏度及雷诺数降低，摩擦压降随之减小。所评估关联式中，Aggour对氟盐换热的总体符合最好，Martin与Sims对氯盐最好；Müller-Steinhagen与Heck在所有算例中给出最准确的摩擦压降预测。这些结果支持选定常规关联式作为临时工程闭合关系，同时指出：未来系统级模型仍需要在代表性反应堆泡状流工况下开展熔盐—气体直接实验验证。

English abstract

Non-condensable gas (NCG) bubbles generated by fission products, radiolysis, helium sparging, and external contamination can significantly affect the thermal-hydraulic performance of molten salt reactors (MSRs). Although modern MSR system analysis codes incorporate gas-transport capabilities, many heat transfer and pressure loss closure relationships are derived from conventional fluids and have received limited validation for molten salt–gas systems. This study evaluates existing two-phase heat transfer and frictional pressure loss correlations for bubbly molten salt flows using computational fluid dynamics (CFD). A two-dimensional axisymmetric model was developed in ANSYS Fluent using the mixture model with an algebraic slip formulation and the SST turbulence model. Simulations were performed for FLiNaK–helium, FLiBe–helium, and KCl-MgCl₂–helium flows at liquid velocities of 3–5 m/s and gas void fractions of 0–10%. The numerical framework was assessed against air–water two-phase correlations and further evaluated using single-phase molten salt heat-transfer correlations. At constant bulk velocity, increasing void fraction reduced the convective heat transfer coefficient because of changes in mixture thermophysical properties and Reynolds number, while increasing velocity enhanced heat transfer through stronger turbulent transport. Frictional pressure loss decreased with void fraction at constant velocity because of reduced mixture density,viscosity, and Reynolds number. Among the evaluated correlations, Aggour provided the best overall agreement for heat transfer for fluoride-based salts, while Martin & Sims was best for the chloride salt. Müller-Steinhagen and Heck provided the most accurate frictional pressure loss predictions in all cases. These results support selected conventional correlations as practical interim closure relationships while highlighting the need for direct molten salt–gas experimental validation for future MSR system-level thermal-hydraulic modeling under representative reactor bubbly-flow conditions.

中文摘要

燃耗计算是预测反应堆整个运行周期中子学行为和燃料演化的关键，但核数据不确定性在计算中传播，可能使重要响应产生显著偏差。对热管冷却反应堆等长寿期、不可换料系统，这一问题尤其突出，因为累积不确定性无法通过燃料管理策略缓解。通常采用保守处理，但这会降低对预测的信心并引入过多安全裕量。本文建立随机不确定度量化框架，研究热管堆燃耗计算中核数据不确定性的传播，通过基于蒙特卡罗的随机燃耗模拟实施传播分析。为约束预测不确定性，引入基于Copula依赖模型的验证方法，利用可测同位素浓度。结果显示，累积效应使有效增殖因子keff的不确定性沿燃耗链逐渐增加。U-235等易裂变同位素的不确定性随燃耗增大，钚同位素则表现出不同的中子俘获生成机制。功率分布分析还显示，因中子泄漏，堆芯边界附近的不确定性较高。加入同位素浓度约束后，keff不确定性约降低30%，为减少不必要的设计裕量提供途径。

English abstract

Burnup calculations play a critical role in predicting the neutronic behavior and fuel evolution of nuclear reactors over their operating cycle. However, uncertainties in nuclear data propagated through these calculations can lead to significant deviations in key reactor responses. This challenge is particularly acute for long-lifetime non-refuelable systems, such as heat pipe-cooled reactors (HPRs), where accumulated uncertainties cannot be mitigated via fuel management strategies. Such uncertainties are typically addressed with conservative treatments, which reduce confidence in model predictions and introduce excessive safety margins. In this study, a stochastic uncertainty quantification framework is developed to investigate the propagation of nuclear data uncertainties in HPR burnup calculations. Uncertainty propagation is executed through Monte Carlo-based stochastic depletion simulations. To constrain predictive uncertainty, a model validation approach based on a Copula dependence model is introduced, leveraging measurable isotopic concentrations. Results indicate that the uncertainty in keff increases progressively along the burnup chain due to cumulative effects. Specifically, fissile isotopes such as U-235 exhibit rising uncertainties with burnup, while plutonium isotopes show distinct neutron capture production mechanisms. Furthermore, power distribution analysis reveals elevated uncertainty levels near the core boundary, attributed to neutron leakage. Incorporation of isotopic concentration constraints reduces the uncertainty in keff by approximately 30%, offering a robust pathway to reduce unnecessary design margins.

Furthermore, a comparison study of various failure models illustrated how different failure models vary in their cladding failure predictions. The outcomes from this work will improve the understanding of how different mechanical and failure models can be used to predict PCMI-induced failure. Future studies aim to reduce the uncertainties associated with thermal-hydraulic (TH) models during the post-PCMI phase in order to foster better interpretation of experimental data.

English abstract

Reactivity-initiated accidents impose rapid thermo-mechanical loading on fuel–cladding systems and can compromise fuel-rod integrity through fuel heating, gap closure, pellet–cladding mechanical interaction (PCMI), and cladding stress buildup. This study presents a reduced-order framework for comparative screening of conventional and accident-tolerant fuel–cladding systems under an RIA-like power pulse. The model solves one-dimensional transient radial heat conduction in the solid fuel pellet using a lumped effective surface boundary condition, evaluates incremental thermal expansion and pulse-scaled swelling, tracks contact onset, estimates PCMI contact pressure, and calculates a normalized total hoop-stress indicator. An Equivalent RIA Pulse Threshold (ERPT) is defined from prescribed fuel-temperature, contact-pressure, and stress-utilization criteria, while gap closure is treated separately as a contact-onset indicator. The framework is applied to UO2, UN, U3Si2, U-Th MOX, U-Mo, and UC fuels with Zircaloy, SS316, FeCrAl, and SiC claddings. Under the baseline assumptions, finite ERPT values are 13.5 for U-Mo, 16.0 for UO2, 17.5 for U-Th MOX, and 20.0 for U3Si2, whereas UN and UC remain above the investigated range. All finite baseline ERPT values are temperature-controlled. Sensitivity analysis shows that fuel thermal conductivity and the prescribed temperature limit most strongly affect ERPT, while swelling, gap size, differential rod pressure, and cladding expansion mainly influence contact onset and PCMI severity. A tenfold increase in effective contact stiffness shifts UO2/Zircaloy and U3Si2/Zircaloy to pressure-controlled behavior. ERPT is intended as a transparent reduced-order screening metric, not an experimentally validated failure threshold.

中文摘要

热管堆作为一种集高效性、固有安全性及模块化优势于一体的先进堆型,已成为国内外核能技术创新的前沿方向。然而,其固态堆芯的复杂物理特性对高保真的中子-热工-力学多物理耦合模拟的精度提出了极高要求。传统数值计算方法耗时较长、计算效率较低,无法满足工程上对快速设计优化及运行状态实时预测的需求。基于人工智能的神经网络方法凭借强大的非线性拟合能力与高效的计算速度,为堆芯参数的快速预测提供了新的途径。面向MegaPower热管堆关键参数的快速预测需求,基于高精度快堆程序SARAX构建了多工况高保真训练数据库,引入本征正交分解(proper orthogonal decomposition,POD)方法对堆芯精细功率分布进行降阶处理,并构建了BP,CNN,RBF等在内的多种神经网络代理模型,实现了反应性与功率分布的快速预测。结果表明,BP神经网络具有较好的预测精度,堆芯精细功率分布的最大相对偏差低于0.5%,有效增殖因子(keff)偏差控制在10 pcm以内,单次预测耗时约为15 ms。该模型在保证精度的同时显著提升了计算效率,展现出良好的泛化能力和工程应用前景。

English abstract
