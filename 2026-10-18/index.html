中文摘要

较高阈值中子反应截面的准确测定关系到核模型和实际应用，也与IAEA CIELO改进U-235及U-238瞬发裂变谱的工作有关。本文在德黑兰研究堆中子谱下研究天然锆同位素，重点为阈值高于10 MeV的Zr-90(n,2n)Zr-89反应。锆箔及监测箔置于堆芯辐照箱，冷却后以高纯锗探测器测诱发活度。用计入燃耗的MCNPX堆芯模型估计箔片处中子通量，并以计算和实验反应率对照验证。测得Zr-90(n,2n)Zr-89谱平均截面1.73×10⁻⁵ barn，不确定度11%；Zr-94(n,γ)Zr-95和Zr-96(n,γ)Zr-97谱平均截面分别为4.18×10⁻²及8.04×10⁻² barn。

English abstract

Accurate determination of threshold neutron-induced reaction cross sections at relatively high threshold energies is of fundamental importance for nuclear model development and practical applications in physics. In addition, the evaluation and re-evaluation of such cross sections are relevant to ongoing efforts to improve the prompt fission neutron spectra of U-235 and U-238 within the IAEA CIELO project. In this work, the reaction cross sections of natural zirconium isotopes were investigated, with particular emphasis on the threshold reaction Zr-90(n,2n) Zr-89, which has a threshold energy above 10 MeV, in the neutron spectrum of the Tehran Research Reactor. Zirconium and monitor foils were irradiated in an irradiation box located in the reactor core. After an appropriate cooling period, the induced activities of the irradiated samples were measured using a high-purity germanium detector. To accurately estimate the neutron flux on the foils, the reactor core was modeled using the MCNPX code while accounting for burnup effects. The calculated foil reaction rates were then compared with the experimental values to validate the computed neutron flux. The spectrum-averaged cross section of the Zr-90(n,2n) Zr-89 reaction was measured to be 1.73 × 10−5 barn with an uncertainty of 11%. The corresponding spectrum-averaged cross sections for the Zr-94(n,γ) Zr-95 and Zr-96(n,γ)Zr-97 reactions were 4.18 × 10−2 and 8.04 × 10−2 barn, respectively.

中文摘要

紧凑钠冷快堆二回路存在不同温度管流的混合。本文数值研究T形接头中两入口以4 s周期同相及异相脉动时的流动和热混合：主管与支管速度分别采用正弦—正弦和正弦—余弦变化。第一种情况动量比恒为3.57，第二种在1.73–7.38变化。两种情况下，T接头下游速度分布逐渐恢复为混合前形状，但异相工况两个半周期的分布不同。主管上部湍流强度变化大于下部；入口相差90°使沿轴向的温度及湍流强度波动增加。上半管热混合以动量相互作用为主，下半管以剪切层渐进对流为主。异相工况在75%的截面上表现出较低温度混合度指标，出口温差降低20%，说明混合更充分。90°相位差还使热脉动位置短时远离壁面，对接头损伤范围具有影响。

English abstract

Mixing of two pipe streams at different temperatures features in the secondary circuit of compact sodium-cooled fast reactors. This study presents the numerical simulation of flow features and mixing characteristics in the T-junction with the inlets subjected to co-pulsating and out-of-phase pulsating flows over a 4-s flow cycle. The main and branch pipe inlets are subjected to sine-sine and sine-cosine velocity variations. The momentum ratio is constant (3.57) in the first case, but varies between 1.73 and 7.38 in the second. The velocity magnitude profile in both cases gradually recovers to its pre-mixing shape after the T-junction; however, the profiles differ between the two half-cycles in Case 2. Turbulence intensity varied more in the upper part than in the lower part of the main pipe. The 90o out-of-phase pulsating flow at the inlet resulted in increased temperature and turbulence intensity fluctuations along the flow axis. Momentum interactions dominate thermal mixing in the upper part of the pipe, while the lower half is dominated by gradual convection in the shear layers. The lower temperature mixing degree in 75% of the flow cross-section and 20% reduction in the temperature difference at the outlet show that more thermal mixing was achieved in Case 2. The 90° out-of-phase difference between the mixing inlets also causes the position of the thermal fluctuation to be momentarily away from the wall, which has implications for the extent of damage expected at the junction.

中文摘要

流动燃料使核热紧密耦合，第二故障可改变事故后果。本文用非线性瞬态程序耦合集总热工与保留先驱核流失和延迟返回的点动力学，并分别解析结构壁与盐。以MSRE数据验证后，用于200 MWth U-233钍熔盐堆，考察10–500 pcm反应性引入后失流LOF或失热阱LOHS。温度反馈均能停堆，但随后衰变热仍可使结构越过文中800℃ Hastelloy N限值。100 pcm时LOF在2299 s达限，停滞使燃料—壁温差59 K；LOHS在5557 s达限，循环使全回路库存作为热缓冲。两者先后次序在200–300 pcm间反转，更高扰动下两种配置均不足以保护结构。仅按盐温评价可把响应时间高估至五倍，固有核反馈不保证结构完整性。

English abstract

In molten salt reactors the mobile fuel couples neutronics and thermal-hydraulics so closely that a second failure can change the outcome of an accident qualitatively. A nonlinear transient code couples lumped-parameter thermal-hydraulics with point kinetics retaining both the loss and the delayed return of drifting precursors, and resolves the structural wall separately from the salt. Validated against Molten Salt Reactor Experiment data, it was applied to a 200 MWth U-233 thorium-based molten salt reactor (TMSR) for reactivity insertions of 10–500 pcm followed by loss of flow (LOF) or loss of heat sink (LOHS). Temperature feedback always shuts the reactor down, but decay heat then carries the structure past the 800 °C Hastelloy N limit. For a 100 pcm insertion the limit is reached at 2299 s under LOF, where stagnation opens a 59 K fuel–wall temperature difference, and at 5557 s under LOHS, where circulation retains the loop inventory as thermal ballast. This ordering reverses between 200 and 300 pcm, above which neither configuration protects the structural boundary. Inherent feedback therefore bounds the fuel temperature but does not ensure structural integrity, and margins assessed against the salt temperature overstate the available response time by up to a factor of five.

空间堆在发射失败后,因高速撞击可能重返临界状态进而引发严重的安全事故,是当前国际核安全与航天安全领域亟需重点解决的问题。本文基于非结构网格(UM)的蒙特卡罗粒子输运与有限元空气动力学仿真理论,研究在反应堆遭受冲击后造成堆芯大变形几何条件下的中子学参数变化。以美国“普罗米修斯”计划中的空间堆作为研究对象,该堆包含402根燃料棒、以He-Xe气体作为冷却剂。分析其在垂直撞击地面过程中反应性随时间变化的规律。结果表明:对于撞击前的初始堆芯,采用基于内部UM的反应堆蒙特卡罗程序RMC即RMC-UM与MCNP-UM计算的有效增殖系数(keff)均与传统构造实体几何(CSG)符合较好,RMC-UM读取网格时间及整体计算时间比MCNP-UM分别减少87%和93%;针对变形后的堆芯,分别采用RMC-UM与基于直接加速几何(DAG)蒙特卡罗方法的RMC-DAG开展中子输运计算,始末时刻的keff增量为876.7pcm(1pcm=10-5),keff均呈现先增加后逐步趋于稳定的趋势,两种方法结果符合良好。本研究为空间堆发射的临界安全分析提出了可行的方法,验证了RMC的内部UM功能应用于空间堆坠落事故等冲击动力学场景的准确性。
