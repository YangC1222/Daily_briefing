中文摘要

铅铋泵是第四代铅冷快堆的关键部件，其水力性能直接影响反应堆安全与能效。但泄漏涡、分离涡、尾流演化及转静干涉等复杂非定常现象会引起严重水力损失，使泵性能下降。本文结合模型实验与非定常数值模拟，研究混流式铅铋泵全工况下的流动不稳定和不可逆能量耗散机制。采用熵产理论定量识别损失来源，建立涡演化与能量耗散的关联。结果表明：（1）强熵产主要集中在叶片前后缘和叶顶间隙，这些区域强剪切、流动分离和湍流脉动使局部熵产率和能量损失较高。（2）转静干涉区压力脉动以62.5 Hz叶频为主，偏离设计工况时涡相互作用增强，高次谐波更显著。（3）叶轮和导叶贡献约90%的总水力损失，进出口区域约占10%；湍流熵产占主导、约60%，其次为壁面熵产、约30%，说明湍流脉动和壁面剪切是主要耗散来源。（4）总熵产在设计流量附近最小，对应最佳水力条件。低流量时，叶顶泄漏和轮毂涡增强，显著增加湍流耗散并加速效率降低。这些发现解释了非定常涡演化与能量损失的耦合，为铅冷快堆冷却剂泵水力优化及效率提高提供理论指导。

English abstract

Lead-bismuth pump (LBP) is a critical component of Generation IV lead-cooled fast reactors, and its hydraulic performance directly determine the reactor safety and energy efficiency. However, complex unsteady phenomena such as leakage vortexes, separation vortexes, wake evolution and rotor-stator interaction (RSI) will induce severe hydraulic loss and deteriorate pump performance. This study combines model experiment and unsteady numerical simulations to investigate the flow instability and irreversible energy dissipation mechanisms of a mixed-flow LBP under full operating conditions. The entropy production theory is employed to quantitatively identify loss sources and establish the intrinsic correlation between vortex evolution and energy dissipation. The results indicate that: (1) Intense entropy production is mainly concentrated near the blade leading/trailing edges, tip clearance regions, where strong shear, flow separation, and turbulent fluctuations occur. Thereby leading to high local entropy production rate (LEPR) and severe energy loss. (2) The pressure pulsation in the RSI zone is dominated by blade passing frequency (62.5 Hz), while high-order harmonics become more significant under off-design conditions due to intensified vortex interaction. (3) Quantitative entropy production analysis reveals that the impeller and guide vane contribute approximately 90% of the total hydraulic loss, while the inlet and outlet domains only account for about 10%. Additionally, turbulent entropy production is dominant (about 60%), followed by wall entropy production (about 30%), confirming that turbulent fluctuation and wall shear effect are the main causes of energy dissipation. (4) The minimum total entropy production occurs near the design flow rate, corresponding to the optimal hydraulic condition. Under low-flow conditions, intensified tip leakage and hub vortexes significantly enhance turbulent dissipation and accelerate efficiency degradation. These findings provide physical insights into the coupling mechanism between unsteady vortex evolution and energy loss, offering some theoretical guidance for hydraulic optimization and efficiency improvement of lead-cooled fast reactor coolant pumps.

液态铅铋共晶LBE作为铅冷快堆冷却剂，其速度分布是热工水力研究的关键参数。然而LBE高温、腐蚀性和不透明性给传统流速测量带来困难。为解决这一问题，本文采用非接触感应流动层析CIFT，设计适用于LBE的流速测量系统。通过比较参考速度场与重建算法得到的速度场，验证测量方案。在传感器总数相近条件下，比较12×4、10×5和8×6三种空间布置。结果显示，8×6布置的重建速度场与参考速度场符合程度最高。此外，在噪声和复杂流场条件下验证该系统的适用性。所提出设计及传感器配置分析，为未来LBE流速测量应用提供参考。

English abstract

liquid lead-bismuth eutectic (LBE) serves as the coolant for lead-cooled fast reactors (LFR), its velocity distribution is a critical parameter in thermal-hydraulic research. However, the high-temperature corrosivity and opacity of LBE pose significant challenges to the application of traditional flow velocity measurement methods. To address this issue, this study applies Contactless Inductive Flow Tomography (CIFT) and designs a measurement system applicable for LBE flow velocity measurement. The validity of the proposed measurement scheme is verified by comparing the reference velocity field with the velocity field reconstructed via the reconstruction algorithm. Maintaining a comparable total number of sensors, we compare three spatial arrangements: 12×4, 10×5, and 8×6. The results indicate that the 8×6 arrangement achieves the highest degree of agreement between the reconstructed and reference velocity field. Furthermore, the applicability of this measurement system is verified under noisy condition and within complex flow field. The proposed design scheme and sensor configuration analysis provide a valuable reference for the future application of CIFT in LBE flow measurement.

可靠排出乏燃料水池余热是核设施的重要安全要求，特别是研究堆，需要在正常及事故条件下安全储存乏燃料组件。本文给出为研究堆乏燃料水池中燃料组件设计的非能动余热排出系统试验装置的实验结果。采用理论与经验方法，描述装置主要热工水力参数、设计特征及实验方法，给出多种运行模式的测试结果，包括回路工质全充装、部分充装及环形热虹吸模式。实验结果确认所提出设计能够运行，并在不同热条件下有效排热。此外，进行了装置三维建模，识别不能直接目视检查的部件设计特征，并优化蒸发器模块配置，使工质流量分布更均匀。还理论评估了将装置作为非能动排热系统安装到乏燃料水池后的核安全影响。所得结果表明，所提系统的安装不导致违反核安全要求，可为研究堆水池全尺寸非能动冷却系统开发提供基础。

English abstract

Ensuring reliable removal of residual heat from spent fuel pools is a key safety requirement for nuclear facilities, particularly for research reactors where the safe storage of spent fuel assemblies must be maintained under both normal and accident conditions. This paper presents the results of experimental studies of a test facility for a passive residual heat removal system designed for spent fuel assemblies in the spent fuel pool of a research nuclear reactor. The study employed both theoretical and empirical methods. The article describes the main thermal-hydraulic parameters and design features of the test facility and provides a description of the experimental methodology. The results of tests carried out under various operating modes of the facility are presented, including operation with full and partial filling of the circuit with the working fluid and operation in an annular thermosyphon mode. The experimental results confirm the operability of the proposed design and demonstrate the capability of the system to provide effective heat removal under different thermal conditions. In addition, three-dimensional modeling of the test facility was performed, which made it possible to identify design characteristics of components not accessible to visual inspection and to optimize the configuration of the evaporator module to ensure a more uniform distribution of the working fluid flow. A theoretical assessment of the impact of using this facility as part of a passive heat removal system on nuclear safety when installed in a spent fuel pool is also provided. The obtained results indicate that the installation of the proposed system does not lead to a violation of nuclear safety requirements and can serve as a basis for the development of a full-scale passive cooling system for spent fuel pools of research reactors.

中文摘要

增材制造进步使三周期极小曲面TPMS晶格等优化形状更适合用于换热器及核燃料元件。本文引入TPMS晶格形堆芯，与传统棒型设计比较。选择一个公开的快谱、超临界CO₂冷却、直接布雷顿循环棒型反应堆设计作为基准，并在TPMS堆芯中使用相同材料以公平比较。由于几何复杂，采用新的建模策略：结合高保真CFD、基于MOOSE的一维热工水力及三维传热，分析TPMS燃料热性能；在Serpent中采用非结构网格蒙特卡罗物理分析，并与OpenMC原生构造实体几何TPMS实现对照验证。对于快谱堆，TPMS与棒型几何在中子学上等效，但TPMS燃料改善换热，在满足最高材料温度限制时，功率密度最多提高59%。缺点是单位长度压降增加；将泵功率成本纳入微堆总发电量后，这一代价可能可以接受。

English abstract

Advancements in additive manufacturing (AM) processes have made it increasingly feasible to consider optimized shapes such as triply periodic minimal surface (TPMS) lattices for heat transfer applications, ranging from heat exchanger designs to nuclear fuel elements. This study introduces a TPMS-lattice-shaped nuclear core and compares its performance against that of a traditional pin-based design. A publicly available design that relies on typical fuel pin elements for a fast spectrum, supercritical CO2 (sCO2)-cooled reactor with a direct Brayton cycle was selected as the reference design. The same core materials were used in the TPMS core to ensure a fair comparison between both geometries. Due to the complexity of this type of geometry, innovative modeling strategies were introduced. The thermal performance of the TPMS fuel element was analyzed using a computational scheme that combined high-fidelity computational fluid dynamics (CFD) with Multiphysics Object-Oriented Simulation Environment (MOOSE)-based 1D thermal hydraulics, coupled with 3D heat transfer. Monte Carlo reactor physics analyses using unstructured meshes in Serpent were employed to characterize the core performance and verified against a native constructive solid geometry TPMS lattice implementation in OpenMC. When it comes to fast spectrum reactors, TPMS geometries are neutronically equivalent to pin geometries. However, TPMS-based fuel elements offer improved heat transfer performance, enabling the power density to be increased up to 59% while still meeting the peak material temperature requirements. A downside of TPMS lattices is the additional pressure drop per unit length, which may be manageable when factoring the pumping power costs into the total energy generated by the proposed microreactor concept.

中文摘要

本文对压水堆过冷沸腾条件下Cr涂层事故容错燃料棒开展三维共轭换热分析，评估热工水力性能及热裕量。在多区域OpenFOAM框架中耦合欧拉—欧拉双流体模型与RPI壁面热流分配，解析15.5 MPa下燃料导热、包壳涂层传热及液汽相互作用。名义工况入口速度5 m/s、过冷度40 K时，在1.604 m出现核态沸腾起始，最大空隙率0.0245，壁面过热度2.76 K，燃料峰温824.6 K，表现为稳定液相主导沸腾。Cr涂层热惩罚很小，峰温变化小于3 K。参数分析显示冷却剂质量通量主导沸腾稳定及热裕量：入口速度至少7 m/s时沸腾完全抑制；0.8 m/s低流量使空隙率达到0.69、燃料温度超过900 K。入口过冷度由5升至51 K使沸腾延后直至抑制，峰温仅变化约7 K，表明燃料导热热阻占主导。功率由70升至140 kW使沸腾提前、空隙率升至0.253，燃料峰温近线性升至约980 K。结果说明，Cr涂层保留稳态热工行为，而质量通量及功率水平主要控制沸腾稳定性和热裕量。

English abstract

A three-dimensional conjugate heat transfer (CHT) analysis of a chromium (Cr)-coated accident tolerant fuel (ATF) pin under pressurized water reactor (PWR) sub-cooled boiling (SCB) conditions is presented to assess thermal-hydraulic (TH) performance and thermal margins. A coupled Eulerian–Eulerian two-fluid model with the Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (RPI) wall heat flux partitioning approach is implemented in a multi-region OpenFOAM® framework to resolve fuel conduction, cladding/coating heat transfer, and liquid–vapor interactions at 15.5 MPa. Under nominal operating conditions (Uin=5ms−1, ΔTsub=40K), onset of nucleate boiling (ONB) occurs at 1.604 m, with a maximum void fraction (VF) of 0.0245, wall superheat of 2.76 K, and peak fuel temperature (PFT) of 824.6 K, indicating stable liquid-dominated boiling. The Cr coating introduces a negligible thermal penalty, with less than 3 K change in PFT. Parametric analysis shows that coolant mass flux strongly governs boiling stability and thermal margins. Boiling is fully suppressed for Uin≥7ms−1, whereas low mass flux (0.8ms−1) produces VFs up to 0.69 and fuel temperatures above 900 K. Increasing inlet sub-cooling (SC) from 5 to 51 K delays and ultimately suppresses boiling, while PFT varies by only approximately 7 K, reflecting dominant fuel conduction resistance. Power escalation from 70 to 140 kW advances boiling inception and increases VF to 0.253, with PFT rising nearly linearly to approximately 980 K. Overall, the results demonstrate that Cr-coated ATF preserves steady-state TH behavior, while coolant mass flux and power level primarily govern boiling stability and thermal margins under PWR operation.

中文摘要

在沸水堆严重事故进程中,压力容器失效至使堆芯熔融物释放到下方地坑形成的深水池中,熔融物与冷却剂将不可避免发生燃料-冷却剂相互作用并形成碎片床,该过程危及安全壳的完整性。为了在经济安全的情况下还原原型中碎片床形成现象,本研究选用锡(Sn)、锡铋合金(Sn-Bi)、锌(Zn)、锡铁合金(FeSn)等金属材料作为实际堆芯熔融物(Zr/Fe混合物)的模拟物,通过高速摄像技术完整记录了熔融物射流破碎、液滴碎裂及碎片沉积聚合过程,并精确测定了碎片床的几何形貌特征、孔隙率分布以及碎片颗粒的形态学参数和粒径分布特征。实验结果表明,不同材料对碎片床的形成影响显著,尤其是高熔点金属(FeSn合金)表现出与低熔点金属截然不同的行为特性。Fe-Sn合金形成了大量结块且孔隙率极低的致密碎片床,与Sn、Zn、Sn-Bi形成的高孔隙率(>90%)蜂窝状结构形成鲜明对比,主要归结于沸腾过程不一致。本研究为完善多组分熔融物体系下碎片床特性数据库提供了重要的实验依据,对深入理解熔融物材料组成对碎片床形成机制的影响具有重要科学价值。

English abstract

During severe accident progression in boiling water reactors(BWRs),reactor pressure vessel failure leads to the release of corium into the deep water pool of the containment pedestal,inevitably triggering fuel-coolant interactions(FCI)that form debris beds—a process threatening containment integrity.The characteristics of the resulting debris bed,including its porosity,morphology,and particle size distribution,critically determine the coolability of the molten material and the potential for steam explosions,thereby directly influencing the effectiveness of severe accident mitigation strategies.To reproduce prototypic debris bed formation phenomena under economically and safely feasible conditions,metallic simulants including tin(Sn),tin-bismuth alloy(Sn-Bi),zinc(Zn),and tin-iron alloy(Fe-Sn)were selected as representatives of actual corium compositions(Zr/Fe mixtures).The fragmentation processes of melt jets,droplet breakup,and debris sedimentation/agglomeration were comprehensively recorded using high-speed videography.The simulant materials were selected based on a simplified scaling analysis considering both hydrodynamic similarity(Froude number)and thermal similarity(modified Stefan number and Abe’s heat transfer regime maps),ensuring that the experimental observations are relevant to prototypical reactor conditions.Precise measurements were obtained for debris bed characteristics including geometric configuration,porosity distribution,as well as morphological parameters and particle size distribution of fragments.The experimental results reveal significant material-dependent behaviors:1)Steam explosion is observed exclusively in the Sn experiments,attributed to its high thermal conductivity combined with high superheat,which maintains the melt in a fully liquid state with excellent fluidity,enabling violent film boiling collapse and instantaneous heat transfer;2)The Fe-Sn alloy experiments produce uniquely dense debris beds with extensive agglomeration and low porosity,in stark contrast to the high-porosity(>90%)honeycomb structures formed by low-melting-point simulants(Sn,Zn,Sn-Bi);3)Despite having comparable superheat levels,Fe-Sn exhibitsno steam explosion,instead undergoing mild film boiling that generated“externally solidified,internally liquid”particles that subsequently bonded upon settling.Analysis of the Fe-Sn results indicates that its intermediate superheat and stable film boiling regime lead to progressive surface solidification while maintaining a liquid core,with particle collision at the pool bottom rupturing the solid shells and allowing internal melt to weld adjacent particles together,forming dense agglomerates.This mechanism provides critical insights for prototypic reactor conditions,suggesting that the Zr/Fe components in actual corium may form similar dense,low-porosity layers that impede coolant penetration and threaten long-term coolability.The work provides crucial experimental data for enhancing the multicomponent corium debris bed database,offering significant scientific value for understanding the mechanisms by which melt material composition influences debris bed formation,fragmentation behavior,and steam explosion potential.These findings contribute to the development of more accurate severe accident models and inform the optimization of mitigation strategies for both BWRs and pressurized water reactors(PWRs)during the ex-vessel phase of severe accidents.
