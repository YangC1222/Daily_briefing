核测量 · 不确定度管理核测量辐射防护

提高盖革–米勒剂量测量准确性：采用6 M石川法分析误差来源与改进流程

Enhancing Geiger-Müller Dosimetry Accuracy: Error Source Analysis and Process Improvement Using the 6 M Ishikawa Method

Nuclear Technology · 2026-09-25 · Ghada Ahmed Khouqeer

核心观点

  • 用6 M石川图梳理GM剂量测量误差来源，再以文献数据构建GUM不确定度预算和缓解优先级。
  • 摘要报告95%水平、k=2的扩展不确定度约54%；死时间与能量/角度依赖为重点因素。

创新点

  • 将定性根因图与定量不确定度预算连接，以诊断结果组织改进资源；不是新的GM探测器或已验证的现场校准系统。

不足

  • 54%是作者所用文献数据与预算假设下的扩展不确定度，不能视为所有GM仪器的普遍测量误差。
  • 12.5%与23%不可直接相加得到54%；需核对标准/扩展不确定度、覆盖因子、相关性与预算合成公式。
  • 摘要未给出具体机型、辐射能谱、实测校准数据和修正后的误差；需要全文确认定量证据。

未来方向

  • 强调全面管理不确定度，避免大不确定度掩盖流行病学和放射生物学剂量–响应关系；摘要未列出具体后续试验计划。
  • 用具有已知参考剂量的能谱和剂量率工况校准死时间、能量及角度响应，并分离随机计数误差与系统偏差。
  • 与HTGR核素热点预测对照前，应先建立探测器响应、屏蔽几何和空间取样模型。
⚑ 核对原始不确定度预算表、GM机型、能谱与死时间修正公式；摘要无法支持直接输入仪器校准参数。

专有名词解释

死时间dead time

记录一次事件后暂时无法记录后续事件的时间。

本文语境 高计数率时漏计需按探测器适用模型修正。

扩展不确定度expanded uncertainty

合成标准不确定度乘覆盖因子后的区间尺度。

本文语境 本文k=2；与平均误差或仪器通用精度不同。

石川图Ishikawa diagram

按类别展示问题的潜在原因及联系。

本文语境 本文6 M类别用于根因整理，不能单靠图定量确认因果贡献。

展开中英文摘要

中文摘要

使用盖革–米勒（GM）剂量计进行准确的剂量测量，对辐射防护和研究至关重要。然而，这些测量受到众多不确定度来源的影响，可能降低剂量评估的可靠性。本研究应用石川图，系统识别和归类 GM 剂量测量误差的根本原因，以满足全面认识不确定度因素的需要。主要目标是以可视化方式梳理所有潜在误差来源，并建立定量不确定度预算，显示各来源的相对贡献。 研究方法是构建因果图，将误差来源归入六大类：人员（操作技能、培训、定位）；设备（死时间、能量依赖性、高剂量率下的非线性、探测器老化）；方法（校准规程、本底扣除、修正程序）；材料（气体组成、滤片相互作用、探测器结构）；环境（温度、压力、湿度、电磁干扰、本底辐射）；测量（计数统计、刻度分辨率、辐射场均匀性）。 依据文献数据、按照《测量不确定度表示指南》（GUM）建立的不确定度预算表明：在 95% 置信水平（k = 2）下，合成后的扩展不确定度约为 54%。主要贡献因素是未修正的死时间损失（约 12.5%），以及能量和角度依赖性（约 23%）。结构化的 6 M 分析显示，将石川图与符合 GUM 的定量不确定度预算结合，既能提供识别误差来源的诊断框架，也能形成纠正措施的优先级排序。 这种整合建立了从定性根因分析到针对性缓解策略的系统路径，使资源能够投向对测量准确性影响最大的因素。研究结果强调，GM 剂量测量需要全面的不确定度管理，以支撑可靠的剂量评估；尤其在流行病学和放射生物学研究中，较大的不确定度可能掩盖剂量–响应关系。

English abstract

Accurate dose measurement with Geiger-Müller (GM) dosimeters is essential for radiation protection and research, but these measurements are affected by numerous sources of uncertainty that can reduce the reliability of dose assessments. In this study, an Ishikawa diagram was applied to systematically identify and categorize the root causes of errors in GM dose measurements, addressing the need for a holistic understanding of uncertainty factors. The primary objective was to visually map all potential error sources and develop a quantitative uncertainty budget showing their relative contributions.The methodology involved constructing a cause-and-effect diagram that organized the error sources into six main categories: manpower (operator skills, training, positioning), machine (dead time, energy dependence, nonlinearity at high dose rates, detector aging), method (calibration protocol, background subtraction, correction procedures), material (gas composition, filter interactions, detector construction), mother nature (temperature, pressure, humidity, electromagnetic interference, background radiation), and measurement (counting statistics, scale resolution, field uniformity).An uncertainty budget, based on literature data and constructed in accordance with the Guide to the Expression of Uncertainty in Measurement (GUM), indicated a combined expanded uncertainty of approximately 54% at the 95% confidence level (k = 2). The main contributors were uncorrected dead-time losses (about 12.5%) and energy and angular dependence (about 23%). The structured 6 M analysis showed that combining the Ishikawa diagram with a quantitative GUM-compliant uncertainty budget provides both a diagnostic framework for identifying error sources and a prioritized hierarchy of corrective actions.This integration establishes a structured pathway from qualitative root-cause analysis to targeted mitigation strategies, enabling resources to be directed toward factors with the greatest effect on measurement accuracy. The findings emphasize the need for comprehensive uncertainty management in GM dosimetry to support reliable dose assessments, particularly in epidemiological and radiobiological studies where large uncertainties can obscure dose-response relationships.

English abstract

high-fidelity calcu⁃lation of three-dimensional relative power distribution heavily relies on large-scale numerical simulations and is computationally expensive,which makes it difficult to satisfy the variables,and the reconstructed power field is obtained through the decoder together with the truncated modal basis.For the online application of the method,by inputting the parameters of physical model,the distribution of latent variables is first ob⁃tained and then sampled in the latent space.The method is able to propagate uncertainty from the latent repre⁃sentation to the reconstructed power field,thereby providing uncertainty estimates and confidence intervals for the prediction results.Simulation experiments are carried out for the steady-state three-dimensional rela⁃tive power distribution of the HPR1000 reactor core of Hualong One,where high-fidelity samples are gener⁃ated by CORCA-3D and 5%Gaussian noise is added to test the robustness of the method.It is shown that,in comparison with traditional linear reduced order modelling and neural-network-based methods,the method can maintain high reconstruction accuracy with much lower latent-space dimension,the relative error can be controlled close to 1%in the representative settings,while the uncertainty estimates are generally consistent with the local prediction quality and can reflect the reliability of the reconstructed results by providing the cor⁃responding confidence intervals.These results demonstrate that the method provides an effective approach for rapid reactor-core power restruction and uncertainty quantification.
