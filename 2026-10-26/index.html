高通量研究堆板状燃料通道可能因堵塞而熔化；系统程序局部分辨率不足，完整CFD又昂贵。本文以三维固体和二维流体耦合描述窄矩形通道堵塞进程，采用窄通道两相/CHF闭合、动态孔隙率更新流通区域，以全隐式推进液、汽、固及熔体。框架涵盖模型、耦合、稳定化、分离效应评价、堆级验证及事故应用。与窄通道流动沸腾压降、CHF、熔化堵塞计算、RA-6换热及JRR-3堆级结果的比较，为主要水力、沸腾、传热及材料演化提供互补评价。基于THFR的瞬态展示单个模拟通道从完整强迫对流，经沸腾和通道内横向再分配，发展到局部过热与熔体输运。

English abstract

Plate-type fuel assemblies in high-flux research reactors are susceptible to flow blockage that may lead to fuel melting. Conventional system codes do not provide the local resolution needed for this problem, whereas full computational fluid dynamics (CFD) simulations are computationally expensive for routine accident analysis. This paper presents a computational framework for narrow rectangular plate-type fuel channels that covers the progression of a blockage event. A blockage scenario is mapped to a coupled three-dimensional solid and two-dimensional fluid model. Narrow-channel closures describe two-phase flow and critical heat flux (CHF), a dynamic porosity field updates flow accessibility, and a fully implicit scheme advances the coupled liquid, vapor, solid, and melt fields. These physical and numerical models are integrated into a broader workflow comprising model formulation, multiphysics coupling, numerical stabilization, separate-effect assessment, reactor-scale validation, and accident-scenario application. Comparisons with narrow-channel flow-boiling pressure-drop data, CHF experiments, melt/blockage and fuel-melting calculations, RA-6 heat-transfer measurements, and Japan Research Reactor-3 (JRR-3) reactor-scale results provide complementary assessment of the principal hydraulic, boiling, thermal, and material-evolution components of the framework. A transient based on the Tsinghua High Flux Reactor (THFR) then demonstrates how the same formulation follows a single modeled channel from intact forced convection through boiling and in-channel lateral redistribution to local overheating and melt transport.

本文在临界安全、燃耗和堆剂量学三个应用评价ENDF/B-VIII.1。VVER燃料几何及结构材料不同于方形轻水堆，需要专门验证。使用MCNP6计算临界和剂量学、Serpent 2计算燃耗，结合ICSBEP、SFCOMPO及VVER-1000全堆剂量模型。343个临界基准平均正偏399 pcm，比VIII.0高70 pcm，作者认为处于统计和建模不确定性范围。对ISTC-2670的312个辐照后测量值，VIII.1能重现核素库存，与VIII.0差异多数较小：76%小于±1%、87%小于±2%。剂量学相较VIII.0明显改善，0.5 MeV以上注量提高12.5%，更接近堆外活化探测响应。改进归因于Fe-56、O-16等截面及角分布修订。作者推荐VIII.1用于所评估的VVER分析。

English abstract

The ENDF/B-VIII.1 nuclear data library has been evaluated for VVER (Vodo-Vodyanoi Energetichesky Reaktor, i.e. water-cooled water-moderated power reactor) calculations in three application areas: criticality safety, fuel depletion, and reactor dosimetry. VVER reactors differ from square-lattice light-water reactor designs in fuel geometry, core structures, and structural materials, making dedicated nuclear data validation for this reactor family necessary. The study used MCNP6 for criticality and reactor dosimetry calculations and Serpent 2 for fuel depletion, together with benchmark data from the International Criticality Safety Benchmark Evaluation Project (ICSBEP) and Spent Fuel Composition Database (SFCOMPO), and a full-core VVER-1000 dosimetry model. Criticality validation of 343 VVER benchmark cases showed an average positive bias of + 399 pcm (1 pcm = 10−5 in Δk/k), only 70 pcm higher than with ENDF/B-VIII.0 and well within statistical and modeling uncertainties. For fuel depletion, ENDF/B-VIII.1 successfully reproduced 312 post-irradiation assay values from the ISTC-2670 benchmark. Calculated isotopic inventories remained very close to ENDF/B-VIII.0 results, with 76% differing by less than ± 1% and 87% by less than ± 2%. Reactor dosimetry showed substantial improvement over ENDF/B-VIII.0. The calculated neutron fluence above 0.5 MeV increased by 12.5%, resulting in better agreement with measured responses from ex-vessel activation detectors. These improvements are attributed to revised cross sections and angular distributions for key nuclides, such as Fe-56 and O-16. Unlike ENDF/B-VIII.0, which significantly underestimated fast neutron fluence and was not recommended for reactor dosimetry, ENDF/B-VIII.1 demonstrates reliable performance across all three application areas and is therefore recommended for VVER reactor analyses.

English abstract

During long-term operation, heat pipe reactors exhibit strong multiphysics coupling effects, encompassing neutronics, heat transfer, structural deformation, and irradiation-induced material evolution. To analyze coupling effects, this study developed a burnup-dependent neutronic–thermal–mechanical–irradiation coupling method for the heat pipe reactor based on the Cardinal framework. The method combines OpenMC with MOOSE-based heat conduction and solid mechanics modules through in-memory coupling, and integrates newly developed irradiation swelling, densification, and creep models through a Python-based external driver. The verifications were performed in two levels. First, a steady-state neutronic–thermal–mechanical calculation was performed using a full-height 1/6 sector model of the MegaPower reactor. The predicted power, temperature, and axial-displacement distributions agree well with the reference results, and the maximum axial displacement is 2.37 cm, approximately 7.1% lower than the reference value of 2.55 cm. Updating the temperature and density fields on the undeformed neutronics geometry produces a total reactivity change of approximately −610 pcm, while geometry reconstruction introduces an additional total geometry-induced reactivity change of approximately −390 pcm. The combined reactivity change relative to the initial undeformed state is approximately −1000 pcm. The results also show that thermomechanical deformation causes noticeable core-power redistribution. Second, an explicit fuel–gap–monolith single-channel model was used for burnup-dependent neutronic–irradiation–thermal–mechanical verification. At 2.0MWd/kgU, the differences in total strain are 1.39×10-3 for the fuel and 1.04×10-3 for the monolith. The principal burnup-dependent trends of fuel swelling, densification, creep, and monolith creep are consistently reproduced. These results demonstrate that the developed method can reproduce both steady-state multiphysics feedback and burnup-dependent irradiation-induced deformation in solid-state heat pipe reactors, providing a foundation for higher-fidelity life-cycle multiphysics analysis.

弱源启动中子种群受随机效应控制，常规确定论或点动力学不足。本文发展直接时变蒙卡方法描述无需外加启动源的过程，通过裂变率演化识别随机向确定性行为转变。相对仅适用于简化几何的传统随机动力学，方法可处理真实三维反应堆，并以KRUSTY热管堆及小型压水堆示范。过渡时间具有内在统计变异，中子种群增长时最大通量的空间分布逐渐集中。该框架为工程尺度弱源启动提供工具。

English abstract

Weak source startup represents a reactor startup regime in which neutron population evolution is dominated by stochastic effects and cannot be adequately described using conventional deterministic or point-kinetics approaches. The weak source startup approach, which operates without an external neutron source, eliminates the need for costly neutron source procurement and improves the economic performance of nuclear power plants. In this study, a Monte Carlo based method is developed for simulating weak source startup processes. The proposed approach enables direct time-dependent simulation of stochastic neutron transport and allows the transition from stochastic to deterministic behavior to be identified through the evolution of the fission rate. Unlike traditional stochastic neutron kinetics methods, which are typically limited to simplified geometries, the present method is applicable to realistic three-dimensional reactor configurations. The method is demonstrated using both a KRUSTY heat-pipe-cooled reactor and a small pressurized water reactor. The results show that the transition time during weak source startup exhibits inherent statistical variability and that the spatial distribution of maximum neutron flux gradually becomes concentrated as the neutron population increases. The proposed framework provides a practical computational tool for analyzing weak source startup phenomena in engineering-scale reactor systems.

中文摘要

MegaPower是一种设计用于偏远地区和军事基地等去中心化能源市场的热管冷却反应堆,其启堆过程涉及复杂的多物理场耦合计算。传统高分辨率仿真方法常面临较高的计算成本。本研究以MegaPower热管堆的启堆过程为研究对象,提出了一种基于Proper Orthogonal Decomposition-Radial Basis Function(POD-RBF)快速预测方法的核热力耦合模型,以实现复杂瞬态工况的高效模拟。通过构建MegaPower高分辨率1/6堆芯模型,并结合OpenMC、FEniCSx和POD-RBF快速预测模型对堆芯的多物理场行为进行模拟,研究了启堆阶段的功率、温度和反应性动态特性。模拟结果表明,POD-RBF方法能够准确稳定地预测堆芯功率分布,最大误差为3.04%,平均误差为0.77%;keff预测的平均误差低于50 pcm(percent mille),最大误差不超过100 pcm。在启堆功率调节阶段,功率上升速率约为每秒0.53%额定功率,最终平稳达到额定功率,功率与反应性的动态变化保持稳定。同时,POD-RBF方法显著提升了计算效率,将单个时间步计算时间从传统高分辨率模型的800 s降低至3 s,整个启堆过程模拟时间由74 d缩短至7.5 h,效率提升约99.6%。研究结果表明,基于POD-RBF方法的热管堆核热力耦合模型能够显著提高计算效率,同时在瞬态问题中具备较高的预测精度,为热管堆的设计优化与安全评估提供了参考,也为多物理场问题的快速仿真提供了新思路。
