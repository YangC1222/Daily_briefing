中文摘要

深穿透蒙卡中，源谱不均匀及能量相关输运使不同到达能群的相对不确定度可相差数十至数百倍。本文提出散射耦合自适应源偏置（SC-ASB），使用源—目标响应矩阵保留高能源中子散射到低到达能群的耦合。通过在归一化及双侧误差平衡约束下最小化相对统计误差之和得到偏置源，并以权重修正保留原始源期望。在大气中子输运算例中，误差分布及效率改善；源高15 km时品质因子为模拟输运的276倍。相对均匀源偏置，对不同源高适应性更好；相对权窗方法，更有效平衡各能群相对统计误差。该方法为分能谱深穿透中子输运提供减方差框架。

English abstract

Reliable neutron arrival spectra are needed for space radiation assessment and radiation protection, but deep-penetration Monte Carlo calculations often yield highly uneven uncertainties across energy groups. In analog Monte Carlo simulations, the nonuniform source spectrum and the energy-dependent transport behavior of neutrons can lead to relative uncertainties that differ by tens or even hundreds of times among arrival energy groups. To address this imbalance in arrival-spectrum uncertainties for deep-penetration transport, this study proposes a scattering-coupled adaptive source biasing (SC-ASB) method that uses a source-to-target response matrix to retain scattering-induced coupling from high-energy source neutrons to lower-energy arrival groups. The biased source distribution is derived by minimizing the sum of relative statistical errors (RSEs) subject to normalization and two-sided error-balance constraints, while the original source expectation is preserved by weight correction. Atmospheric neutron transport calculations showed that SC-ASB improved the relative-error distribution and increased computational efficiency. For a 15 km source height case, the figure of merit was 276 times that of analog simulation. Compared with uniform source biasing, SC-ASB showed better adaptability across source heights. In contrast to the WW variance-reduction method, SC-ASB provides more effective balancing of the RSEs across different energy groups. The method provides a variance-reduction framework for spectrum-resolved deep-penetration neutron transport.

中文摘要

为研究自动卸压系统（ADS）实际运行中的高压两相排放，以原型喷淋管排放参数为依据，实验考察跑道形孔口喷淋管射流凝结导致的压力振荡。单相排放时，最大振荡随水温先增后减；蒸汽质量流量增加时，中高水温下振荡增强，低水温下变化不明显。两相排放水温高于90℃时，最大振荡随两相质量流量增大及平衡干度降低而增强。单相最大振荡123 kPa出现在凝结振荡区；两相最大398 kPa出现在不完全凝结区，后者更高。水箱内沿径向距孔口越远，最大振荡越小，衰减幅度也随距离减小；轴向则先降后升。

English abstract

To investigate the high pressure two phase discharge characteristics of the sparger during the actual operation of automatic depressurization system (ADS), based on the discharge parameter conditions of the prototype sparger, the pressure oscillation behavior caused by the jet condensation of the racetrack orifices sparger was experimentally studied in this paper. The results show that under the condition of single phase discharge, the maximum oscillation increases first and then decreases with the increase of water temperature. With the increase of steam mass flow rate, it increases in the middle and high water temperature, and does not change significantly in the low water temperature. Under the condition of two phase discharge, when the water temperature is higher than 90 °C, the maximum oscillation increases with the increase of two phase mass flow rate and the decrease of equilibrium quality. Under single phase discharge conditions, the maximum oscillation appears in the condensation oscillation regime map, which is 123 kPa. Under the two phase discharge condition, it appears in the incomplete condensation regime map, which is 398 kPa. The maximum oscillation of the two phase condition is higher than that of the single phase condition. For the oscillation propagation law in the water tank, the maximum oscillation in the radial direction decreases with the increase of the distance from the sparger orifices, and the decrease amplitude decreases with the increase of the distance. In the axial direction, it shows a trend of decreasing first and then increasing.

中文摘要

月球基地基础设施、极区探测与月球车需要充分利用就地资源的能源系统，以减少从地球运送的质量。本文研究以超临界一氧化二氮和氦为工质的再压缩布雷顿循环，评估系统质量，并采用小型模块化反应堆预测给定循环工况下的最小质量堆。通过在反应堆、辐射屏蔽、循环机组、回热器及散热器之间权衡，优化系统质量和比质量。结果给出的系统比质量为17.37 kg/kWe。使用月球就地资源后，反应堆屏蔽质量和体积分别降低3.55%和14.20%。研究提出的能源方案以轻量、高效及连续供电为目标，面向月球基地应用。

English abstract

The Moon is the only natural satellite of the Earth and also the closest celestial body to the Earth. The Moon is regarded as an outpost of deep space exploration. The construction of lunar base infrastructure, polar exploration, and rovers constitutes the first step for humans to venture into the Moon. In the process of constructing a lunar base, it is essential to establish an energy system that can fully utilize local resources. This system will reduce the weight of materials transported from Earth and ultimately solve the energy shortage problem on the Moon. A recompression Brayton cycle that utilizes in-situ resources, with supercritical nitrous oxide and helium as the working media, and evaluates the mass of the energy system. A small modular reactor is applied to predict the minimum-mass reactor suitable for the given cycle conditions. The system mass and specific mass optimization explored the trade-offs among the reactor, radiation shield, Brayton cycle unit, recuperator, and radiator to obtain the minimum mass of the lunar base energy system. The results indicate that the specific mass of the energy system is 17.37 kg∙kWe−1. In addition, it is found that, by utilizing in-situ lunar base resources, the mass and volume of the radiation shield for the reactor can be reduced by 3.55% and 14.20%, respectively. In conclusion, the proposed energy system scheme offers several advantages, including light weight, high efficiency, and continuous electricity supply, for application in lunar bases.

中文摘要

核反应堆堆芯构件需要在高温、强辐照及复杂机械载荷的极端环境下长期服役。特种石墨因其优异的中子慢化性能、抗热冲击性能、耐高温化学稳定性等特性,广泛应用于制造关键堆芯构件。选用日本东洋碳素公司的IG-110和四川福碳新材料科技有限公司的FT-101两种特种石墨,研究二者热应力性能和辐照诱导的性能变化,分析特种石墨的失效机制,预测寿命。测试两种石墨的热膨胀系数随温度、压应力和拉应力变化的规律,基于测试结果,采用多元线性回归方法建立了特种石墨热膨胀系数与温度和应力间的关联模型,用于堆芯构件的热应力计算。同时研究了温度和压力对两种石墨弹性模量和抗压强度的影响,并建立了力学性能关联模型,用于堆芯构件的力学响应计算。研究辐照诱导对膨胀率和力学性能的影响,建立辐照性能关联模型,利用对称性建立1/4三维有限元模型进行石墨堆芯构件“辐照-热-力”多场耦合应力分析,评价使用风险。对FT-101特种石墨构件进行失效机制分析及寿命预测,通过实验与模拟,深入剖析了辐照及热应力引发失效等关键失效机制,并构建基于有限元法的失效概率评估模型。研究满功率年对失效概率的影响,进行了不同蠕变泊松比、一次蠕变的石墨构件寿命预测,可达25.2—32.5年。本研究为高温气冷堆石墨构件的优化设计、安全评定以及寿命预测提供重要的理论依据和技术支撑。
