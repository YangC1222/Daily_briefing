English abstract

Maintaining a uniform chromium (Cr) coating thickness on zirconium-alloy cladding is critical to nuclear-fuel accident tolerance. Eddy current testing (ECT) with machine learning (ML) is a promising non-destructive method, but existing studies often use random train–test splits that leak specimen identity, overestimating accuracy on unseen rods, and rarely quantify the measurement noise floor. We propose a specimen-level evaluation framework, leave-one-specimen-out (LOSO) cross-validation with nested hyperparameter tuning, for ML-based ECT thickness estimation. Applied to a six-frequency (3.5–6 MHz) dataset of ten Cr-coated specimens (3.9–20.1 ), it exposes substantial over-optimism: a greedy frequency-selection scheme scoring 0.196 RMSE in-sample degrades to 0.506 under leakage-free evaluation. A mixed-effects variance decomposition establishes a single-channel measurement repeatability floor of 0.10 as a conservative lower bound for achievable RMSE and prediction intervals. A uniform multi-frequency Gaussian process regression improves RMSE from 0.265 to 0.205 and halves prediction-interval miscalibration, a consistent trend whose 95% bootstrap confidence interval still includes zero at ten specimens (P(better) = 0.84). Learning frequency or component relevance overfits on small datasets, confirming uniform fusion as the robust choice. This work establishes a leakage-free evaluation standard and a repeatability-based performance reference for ECT thickness estimation.

本研究评估了与高温反应堆热源耦合的开放式布雷顿循环（OABC）二次系统的适用性，评估内容包括循环性能、涡轮机械尺寸和经济可行性。在考虑中间换热器（IHX）两侧至少5℃的温差以及1%的IHX空气侧压降后，利用反应堆特定的堆芯出口温度确定了OABC涡轮机的入口温度。通过压力比优化确定了热效率、净比功和所需质量流量。利用基于史密斯圆图的初步尺寸计算估算了压缩机和涡轮机的等效特征直径，并利用相应的热力学条件评估了单位资本支出（CAPEX）和平准化度电成本（LCOE）。结果表明，提高涡轮机入口温度可以提高优化后的系统效率和净比功，同时降低产生相同电能输出所需的质量流量。因此，低温工况（例如BWRX-300和ENTRA1）在给定电输出功率下需要更大的等效特征直径，而高温工况（例如AFR-100、IMSR和Xe-100）由于所需空气质量流量较低，因此对涡轮机械流通面积的要求较小。经济性评估表明，提高电功率和涡轮进口温度均可降低OABC二次系统的单位资本支出（CAPEX）和度电成本（LCOE）。在经济边界中明确纳入反应堆—循环中间换热器（IHX）后，在所研究的100-600兆瓦功率范围内，所有反应堆工况的单位资本支出均高于AP-1000和MIGHTR-4二次系统的等效参考值。所有工况的计算度电成本也均高于AP-1000 NOAK和MIGHTR-4 NOAK工况的参考度电成本。这些结果表明，较高的反应堆温度条件大幅降低了 OABC 二次系统的成本，尽管计算出的特定 CAPEX 和 LCOE 仍然高于当前假设下采用的参考值。

English abstract

In this study, the applicability of an open-air Brayton cycle (OABC) secondary system coupled with high-temperature reactor heat sources was evaluated in terms of its cycle performance, turbomachinery sizing, and economic viability. Reactor-specific core outlet temperatures were used to determine the OABC turbine inlet temperatures after accounting for a minimum temperature difference of 5 °C across the intermediate heat exchanger (IHX), while a 1% air-side pressure loss across the IHX was also considered. Pressure-ratio optimisation was performed to determine the thermal efficiency, net specific work, and required mass flow rate. The equivalent characteristic diameters of the compressor and turbine were estimated using Smith chart-based preliminary sizing, while the corresponding thermodynamic conditions were used to evaluate the specific capital expenditure (CAPEX) and levelised cost of electricity (LCOE). The results revealed that increasing the turbine inlet temperature improved the optimised system efficiency and net specific work while reducing the mass flow rate required to produce the same electric power output. Accordingly, low-temperature cases such as BWRX-300 and ENTRA1 required substantially larger equivalent characteristic diameters at a given electrical output, whereas the higher-temperature AFR-100, IMSR, and Xe-100 cases exhibited smaller turbomachinery flow-area requirements owing to their lower required air mass flow rates. The economic assessment indicated that increasing both electric power and turbine inlet temperature reduced the specific CAPEX and LCOE of the OABC secondary system. After explicitly including the reactor–cycle IHX in the economic boundary, the specific CAPEX of all reactor cases remained above the AP-1000 and MIGHTR-4 secondary-system-equivalent reference values throughout the investigated 100–600 MWe range. The calculated LCOE for all cases also exceeded the reference LCOE for the AP-1000 NOAK and MIGHTR-4 NOAK cases. These results indicate that higher-temperature reactor conditions substantially reduce the OABC secondary-system cost, although the calculated specific CAPEX and LCOE remain above the adopted reference values under the present assumptions.

核电站正常运行和异常运行条件下均会出现过冷沸腾流动。因此，准确预测这些现象对于反应堆设计和安全分析至关重要。本研究针对垂直向上流动，提出了一种改进的过冷沸腾模型，旨在提高最佳估算热工水力安全分析程序的预测性能。主要改进包括：半机理壁面沸腾传热模型、改进的蒸汽生成率公式、气泡流的气泡冷凝模型以及考虑界面摩擦的更新漂移通量模型。这些模型已集成到MARS-KS1.6程序中，该程序是一个典型的最佳估算安全分析平台，并利用在1-68 bar压力范围内从环形和矩形通道获得的实验数据对其进行了评估。结果表明，壁面温度、空隙率和相速度的预测结果显著提高，与实验数据的吻合度远高于原始程序。此外，蒸汽生成率模型和壁面沸腾传热模型的耦合集成进一步增强了预测的稳健性。总体而言，所提出的过冷沸腾模型提高了最佳估计安全分析代码中热工水力预测的可靠性和准确性。

English abstract

Subcooled boiling flows are expected to occur under both normal and abnormal operating conditions in nuclear power plants. Therefore, accurate prediction of these phenomena is essential for reactor design and safety analysis. This study introduces improved subcooled boiling models for vertical upward flows aimed at enhancing the predictive performance of best-estimate thermal-hydraulic safety analysis codes. The key advancements include a semi-mechanistic wall-boiling heat transfer model, an improved vapor generation rate formulation, a bubble condensation model for bubbly flows, and an updated drift-flux model accounting for interfacial friction. These models were implemented in the MARS-KS1.6 code as a representative best-estimate safety analysis platform and evaluated against experimental data obtained from annular and rectangular channels over a pressure range of 1–68 bar. The results demonstrate significantly improved predictions of wall temperature, void fraction, and phasic velocities, showing much better agreement with experimental data than the original code. Moreover, the coupled integration of the vapor generation rate and wall-boiling heat transfer models further enhances the robustness of the predictions. Overall, the proposed subcooled boiling models improve the reliability and accuracy of thermal-hydraulic predictions in best-estimate safety analysis codes.

English abstract

English abstract

Multi-element-doped UO2 SIMFUEL pellets were fabricated by high-pressure compaction and sintering at 1550–1700 °C under a reducing atmosphere to simulate chemically complex, high-burnup-equivalent fuel compositions. SEM, XRD, EDS, and ICP-MS characterization revealed pronounced microstructural evolution with increasing sintering temperature. Sintering at 1700 °C promoted substantial grain coarsening and densification up to 96.3% theoretical density, reducing interconnected porosity and effective grain boundary area relative to pellets sintered at 1550 °C. This microstructural stabilization enhanced resistance to aqueous dissolution during one-year semi-dynamic leaching tests conducted at 25–90 °C in accordance with the ASTM C1308 protocol. Dopants partitioned into chemically resilient metallic ε-type phases and more soluble dopant-bearing oxide regions, giving rise to element-specific dissolution kinetics governed by phase chemistry and microstructural connectivity. Mo release reflected both normalization artifacts and genuinely faster dissolution at lower burnup. Beyond compositional effects, elevated leaching temperatures consistently accelerated elemental release and were accompanied by subtle lattice contraction, consistent with partial oxidation of the fluorite matrix. Collectively, these processing–microstructure–dissolution relationships provide mechanistic insights for improving the chemical durability of multi-component fluorite-structured nuclear fuels in aqueous environments.

为进一步提高燃料性能预测精度、拓展燃料分析工具适用范围。本文针对典型棒状燃料元件,基于商用有限元分析程序COMSOL、系统安全分析程序ARSAC以及蒙特卡罗燃耗计算程序RMC,搭建了燃料元件多物理场耦合分析平台MCAT,实现了燃料模块、热工水力模块及中子物理模块的双向耦合。耦合平台采用模块化设计思想,利用中间数据接口管理耦合参数并明确各模块的“边界”,借助各模块的输入/输出文本文件实现耦合参数的更新与反馈,避免了对已有程序进行源码级修改。采用非对称Picard迭代算法实现模块间的双向耦合,将物理和热工模块看作黑盒子程序,在燃料热力学求解过程中依次调用RMC和ARSAC执行计算并交换数据,反复迭代直到收敛。本文从模块、接口及综合预测结果等方面对MCAT进行初步验证,结果表明MCAT能够准确预测燃料元件内功率、温度、结构变形及冷却剂状态等参数分布,表明MCAT平台在模块开发、耦合流程搭建及编码实现等方面的正确性。
