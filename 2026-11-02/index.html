中文摘要

燃料棒流致振动（FIV）是影响反应堆结构完整性和运行安全的关键问题。基于ANSYS Workbench的常规流固耦合（FSI）方法通常能够准确预测振动，但计算成本较高。本研究为螺旋十字形燃料（HCF）棒建立降阶FSI框架。利用大涡模拟（LES）计算湍流激励力，以传递矩阵法（TMM）获得HCF棒模态特性，再用模态叠加法（MSM）结合Newmark-β法重构瞬态振动响应。通过与Workbench结果比较，评价精度和效率。所提TMM框架预测的前三阶干模态频率与Workbench模态分析相符，相对误差依次约0.15%、0.53%和1.34%；两种方法的瞬态位移响应及功率谱密度（PSD）接近。在保持相当预测精度的同时，框架实现约6.29倍整体计算加速。本文框架为HCF棒流致振动提供高效且准确的求解途径，有望用于燃料组件结构优化和安全评估。

English abstract

Flow-induced vibration (FIV) of fuel rods is a critical issue affecting the structural integrity and operational safety of reactors. Although the conventional fluid–structure interaction (FSI) approach based on the ANSYS Workbench platform generally provides accurate vibration predictions, it incurs substantial computational cost. This study develops a reduced-order FSI framework for the FIV analysis of helical cruciform fuel (HCF) rods. The proposed framework employs large eddy simulation (LES) to compute the turbulent excitation forces. The modal characteristics of the HCF rod are obtained using the transfer matrix method (TMM), while the transient vibration response is reconstructed by the modal superposition method (MSM) combined with the Newmark-β method. The accuracy and computational efficiency of the framework are evaluated through a comparison with the results obtained from Workbench. The results show that the first three dry modal frequencies predicted by the proposed TMM framework agree well with the Workbench modal analysis results, with relative errors of approximately 0.15%, 0.53%, and 1.34%, respectively. The transient displacement responses and power spectral density (PSD) characteristics predicted by the two methods are in close agreement. Moreover, the proposed framework achieves an overall computational speedup ratio of approximately 6.29 while maintaining comparable prediction accuracy. The coupled framework proposed in this study provides an efficient and accurate solution approach for the FIV analysis of HCF rods, and serves as a highly promising computational tool for the structural optimization design and safety assessment of fuel assemblies.

中文摘要

高温气冷堆（HTGR）因其高效率、燃料完整性和非能动余热移除能力，成为第四代核反应堆的热门候选方案。本文全面综述了棱柱形HTGR在失流事故（LOFA）条件下的热工水力行为、自然循环现象和传热机制。重点关注实验研究和计算模型，这些研究阐明了带压及失压导热冷却（PCC/DCC）瞬态过程中的传导、辐射和浮力驱动的对流。本文还回顾了HTGR的历史和当前设计，以追溯非能动安全概念的演变。此外，本文还结合近期基准研究，分析了流动层流化、浮力和加速效应、旁路和腔室混合等关键现象。本文首先总结了现有的强制对流、混合对流和自然对流三种传热模式下的传热关联式，然后回顾了对建模精度至关重要的氦气和石墨热物理性质关联式。该综述指出了多维自然循环、湍流建模和物性耦合方面的研究空白，这些空白对于验证依赖于固有安全性和长期非能动冷却的下一代模块化和微型高温气冷堆（HTGR）设计至关重要。

English abstract

High-Temperature Gas-Cooled Reactors (HTGRs) are prominent Generation IV candidates owing to their high efficiency, fuel integrity, and capability for passive decay-heat removal. This paper provides a comprehensive review of thermal-hydraulic behavior, natural-circulation phenomena, and heat-transfer mechanisms in prismatic HTGRs under loss-of-flow accident (LOFA) conditions. Emphasis is placed on experimental investigations and computational modeling that clarify conduction, radiation, and buoyancy-driven convection during pressurized and depressurized conduction-cooldown (PCC/DCC) transients. Historical and current HTGR designs are surveyed to trace the evolution of passive-safety concepts. Key phenomena—such as flow laminarization, buoyancy and acceleration effects, bypass, and plenum mixing—are analyzed alongside recent benchmark studies. Existing heat-transfer correlations for forced, mixed, and natural-convection regimes are summarized, followed by a review of helium and graphite thermophysical-property correlations critical to modeling accuracy. The synthesis identifies research gaps in multi-dimensional natural circulation, turbulence modeling, and property coupling that remain central to validating next-generation modular and micro-HTGR designs relying on inherent safety and long-term passive cooling.

中文摘要

本研究基于福岛第一核电站1号机组的内部调查结果，提出了估算构型（EC），用于解释燃料碎片迁移至基座后熔融堆芯-混凝土相互作用（MCCI）相关的可能物质分布和传热条件。通过对电厂数据分析和简化的能量平衡评估，检验了这些构型与观测到的事故进程的一致性。结果表明，混凝土的大量烧蚀可能主要发生在碎片迁移后的最初1.5天内，主要集中在内裙和下部主安全壳围成的区域。研究还表明，部分源自混凝土的熔融物质可能以二次熔融相的形式存在，并与燃料碎片部分分离。在随后的长期阶段，其他散热机制——例如熔融物质内部的对流换热、基座和干井区域的气相换热以及通过周围结构的传热——可能有助于限制混凝土的进一步大规模侵蚀。总体而言，所提出的基于 EC 的框架提供了物理上一致的解释，将内部观测与电厂数据联系起来，并提出了传统 MCCI 分析中未明确表示的冷却过程。

English abstract

Based on internal investigation results from Fukushima Daiichi Unit 1, this study proposes Estimated Configurations (ECs) to interpret possible material distributions and heat transfer conditions associated with molten core–concrete interaction (MCCI) after fuel debris relocation into the pedestal. Plant data analyses and simplified energy-balance evaluations were performed to examine their consistency with the observed accident progression.　The results indicate that substantial concrete ablation likely occurred mainly within approximately the first 1.5 days after debris relocation, primarily in the region bounded by the inner skirt and the lower primary containment vessel shell. They also suggest that part of the concrete-derived molten material may have existed as a secondary molten phase partially separated from the fuel debris.　For the subsequent long-term phase, additional heat removal mechanisms—such as convective heat transfer within molten materials, gas-phase heat transfer in the pedestal and drywell regions, and heat transfer through surrounding structures—may represent additional heat-removal mechanisms that could have contributed to limiting further large-scale concrete erosion.　Overall, the proposed EC-based framework provides a physically consistent interpretation linking internal observations with plant data and suggests cooling processes not explicitly represented in conventional MCCI analyses.

中文摘要

本文研究铝6061-T6与钢构成的双金属半球壳，在极端外压及温度梯度下的非线性屈曲和缺陷敏感性，作为严重工业及核事故条件的理想化代表。几何与材料非线性分析（GMNA）表明，把高模量钢放在外侧显著增强抗弯刚度，并在较厚几何（R/t=136）下延迟极限点失稳。相反，当R/t≥500时，失效转为纯弹性几何失稳，单种材料屈服优势不再发挥作用。为评价严苛环境下的稳健性，采用GMNIA进一步加入局部凹坑及0–500℃的耦合热软化。在室温下，壳体对幅值不超过壳厚（w/t≤1.0）的缺陷有较好容忍度；但同时承受高温（T>100℃）时，会发生热与几何协同失稳。两种金属的差异性热软化削弱双金属载荷分担，导致局部提前突跳。与工业规范对照显示，仅采用单一材料热折减标准可能得到严重不保守的预测，因此双金属压力容器和安全壳的分析设计需要耦合热—几何评价。

English abstract

This study investigates the non-linear buckling mechanics and imperfection sensitivity of bimetallic (Aluminum 6061-T6 and Steel) hemispherical shells subjected to extreme external pressure and thermal gradients, representing conditions typical of severe industrial and nuclear accident scenarios. Utilizing Geometrically and Materially Nonlinear Analysis (GMNA), the idealized parametric framework reveals that positioning the higher-modulus steel on the exterior significantly enhances flexural rigidity, delaying limit-point instability in thicker geometries (R/t = 136). Conversely, at extreme slenderness (R/t ≥ 500), failure transitions to purely elastic geometric instability, nullifying individual material yield advantages. To evaluate operational robustness under severe environments, the framework was extended via GMNIA to incorporate localized dimple imperfections and coupled thermal softening (0°C to 500°C). While the shells exhibit exceptional defect tolerance at ambient temperatures for imperfection amplitudes up to the shell thickness (w/t ≤ 1.0), simultaneous exposure to elevated temperatures (T > 100°C) triggers a synergistic thermo-geometric collapse. Differential thermal softening of the constituent metals neutralizes the bimetallic load-sharing mechanism, forcing premature localized snap-through. Benchmarking against industrial codes demonstrates that relying on single-material thermal reduction standards yields dangerously non-conservative predictions, establishing the critical necessity for coupled thermo-geometric evaluations in the Design by Analysis (DbA) of bimetallic pressure and containment vessels.
