中文摘要

本文研究含超铀元素（TRU）的钍基燃料作为环形燃料小型模块化反应堆替代燃料的可行性。采用蒙特卡罗程序RMC 3.5.0和ENDF/B-VIII.1截面库建立中国环形燃料SMR堆芯模型，分析(233U, TRU, 0.94Th)O2、(233U, TRU, 0.945Th)O2及(233U, TRU, 0.95Th)O2三种配方的中子学特性。在2000有效满功率日内，考察有效增殖因数、易裂变核素质量演化、堆级钚、次锕系元素以及135Xe和149Sm中子毒物质量变化，并与传统UO2比较。还分析中子通量分布、有效缓发中子份额、燃料及慢化剂温度反应性系数、瞬发中子寿命、中子代时间和功率分布。作者认为，相较UO2，所提配方具有可接受的安全参数及有利的堆芯功率分布，因此中子学分析支持其作为替代燃料的可行性。

English abstract

This work investigates the feasibility of using Th-based fuels containing transuranic (TRU) as alternative fuel for small modular reactors (SMRs) with Annular fuel. Monte Carlo code RMC 3.5.0 and cross-section library ENDF/B-VIII.1 were employed to establish the Chinese SMR core with annular fuel. This code was used to study the neutronic characteristics of the proposed Th-based fuel (233U, TRU, 0.94Th)O2, (233U, TRU, 0.945Th)O2 and (233U, TRU, 0.95Th)O2. Fuel burn-up parameters, including effective multiplication factor (keff), fissile nuclide mass evolution, reactor-grade plutonium (rgPu) mass, minor actinides (MA), neutron poison (135Xe and 149Sm) mass variation, were analyzed during 2000 effective full power days (EFPDs) for proposed Th-based fuel types and compared with traditional UO2 fuel. Analysis of the neutron flux distribution, effective delayed neutron fraction (βeff), fuel temperature reactivity coefficient (FTC), moderator temperature reactivity coefficient (MTC), prompt neutron lifetime (l), neutron generation time (Λ) and visualized power distribution provide valuable insights into the neutronic behavior and performance of the proposed fuel types. The neutronic analysis results confirm the viability of using the proposed Th-based fuel types as alternatives to UO2, because of their acceptable safety parameter values and favorable power distribution across the core compared to UO2.

本研究开展了高密度测点换热实验，考察液态钠流过电加热钠冷快堆燃料元件模拟器的过程。模拟器以空心毛细管螺旋缠绕来模拟绕丝，可布置空间分辨率达2.6 mm的分布式光纤温度传感器。传感器分别嵌入绕丝、模拟器包壳和环形流道，并辅以热电偶测量温度及换热。实验在余弦轴向热流分布下获得内部和周围高分辨率温度场，局部热流最高950 kW/m²、Pe为40–400（8400<Re<75000）、钠温200–550℃。36个稳态工况覆盖体积流量0–2.73 m³/h、输入功率最高37 kW。绕丝温度存在局部变化，归因于加热丝偏心、绕丝内传感器位置以及加热器内部空间变化的接触热阻。二维/三维有限元表明，这些机制能产生与实验同量级的表面温差。通过空间平均考虑这些效应后，Nu在所测Pe范围内与Seban–Shimazaki及Bailey环形通道关联一致。该装置复现绕丝几何、余弦热流和SFR燃料棒温区，但环形通道没有复现六角棒束的子通道混合和通道间横流。研究展示了分布式光纤测量液态金属温度场的能力，并提供带详细几何和边界条件的实验数据，以支持绕丝燃料组件及其他先进液态金属换热系统的模型发展。

English abstract

A highly instrumented heat transfer experiment was conducted to investigate liquid sodium flows around an electrically heated prototypical sodium fast reactor fuel element simulator. The simulator was helically wrapped with a hollow capillary tube mimicking a wire wrap spacer, enabling the deployment of distributed fiber optic temperature sensors with spatial resolution down to 2.6 mm. Fiber optic temperature sensors were embedded within the wire wrap, inside the fuel simulator cladding, and in the annular flow channel, complemented by thermocouples to capture detailed temperature profiles and provide data for determining heat transfer behavior. This setup enabled high-resolution measurements of temperature fields in and around a wire-wrapped fuel pin simulator in liquid sodium under cosine-shaped axial heat flux profiles, with local heat fluxes up to 950 kW/m2, Peclet numbers between 40 and 400 (8400 < Re < 75,000), and sodium temperatures from 200 °C to 550 °C. Sodium flow rates from 0 to 2.73 m3/h and power inputs up to 37 kW were tested across 36 steady state cases. The measured wire wrap temperature profiles exhibit local variations attributed to filament eccentricity, sensor position within the wire wrap, and spatially varying internal contact resistance within the heater assembly. Two- and three-dimensional finite element analyses show that these mechanisms can produce surface temperature variations of the same magnitude as those observed experimentally. When spatial averaging is applied to account for these effects, the resulting Nusselt numbers agree with the Seban & Shimazaki and Bailey annular correlations over the tested Peclet range. This agreement is obtained in a configuration that reproduces the wire wrap geometry, the cosine-shaped axial heat flux profile, and the sodium temperature range of an SFR fuel pin, although the annular channel does not reproduce the subchannel mixing or interchannel crossflow of a hexagonal bundle. The results also demonstrate the capability of distributed fiber optic sensing for resolving temperature fields in liquid metal systems and provide an experimental dataset with detailed geometry and boundary conditions to support the development of thermal hydraulic models of wire wrapped fuel assemblies and other heat exchange systems relevant to advanced liquid metal-cooled reactor design.

English abstract

空泡反应性系数是水慢化反应堆安全分析的重要反馈参数。本文用OpenMC研究刚果民主共和国CGEA运行的TRICO II TRIGA Mark II研究堆。模拟CA（1972–1974）和CB（1974–2008）两种历史构型的冷态零功率状态，两者均装填新鲜标准TRIGA U–ZrH1.6燃料，含铀8.5 wt%，235U富集度约20 wt%。分别在燃料棒周围、活动控制棒跟随段、反射层附近轴向水隙和侧向石墨反射层逐渐引入空泡，记录keff随空泡率α的变化。采用293 K的ENDF/B-VIII.0核库。两种构型在所有慢化剂区域呈负空泡系数，但在控制棒B4C段引入空泡时响应变正。CB对中心区域扰动略不敏感，侧反射层响应则几乎相同。拟合系数及传播后的统计误差与已发表TRIGA Mark II结果比较。专门计数证实，中心区域响应由谱硬化与增强共振俘获主导，反射层响应由泄漏增加主导。有效缓发中子份额CA为0.00718±0.00019、CB为0.00732±0.00019，与文献值一致。

English abstract

The void reactivity coefficient is among several reactivity feedback parameters that are essential in water-moderated reactor safety analysis. This study quantifies the void coefficient of the TRICO II TRIGA Mark II research reactor, operated by the CGEA (Commissariat Général à l’Énergie Atomique) in the Democratic Republic of Congo, using the open-source Monte Carlo code OpenMC. Two core configurations, CA (1972–1974) and CB (1974–2008), were modeled under cold, zero-power conditions, both loaded with fresh standard TRIGA U-ZrH1.6 fuel (8.5 wt% uranium enriched to ∼20 wt% 235U, identical in both configurations). For each, voids were gradually introduced into four regions: around the fuel rods, the active control-rod followers, the axial water gaps near the reflectors, and the lateral graphite reflector. keff was tracked as a function of void fraction α. Simulations used the ENDF/B-VIII.0 cross-section library at 293 K. The results show a negative void coefficient in all moderator regions for both configurations, becoming positive when voids are introduced into the B4C section of the control rods. The CB configuration exhibits slightly lower sensitivity to perturbations in central regions compared to CA, whereas the lateral reflector responses are almost identical. The fitted coefficients, along with their propagated statistical uncertainties, are compared against published TRIGA Mark II values. Dedicated tallies confirm that spectrum hardening with enhanced resonance capture drives the central-region responses, while increased neutron leakage drives the reflector-region responses. The effective delayed neutron fraction is 0.00718 ± 0.00019 (CA) and 0.00732 ± 0.00019 (CB), consistent with documented TRIGA Mark II values.

English abstract

Owing to the exceptional thermal conductivity of lead–bismuth eutectic (LBE), such coolant temperature fluctuations can be transmitted to surrounding structural components, which constitutes a non-negligible safety risk for reactor operation. In this study, numerical simulations of temperature fluctuations in the upper plenum at MYRRHA pool-type LBE-cooled reactor are carried out using the detached eddy simulation (DES) method. Spectral analysis reveals that the dominant frequencies of high-intensity regions fall within the range of 0–1 Hz, and the effective fluctuation energy is concentrated within the 0–6 Hz frequency band. The findings of this study can provide important theoretical basis and data support for the safety assessment of structural thermal fatigue.

English abstract

Lead-bismuth eutectic (LBE)-cooled fast reactors are key candidates for Generation IV nuclear energy systems. However, the release and migration behaviors of radioactive fission gases after fuel cladding failure in an LBE environment remain critical issues for reactor safety assessment and source-term analysis and still lack systematic experimental verification. The integrity of fuel cladding is crucial for ensuring the safety and economic operation of nuclear reactors. This paper focuses on the defect-induced release of simulated radioactive fission gases (krypton and xenon) in LBE, designs and builds a dedicated experimental loop, and conducts scaled simulation experiments. The experimental system reproduces key thermal–hydraulic conditions, including turbulent flow (Re ∼ 104), and uses GC–MS for high-precision quantitative measurement of gas concentration with an uncertainty of less than 1%. The effects of defect size on the release rate and cumulative release amount of Kr and Xe are systematically investigated. Both the release rate and cumulative release fraction of Kr and Xe exhibit a clear monotonic dependence on defect size (35–500 μm), with shorter stabilization times at larger defect sizes. Notably, under identical geometric and hydrodynamic constraints, Kr consistently displays a higher transport rate than Xe. This difference is quantified and interpreted in terms of microscopic diffusion mechanisms, with the lower molecular weight and lower solubility of Kr in liquid LBE identified as the primary kinetic drivers. These findings provide key experimental data for the construction and validation of fission-gas release models and support safety analysis and source-term evaluation for LBE-cooled fast reactors.

本文语境 改进源迭代、SN、间断有限元。

查看中文摘要

中文摘要

为求解稳态一阶中子输运方程并研究中子运动规律,本研究对源迭代法进行了改进.在变量离散过程中,能量离散采用分群法,角度离散采用离散纵标法,空间离散则结合了间断有限元法与迎风数值通量方案.在求解过程中,改进后的源迭代法将所有角度和能量的中子通量组装到一个矩阵中,通过保留方程组的耦合项,刻画了不同能群与不同角度下中子通量的相互作用关系.最后本研究分别使用日本京都大学临界装置(KUCA)的实验数据和三维中子输运基准问题报告(NEACRP-L-330)中的第二计算模型作为基准题,对计算结果的有效增殖因子和平均中子通量进行验证.结果表明与源迭代法相比,改进源迭代法误差更小,耗时更短,本研究为发展中子输运方程的高效求解算法提供了有效思路.
