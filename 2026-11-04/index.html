中文摘要

本研究采用OpenFOAM中的几何流体体积法，分析静止液态金属中直径2.88–5.76 mm的单气泡上升。考虑四种体系：汞中的氮气（N₂/Hg）、铅铋共晶中的蒸汽（Steam/LBE）、铅锂共晶中的氦气（He/LLE）和铅铋共晶中的氮气（N₂/LBE）。以平均上升速度对照Mori等（1977）的N₂/Hg实验，平均绝对相对偏差为4.0%。小气泡呈振荡的之字形轨迹，大气泡轨迹逐渐接近竖直。所有工况处于椭球—摆动区，终态形状主要为振荡的扁椭球。各体系的终端速度对直径依赖较弱。蒸汽/LBE预测值比透明液体相似实验推算值最多低约30%。将仅依赖Eötvös数的相似准则外推到LBE极低的Morton数，可能是重要原因，但不能排除残余CFD偏差、相似性数据和热物性不确定性的影响。分别评价16个已有终端速度和阻力系数关联式。Maneri和Vassallo（2000）的终端速度平均绝对相对偏差最低，为8.9%；Yan等（2018）的阻力系数该偏差最低，为2.9%。这两个数值不能直接比较，因为参考阻力系数由终端速度推导，且Yan等的关联式部分用VOF数据标定。所得数据及验证方法可作为先进裂变和聚变系统单气泡动力学与关联式选择的参考。

English abstract

This study analyzes single-bubble rise in stagnant liquid metals for bubble diameters of 2.88–5.76 mm using the geometric volume-of-fluid method in OpenFOAM. Four systems are considered: nitrogen in mercury (N2/Hg), steam in lead–bismuth eutectic (Steam/LBE), helium in lead–lithium eutectic (He/LLE), and nitrogen in LBE (N2/LBE). Validation against the N2/Hg experiments of Mori et al. (1977), based on mean rise velocity, yields a mean absolute relative deviation of 4.0%. Smaller bubbles follow oscillatory zigzag paths, whereas larger bubbles rise along increasingly near-vertical paths. All cases lie in the ellipsoidal–wobbling region, with terminal shapes remaining predominantly oscillating oblate ellipsoids. Terminal velocities depend only weakly on diameter within each system. Steam/LBE predictions are up to approximately 30% lower than estimates inferred from transparent-liquid similarity experiments. Extrapolation of an Eötvös-number-only similarity law to the extremely low Morton numbers of LBE is likely an important contributor, although residual CFD bias and uncertainty in the similarity data and thermophysical properties cannot be excluded. Sixteen published correlations are assessed separately for terminal velocity and drag coefficient. Maneri and Vassallo (2000) yields the lowest terminal-velocity MARD (8.9%), whereas Yan et al. (2018) yields the lowest drag-coefficient MARD (2.9%). These values should not be compared directly because the reference drag coefficients are derived from terminal velocities, and the Yan et al. correlation was calibrated partly against VOF data. The resulting dataset and validated methodology provide a reference for single-bubble dynamics and correlation selection in advanced fission and fusion systems.

中文摘要

两相自然循环在浮式核电站的非能动安全中发挥着至关重要的作用。然而，由于缺乏专门的实验研究，人们对其在海洋运动下的运行特性仍缺乏充分的了解。本研究利用摇摆平台自然循环回路开展了两相自然循环实验，系统地研究了其在摇摆和倾斜条件下的热工水力特性。在摇摆运动下，观察到质量流量和温度呈现明显的双峰振荡模式。这种现象归因于运动引起的惯性效应和沸腾引起的蒸汽生成之间的耦合作用，为理解两相自然循环系统的动态特性提供了新的视角。减小摇摆周期会显著增强振荡，而增大周期则会抑制波动，并使波形逐渐由正弦波转变为梯形波。此外，倾斜会降低有效浮力驱动力，导致流量降低和流体温度升高。为了扩展分析，我们开发了一种改进的热工水力系统代码，该代码结合了适用于海洋环境的传热关联式，并利用实验数据对其进行了验证，偏差通常在15%以内。该代码成功地再现了整体振荡特性，并能够进行超越实验限制的进一步参数研究。数值结果表明，较短的摇摆周期和较大的振幅会显著增强流动不稳定性，并降低加热段出口处的液相体积分数，这可能会降低传热性能。本研究为海洋条件下两相自然循环提供了新的实验证据和机理理解，并为浮式核反应堆非能动排热系统的设计和安全评估提供了有益的指导。

English abstract

Two-phase natural circulation plays a critical role in the passive safety of floating nuclear systems. However, its behavior under ocean-induced motions remains insufficiently understood, particularly due to the lack of dedicated experimental investigations. In this study, two-phase natural circulation experiments were conducted using a rolling-platform natural circulation loop to systematically investigate the thermal-hydraulic characteristics under rolling and inclination conditions. A distinct dual-peak oscillation pattern of the mass flow rate and temperature was observed under rolling motion. This phenomenon is attributed to the coupling between motion-induced inertial effects and boiling-induced vapor generation, providing new insights into the dynamic behavior of two-phase natural circulation systems. Decreasing the rolling period significantly intensifies the oscillations, while increasing the period suppresses the fluctuations and gradually transforms the waveform from sinusoidal to trapezoidal. In addition, inclination reduces the effective buoyancy driving force, leading to a decrease in flow rate and an increase in fluid temperature. To extend the analysis, a modified thermal-hydraulic system code incorporating heat transfer correlations applicable to ocean conditions was developed and validated against the experimental data, with deviations generally within 15%. The code successfully reproduces the overall oscillation characteristics and enables further parametric investigations beyond experimental limitations. Numerical results indicate that shorter rolling periods and larger amplitudes significantly enhance flow instability and reduce the liquid volume fraction at the outlet of the heated section, which may deteriorate heat transfer performance. This study provides new experimental evidence and mechanistic understanding of two-phase natural circulation under ocean conditions, and offers useful guidance for the design and safety assessment of passive heat removal systems in floating nuclear reactors.

中文摘要

由于涉及高度非线性和多阶段沸腾传热，准确预测再淹没瞬态过程中的淬冷时间 (QT) 和包壳峰值温度 (PCT) 仍然极具挑战性。尽管近期的机器学习研究已展现出良好的性能，但大多数模型都是使用系统热工水力代码生成的合成数据集进行训练的，这些模型不可避免地继承了代码的建模假设和偏差，而非直接从实验物理数据中学习。相比之下，本研究开发了一种深度神经网络 (DNN) 框架，该框架完全基于从 FLECHT SEASET 和 RBHT 项目开展的全部可用再淹没实验中收集的稀缺实验数据进行训练。由于无法生成额外的实验数据，本研究提出了一种系统的多设施数据融合方法，将异构实验数据集整合到一个统一的机器学习数据集中，从而建立了一种在实际数据稀缺条件下进行数据驱动型热工水力研究的可重用方法。为了实现基于有限数据的稳定学习，我们开发了一种定制的深度神经网络（DNN）架构，并使用基于采样的超参数优化框架CASOH对其超参数进行了优化。优化后的模型与实验测量结果以及SPACE和MARS系统代码的预测结果进行了对比评估，关键安全相关量的最高决定系数R²达到0.98。结果表明，即使在实验数据严重匮乏的情况下，也能实现可靠的预测，并且直接从实验观测中学习可以减少由模拟训练的机器学习模型固有的模型偏差。这些发现凸显了实验训练的数据驱动模型作为可靠的补充工具在预测再淹没瞬态过程中安全关键参数方面的潜力。

English abstract

Accurate prediction of quenching time (QT) and peak cladding temperature (PCT) during reflood transients remains challenging because of the highly nonlinear and multi-regime boiling heat transfer involved. While recent machine learning studies have shown promising performance, most models have been trained using synthetic datasets generated by system thermal-hydraulic codes, which inherently inherit the modeling assumptions and biases of those codes rather than learning directly from experimental physics. In contrast, this study develops a deep neural network (DNN) framework trained exclusively on scarce experimental data collected from the complete set of available reflood experiments conducted in the FLECHT SEASET and RBHT programs. Since no additional experimental data can be generated, a systematic multi-facility data fusion procedure is proposed to integrate heterogeneous experimental datasets into a unified machine learning dataset, establishing a reusable methodology for data-driven thermal-hydraulic studies under genuine data-scarce conditions. To enable stable learning from the limited data, a customized DNN architecture is developed, and its hyperparameters are optimized using CASOH, a combined sampling-based hyperparameter optimization framework. The optimized model is evaluated against experimental measurements as well as predictions from the SPACE and MARS system codes, achieving a maximum coefficient of determination (R2) of 0.98 for key safety-related quantities. The results demonstrate that reliable prediction is achievable even under severe experimental data scarcity and that learning directly from experimental observations can reduce the model bias inherited by simulation-trained machine learning models. These findings highlight the potential of experimentally trained data-driven models as reliable complementary tools for predicting safety-critical parameters during reflood transients.

中文摘要

对棒束内流体流动和传热特性的评估对于核反应堆堆芯的安全评估至关重要。子通道分析（SA）是反应堆堆芯热分析中最常用的工具。在单相流条件下，湍流混合参数、壁面摩擦系数和传热系数在控制相邻子通道间的质量、动量和能量交换方面起着主导作用。这些参数受棒束几何形状、子通道间隙尺寸以及棒束配置中的运行流动条件的显著影响。本研究分为两部分，针对加压重水堆（PHWR）、某些类型的压水堆（PWR）和快中子增殖堆（FBR）中使用的六边形棒束几何形状，建立了改进的湍流混合参数、壁面摩擦系数和努塞尔数关联式。特别地，本文考虑了壁面的影响。在本文A部分，我们利用计算流体动力学（CFD）分析方法，对19棒和169棒的六边形密闭棒束结构进行了计算，以清晰地了解通道壁面的影响。计算采用商业CFD求解器ANSYS（Fluent）。所得结果被转化为适用于子通道分析（SA）的数学关联式。所提出的关联式明确地包含了局部雷诺数、棒间距、沿流动方向的轴向位置以及通道壁面的影响。这些关联式适用于节径比（P/D）为1.126至1.344、间隙直径比（S/D）为0.126至0.344、入口雷诺数为20,000至70,000的棒束结构。所建立的相关性成功地捕捉到了束内摩擦阻力和湍流混合的轴向演变，而这一方面在传统的子通道模型中通常被忽略。

English abstract

Assessment of fluid flow and heat transfer characteristics within rod bundles is essential for the safety assessment of nuclear reactor cores. Subchannel Analysis (SA) is the most common tool utilized for the thermal analysis of reactor cores. Under single-phase flow conditions, the turbulent mixing parameter, wall friction factor and heat transfer coefficient play a dominant role in governing the exchange of mass, momentum and energy between adjacent subchannels. These parameters are strongly influenced by the bundle geometry, inter-subchannel gap size, and operating flow conditions in rod bundle configurations. In the present two-part study, improved correlations for the turbulent mixing parameter, wall friction factor and Nusselt number are developed for hexagonal rod bundle geometries that are employed in Pressurized Heavy Water Reactors (PHWRs), certain class of Pressurized Water Reactors (PWRs) and Fast Breeder Reactors (FBRs). In particular, the effect of wall is factored in. In Part-A of the work, computations have been carried out using CFD (Computational Fluid Dynamics) analysis for hexagonal closed pack configurations with 19-rod as well as 169-rod bundle geometry to clearly understand the influence of channel wall. Commercial CFD Solver ANSYS (Fluent) is used for this purpose. The results obtained have been cast into mathematical correlations that are suitable for SA. The proposed correlations explicitly incorporate the effects of the local Reynolds number, inter-rod spacing, axial position along the flow direction and the influence of channel wall. The correlations are established for rod bundle configurations with pitch-to-diameter ratios ranging from 1.126 to 1.344, S/D varying from 0.126 to 0.344 and inlet Reynolds numbers varying between 20,000 and 70,000. The developed correlations successfully capture the axial evolution of the frictional resistance and turbulent mixing within the bundle, an aspect commonly neglected in conventional subchannel models.
