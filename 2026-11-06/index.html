中文摘要

先进反应堆（AR）燃料元件在尺寸、成分和形状上与轻水反应堆（LWR）燃料显著不同，许多AR燃料元件采用高丰度低浓铀（HALEU，5–20 wt% 235U），这给将现有的无损检测（NDA）国际保障监督验证仪器应用于新的AR燃料元件带来了潜在挑战。我们利用蒙特卡罗N粒子模型，模拟了铀中子符合环II和快中子环的探测方法，评估了现有中子环技术对AR燃料元件的适用性。这些模型选取了美国能源部先进反应堆示范计划支持的10种设计中的7种，该计划为三种不同的AR燃料元件提供了通用的筛选比较。我们通过用贫铀替代燃料中的HALEU来模拟部分缺陷情况。相对于轻水堆组件，几种AR几何形状与探测器设计不兼容，导致响应较低，对富集度/可裂变物含量的灵敏度较弱，并且某些设计对部分缺陷的探测能力下降。灵敏度随燃料形态、慢化剂和元件尺寸的变化而显著变化。在某些情况下，如果没有特定设计的夹具或改进的操作模式，环状装置的响应不足以进行可靠的富集度验证或部分缺陷检测。这些发现表明，基于环状装置的验证方法并非普遍适用于AR燃料，某些类型的燃料需要采用替代或经过大幅改进的NDA方法。因此，需要尽早进行开发和鉴定，以满足保障监督的时间要求。

English abstract

Advanced reactor (AR) fuel elements differ markedly from light water reactor (LWR) fuels in size, composition, and form, and many employ high-assay low-enriched uranium (HALEU, 5–20 wt % 235U), creating potential challenges in applying existing nondestructive assay (NDA) international safeguards verification instruments to fresh AR fuel elements. We evaluated the applicability of existing neutron collar technologies to AR fuel elements using Monte Carlo N-Particle models of Uranium Neutron Coincidence Collar-II and Fast Neutron Collar interrogations for elements representative of 7 of the 10 designs supported by the US DOE Advanced Reactor Demonstration Program, which provides a common screening comparison across three different AR fuel forms. Partial defect scenarios were simulated by substituting HALEU in the fuel with depleted uranium. Relative to LWR assemblies, several AR geometries were incompatible with detector designs and produced lower responses, yielding weak sensitivity to enrichment/fissile content and degraded detectability of partial defects for some designs. Sensitivities varied strongly with fuel form, moderation, and element dimensions. In several cases the collars’ responses were insufficient for reliable enrichment verification or partial defect detection without design-specific fixtures or modified operating modes. These findings indicate that collar-based verification is not universally transferable to AR fuels, and that alternative or significantly adapted NDA approaches will be required for some fuel types. Early development and qualifications are therefore needed to meet safeguards timelines.

中文摘要

无可溶硼SMR堆芯依赖可燃吸收体和控制棒管理反应性，燃耗预测因此对中子学近似敏感。本文将两步CMS5确定论系统与连续能SERPENT2模型对照，覆盖PRATIC启动循环的组件到三维全芯燃耗。考虑全棒提出与准临界构型；后者将SIMULATE5的临界插棒位置施加到SERPENT2模型。考察能量凝聚和散射各向异性阶数。无限介质组件计算中P3散射已足够，CASMO5在586/95群达到能量收敛，相较586/19群的改善主要限于钆耗尽窗口。全芯尺度进一步细化超过586/19群的凝聚，对CASMO5 MxN及SIMULATE5截面生成无收益。SIMULATE5取决于堆芯能群数，4群与8群较接近，在循环初末比2群更接近SERPENT2。钆耗尽期的最大反应性差异在二维CASMO5 MxN约385 pcm，三维SIMULATE5约600 pcm，后者含额外节点扩散近似；更细凝聚或更多堆芯能群均未降低差异。黑反射层边界结果系统性地比无限反射层更接近SERPENT2。该蒙卡模型为PRATIC比较提供基线，也展示全芯蒙卡燃耗的计算限制。

English abstract

Soluble-boron-free SMR cores rely strongly on burnable absorbers and control rods for reactivity management, making depletion predictions particularly sensitive to neutronic modelling approximations. This work compares the two-step CMS5 deterministic code system with a continuous-energy SERPENT2 Monte Carlo model of the PRATIC startup cycle, from assembly to three-dimensional full-core depletion. Both All Rods Out and pseudo-critical configurations are considered; in the latter, SIMULATE5 critical rod insertions are imposed on the SERPENT2 model. The deterministic energy condensation and scattering anisotropy order are investigated. At the infinite-medium assembly level, P3 scattering is sufficient and CASMO5 is converged in energy at 586/95 groups, the gain over 586/19 groups being mainly confined to the gadolinium depletion window. At the core scale, refining the condensation beyond 586/19 groups brings no benefit for the CASMO5 MxN calculation or for SIMULATE5 cross-section generation. The SIMULATE5 solution is driven by the number of energy groups at the core scale: four- and eight-group results agree closely and are closer to SERPENT2 than two-group results at beginning and end of cycle. The largest reactivity discrepancies with SERPENT2 occur during gadolinium depletion, reaching about 385 pcm in 2D CASMO5 MxN and 600 pcm in 3D SIMULATE5, the latter reflecting the additional nodal diffusion approximation. They are reduced neither by finer condensation nor by more core groups. The black reflector boundary condition lies systematically closer to SERPENT2 than the infinite reflector. The SERPENT2 model provides a comparison basis for the PRATIC benchmark and illustrates the computational constraints of full-core Monte Carlo depletion.

中文摘要

巴基斯坦研究堆-1 (PARR-1) 是一座材料试验堆 (MTR)，已使用确定性反应堆物理代码对其进行了广泛的分析。尽管一些研究已通过蒙特卡罗模型进行，但由于一些简化假设（例如外部多群燃耗、均匀化结构区域和均匀轴向燃耗假设）而受到限制。本研究利用 OpenMC 代码，开发了首个高保真度、三维、与运行历史相关的 PARR-1 蒙特卡罗模型，以弥补这些不足。本研究考虑了三种堆芯装药方案：首个低浓缩铀 (LEU) 高功率堆芯、93 号堆芯和 93-A 号堆芯。模拟结构旨在模拟实际运行程序。本研究进行了全面的三方基准测试，将 OpenMC 的预测结果与已有的实测运行数据和 WIMS/CITATION 确定性计算结果进行了比较。对于堆芯装载93，采用实验评价方法得到的OpenMC预测结果与测量结果吻合良好，超额反应性、停堆裕度和控制棒综合反应性价值的偏差分别为+61 pcm、-77 pcm和-16 pcm。计算得到的堆芯装载93-A的热中子通量分布与测量的空间趋势一致，燃料元件C/E比值在0.72至1.13之间。燃耗计算结果也重现了已报道的U-235在连续堆芯装载过程中的燃耗演化。总体而言，所建立的模型保留了燃料特定的辐照历史，并重现了PARR-1在多种运行工况下的中子行为。本研究建立了一个经过验证的、连续能量蒙特卡罗输运-燃耗框架，用于PARR-1多循环分析。

English abstract

Pakistan Research Reactor-1 (PARR-1) is a Material Testing Reactor (MTR) that has been extensively analyzed using deterministic reactor physics codes. Although some studies have been performed through Monte Carlo models but they were limited by simplifications, such as external multi-group depletion, homogenized structural regions, and uniform axial burnup assumptions. This work addresses these gaps by developing the first high-fidelity, three-dimensional, operational history-dependent Monte Carlo model of PARR-1 using OpenMC code. Three core loadings considered in this work are the first Low-Enriched Uranium (LEU) based high power core, core loadings 93 and 93-A. Simulations were structured to mirror the physical operational procedures. A comprehensive three-way benchmarking was performed, evaluating OpenMC predictions against available measured operational data and WIMS/CITATION deterministic calculations. The depletion calculations also reproduce the reported U-235 burnup evolution across successive core loadings. Overall, the developed model preserves fuel-specific irradiation histories and reproduces the neutronic behavior of PARR-1 across multiple operational configurations. This study establishes a validated, continuous-energy Monte Carlo transport-depletion framework for multi-cycle PARR-1 analysis.

中文摘要

无可溶硼堆芯具有较强负慢化剂温度系数，过冷会引入较大正反应性。没有可溶硼时，控制元件组件CEA须在停堆冷却期间独自维持次临界，因此完全插棒后的停堆裕量SDM是关键指标。本文以GPU蒙卡程序PRAGMA评价BANDI-60。静态研究的均匀和非对称冷却中，循环初BOC的均匀冷零功率CZP最限制。以传统PWR反应性平衡和类似BWR的直接keff评价两种框架计算，因为停堆功能由CEA承担。直接keff为0.9819，SDM为1.85%Δρ，名义比较满足参考准则keff<0.99；反应性平衡仅因10%紧急停堆棒价值裕量这一PWR不确定性惯例而未满足准则。三个循环的CEA燃耗最多使SDM降低0.12%Δρ，但调节组底部四分之一段的B-10在一个循环内耗损32%。

English abstract

Soluble boron-free (SBF) cores exhibit a strongly negative moderator temperature coefficient, inserting a large positive reactivity during overcooling events. Without soluble boron, the control element assemblies (CEAs) alone must hold the core subcritical throughout the post-trip cooldown, making the shutdown margin (SDM) after complete rod insertion the key measure of shutdown capability. This study evaluates the SDM of the SBF BANDI-60 core with the GPU-based Monte Carlo code PRAGMA. Among the uniform and asymmetric cooldowns investigated in static calculations, the uniform cold zero power (CZP) state at the beginning of cycle (BOC) is limiting. The SDM at this state is evaluated in two frameworks: the reactivity balance of conventional PWRs and the direct evaluation of the effective multiplication factor (keff) as in boiling water reactors (BWRs), whose shutdown function the CEAs serve. The directly evaluated keff is 0.9819, an SDM of 1.85%Δρ, satisfying the reference acceptance criterion keff < 0.99 in a nominal comparison; the reactivity balance falls short of the criterion only through its 10% scram-worth allowance, a PWR uncertainty convention. CEA depletion reduces the SDM by at most 0.12%Δρ over three cycles but drives the B-10 depletion of the regulating group's bottom quarter to 32% within one cycle.
