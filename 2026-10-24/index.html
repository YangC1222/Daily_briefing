中文摘要

双流体堆是一种先进熔盐堆概念，燃料盐内热源会改变其与液铅冷却剂的耦合流动和传热。本文对代表性单通道进行稳态CFD，考察热源强度及轴向分布。内热源增强浮升力、削弱近壁动量交换并增大摩擦因子，同时降低黏度、加速主流。余弦热源比均匀热源产生更强热变化，传统外加热通道关联式不能准确预测摩擦及Nu，后者被系统低估。加入黏度比和热源修正因子后，摩擦模型最大预测误差降至0.8%以内，Nu关联最大相对误差为3.82%。液铅Re对盐侧摩擦影响很小，但通过改变壁面热边界明显影响换热。结果为双流体堆热工设计提供关联及机理线索。

English abstract

The Dual Fluid Reactor (DFR) is an advanced molten salt reactor concept. In its core, the internal heat source within the fuel salt fundamentally alters the coupled flow and heat transfer between the fuel salt and the lead coolant. In this study, steady-state CFD simulations were performed on a single representative channel of the DFR core to investigate the effects of heat source intensity and axial distribution on the flow structure and heat transfer performance. The results reveal that the internal heat source enhances buoyancy effects, weakening near-wall momentum exchange and increasing frictional factor, while also reducing viscosity and accelerating the mainstream flow. A cosine-distributed heat source induces stronger thermal fluctuations than a uniform one. Consequently, classical correlations for externally heated channels fail to predict the friction factor and Nusselt number, with the latter being systematically underestimated under internal heat source conditions. To address this, modified correlations were developed that explicitly incorporate a viscosity ratio and a heat source correction factor. The proposed friction factor model reduces the maximum prediction error to within 0.8%, and the modified Nusselt number correlation achieves a maximum relative error of 3.82%. Furthermore, the Reynolds number of the lead coolant has a negligible effect on the fuel salt’s friction factor but significantly influences its heat transfer by altering the wall thermal boundary condition. These findings provide validated correlations and mechanistic insights crucial for the thermal–hydraulic design and optimization of DFR cores.

钠冷快堆采用浸没式直接换热器排余热时，容器内热分层、堆芯出口射流和堆芯—腔室相互作用会影响温度及一次回路自然循环，回路流量又反馈局部温度。本文通过接口耦合系统程序Super-COPD与容器CFD，并在RV-CFD中加入组件子通道模型以降低成本。首先模拟PLANDTL-1钠实验装置从额定至自然循环的PRACS停堆瞬态，重现热分层、射流以及一次环路温度流量。与单独Super-COPD比较，在本PRACS工况下差异有限，因为局部三维热工影响较小。本研究是向D-DHX参与的DRACS工况扩展的第一步，后者预期有更复杂容器现象。

English abstract

In a sodium-cooled fast reactor (SFR), thermal stratification, a sodium jet at the core outlet, and core–plenum interactions simultaneously occur in the reactor vessel (RV) under natural circulation (NC) decay heat removal when using a dipped-type direct heat exchanger (D-DHX). These localized three-dimensional (3D) thermal–hydraulic phenomena affect the temperature field in the RV, which consequently affects the NC flow rate in the primary loop. In turn, the NC flow feeds back into the temperature field in the RV. To accurately evaluate these mutual effects in the transient conditions of an SFR plant, a coupled analysis method combining the plant dynamics analysis code (system code) with the computational fluid dynamics (CFD) code for the 3D thermal hydraulics in the RV is necessary. In this study, we develop a coupled analysis framework by combining the Super-COPD system code for the primary loop flow with the CFD code for the RV using a coupling interface. In particular, we employ a CFD model for the RV (RV-CFD), which enables the simulation of 3D local phenomena in the RV with reduced computational cost while maintaining the prediction accuracy by incorporating the subchannel analysis model for assemblies. As a first step toward investigating the applicability of the coupled analysis method using Super-COPD and RV-CFD, we simulate a reactor scram transient behavior (from the rated operation to NC conditions) using the primary reactor auxiliary cooling system (PRACS) in the PLANDTL-1 sodium experimental apparatus. The result indicated that the coupled analysis successfully reproduced key RV thermal–hydraulic features, including thermal stratification and jet behavior, via feedback with the thermal hydraulics in the primary loop. In addition, the coupled analysis generally reproduces the temperature and flow behavior in the primary loop in agreement with experimental results. A comparison with overall plant analysis using Super-COPD alone reveals that the difference between the two approaches remains limited under the present PRACS operation case. This is attributed to the relatively small impact of 3D thermal hydraulics under this condition. However, the present study serves as an important first step toward applying the coupled analysis method to DRACS operation using D-DHX, where more complex phenomena are expected to occur in the RV.

English abstract

To enhance the safety and long-term cooling performance of passive residual heat removal systems (PRHRS) in offshore floating nuclear power plants, an air–water parallel passive residual heat removal system (AWPRHRS) is proposed and experimentally investigated. The system combines an air-cooled heat exchanger (AHX) and a water-cooled heat exchanger (WHX) in parallel, enabling efficient redistribution of thermal loads under varying heat conditions and reducing reliance on stored cooling water. The experimental facility was designed to evaluate key operational characteristics, including pressure response, natural circulation behavior, and thermal–hydraulic performance. Results demonstrate that the AWPRHRS can maintain high heat removal capacity under early-stage high heat load and achieve stable, long-term cooling without excessive water consumption. Under heat loads ranging from 35 to 45 kW, the system reaches a quasi-steady-state pressure of 0.65–0.66 MPa, while the air-cooled subsystem consistently carries approximately 37% of the total flow. The AWPRHRS effectively integrates the advantages of water- and air-cooled subsystems, providing both rapid initial decay heat removal and reliable long-term operation in the freshwater-limited OFNP environments.

为满足紧凑尺寸、低重量、高可靠性要求,新型铀钼(UMo)合金燃料被视为空间核电源具有潜力的燃料类型之一。为推动该新型燃料工程应用,有必要开展面向UMo合金燃料的反应堆辐照实验,获得燃料辐照前后性能变化。本研究基于反应堆蒙特卡罗程序RMC与计算流体动力学(CFD)程序,开展典型UMo合金燃料辐照装置方案的安全特性研究。通过RMC精确计算获得不同材料释热率,再使用CFD程序进行流动传热模拟,分析辐照装置在额定工况与堵流事故工况下的传热状态及运行特征。研究表明,额定工况下燃料样品处于850℃的高温状态,各材料最高温度均远低于熔点,装置方案设计合理。即使在严重堵流事故条件下,极少流量的工质仍能安全带走辐照装置产生的热量,材料峰值温度均在安全温度范围内,仍然能保持安全性。

中文摘要

为解决气冷微堆因其复杂的几何结构、材料分布和强泄漏特性而导致的传统堆芯计算困难,提出了一种基于扩散均匀化的计算方法。首先,采用蒙特卡罗程序RMC进行超组件计算,以获取组件的均匀化少群截面;随后,利用超级均匀化(SPH)因子对截面进行修正;最后,结合六角形堆芯扩散程序完成堆芯计算分析。此外,为提升计算精度与效率,本文系统对比了不同能群结构对计算结果的影响。数值结果表明,采用经SPH修正的25群截面时,不同燃耗深度点、不同控制棒棒位的有效增殖系数keff相对偏差分别在100pcm(1pcm=10−5)和300pcm以内,堆芯功率分布的相对偏差在0.5%以内,单个状态点的计算耗时约为1 s左右,堆芯计算结果与基准值吻合良好,且计算效率较高。综上,本研究为气冷微堆的核设计分析提供了一种可行且有效的技术思路。
