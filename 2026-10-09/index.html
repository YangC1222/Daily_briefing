中文摘要

碱金属热管是微型反应堆高温排热的关键部件，堆芯中直接嵌入多根热管。它们的集体热行为，包括非正常工况下的相互作用，尚未得到充分表征。本研究考察十根热管组成的阵列在多种运行条件下的性能，其中包括模拟失效情景。在稳态运行时，竖直布置使各热管的蒸汽温度更均匀，但与水平布置相比，启动时间略长、平均运行温度略高。研究量化了与热管阵列配置有关的系统不确定性，为后续模型验证提供支持。通过将一根或两根钠热管替换为空管，模拟完全丧失功能的状态。在这些情况下，相邻热管重新分配热负荷，导致局部热流密度增大，并使系统温度相应上升。局部过载进一步增大了相邻热管的热应力。实验结果为热管阵列中热相互作用的表征提供了数据，可指导后续实验设计，以及用于热管冷却微型反应堆系统分析的数值模型验证。

English abstract

Alkali metal heat pipes are key components for high-temperature heat removal in microreactors, where multiple heat pipes are embedded directly within the reactor core. Their collective thermal behavior, including interactions under off-normal conditions, remains insufficiently characterized. This study investigates the performance of a ten-heat-pipe array under a range of operating conditions, including simulated failure scenarios. Under steady-state operation, the vertical configuration produced more uniform vapor temperatures among the heat pipes, albeit with slightly longer startup times and marginally higher average operating temperatures compared to the horizontal orientation. System uncertainties associated with the heat pipe array configuration were quantified to support future model validation. Complete loss-of-function conditions were simulated by replacing one or two sodium heat pipes with empty tubes. In these cases, neighboring heat pipes redistributed the thermal load, resulting in increased local heat fluxes and a corresponding rise in system temperature. This localized overloading further amplified thermal stresses on adjacent heat pipes. The experimental results provide data for characterizing thermal interactions within heat-pipe arrays, offering useful guidance for the design of future experiments and the validation of numerical models used in the analysis of heat-pipe-cooled microreactor systems.

中文摘要

快速、准确地预测径向功率分布，对空间核反应堆的设计和运行至关重要。然而，传统蒙特卡罗方法的计算成本高昂，常规数据驱动代理模型在小样本和数据分布偏移条件下面临严重过拟合及泛化能力不足的问题。针对控制棒调节的空间反应堆径向功率预测，本文提出一种物理信息神经网络架构F-MNet（通量调制网络）。F-MNet采用逐特征线性调制（FiLM）机制，以堆芯平均中子通量作为条件信号，动态调整隐藏层特征变换，从而学习局部组件功率相对于通量的非线性灵敏度函数；这一关系内在地受到控制棒微分价值的约束。为评价小样本条件下的外推性能，研究构建了四种控制棒位置分布特征不同的数据划分。消融实验表明，在所有划分方案中，F-MNet均优于各基线模型，即普通多层感知机、残差结构加FiLM，以及仅残差结构。尤其在具有挑战性的Case 1外推情景中，F-MNet将普通多层感知机的R²从0.4540提高到0.9429，MAE、RMSE和MAPE均降至约三分之一。此外，研究开展了从次临界（keff约0.62）到超临界（keff约1.05）状态的跨临界性案例实验，各组件的最大相对偏差均低于1.55%，进一步验证了该模型的工程适用性。上述结果表明，F-MNet是一种高效、准确且与物理关系一致的模型框架，可为小样本和数据分布偏移情景下的代理模型提供参考设计范式。

English abstract

Rapid and accurate prediction of radial power distribution is crucial for the design and operation of space nuclear reactors. However, traditional Monte Carlo methods incur prohibitively high computational costs, while conventional data-driven surrogate models face severe overfitting and insufficient generalization capabilities under small-sample and data distribution shift conditions. Addressing the problem of radial power prediction for rod-controlled space reactors, this paper proposes F-MNet (Flux-Modulated Network), a physics-informed neural network architecture. F-MNet employs the Feature-wise Linear Modulation (FiLM) mechanism to take the core-average neutron flux as a conditioning signal and dynamically adjust hidden-layer feature transformations, thereby learning the nonlinear sensitivity function of local assembly power with respect to flux—a relationship intrinsically constrained by the control rod differential worth. To evaluate the extrapolation performance under small-sample conditions, four dataset partitions with distinct characteristics of control rod position distributions were constructed. Ablation experiments demonstrate that F-MNet consistently outperforms all baseline models (Plain MLP, Res + FiLM, Res Only) across all partition schemes. Notably, in the challenging extrapolation scenario Case 1, F-MNet increased the R2 from 0.4540 for Plain MLP to 0.9429, with the MAE, RMSE, and MAPE all reduced to approximately one-third. Furthermore, a cross-criticality case experiment was conducted, spanning subcritical (keff ≈ 0.62) to supercritical (keff ≈ 1.05) states, where the maximum relative deviation for all assemblies remained below 1.55%, further validating the engineering applicability of the model. The aforementioned results indicate that F-MNet is an efficient, accurate, and physically consistent model framework, providing a reference design paradigm for surrogate models dealing with small-sample and data distribution shift scenarios.

中文摘要

在液态燃料熔盐反应堆中，不溶性裂变产物（IFP）的非均匀分布显著影响源项表征、衰变热评估和反应堆安全。然而，高功率运行下显著的颗粒团聚使IFP迁移的单原子输运假设失效。为明确各种物理效应下IFP的相对重要性，并缓解多物理场模拟的计算瓶颈，本研究在保守的包络累积假设下，评价一座150 MWth熔盐堆中IFP的演化特征。采用混合截断准则，对质量沉积、放射性活度、衰变热和堆芯中子吸收这四种宏观物理参数进行定量筛选，将数百种动态演化同位素缩减为由24种关键核素组成的核心核素清单。该缩减保留了原系统超过95%的主要物理特征，同时显著降低计算成本。对缩减清单的分析揭示了若干重要输运现象：首先，宏观沉积由高产额、长寿命核素构成的主要骨架支配，其中以占比超过40%的Mo为主，并伴有Ru。其次，质量与活度/衰变热之间存在显著差异，少量高风险核素，如95Nb和103Ru，仅通过附着于Mo–Ru颗粒发生耦合迁移。第三，从中子学角度看，颗粒迁移驱动长寿命固体毒物103Rh和99Tc重新分布，引起不可忽略的正反应性扰动。此外，95Nb→95Mo、103Ru→103Rh等衰变链，把早期热工与安全问题，同中长期多相沉积及中子学紧密联系起来。本文建立的IFP输运特征和24核素基准清单，为后续高保真多物理场耦合模拟、在线净化系统设计以及非放射性实验回路的替代材料选择提供了物理上自洽的输入。

English abstract

In liquid-fueled Molten Salt Reactors (MSRs), the non-uniform distribution of insoluble fission products (IFPs) significantly affects source term characterization, decay heat evaluation, and reactor safety. However, the single-atom transport assumption for IFP migration becomes invalid under high-power operations due to significant particle agglomeration. To clarify the relative importance of IFPs across various physical effects and alleviate computational bottlenecks in multiphysics simulations, this study evaluates the evolution characteristics of IFPs in a 150 MWth MSR under a conservative bounding accumulation assumption. By quantitatively screening four macroscopic physical parameters—mass deposition, radioactivity, decay heat, and core neutron absorption—using a hybrid cutoff criterion, this study reduces hundreds of dynamically evolving isotopes to a core inventory of 24 key nuclides. This reduction preserves over 95% of these original system's core physical features characteristics while significantly minimizing computational cost. Analysis of this reduced inventory reveals several critical transport phenomena: First, the macroscopic deposits are driven by a primary framework composed of high-yield, long-lived nuclides (primarily Mo, which accounts for >40%, alongside Ru). Second, a significant disparity between mass and activity/decay heat is observed, wherein trace high-risk nuclides (such as 95Nb, 103Ru) undergo coupled transport exclusively through attachment to these MoRu particles. Third, from a neutronics perspective, the spatial redistribution of long-lived solid poisons (103Rh, 99Tc) driven by particle migration induces non-negligible positive reactivity perturbations. Furthermore, decay chains (e.g., 95Nb95Mo, 103Ru103Rh) tightly couple early-stage thermal and safety issues with medium-to-long-term multiphase deposition and neutronics. In conclusion, the IFP transport characteristics and the 24-nuclide benchmark inventory established in this paper provide physically self-consistent inputs for future high-fidelity multiphysics coupled simulations, online purification system design, and surrogate material selection for non-radioactive experimental loops.

在第三代核电技术中，堆芯熔融物堆内滞留（IVR）是应对堆芯熔化严重事故的重要保障。目前第三代核电厂通常采用反应堆压力容器外部冷却技术实现IVR。为进一步增强严重事故应对能力，华龙一号创新采用主动与被动方式相结合的堆腔注水冷却系统（CIS）。本文用等宽矩形加热块模拟压力容器下封头，用不锈钢板模拟金属保温层，构成封闭流道，以模拟下封头与金属保温层之间的环形流道。在池沸腾和强制驱动冷却条件下，实验研究入口温度、冷却流量及压力对临界热流密度（CHF）的影响。结果表明，冷却剂入口温度对CHF的影响主要集中在入口区域。在强制驱动冷却条件下，入口温度降低、冷却流量增大或运行压力提高，均使各角度位置的CHF增大。与中等流量相比，高流量条件下入口温度对CHF的影响更明显。在低入口温度时，CHF对冷却流量变化具有显著敏感性。

English abstract

In the third-generation nuclear power technology, In-Vessel Retention (IVR) of molten core material is a key guarantee to deal with the severe accident of core melting. The external reactor vessel cooling technology is generally adopted to achieve IVR in the present third-generation nuclear power plants. To further enhance the ability to cope with severe accidents, HPR1000 innovatively adopts a Reactor Cavity Injection and Cooling System (CIS) that combines active and passive methods. In this paper, the lower head of the pressure vessel was simulated by an equal-width rectangular heating block, and the metal insulation layer was simulated by a stainless-steel plate. A sealed flow channel was constructed to simulate the annular flow channel between the lower head of the pressure vessel and the metal insulation layer. Under pool boiling and forced driving cooling, the effects of inlet temperature, cooling flow rate and pressure on the critical heat flux (CHF) were experimentally investigated. The results indicate that the effect of coolant inlet temperature on CHF is mainly concentrated in the inlet region. Under the forced driving cooling, as the inlet temperature decreases or the cooling flow rate increases or the operating pressure increases, CHF increases at all angles.
