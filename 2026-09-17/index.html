04 其他地面先进堆钠冷快堆绕丝棒束CFD浸入边界

用于 NekRS 谱元纳维–斯托克斯求解器的浸入边界方法

Immersed boundary methods for the NekRS spectral element Navier–Stokes solver

Annals of Nuclear Energy · 2026-09-10 · Aya Hegazy , Dillon Shaver , Rizwan-uddin , April Novak

核心观点

  • 在 NekRS 中实现两种浸入边界方案，并从球体扩展到绕丝钠冷快堆棒束。
  • 与贴体网格法的运行时间相近，明显节省前处理建网工作量。

创新点

  • 将 VMDG 界面项经拉格朗日表面点投影为欧拉体积源项，比较谱元阶数、背景网格和流动条件。

不足

  • 摘要未给绕丝束局部壁温、摩擦或压降验证误差；流场算例不能直接等同于热—结构载荷准确性。

未来方向

  • 用局部换热与压降实验校准绕丝束流场，再向 ANSYS 棒束热弯曲和接触模型传递壁面载荷。
⚑ 核对绕丝节距、棒径、网格阶数、Re、壁面力与收敛数据，确认可移植的流固边界。

专有名词解释

浸入边界法immersed boundary method

用背景网格和附加界面力表示复杂固体边界的 CFD 方法。

本文语境 减少绕丝束贴体网格制作成本。

谱元法spectral element method

在单元内采用高阶多项式近似流场的数值方法。

本文语境 NekRS 的空间离散基础。

展开中英文摘要

中文摘要

浸入边界（IB）方法把非贴体表面嵌入背景网格，并施加适当的无滑移边界，因此可大幅简化复杂几何的网格生成。本文在 NekRS 计算流体力学程序中实现 IB，以模拟复杂几何内的湍流。作者研究两种方法：直接力法，以及改进的基于 VMDG 的力投影法；后一方法把原变分多尺度间断伽辽金形式的部分界面项在拉格朗日表面点上计算，再以体积源项投影至欧拉网格。不可压流算例验证了方法能力，并从背景网格大小、谱元多项式阶数和流动特性等方面与传统贴体网格法比较。应用从单球扩展到绕丝钠冷快堆棒束等核工程复杂表面。结果表明，IB 在保留高保真与可扩展性的同时显著降低建网工作量。两种方法的运行时间相近，因为 IB 可得到质量更好的单元，减少压力与速度迭代；其最显著收益是前期建网时间减少。

English abstract

The Immersed Boundary (IB) method allows for simulations of fluid flows around complex geometries with either greatly simplified or entirely bypassed body-fitted meshes, thereby significantly reducing mesh generation effort. By embedding non-conformal surface geometries within a background mesh, and applying the appropriate no-slip boundary conditions, the IB method streamlines analysis workflows. This work presents the implementation of IB methods to model turbulent flow in complex geometries in the NekRS computational fluid dynamics code. Two IB methods are explored in this work: a direct forcing method and a modified VMDG-based force projection (VMDG-FP) formulation in which selected interfacial terms from the original Variational Multiscale Discontinuous Galerkin formulation are evaluated at Lagrangian surface points and projected onto the Eulerian grid points as volumetric source terms. Results of incompressible flow over different geometries are presented, demonstrating the capability of the IB methods. This work also compares the performance of IB with traditional body-fitted methods for several parameters, including background mesh size, spectral element polynomial order, and flow characteristics. The research extends the application from one sphere to more complex surfaces found in nuclear engineering, such as the wire-wrapped Sodium Fast Reactor (SFR) bundle, highlighting the capabilities of this approach for high-fidelity reactor simulations. The results demonstrate that the proposed IB capability in NekRS enables accurate and scalable simulation of complex geometries, greatly reducing the meshing effort while retaining high-fidelity simulations. A comparison of the body-fitted and IB methods shows similar runtime due to the ability to obtain higher element quality in the IB approach which decreases the number of pressure and velocity iterations. The most significant improvement offered by the IB technique is the large reduction in upfront meshing effort.

07 多物理场耦合（热工结构）冷凝VOF相变热边界层

面向 VOF 方法的多层汽液相变模型及蒸汽冷凝模拟

Development of a multilayer vapor-liquid phase change model for the VOF method and simulation of vapor condensation process

Nuclear Engineering and Design · 2026-09-06 · Xiaohong Yan, Hekai Ye, Ting Ma, Qi Xiao

核心观点

  • 通过能量守恒定义多层虚拟界面相变源项，一维/二维冷凝与解析解一致。
  • 汽泡寿命对容器尺度、入口速度和尤其 200 μm 初始热边界层高度敏感。

创新点

  • 避免经验相变参数，并显示仅凭汽泡外形或寿命不足以严格校准局部相变传热。

不足

  • 摘要承认缺少热边界层测量；未证明该模型在核反应堆实际压力、非凝气体和结构壁面条件下准确。

未来方向

⚑ 核对质量/能量源项推导、界面层数、200 μm 边界条件、网格与时间步，以及核用换热器适用性。

专有名词解释

VOFvolume of fluid method

用体积分数追踪互不相溶流体界面的数值方法。

本文语境 为相变模型提供局部汽液分布。

吸入流condensation-induced suction flow

汽相收缩引起的周围流体向冷凝界面补入。

本文语境 控制邻近汽泡寿命。

展开中英文摘要

中文摘要

现有汽液相变模型常依赖经验参数，或与流体体积分数法（VOF）耦合时不一致。本文提出新模型：根据体积分数构造虚拟相，利用网格面建立多层虚拟界面，并由计算单元内能量守恒推导质量与能量源项。一维、二维蒸汽冷凝结果与解析解吻合。作者还模拟密闭容器与开放通道中汽泡冷凝，发现尖锐界面与弥散界面两种表示可得到等效预测；冷凝产生的吸入流显著影响汽泡行为。容器尺寸变化使汽泡寿命偏差达 37%；多个汽泡同时冷凝时吸入流增强，靠近出口的汽泡冷凝加快。开放通道入口速度增加 0.05 m/s 可使汽泡寿命减少 31%。初始热边界层厚度尤为重要：200 μm 厚边界层可将汽泡寿命延长 50 倍。由于该边界层的实测数据稀缺，仅凭实验观察的汽泡形貌与寿命不能严格定量验证相变模型。

English abstract

Current vapor-liquid phase change models often rely on empirical parameters or exhibit inconsistencies when coupled with the Volume of Fluid (VOF) method. To address these limitations, a novel phase change model is proposed. Virtual phases are constructed based on volume fraction, and multi-layer virtual interfaces are established using mesh faces. Mass and energy source terms are derived from energy conservation within computational cells. Simulation results for a one-dimensional and two-dimensional vapor condensation problem show excellent agreement with analytical solutions, thereby validating the accuracy of the proposed model. To gain deeper insights into the condensation mechanisms of vapor bubbles, numerical simulations are conducted for bubble condensation in both enclosed tanks and open channels. The results demonstrate that both sharp-interface and dispersed-interface modeling approaches can yield equivalent predictions. The suction flow induced by bubble condensation significantly influences bubble dynamics. Specifically, variations in tank dimensions lead to a 37% deviation in bubble lifetime. In cases involving multiple simultaneously condensing bubbles, this suction flow is intensified, resulting in accelerated condensation of bubbles located near the tank outlet. Furthermore, an increase in inlet velocity by 0.05 m s−1 during bubble condensation in an open channel reduces the bubble lifetime by 31%. Notably, the initial thermal boundary layer surrounding the bubble imposes a significant effect on the bubble lifetime. Specifically, a thermal boundary layer thickness of 200 μm is capable of prolonging the bubble lifetime by a factor of 50. Owing to the scarcity of measured experimental data regarding the thermal boundary layer, observations of bubble morphology and lifetime acquired via experiments cannot serve as strict quantitative validation benchmarks for phase change numerical models.

展开中英文摘要

中文摘要

本文用实验研究双导热表面的环氧图案对池沸腾的影响。由于环氧与铜的导热率差异很大，相比铜，经低导热环氧传递的热量可忽略。改变环氧图案能够形成指定的表面温度空间分布，并诱导有序的汽泡脱离与液体补给路径。研究在铜面积比例固定为 60% 的条件下，设计两类网格状环氧图案：铜区中心有或没有方形环氧，并改变网格条纹的数目与宽度，以单独分析图案几何效应。几乎所有网格状样品的临界热流密度均高于裸铜；随着条纹数增加，临界热流密度先升后降，最高增幅为 77.2%。在条纹数相同且宽度合适时，带中心方块的样品因液体铺展增强，换热系数和临界热流密度均高于仅有网格环氧的样品。过窄的环氧在高热流下会失效，使临界热流提前出现。结果为高效、安全换热表面设计提供思路。

English abstract

This paper experimentally studies the influence of epoxy patterns of bi-conductive surfaces on pool boiling. Due to the significant difference in thermal conductivity, the heat transferred through the low-conductive epoxy can be ignored compared to that through high-conductive copper. This provides a simple method to create specifically appointed spatial surface temperature variations and induce an orderly arranged circuit for bubble detachment and liquid replenishment by changing epoxy patterns. This study isolates pattern geometry effects while keeping copper area constant by designing two types of epoxy patterns with a fixed copper area ratio of 60%, including reticular epoxy samples with and without square epoxy set in the middle of individual copper areas. At the same time, the number and width of strips in the reticular epoxy are changed in each category. According to the result, almost all reticular epoxy samples enhance the CHF compared with bare copper. With the increase in the number of strips in the reticular epoxy, the CHF displays the tendency to first rise and then decline, reaching a peak enhancement of 77.2%. Under the same number of strips with appropriate width, samples with epoxy squares have higher HTC and CHF compared with samples with only reticular epoxy because of the enhanced liquid spreading. However, the results also suggest that overly narrow epoxy ceases to be effective under high heat flux and leads to earlier CHF. The conclusion obtained can provide inspiration for the design of heat transfer surfaces with efficient heat transfer capability and safety.

中文摘要

超临界二氧化碳布雷顿循环是潜在发电系统方案。超临界二氧化碳腐蚀会在印刷电路换热器（PCHE）流道内形成氧化层，不仅增加导热热阻，还影响对流换热与流阻，而长期服役性能预测仍不足。本文通过腐蚀试验提出新的氧化层演化模型，建立努塞尔数和摩擦因子关联式，并研究长期传热演化。结果表明，30 年后氧化层热阻增量约为初始热阻的 7 倍。由于氧化层导热热阻增加与对流热阻降低共同作用，总热阻随时间先降后升；当温度超过 862.5 K 时会出现传热退化。30 年内，表面粗糙度增长使努塞尔数和摩擦因子均增加。

English abstract

The supercritical carbon dioxide Brayton cycle has become an important choice for the power system. Supercritical carbon dioxide corrosion leads to the oxide layer formation in the flow channels of PCHE, which would not only increase the thermal resistance of heat conduction, but also affect the convective heat transfer characteristics and flow resistance characteristics. However, the current study lacks prediction of long-term service performance of PCHE with oxide layer growth. In this study, new oxide layer evolution models were proposed through corrosion experiments. New correlations of Nusselt number and the friction factor were developed. Using the proposed models, the long-term heat transfer evolution is investigated. The results show that the influence of oxide layer growth on thermal resistance is significant as the increment is about 7 times the original thermal resistance after 30 years. Due to the combined effect of oxide layer thermal resistance increase and convective thermal resistance decrease, the overall thermal resistance first decreases and then increases over time. Heat transfer deterioration will occur if the temperature exceeds 862.5 K. In 30 years, both the Nusselt number and friction factor increase as the roughness grows.
