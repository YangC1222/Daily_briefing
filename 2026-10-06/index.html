中文摘要

铅铋共晶冷却剂中的腐蚀产物颗粒可能滞留在绕丝燃料组件上，不同壁面滞留颗粒工况可能改变局部流体激励。本文针对七棒LBE冷却绕丝组件建立CFD—离散相—流致振动框架，结合SST k–ω模型和单向流固耦合，研究颗粒质量流率、入口速度和粒径影响。参考颗粒工况下，壁面捕获颗粒的73.87%位于燃料棒包壳，12.68%位于绕丝，13.46%位于六角组件盒壁。粒径和入口速度主要改变滞留颗粒表面分布，质量流率主要改变颗粒供给。尽管整体热工水力参数仅略变，振动响应对滞留工况敏感。相较无颗粒工况，高颗粒质量流率和大颗粒工况使最大总变形从85.208 μm增至约99.0 μm，最大von Mises应力从0.2976 MPa增至约0.360 MPa。中心棒中跨的位移均方根在X方向从12.573增至约22.96 μm，Z方向从33.774增至约63.13 μm，主响应频率保持约22 Hz。结果说明，在严重宏观堵塞出现之前，易沉积颗粒工况即可放大模型预测的流致振动响应。

English abstract

Corrosion-product particles generated in lead–bismuth eutectic (LBE) coolant may be retained on wire-wrapped fuel assemblies, and different particle-laden conditions associated with wall retention may modify local fluid excitation. A computational fluid dynamics–discrete phase model–flow-induced vibration (CFD-DPM-FIV) framework combining the shear stress transport (SST) k–ω model and one-way fluid–structure coupling is developed for a seven-pin LBE-cooled wire-wrapped fuel assembly to investigate the effects of particle mass flow rate, inlet velocity, and diameter. Under the reference particle condition, 73.87% of wall-trapped particles are located on the fuel-rod cladding surface, while 12.68% and 13.46% are located on the wire-wrap surface and hexagonal duct wall. Particle diameter and inlet velocity mainly alter the surface distribution of retained particles, whereas particle mass flow rate mainly changes the particle supply to the bundle. Although the global thermal–hydraulic parameters vary only slightly, the vibration response is sensitive to particle-retention conditions. Compared with the particle-free case, the high particle mass-flow-rate and large-particle cases increase the maximum total deformation from 85.208 μm to about 99.0 μm and the maximum von Mises stress from 0.2976 MPa to about 0.360 MPa. At the center-rod midspan, the fluctuating root-mean-square (RMS) displacement increases from 12.573 to about 22.96 μm in the X direction and from 33.774 to about 63.13 μm in the Z direction, while the dominant response frequency remains near 22 Hz. These results indicate that deposition-prone particle conditions can amplify the predicted FIV response before severe macroscopic blockage develops.

小型钠冷快堆堆芯通常设计紧凑，以满足监管和经济目标。波瓣壁组件盒内紧密布置环形燃料棒可以强化紧凑堆芯散热。本文研究三角形紧密栅格、节径比P/D=1.08的波瓣壁环形燃料棒组件中液钠稳态和周期性流动。相较平壁组件，波瓣壁使中心子通道与间隙间温差最多降低30%。组件内外子通道流量分配为10%–13%。雷诺数与流动摩擦阻力的关系与先前轻水堆燃料组件结果一致。周期流动强化了子通道内湍流分布。本文还针对这种三角紧密栅格波瓣壁环形棒组件提出新的内外子通道流量分配关联式。

English abstract

The core of small sodium-cooled fast reactors is designed to be compact, meeting various regulatory and economic objectives. Heat removal in a compact core can be enhanced by using a tightly arranged annular rod fuel assembly within a scalloped-wall duct. This study investigates the steady and periodic flow of liquid sodium coolant through the triangular tight lattice (P/D = 1.08) annular rod scalloped-wall fuel assembly. The scalloped wall reduced the temperature difference between the center subchannel and gaps by up to 30% compared to a flat-walled fuel assembly. The flow split between the inner and outer subchannels is 10% – 13% in the fuel assembly. The relationship between the Reynolds number and flow friction resistance aligns with previous results for a light water reactor fuel assembly. Periodic flow intensifies the turbulence distribution within the subchannels. A new correlation was proposed for the flow split between the inner and outer subchannels in the tight triangular-lattice scalloped-wall annular rod fuel assembly.

展开中英文摘要

中文摘要

铅铋共晶等液态金属中，低普朗特数下强迫与自然对流强耦合，使流动换热复杂，准确预测对先进堆热工水力安全和设计重要。本文提出混合对流换热物理信息神经网络MCHT-PINN，逐步研究二维后台阶、二维圆管和三维19棒燃料组件。为应对多物理场强耦合，架构引入GradNorm自适应权重算法，动态平衡偏微分方程和边界条件损失梯度。结果表明，模型对速度和温度场预测与OpenFOAM参考解很好一致，各算例平均相对L2误差均低于3%。模型还描述浮力导致的速度剖面畸变、局部热边界层抬升及后续流场演变。作者认为，这些结果核验了模型跨几何的稳健性，为液态金属堆构件的复杂热工水力评估提供可靠计算工具。

English abstract

Mixed convection in liquid metals, such as lead-bismuth eutectic (LBE), presents intricate flow and heat transfer characteristics due to the strong coupling of forced and natural convection in low Prandtl number fluids. Accurately predicting these phenomena is paramount for the thermal-hydraulic safety and design of advanced nuclear reactors. In this study, a novel Mixed Convection Heat Transfer Physics-Informed Neural Network (MCHT-PINN) is proposed to conduct progressive case studies across a two-dimensional backward-facing step, a two-dimensional circular pipe, and a three-dimensional 19-pin fuel assembly. To address the highly coupled multi-physics fields, the MCHT-PINN architecture integrates the GradNorm adaptive-weight algorithm, which dynamically balances the loss gradients associated with the Partial Differential Equations (PDEs) and boundary conditions. The results demonstrate that the MCHT-PINN model achieves accurate predictions of both velocity and temperature fields, demonstrating excellent agreement with OpenFOAM reference solutions. The average relative L2 errors are consistently maintained below 3% across all cases. Furthermore, the model accurately resolves complex coupled thermo-fluid phenomena, capturing not only buoyancy-driven velocity profile distortions but also the localized lifting of the thermal boundary layer and subsequent flow field evolution. Ultimately, these findings not only verify the robustness of the MCHT-PINN across varying geometries but also provide a reliable computational tool for evaluating complex thermal-hydraulics in liquid metal reactor components.

核设施退役中活化构件的拆卸和迁移显著改变辐射场，使快速计算更复杂。本文开发基于CPU/GPU异构并行框架、用于动态作业阶段的快速剂量场程序VDPKHP。CPU执行辐射源切割和几何更新，GPU通过射线追踪与点核积分计算剂量，并针对复杂动态情景优化射线追踪适应性。核验表明，针对不同屏蔽和动态情景，VDPKHP相较OpenMC计算可合理预测辐射场，并有效捕捉构件切割时的场演变。动态切割性能评估显示，相较CPU串行执行，加速比为193.9–254.7；相较并行效率约95%的8核OpenMP实现，加速比为25.7–33.3。这展示异构框架对动态剂量计算的加速作用，为核设施退役快速辐射评估和人员防护提供技术支持。

English abstract

The dismantling and relocation of activated components during nuclear facility decommissioning lead to significant changes in the radiation field, which complicates rapid radiation field calculation. To address this challenge, we developed VDPKHP, a fast radiation field calculation code designed for dynamic operational stages, based on a CPU/GPU heterogeneous parallel framework. The method executes radiation source cutting and geometry updates on the CPU, while dose calculation is performed on the GPU using ray tracing and point kernel integration. Adaptability optimizations were applied to the ray tracing process to support complex dynamic scenarios. Validation results demonstrate that VDPKHP can reasonably predict radiation fields compared with OpenMC calculations for different shielding and dynamic scenarios and can effectively capture radiation field evolution during component cutting. Performance evaluation in dynamic cutting scenarios shows that the proposed heterogeneous framework achieves speedups of 193.9 to 254.7 compared with serial CPU execution and 25.7 to 33.3 compared with an 8-core OpenMP implementation achieving approximately 95% parallel efficiency. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed CPU/GPU heterogeneous framework for accelerating dynamic radiation field calculations and provide technical support for rapid radiation assessment and personnel protection during nuclear facility decommissioning.

Twisted elliptical tubes are a proposed heat transfer enhancement (HTE) for use in Molten Salt Reactors (MSRs) due to their enhanced thermal performance compared to plain tubes. preventive and mitigating device.The structure of the core catcher directly impacts the shape and distribution of the debris bed,which subsequently affects its re-criticality and long-term decay heat removal capability.This study aims to perform a numerical investigation to optimize the structural design of the core catcher,with a specific focus on the mechanisms and laws of the chimney structure the debris bed,the debris particles demonstrate a secondary scattering effect,which plays a vital role in enhancing the uniformity of the debris bed.This study will provide valuable engineering references for optimizing the design of the core catcher.
