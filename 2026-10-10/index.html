现有快堆燃料组件性能分析面临高保真度与计算效率之间的平衡难题。本研究基于ABAQUS建立三维辐照变形分析模型，以应对这一矛盾。模型以壳单元模拟包壳和六角形组件盒，以梁单元模拟绕丝，并利用绑定约束、轴向连接器单元和通用接触简化复杂机械相互作用。通过用户子程序引入材料辐照行为和非均匀运行工况，同时开发参数化自动建模方法，以构建简化结构及机械相互作用。模型通过127棒燃料束堆外压缩试验和169棒燃料组件辐照变形基准算例核验，考察间隙变化、椭圆化等参数。结果较好一致，误差在20%以内。与BAMBOO中的简化模型相比，本模型对机械相互作用的模拟更准确，并能捕捉包壳周向效应；与全尺寸精细模型相比，在7棒燃料组件上得到相近结果，求解速度提高超过100倍。作者认为，该模型可靠、高效、易于扩展，可用于快堆燃料组件辐照变形分析和设计优化。

English abstract

The existing performance analysis of the fast reactor fuel assembly faces the challenge of balancing high-fidelity and computational efficiency. This study develops a 3-D irradiation deformation analysis model based on ABAQUS to address this conflict. The model employs shell elements to simulate claddings and the hexagonal duct, beam elements for wrap wires, and simplifies complex mechanical interactions using Tie Constraint, Axial Connector element, and General Contact. User subroutines are implemented to incorporate material irradiation behaviors and the non-uniform operating conditions. A parametric automatic modeling method is also developed to construct simplified structures and mechanical interactions. The analysis model has been verified against out-of-pile compression test for a 127-pin fuel bundle and an irradiation deformation benchmark case for a 169-pin fuel assembly, with verification parameters such as gap variation and ovalization. Results demonstrate good agreement with errors within 20%. Furthermore, compared with the simplified model in BAMBOO, the present model demonstrates higher accuracy in the simulation of mechanical interactions and possesses the capability to capture cladding circumferential effects; compared with the full-scale detailed model, the present model achieves comparable results while improving solution speed by over 100-fold for a 7-pin fuel assembly. This model is reliable, efficient and easily extensible for irradiation deformation analysis and design optimization of fast reactor fuel assemblies.

流动系统中的熔盐冻结是熔盐堆的重要安全问题之一，因为排盐管路或非能动余热排出系统中的凝固可能堵塞应急冷却管路，使堆芯温度发生失控升高。为预测这一行为，本文开发可用于热工水力系统程序的改进凝固模型。在既有燃料盐冻结模型基础上，增加管壁能量方程，恢复原模型忽略的管壁热惯性。控制方程采用控制体积形式，并在MATLAB程序中使用半隐式数值方法求解和评估。首先对概念性FLiBe和水工况计算：不考虑管壁时复现原模型，计入管壁热惯性后预测冻结更慢。随后通过动态和静态水冻结试验验证，相比参考模型与测量数据更一致。结果表明，所提模型能够描述熔盐冻结物理，并为在MARS程序中开展熔盐堆安全分析提供有用基础。

English abstract

The freezing of molten salt in flow systems is one of the major safety concerns for molten salt reactors (MSRs), since solidification in drain lines or passive heat removal systems can obstruct emergency cooling lines and trigger uncontrolled temperature excursions in the reactor core. To predict this behavior, an improved solidification model is developed, which can be adopted in thermal-hydraulic system codes. Building on an existing fuel salt freezing formulation, the present model adds an energy equation for the pipe wall to restore the wall thermal inertia that the earlier model neglects. The governing equations are cast in control-volume form with semi-implicit numerical solution and solved in a MATLAB program for assessment. The model is first applied to the conceptual FLiBe and water cases, where it reproduces the existing model without the wall and predicts slower freezing once the wall thermal inertia is included. The model is then validated against dynamic and static water-freezing experiments and showing better agreement with measured data than the reference models. The results indicate that the proposed model captures the physics of molten salt freezing and provides a useful basis for MSR safety analysis within the MARS code.

熔盐堆中，缓发中子先驱核随燃料盐漂移。系统程序能较好描述稳态后果，即有效缓发中子份额减小；但漂移反应性此前只能针对单一指数模态获得，或者只能在恒功率条件下在线获得，此时它等于库存读数β0−βeff(t)。功率变化的瞬态中，该读数混合了流动效应与普通先驱核滞后，而程序内置反应性有意不包含漂移通道。本文推导一种影子先驱核观测器，使任意功率历程下的漂移分量被分离为在线等效反应性；每组先驱核仅需一个微分方程，且无需侵入式修改。同时给出指数模态解析式及堆芯—回路几何的循环燃料反应堆倒时方程。在Modelica/TRANSFORM中执行的三层、不依赖实验的基准，利用程序自身离散方程的解析参考核验这三种结果：观测器精确恢复恒功率库存读数，并在自由功率流动瞬态下与倒时方程和指数模态参考一致。在恒定流量下施加+50 pcm阶跃时，观测器显示+67.1 pcm的漂移调制；常见的静态βeff替代会将该插入工况的反应堆周期高估28%。

English abstract

In molten salt reactors the delayed neutron precursors drift with the fuel salt. The steady-state consequences — a reduced effective delayed neutron fraction — are well captured by system codes, but the reactivity of the drift has been available only on a single exponential mode or, on-line, only at constant power, where it equals the inventory reading β0−βeff(t). In power-varying transients that reading conflates the flow effect with ordinary precursor lag, and the built-in reactivity of the codes deliberately omits the drift channel. This paper derives a shadow-precursor observer that isolates the drift component as an on-line equivalent reactivity for arbitrary power histories — one differential equation per precursor group, non-intrusive — together with an exponential-mode closed form and the circulating-fuel inhour relation for the core–loop geometry. A three-tier, experiment-free benchmark set executed in Modelica/TRANSFORM verifies all three against closed-form references in the code’s own discrete equations: the observer recovers the constant-power reading exactly and matches the inhour and exponential-mode references in free-power flow transients. Under a ＋50 pcm step at constant flow, the observer exposes a drift modulation of +67.1 pcm, and the common static-βeff substitution overestimates the period of that insertion by +28%.

English abstract

In this paper, we demonstrate a remote passive optical fiber sensor system based on space division multiplexing for water level monitoring targeting to extreme environments, such as a spent fuel pool in nuclear power plant. The proposed system is evaluated under laboratory conditions to demonstrate its feasibility for future nuclear safety instrumentation applications. It employs a broadband light source as a seed light and a 1 × 4 optical splitter for multiplexing/demultiplexing multiple optical fiber sensors. Four sensors are vertically installed in a water pool at predefined heights. By measuring the reflected optical power from each sensor with an optical receiver, the discrete water level state corresponding to the predefined sensor positions can be determined in real-time. To mitigate the effect of Rayleigh backscattering in remote sensing, a simple dual-path configuration is introduced. Experimental results show that the proposed system can monitor water levels over a distance of approximately 19 km, limited by the performance of the detector. On the other hand, providing intuitive and real-time information for water level monitoring to operators remains a significant challenge. To address this issue, a human-machine interface (HMI) module is also implemented. By providing real-time information on the discrete water level states and system status through the HMI display, it enables operators to intuitively monitor the current water level status, as well as perform trend analysis and abnormal condition detection.

[Background]In small integrated reactors,the control rod drive mechanism(CRDM)is locatedwithin a high-intensity radiation field.The sealing coil of the CRDM may experience performance degradation due tointense irradiation,making accurate dose rate assessment essential for predicting maintenance cycles.[Purpose]Thisstudy aims to evaluate the irradiation dose rate at the CRDM sealing coil in a small reactor during normal operation,identifying the main contributors to the dose rate.[Methods]Radiation source terms,including core fission neutronsand photons,fission and activated corrosion products in the primary coolant,and activation product N-16,werecalculated.Computational models were developed using the Monte Carlo methods for photon transport and the point-kernel integration for dose rate evaluation.Conservative assumptions were applied to coolant density and sourcedistribution.[Results]The total dose rate at the CRDM sealing coil was found to be 4.1 Gy·h−1.N-16,produced vianeutron activation in the coolant,was the dominant contributor,accounting for nearly the entire dose.Contributionsfrom fission products,activated corrosion products,and core fission photons were negligible(less than 1%).[Conclusions]The irradiation dose rate at the CRDM sealing coil is primarily due to N-16 decay gamma rays,with themajority originating from coolant within a 1.5-meter thick region centered around the dose point.These results providea basis for predicting coil lifespan and planning replacement intervals.

展开中英文摘要

中文摘要

随着核反应堆服役年限的增加,反应堆一回路系统的设备、管道、阀门内表面存在一定的剥落,形成腐蚀产物悬浮于冷却剂中,最终变为活化腐蚀产物。随着活化腐蚀产物的不断增加会造成冷却剂中总固水平增加和反应堆厂房内空间剂量水平升高,也会对设备的运行造成一定的影响。为解决这一问题,本文采用在一回路系统上增设并联支路,支路中设置腐蚀产物过滤系统,采用物理去除的方法不断对冷却剂过滤去除其内的腐蚀产物,进而降低冷却剂中的总固水平。对于腐蚀产物过滤系统的过滤器,采用碟片式结果,采用有限元方法进行了过滤器单层固液两相分离仿真。经工程应用验证表明,本文设计的腐蚀产物过滤系统可有效降低冷却剂中的总固水平,表明对去污冷却剂中的腐蚀产物效果明显。

English abstract · 维普详情页英文摘要原文；中英文详略可能不同，分别保留。

With the increase of the service life of nuclear reactors,the inner surface of the equipment,pipes and valves of the reactor primary circuit system has some spalling,forming corrosion products suspended in the coolant and eventually becoming activated corrosion products.With the continuous increase of activated corrosion products,the total solid level in the coolant and the spatial dose level in the reactor building will increase,which will also have a certain impact on the operation of the equipment.In order to solve this problem,a parallel branch is added to the primary system,and a corrosion product filtration system is set up in the branch,and the corrosion products in the coolant are constantly filtered and removed by physical removal method,thus reducing the total solid level in the coolant.For the filter of corrosion product filtration system,the simulation of single-layer solid-liquid two-phase separation of the filter was carried out by finite element method using the disc results.The engineering application verification shows that the corrosion product filtration system designed in this paper can effectively reduce the total solid level in the coolant,indicating that the effect on the decontamination of corrosion products in the coolant is obvious.
