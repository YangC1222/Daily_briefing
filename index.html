02 热管堆热管吸液芯毛细极限实验与验证

带环形间隙的制造态热管丝网吸液芯上升速率表征

Rate-of-rise characterization of as-manufactured heat pipe mesh screen wick structures with an annular gap

Nuclear Engineering and Design · 2026-08-14 · Scout Bucks, Luke Price, Christopher Balbier, Saya Lee

核心观点

  • 使用上升速率试验表征316不锈钢100目丝网环形吸液芯，重点处理约0.63 mm环隙如何计入截面和孔隙率。
  • 将环隙纳入总面积并使用平均孔隙率更符合解析模型，但对品质因数的增益估计明显低于另一算法。

创新点

  • 直接面向制造态环形丝网吸液芯，对两种环隙数据换算定义进行对照。

不足

  • 摘要显示结论仍依赖简化解析模型；渗透率和毛细半径尚未通过独立方法交叉验证。

未来方向

  • 用独立的渗透率和毛细半径测量方法复核上升速率法。
  • 将制造偏差、环隙非均匀性和液态金属润湿性纳入热管毛细极限不确定性分析。
⚑ 摘要给出约21 mm外径、0.63 mm环隙、316不锈钢100目丝网；全文应提取样件几何、孔隙率、液体性质和拟合公式，用于热管等效模型校核。
展开中英文摘要

中文摘要

液态金属热管正在多种空间和微型反应堆应用中接受评估。这类装置的运行包络主要受毛细极限支配，而毛细极限很大程度上由热管吸液芯结构的毛细半径和渗透率决定。为同时改善这两个参数，研究者提出了在丝网与包壳之间保留间隙的卷绕丝网吸液芯，但对制造态环形吸液芯样件的表征仍然有限。本研究采用上升速率试验，测量由316不锈钢100目丝网制成的矩形和环形吸液芯样件的毛细半径与渗透率。环形样件外径约21 mm，并与包壳组合形成约0.63 mm的环形间隙。依据带环隙上升速率数据计算毛细半径和渗透率时，可使用裸丝网截面积与孔隙率，也可使用丝网加间隙的总面积与平均孔隙率。研究评估了两种方法，并与简单解析模型比较，结果表明后一种方法更能代表实际现象；但采用该方法时，吸液芯品质因数仅提高1.3至1.6倍，而其他研究采用前一种方法得到约3倍提升。作者认为，应通过独立测量渗透率和毛细半径，进一步核查这种差异。

English abstract

Liquid metal heat pipes are being evaluated for a variety of space and microreactor applications. The operating envelope of these devices is dominated by the capillary limit, which is determined largely by the capillary radius and permeability of the heat pipe wick structure. To simultaneously enhance both parameters, wrapped mesh screen wicks that leave a gap between the mesh and cladding have been proposed. However, characterization of as-manufactured annular wick samples remains limited. This work used rate-of-rise testing to measure the capillary radius and permeability of rectangular and annular wick samples made from 316 stainless steel 100-mesh screen. The annular samples, around 21 mm in outer diameter, were then measured with cladding that created an annular gap of around 0.63 mm. Two possibilities exist when calculating the capillary radius and permeability from rate-of-rise data with an annular gap: using the bare mesh cross-sectional area and porosity or using the total mesh plus gap area with an averaged porosity. Both methods were evaluated, and a comparison to a simple analytic model suggested that the latter was more representative of the actual phenomena. However, this resulted in only a 1.3 to 1.6-times increase in the wick figure-of-merit compared to a three-times increase using the former method employed by other researchers. These differences need to be further investigated by comparing the rate-of-rise method with independent measurements of permeability and capillary radius.

07 多物理场耦合（热工结构）燃料包壳热蠕变本构模型

不同温度下析出强化锻造FeCrAl包壳管的热蠕变行为

Thermal creep behavior of precipitation-strengthened wrought FeCrAl cladding tube at different temperatures

Nuclear Engineering and Technology · 2026-08-17 · Jingze Zhou, Yuntao Zhong, Ruiqian Zhang, Huan Sheng Lai, Peinan Du

核心观点

  • 在360℃和475℃试验下确认FeCrAl包壳存在蠕变阈值应力，并建立含阈值的幂律本构模型。
  • 失效主要与剪应力有关，应力增加还伴随晶粒、位错和小角度晶界的系统演化。

创新点

  • 将工程尺度包壳管轴向蠕变试验、阈值应力本构式与微观组织演化联系起来。

不足

  • 摘要仅显示两个温度点，辐照、内压、环向载荷和瞬态温度历史的适用性待全文核查。

未来方向

  • 将本构参数实现到ANSYS用户材料模型中，并用包壳内压、辐照蠕变和热循环试验验证。
⚑ 全文应提取360℃/475℃各应力点、阈值应力、幂律参数和破坏判据，可直接支撑ANSYS包壳蠕变本构标定。
展开中英文摘要

中文摘要

FeCrAl合金被认为事故容错燃料包壳管的优秀候选材料，但其包壳管的热蠕变及相关微观组织演化尚缺少系统研究。本工作分别在360℃和475℃评估析出强化锻造FeCrAl包壳管的轴向热蠕变行为。试验显示材料存在阈值应力：当热蠕变应力超过特定水平后，蠕变应变率迅速增加。据此，研究建立了含阈值应力的幂律热蠕变本构模型。蠕变失效主要由剪应力导致。微观组织分析表明，随外加应力增大，晶粒尺寸先增大后减小，位错密度和小角度晶界比例均呈下降趋势。

English abstract

FeCrAl alloys are recognized as an excellent candidate material for accident-tolerant fuel cladding tubes. However, thermal creep behaviors of FeCrAl alloy cladding tubes and the associated microstructural evolution have not been comprehensively investigated. This work addressed this gap by evaluating the axial thermal creep behavior of precipitation-strengthened wrought FeCrAl alloy cladding tubes at the temperatures of 360°C and 475°C, respectively. Creep test results indicated that there was a threshold stress for the FeCrAl alloy cladding tubes, because the thermal creep strain rate increased rapidly when the thermal creep stress was larger than a specific stress level. Consequently, a power-law thermal creep constitutive model with a threshold stress was established according to the thermal creep test results. Creep failure was primarily caused by shear stress. In addition, microstructural analysis indicated that the grain size of the FeCrAl alloy cladding tubes initially increased and subsequently decreased with increasing applied stress. Both the dislocation density and the proportion of low-angle grain boundaries demonstrated a decreasing trend with increasing stress levels.

02 热管堆热管微型堆中子学燃耗安全控制

基于MCS的5 MWth TRISO燃料—氟盐热管冷却反应堆中子学与耗损分析

Neutronic and depletion analysis of a 5 MWth heat pipe cooled reactor with TRISO fuel and fluoride salt using MCS

Nuclear Engineering and Technology · 2026-08-10 · Kyeongwon Kim, Hae-Yong Jeong, Deokjung Lee

核心观点

  • MCS三维燃耗计算显示，5 MWth、21,600个TRISO球和665根钠热管的概念堆在5年循环内反应性平稳下降。
  • 慢化材料、F-19吸收与多套停堆机构共同决定能谱、控制余量和故障容错性。

创新点

  • 在同一堆芯概念中联合TRISO球床、FLiBe、BeO与钠热管，并同时评估长循环耗损和控制机构故障。

不足

未来方向

  • 构建中子学—热管传热—燃料/反射层热应力闭环，分析单根和多根热管失效下的峰值温度与反应性反馈。
⚑ 摘要披露5 MWth、21,600个燃料球、665根钠热管、5年循环及keff结果；全文应提取堆芯几何、材料组分、功率分布和控制鼓参数。

中文摘要

本研究考察5 MWth Sejong TRISO燃料、熔盐充填的热管冷却反应堆之中子学性能、耗损行为和安全特性。堆芯采用TRISO结构的碳氧化铀燃料、LiF-BeF2（FLiBe）熔盐冷却剂以及氧化铍反射层，并用蒙特卡洛程序MCS完成5年循环的三维燃耗分析。堆芯包含21,600个嵌入TRISO的球形燃料元件和665根钠热管，实现非能动排热；热中子能谱改善了中子经济性和控制鼓效果。在所有控制鼓全抽出状态下，keff由循环初的1.06075平稳降至循环末的1.01138，同时仅出现有限的能谱硬化和较平坦的径向功率分布。参数研究阐明了FLiBe、BeO和碳的能谱作用，显示BeO和碳的显著慢化效应，以及F-19共振吸收对中间能区通量的影响。对控制鼓转动、控制鼓卡滞和停堆棒插入失败的分析显示，设计具有足够的控制余量和故障容错能力。非能动热管、完整型燃料与冷却剂，加上冗余停堆系统，支撑了以安全为导向的微型堆概念。

English abstract

This study investigates the neutronic performance, depletion behavior, and safety characteristics of a 5 MWth Sejong TRISO-fueled molten salt filled heat pipe cooled reactor. It employs tristructural-isotropic (TRISO) uranium-oxycarbide fuel, LiF-BeF2 (FLiBe) molten salt coolant, and a beryllium-oxide (BeO) reflector. The Monte Carlo code MCS is used for three-dimensional burnup analysis over a 5 year cycle. The core adopts a pebble bed with 21,600 TRISO-embedded pebbles and 665 sodium heat pipes, achieving passive heat removal and a thermal spectrum that enhances neutron economy and control drum effectiveness. Depletion under all-control-drums-out shows smooth reactivity decrease from k-eff starting at 1.06075 at beginning-of-cycle and reaching 1.01138 at end-of-cycle. This is accompanied by limited spectral hardening and flattened radial power distributions, indicating benign fuel utilization and power peaking. Parametric studies clarify the roles of FLiBe, BeO, and carbon in shaping the spectrum, demonstrating pronounced BeO and carbon moderation and F-19 resonance absorption effects on the intermediate energy flux. Safety analyses of drum rotation, drum seizure, and shutdown rod insertion failure confirm sufficient control margin and fault tolerance. The combination of passive heat pipes, integrity fuel and coolant, and redundant shutdown systems supports a safety-driven microreactor concept.

研究提出一种降阶热—力框架，用于分析芯块式核燃料棒在类RIA瞬态功率跃升中随燃耗变化的燃料—包壳相互作用。该模型在计算高效的统一表述中，结合了瞬态径向导热、燃耗相关的间隙导热退化、热膨胀、燃料重定位、间隙闭合演化、接触压力形成、包壳环向应力估算以及PCI风险评估。模型采用高斯型瞬态脉冲表示快速功率跃升，并对0至60 GWd/tHM燃耗范围内的耦合热响应与力学响应进行评估。

A reduced-order thermo-mechanical framework is presented for investigating burnup-dependent fuel–cladding interaction behavior during RIA-like transient power excursions in pelletized nuclear fuel rods. The model combines transient radial heat conduction, burnup-dependent gap conductance degradation, thermal expansion, fuel relocation, gap closure evolution, contact pressure development, cladding hoop stress estimation, and PCI risk assessment within a computationally efficient formulation. A Gaussian-type transient pulse is employed to represent rapid power excursions, while coupled thermal and mechanical responses are evaluated for burnup conditions ranging from 0 to 60 GWd/tHM.

中文摘要

在轻水反应堆中，燃料/包壳组件的可靠性很大程度上取决于能否量化燃料与包壳材料之间的力学相互作用。本研究为Zircaloy-4包壳提出一种弹—黏塑性和蠕变的多尺度建模框架，引入样本外验证，并显式考虑位错密度等微观结构演化，以改进棒尺度燃料—包壳相互作用预测。研究从多晶蠕变仿真数据库的关联中构建代理模型，并将其集成进有限元求解器，用于部件长度尺度仿真。多晶模型将黏塑性应变率分解为位错运动和空位介导扩散的贡献，并包含位错密度演化。通过系统改变影响力学响应的输入变量，构建了合成数据库，训练响应面代理模型捕捉关键输入—输出关系。最后，代理模型被集成到有限元框架中，并与行业标准、经实验建立的事故情景部件级算例比较，以实现代表真实工况的稳健交叉验证。

English abstract

In light water nuclear reactors, the reliability of fuel/clad assemblies largely depends on the degree to which mechanical interactions between the fuel and cladding materials can be quantified. This work introduces a multi-scale modeling framework for elasto-viscoplasticity and creep in Zircaloy-4 cladding that incorporates out-of-sample validation and explicitly accounts for microstructural evolution, including dislocation density, to improve rod-scale fuel–cladding interaction predictions. Specifically, this work focuses on deriving a surrogate model from correlations in a polycrystal creep simulation database, and integrating the surrogate into a finite element solver for simulations at the component length-scale. The polycrystal simulations employ a mechanistic model that describes the viscoplastic strain rate in terms of contributions from dislocation motion and vacancy-mediated diffusion processes, and includes a dislocation density evolution model. A synthetic database is meticulously constructed by systematically varying relevant input variables in polycrystal creep simulations that influence the mechanical response outputs. A response surface surrogate is trained on the database to capture these critical input–output correlations. The surrogate is then integrated into a finite element modeling framework and validated against industry-standard, experimentally established accident-scenario test cases at the component scale, ensuring robust cross-validation representative of real operational conditions.

中文摘要

螺旋十字形燃料（HCF）元件因具有更大传热面积和更强冷却剂混合能力，是下一代反应堆设计的关键部件。但腐蚀产物CRUD在燃料包壳表面堆积会增加热阻，显著影响安全和运行效率。本研究采用ANSYS FLUENT，集成定制的UDF沉积—冲蚀模型，模拟4×4 HCF通道阵列中的CRUD行为。结果表明，CRUD沉积在空间上高度非均匀，最大厚度出现在靠近通道入口的迎风侧。该沉积层形成额外热障，在基准工况下使燃料包壳表面温度最高升高12.6 K。参数分析显示，冷却剂入口速度和壁面热流密度增大时，峰值CRUD厚度减小；而入口温度升高时，峰值厚度显著增加。研究对CRUD导致的HCF热工水力退化给出了定量评估，可为先进压水堆的安全余量和性能优化提供参考。

English abstract

The helical cruciform fuel (HCF) element is a critical component in the design of next-generation nuclear reactors due to its enhanced heat transfer area and coolant mixing capabilities. However, the accumulation of corrosion products, known as CRUD, on the fuel cladding surface significantly compromises reactor safety and operational efficiency by increasing thermal resistance. This study utilizes the ANSYS FLUENT computational framework, integrated with a customized User-Defined Function (UDF) deposition–erosion model, to simulate CRUD behavior within a 4*4 HCF channel array. This numerical results reveal that CRUD deposition exhibits a highly non-uniform spatial distribution, with the maximum thickness occurring on the windward side near the channel entrance. This accumulation creates an additional thermal barrier that increases the fuel cladding surface temperature by up to 12.6 K under baseline operating conditions. Parametric analysis demonstrates that the peak CRUD thickness decreases with higher coolant inlet velocities and wall heat fluxes, whereas it increases substantially as the inlet temperature rises. The findings provide a comprehensive quantitative assessment of CRUD-induced thermal–hydraulic degradation in HCF elements, offering vital insights for the optimization of reactor safety margins and performance in advanced pressurized water reactors.
