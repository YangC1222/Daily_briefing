中文摘要

内外冷却环形燃料（IXAF）是一种新型核燃料概念，因其具有增强传热、降低峰值燃料温度（PFT）和减少裂变气体释放等诸多优势而备受关注。近年来，由于其增强的事故容错能力，该燃料概念获得了一定的发展势头。本研究比较了两种不同的IXAF制备工艺。放电等离子烧结（SPS）是一种新型粉末固结技术，与传统烧结（CS）相比，具有更好的近净成形能力。本研究对比了采用放电等离子烧结和传统烧结工艺制备的环形燃料芯块的表征结果。对烧结后的芯块进行了表征，比较了它们的密度、尺寸变化、微观结构和晶粒尺寸。采用一种新型的复制测量技术测量了烧结后芯块的内径并进行了比较。出乎意料的是，SPS技术在改善IXAF的近净成形方面不如CS有效。

English abstract

Internally and EXternally cooled Annular Fuel (IXAF) is a novel nuclear fuel concept that is being researched due to several advantages such as enhanced heat transfer, lower Peak Fuel Temperature (PFT), and lower fission gas release. The fuel concept has gained some momentum recently due to its enhanced accident tolerance. In this study the fabrication of IXAF by two different processing routes are compared. Spark Plasma Sintering (SPS) is a novel powder consolidation technique with good near net shaping ability as compared to Conventional Sintering (CS). This publication compares the results obtained from the characterisation of annular pellets fabricated from Spark Plasma Sintering to that from conventional sintering. The as-sintered pellets were characterised to compare their densities, dimensional change, microstructure, and grain size. The internal diameter of the as-sintered pellets was measured by a novel replicate technique and compared. Unexpectedly, the SPS technique proved to be a less viable alternative to CS in improving the near net shaping of the IXAF.

中文摘要

核电站管道系统的管道支架设计传统上依赖于工程经验，通过迭代管道应力分析进行，耗时耗力。本研究提出了一种混合优化框架，该框架结合了遗传算法（GA）、基于图神经网络（GNN）的代理模型和PIPESTRESS分析，用于管道支架优化。支架类型被定义为设计变量，而ASME规范的应力比则被用作设计约束。GA优化成功地减少了支架数量，同时保持了可接受的应力比水平。为了降低重复PIPESTRESS分析的计算成本，本研究利用管道几何形状和支架配置的图表示，开发了一种GNN代理模型。训练和验证使用了包含三种管道几何形状共3600个样本的数据集。该代理模型旨在评估预定义管道几何形状中未见过的支架配置，而不是任意的未见过的管道布局。尽管代理模型总体预测性能良好，但在某些评估指标上准确率较低，这些预测误差会影响单独使用图神经网络（GNN）时的优化结果。为了提高优化可靠性，本文提出了一种混合方法：首先使用GNN评估所有候选方案，然后使用PIPESTRESS对筛选出的方案进行重新分析。该方法在保持与传统基于PIPESTRESS的优化方法相当的支护配置和应力比水平的同时，将计算时间减少了36.0%至59.7%。

English abstract

Pipe support design for nuclear power plant piping systems is traditionally performed through iterative piping stress analyses based on engineering experience, requiring significant time and effort. This study proposes a hybrid optimization framework combining a Genetic Algorithm (GA), a Graph Neural Network (GNN)-based surrogate model, and PIPESTRESS analysis for pipe support optimization. Support types were defined as design variables, while ASME Code stress ratios were used as design constraints. GA optimization successfully reduced the number of supports while maintaining acceptable stress-ratio levels. To reduce the computational cost of repeated PIPESTRESS analyses, a GNN surrogate model was developed using graph representations of piping geometries and support configurations. A dataset consisting of 3600 samples from three piping geometries was used for training and validation. The surrogate model was developed to evaluate unseen support configurations within predefined piping geometries rather than arbitrary unseen piping layouts. Although the surrogate model showed good overall prediction performance, lower accuracy was observed for some evaluation criteria, and these prediction errors affected the optimization results when the GNN was used alone. To improve optimization reliability, a hybrid approach was introduced in which all candidates were evaluated by the GNN and selected candidates were reanalyzed using PIPESTRESS. The proposed method reduced computational time by 36.0–59.7% while maintaining support configurations and stress-ratio levels comparable to those obtained using conventional PIPESTRESS-based optimization.
