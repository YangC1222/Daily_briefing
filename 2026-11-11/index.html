为评估国内核电站的安全性能，开发了一套名为“严重事故分析集成程序”（PISAA）的严重事故程序。为了评估其模拟堆芯升温和再注水阶段燃料组件行为的能力，我们针对QUENCH-06试验装置开发了一系列输入模型，并使用PISAA程序进行了模拟。目标是研究节点划分、氧化模型和传热对关键输出的影响。节点敏感性分析表明，堆芯节点划分对氢质量和温度分布有显著影响。对比结果表明，4环64级节点划分方案总体上能够捕捉不同轴向位置的温度变化趋势，但试验段下部和中部区域的温度明显偏高，导致这些区域的氧化层厚度也被高估。此外，不确定性分析表明，氢质量和氧化层厚度均受氧化模型的影响，其中节点间径向传热的影响最为显著。因此，可以得出结论，通过适当的节点化输入文件，PISAA 代码能够捕捉 QUENCH-06 实验中的温度分布和氢气含量。

English abstract

A severe accident code, program integrated for severe accident analysis (PISAA), was developed for the safety assessment of domestic nuclear power plants. To evaluate its capability in simulating fuel bundle behavior during the core heat-up and reflood phases, a series of input decks for the QUENCH-06 facility were developed and simulated using the PISAA code. The objective was to investigate the influence of nodalization, oxidation models, and heat transfer on key outputs. The nodal sensitivity shows that core nodalization has a significant influence on hydrogen mass and temperature distribution. Comparative results indicate that the 4-ring, 64-level nodalization scheme can generally capture temperature trends at different axial locations, although temperatures in the lower and intermediate regions of the test section are clearly overestimated, leading to a corresponding overestimation of oxide thickness in these regions. Furthermore, uncertainty analysis indicates that both hydrogen mass and oxide thickness are affected by the oxidation models, and that inter-node radial heat transfer has the most significant influence among all uncertain inputs. Therefore, it is concluded that, with an appropriate nodalization input deck, the PISAA code is capable of capturing the temperature distribution and the amount of hydrogen in the QUENCH-06 experiment.

中文摘要

铅铋共晶（LBE）冷却剂中产生的腐蚀产物可以以悬浮氧化物颗粒的形式传输，并沉积在燃料棒表面，从而增加热阻，提高局部流动阻塞的风险，并降低传热性能。本研究采用ANSYS Fluent中的离散相模型，对铅铋快堆梯形肋燃料棒通道内的颗粒传输和沉积进行了数值模拟。比较了三种单棒通道结构（分别具有一个、两个和四个螺旋肋），以阐明肋数对沉积分布和颗粒-壁面相互作用行为的影响。结果表明，单肋通道中的颗粒沉积呈空间离散分布，而双肋和四肋通道则沿肋表面呈现连续的螺旋沉积带。增加肋数会增强近壁二次流和颗粒-壁面碰撞频率，从而导致更高的沉积速率和更明显的入口区域沉积。进一步分析表明，单肋通道中的颗粒主要通过低频弱碰撞和低法向速度冲击沉积，而多肋通道中的颗粒更容易在反复碰撞-反弹循环后，在近壁区域因动能耗散而被捕获。这些发现为肋包覆式铅铋合金燃料棒通道中腐蚀产物沉积风险提供了数值分析，并为铅铋反应堆燃料组件的热工水力设计提供了有价值的指导。

English abstract

Corrosion products generated in the lead‑bismuth eutectic (LBE) coolant can be transported as suspended oxide particles and may deposit on fuel-rod surfaces, thereby introducing additional thermal resistance, increasing the risk of local flow blockage, and reducing heat-transfer performance. In this study, particle transport and deposition in trapezoidal-rib fuel-rod channels of a lead‑bismuth fast reactor were numerically investigated using the discrete phase model in ANSYS Fluent. Three single-rod channel configurations with one, two, and four helical ribs were compared to clarify the influence of rib number on deposition distribution and particle-wall interaction behavior. The results show that particle deposition in the single-rib channel is spatially discrete, whereas the double-rib and four-rib channels exhibit continuous helical deposition bands along the rib surfaces. Increasing the number of ribs enhances near-wall secondary flow and particle-wall collision frequency, resulting in a higher deposition rate and more pronounced deposition in the inlet region. Further analysis indicates that particles in the single-rib channel mainly deposit through low-frequency weak collisions and low-normal-velocity impaction, while particles in multi-rib channels are more likely to be captured after repeated collision-rebound cycles and kinetic-energy dissipation in the near-wall region. These findings provide numerical insights into corrosion-product deposition risks in rib-wrapped LBE fuel-rod channels and offer valuable guidance for the thermal-hydraulic design of lead‑bismuth reactor fuel assemblies.

English abstract

Accurate thermal-hydraulic prediction in rod bundles remains challenging, since many conventional Subchannel Analysis Code (SAC) employ closure relations that are primarily obtained from experimental tests that have uncertainties. In Part-A of this work, new single-phase closure correlations were developed using Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) analysis to improve the predictive accuracy in subchannels by explicitly incorporating local geometric characteristics and local operating conditions for each subchannel. In Part-B of this work, the proposed correlations are implemented in a SAC and assessed for its predictability using established experimental databases for the GE 3 × 3, CNEN 4 × 4 and CE 5 × 5 rod bundles. The correlations developed for three distinct regions are blended with a cubic function to ensure the functional and slope continuity. This has been done to prevent any oscillations that may arise during the solution of the subchannel governing equations using an iterative solution procedure. It was noted that the correlations developed for the hexagonal rod arrangements predicted the results of square rod arrangements very well. The improved SAC is further benchmarked against three-dimensional Navier-Stokes simulations for a typical 169-rod hexagonal bundle, where the predicted subchannel results show close agreement with the CFD results. The code reproduces local variations in mass flow rate, bulk coolant temperature and pressure drop as obtained by CFD results, demonstrating that the modified closures enhance predictability while retaining the computational efficiency required for design and safety evaluations of advanced reactor cores.

中文摘要

对于先进节块扩散求解器，强空间非均匀堆芯（如无硼SMR）的棒内功率分布预测仍有困难。功率重构可能不能充分捕捉高度局部化通量梯度，使热流和中子注量估计偏差。蒙卡可以分辨这些效应，但计算成本限制常规堆芯跟踪应用。本文研究代理模型快速估算棒内功率，基于Kraken框架内二维PRATIC无硼压水SMR燃耗计算，用Serpent蒙卡和ANTS扩散一致建模，生成额定条件下棒内功率训练数据。比较多元线性回归和前馈神经网络，输入为可快速得到的节块输出，如棒功率及棒燃耗。线性模型测试R²为0.97030，非线性表达有限；NN达到0.99962，RMSE降低近一个数量级，相对蒙卡的平均偏差为(0.02±0.85)%。NN预测需秒级，蒙卡模拟需数天。结果支持在训练有效范围内快速估算随燃耗变化的棒内功率，该范围为全部控制棒提出、平衡氙、固定热工水力条件及燃耗不超过17.5 MWd/kgU。未来扩展运行条件和堆芯状态的训练数据，可扩大有效范围，服务在线监测和燃料材料辐照方案的设计、监测与评价。

English abstract

Accurate prediction of intra-pin power distributions can be challenging for state-of-art nodal diffusion solvers, particularly in cores exhibiting strong spatial heterogeneities such as boron-free small modular reactors. Under such conditions, pin power reconstruction methods may not fully capture the potential presence of highly localised flux gradients, leading to biased heat flux and neutron fluence estimates. While Monte Carlo methods can resolve these effects, their computational cost limits their applicability for routine core-follow analyses. This work investigates data-driven surrogate models as a computationally efficient alternative to MC simulations for intra-pin power estimation. The study is based on a depletion calculation of a two-dimensional version of the PRATIC boron-free PWR SMR benchmark, analysed within the Kraken multi-physics framework. For that purpose, the core was consistently modelled with the MC Serpent code and the ANTS diffusion solver to generate a detailed intra-pin power dataset used for surrogate model training under nominal operating conditions. Two modelling approaches were considered: a multivariate linear regression model and a feed-forward neural network (NN), both using rapidly available nodal outputs such as pin power or pin burnup as input. The linear model achieved a coefficient of determination of R2=0.97030 on the test dataset, indicating reasonable predictive capability but limited ability to capture nonlinearity effects. In contrast, the NN significantly improved performance, achieving a R2=0.99962 and reducing the root mean square error by nearly an order of magnitude. The NN predictions showed excellent agreement with MC reference data, with a mean relative deviation of (0.02±0.85)%, while maintaining prediction times on the order of seconds compared to several days for MC simulations. This study demonstrates the potential of surrogate models to provide rapid intra-pin power estimates across burnup states for the investigated benchmark within the validity envelope of the operating conditions used to generate the training dataset (all rod out, equilibrium xenon, fixed thermal-hydraulic conditions, and burnup states up to 17.5 MWd/kgU). Future developments incorporating a wider range of operating conditions and core states into the training dataset could further extend this validity envelope and enhance the model’s applicability to online core monitoring, as well as to the design, monitoring, and evaluation of fuel material irradiation programmes.

English abstract

However, the current models for hydrodynamic load exhibit limited predictive capability due to the inadequate representation of transient energy exchange, particularly the neglect of energy dissipation mechanism. 中文摘要

核燃料组件作为核反应堆内的释热部件,是核电站第一道安全屏障,可以有效包容裂变产物,防止放射性物质外泄,保持燃料元件形状并提供结构强度。然而,由于包壳受到冷却剂的冲刷发生氧化反应,生成氧化膜,会严重影响燃料组件的结构强度和韧性,导致核燃料组件功能失效。为精确评估包壳异质膜结构状态,需对其壁厚和电磁特性等多个参数进行高精度测量。针对该问题,该文提出一种核燃料包壳管多层异质膜叠阵多频涡流检测方法。首先,设计由三个同轴线圈组成的叠阵涡流传感器,建立了包含包壳层和氧化膜层的双层介质解析模型,进而推导了叠阵涡流结构的线圈互感表达式;其次,提出核燃料包壳管异质膜多参数分步解耦反演方法,利用高频响应信号对氧化膜层厚度进行反演,同时结合低频响应与线圈阵列特征实现包壳基体厚度与电导率的解耦测量;最后,采用有限元数值仿真与实验对所提方法进行验证。结果表明,基于叠阵涡流趋肤效应,通过多频响应差异可以实现异质膜参数的准确估计,各参数的平均相对误差低于3.5%,证明了所提方法的可行性与准确性。
