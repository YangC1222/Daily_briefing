中文摘要

蒙卡输运的计数网格累积关注响应。在多物理场模拟中，人工划分空间计数区域需要较多先验知识，过度细化又增加时间与统计代价。本文发展非结构网格计数的自适应细化（AMR），迭代减小空间离散误差，同时控制统计惩罚。在开源多物理场驱动程序Cardinal中实现，将libMesh的AMR连接到OpenMC连续能量输运网格计数。用不同径向尺度的轻水堆问题和AMR品质因子比较方案。相对两级均匀细化，AMR改善品质因子，其中利用统计信息的“前瞻”细化收益最大。测试的误差启发式中，按裂变热数值跃变与净流大小细化效果最佳。结果表明，一至两级AMR对单物理场组件及全堆问题已有一定收益。

English abstract

An integral component of Monte Carlo radiation transport methods is the specification of tallies, which accumulate quantities of interest during transport. Selecting an appropriate set of spatial regions for tallies, especially in a multiphysics simulation, requires a great deal of a priori knowledge to avoid the runtime and stochastic penalties associated with refinement while still capturing the spatial detail of the solution field. This work develops unstructured mesh tally adaptive mesh refinement (AMR) as an alternative to manual tally refinement in order to reduce tally spatial discretization error iteratively while minimizing the stochastic penalty of refinement. Unstructured mesh tally AMR has been implemented in the open-source multiphysics driver application Cardinal, which connects the AMR implementation in libMesh to mesh tallies in the continuous energy Monte Carlo transport code OpenMC. The different methods proposed are evaluated against light water reactor (LWR) problems of different radial length scales using a figure of merit (FOM) for AMR. This work found that AMR resulted in an improvement in FOM compared to two stages of uniform refinement, where the statistics-informed “look-ahead” refinement scheme yielded the largest improvements. Refinement based on value jumps in fission heating and the magnitude of the net current were found to yield the largest improvements in FOM out of the error heuristics tested. Results show that one to two stages of AMR provides some benefit to single-physics assembly and whole-core LWR problems.

中文摘要

本文研究从公开NUREG资料整理的24283组CHF测量，来自60个以干涸为主的来源。表列出口含汽率通过热平衡几乎重构实测CHF（相关系数0.99989），因此作为目标泄漏变量排除。按留一来源验证，避免泄漏的模型误差为23.2%，由入口条件求解的2006 Groeneveld查找表为21.2%。零样本共形区间总体覆盖率0.916，但在有偏来源上按来源的覆盖率可低至0.40，学习模型和查找表均有此问题。提出与主干模型无关的校准：少量目标来源标记点修正乘性偏差，并重新校准经验共形区间。适配与校准严格分开时，20–30个目标点可使按来源经验覆盖接近名义值；交叉共形变体约需10点。以查找表为锚的误差约11%、区间最窄，但因查找表源于该工作数据，属样本内结果；留出该来源重新建表使误差约增3个百分点，其他留出对照约17%–19%，接近学习主干。所有覆盖率均为经验值。50次目标抽样和按实验室/网络分组下预算稳定，但标记点只覆盖一个质量通量或压力块时，覆盖降至0.83–0.93。

English abstract

The public NUREG derived set of 24,283 critical heat flux (CHF) measurements from 60 dryout dominated sources is studied under source shift. The tabulated outlet quality reconstructs the measured CHF from the heat balance (correlation 0.99989) and is excluded as target leakage. Under a leave one source out protocol the leakage safe model reaches 23.2 percent error and an inlet solved 2006 Groeneveld look up table 21.2 percent. A zero shot conformal interval gives a pooled coverage of 0.916 but source conditional coverage as low as 0.40 on biased sources, for the learned model and the table alike. A backbone agnostic calibration is proposed in which a few labelled target points correct a multiplicative bias and recalibrate an empirical conformal interval. A strict split protocol that separates adaptation from calibration restores near nominal empirical source conditional coverage with twenty to thirty target points, a cross conformal variant with about ten. Anchored on the look up table the calibration reaches about eleven percent error with the sharpest intervals, an in sample figure because the table was derived from this working set; re-deriving the table with the source held out costs about three points, and held out analogues anchor the calibration at seventeen to nineteen percent, near the learned backbone. All coverage values are empirical. The budget is stable over fifty target draws and under grouping by laboratory or network, but coverage falls to 0.83 to 0.93 when the labelled points cover only one mass flux or pressure block.

中文摘要

核工程热工水力系统需要可靠监测与对非线性时变行为的准确建模。高空泡份额流动界面混沌、转换非线性，增加诊断难度。本文用中心电导探针测量建立环状气液流子流型分类。将连续实验分为短时片段，以体积、频域和形态特征表征几何结构，比较K近邻、支持向量机、随机森林和梯度提升树。特征从6个减至3个后，多数模型改善。随机森林局部片段宏平均F1为0.9263，按整次实验多数投票时平衡准确率达到1。以期望/最大校准误差及Brier分数评估，随机森林置信度最可靠。研究说明机器学习可辅助复杂流型识别，并强调验证方法和概率校准而不仅是准确率。

English abstract

Thermal-hydraulic systems in nuclear engineering require reliable monitoring, accurate characterization and models capable of handling nonlinear, time-dependent behavior. High-void-fraction multiphase flows introduce significant diagnostic challenges, as the chaotic, nonlinear nature of interfacial transitions makes conventional modeling and rigid thresholding approaches less practical. This study develops a diagnostic framework for classifying annular gas–liquid flow sub-regimes using centerline conductivity probe measurements. Continuous experimental runs were partitioned into short temporal segments, with feature vectors constructed from volumetric, frequency-domain and morphological characteristics to represent the geometric structure of the flow. Four supervised learning architectures, K-nearest neighbors (KNN), support vector machines (SVM), random forest (RF) and gradient boosted trees (GBT), were evaluated. Feature selection reduced the initial six-feature configuration to an optimal three-feature subset, improving classification performance across most architectures. The RF model achieved the best overall results, with a macro F1 score of 0.9263 at the local segment level and perfect balanced accuracy under global run-level majority voting. Probabilistic evaluation using expected calibration error (ECE), maximum calibration error (MCE) and Brier scores confirmed that the RF model produced the most reliable confidence estimates among all evaluated classifiers. Overall, the study shows that machine learning can be applied to flow characterization problems to address challenges that traditional methods handle poorly. The results also highlight the importance of understanding model behavior, not just predictive accuracy, through analysis of evaluation methodology and calibration of predicted probabilities.
