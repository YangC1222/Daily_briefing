中文摘要

在存在不凝性气体（NCG）时准确预测凝结换热，是保障热系统安全、可靠和高效运行的关键挑战；核反应堆非能动安全壳冷却系统尤其依赖可靠的蒸汽凝结。现有机理模型和经验模型通常难以覆盖多样的热工水力条件，限制了其实时安全分析应用。本研究汇集20个文献来源的3431个实验数据点，采用两组输入特征训练Transformer、人工神经网络、随机森林、XGBoost和AdaBoost五种机器学习模型：一组为关键实验参数，另一组增加无量纲数。经贝叶斯超参数优化后，AdaBoost精度最高，MAE为0.044，R²为0.99。SHAP分析确认无量纲参数对预测具有显著影响，并与热物理规律一致。该框架能够覆盖较宽运行范围，并为失水事故期间的非能动安全壳冷却提供实时决策支持。但在高NCG浓度和低过冷度等极端条件下不确定性较高，仍需扩展实验数据并发展物理信息混合模型，以改善外推和稳健性。

English abstract

Accurate prediction of condensation heat transfer in the presence of non-condensable gases (NCGs) is a critical challenge for ensuring the safety, reliability and efficiency of thermal systems, particularly in nuclear power reactors where passive containment cooling systems (PCCS) rely on reliable steam condensation. Existing mechanistic and empirical models are often limited in handling diverse thermal-hydraulic conditions, restricting their use for real-time safety analysis. This study develops artificial intelligence (AI)-driven models to predict condensation heat transfer coefficients (HTC) using 3431 experimental data points from 20 literature sources. Five machine learning algorithms—Transformer, Artificial Neural Network (ANN), Random Forest (RF), XGBoost, and AdaBoost—were trained using two input feature sets: key experimental parameters and an extended set including dimensionless numbers. Bayesian hyperparameter optimization enhanced model performance, with AdaBoost achieving the best accuracy (MAE = 0.044, R2 = 0.99). SHAP analysis confirmed that dimensionless parameters strongly influence predictions, consistent with thermophysical principles. The proposed AI framework generalizes across wide operating conditions and offers real-time predictive capability for PCCS safety, functioning as a virtual safety engineer during loss-of-coolant accidents (LOCA). However, relatively higher uncertainty is observed under extreme conditions, particularly at high NCG concentrations and low subcooling, highlighting the need for expanded experimental datasets and physics-informed hybrid approaches to improve extrapolation and robustness. This work demonstrates AI's potential as a decision-support tool to improve accident resilience and uphold ALARP (As Low As Reasonably Practicable) safety principles.

English abstract

Currently, austenic steel Cr16-Ni19 is in use as a basic material in the fuel element claddings of the fast neutron reactors. Formation and evolution of displacement cascades when steel is exposed to neutron irradiation is the first stage of microstructure transformation that affects the fuel element durability. Improved statistic model of defect migration was applied to describe the process taking place in the steel during irradiation in the BN-600 reactor for the first time. That allowed to characterize defect evolution at all stages of atomic displacement cascade formation: (i) estimate generation rate of point defects in the steel at the dynamic stage; (ii) calculate the rate of spontaneous processes, namely, recombination and formation of complexes of similar defects at the kinetic stage; and (iii) for remained single defects evaluate the migration rate from cascade regions to crystal matrix at the thermodynamic stage. The fitting procedure using analytic expressions was performed to describe neutron flux density and cross section for neutrons and atoms, which is not conventional for the displacement cascade calculations. To achieve this purpose, neutron energy range was divided into peculiar intervals. Using instruments for analytic description, (i) calculations were performed for conditions corresponding to the fuel elements irradiation in the low-enrichment core of the BN-600 reactor; (ii) comparison of calculated results with radiation damage data for the steel Cr16-Ni19 obtained from the reactor was carried out; and (iii) an opportunity to use data from BN-600 for theoretical characterization of structural variations was established. The obtained results intended to be useful for characterization of point defects flow to microstructure (sink) and their evolution during fuel element operating in the fast neutron reactors. Previously, there were no ordered results for such extreme high operation performance in the industrial fast neutron reactor in scientific papers.

展开中英文摘要

中文摘要

核潜艇推进回路热力循环设计必须覆盖广泛航速及正常、事故瞬态。该回路包含反应堆侧单相换热器和推进侧凝汽器，在全工况范围内解析温度、速度和压力场计算代价很高。研究为简化单相换热器和小型凝汽器构建降阶模型（ROM），比较三种基于适当正交分解的非侵入方法。全局基方法从高保真训练数据建立一个统一降阶基，并针对入口温度或蒸汽流量等新参数插值降阶系数；Bi-CITSGM方法同时插值降阶系数和Grassmann流形切空间中的空间基；识别法则预设ROM形式，通过优化识别控制系数，再求解低维微分方程预测新参数下的响应，因而保留更多物理结构。三种方法与高保真求解结果符合良好，并显著缩短计算时间。

English abstract

The thermal cycle sizing of a nuclear submarine’s propulsion loop requires accounting for a wide range of cruising velocities and transient phases, in both nominal and accidental operating modes. This loop comprises a single-phase heat exchanger on the reactor side and a condenser on the propulsion side, whose detailed temperature, velocity and pressure fields are computationally intensive to resolve over the full range of operating conditions. To drastically reduce the computational time, reduced order models (ROMs) have been built to calculate temperature, velocity and pressure fields in a simplified single-phase heat exchanger and in a small condenser. Three non-intrusive ROMs based on Proper Orthogonal Decomposition have been investigated. First, the global basis method constructs a single global reduced basis from high-fidelity training data and predicts a new solution by interpolating only the associated reduced coefficients for a new parameter (such as the inlet temperature of the inner tubes or the steam flow rate). Secondly, the Bi-CITSGM method (Oulghelou and Allery 2020) (Bi-Calibrated Interpolation on Tangent Subspace of Grassmann Manifold) follows a different interpolation strategy by interpolating not only the reduced coefficients, but also the spatial reduced basis for a new parameter. In contrast to these interpolation-based approaches, a physics-based reduced-order modeling strategy, referred to as the identification method, has been investigated. This approach assumes the form of the ROM and identifies its governing coefficients by solving an optimization problem. Once this ROM is constructed, it is solved for a new parameter to predict the corresponding system response. Unlike the two previous methods, which are purely data-driven, this method is physics-based because it relies on the identification and resolution of a low-dimensional system of differential equations that describes the physical behavior of the process. The results obtained with these methods have shown a satisfactory agreement with results obtained with the high fidelity solvers. In addition, a drastic reduction of the computational time has been highlighted.

中文摘要

铅铋共晶（LBE）是铅冷快堆的候选冷却剂，但裸棒阵列横向流动阻力实验数据有限。本文所称横流，是横向流体依次穿过多排错列棒，而非相邻轴向子通道之间的侧向分流。研究针对该构型建立经验阻力关联式。在约270 ℃的LBE中，对6×20错列裸棒束开展压降实验，节径比P/D为1.133，雷诺数为4.9×10³至4.1×10⁴。由测得压降得到按区间和棒排归一化的等效阻力系数K。K随Re增加总体下降，低Re时下降更快，高Re时趋缓。拟合得到K=10.32Re^-0.263，R²=0.951，总体平均绝对相对偏差为2.35%；Re和K的最大合成相对不确定度分别为8.1%和1.7%。CFD压降分解表明，进出口净贡献低于实测区间压降的0.1%。水工质棒束和传统错列管束关联式不能同时重现本实验中K的量级及Re依赖。该数据和关联式可作为类似错列LBE裸棒束初步压降评估与水力模型核查的特定构型基线；超出当前P/D、几何、测压布置和系数定义时需要额外验证。

English abstract

Lead–bismuth eutectic (LBE) is a promising coolant for lead-cooled fast reactors, but experimental data on transverse-flow resistance in bare-rod arrays remain limited. Here, cross-flow denotes controlled transverse flow across successive staggered rod rows rather than lateral diversion flow between adjacent axial subchannels. This study develops an empirical resistance correlation for the present configuration. Pressure-drop experiments were conducted in a 6 × 20 staggered bare-rod bundle with P/D = 1.133 using LBE near 270 °C over Re = 4.9 × 103–4.1 × 104. An interval-based equivalent row-normalized resistance coefficient K was determined from the measured pressure drop. K generally decreased with increasing Re, with a stronger decrease at lower Re and a flatter response at higher Re. The data were correlated by K = 10.32Re−0.263, with R2 = 0.951 and an overall mean absolute relative deviation of 2.35%. The maximum combined relative uncertainties in Re and K were 8.1% and 1.7%, respectively. CFD-based pressure decomposition indicated that the net inlet/outlet contribution was below 0.1% of the measured interval pressure drop. Selected water-based rod-bundle and conventional staggered tube-bank correlations did not simultaneously reproduce the magnitude and Reynolds-number dependence of the present coefficient. The resulting database and correlation provide a configuration-specific baseline for preliminary pressure-drop evaluation and hydraulic-model assessment of comparable staggered LBE bare-rod bundles. Application beyond the present P/D, geometry, pressure-tap arrangement, and coefficient definition requires additional validation.

English abstract

The objective of this study is to document a neutron irradiation experiment campaign intended to enable evaluation of embrittlement in electron beam (EB) welded SA508, Grade 3 reactor pressure vessel (RPV) steel for small modular reactors. Arc welds in RPVs have historically experienced severe irradiation embrittlement, but advanced techniques such as EB welding, offer promise for minimizing embrittlement. Here, EB welds are made on a forged SA508 ingot and a SA508 compact manufactured by powder metallurgy with hot isostatic pressing (PM-HIP). Two different post-welding heat treatments are applied. Miniature compact tension (CT) specimens are machined from each weld region (weld centerline, heat affected zone, base metal) then fatigue pre-cracked. The average number of cycles to achieve the pre-crack length is negatively correlated with grain size, but positively correlated with hardness and ferrite phase fraction. These results imply that ductile ferrite phases absorb more energy than harder martensite and/or bainite phases. Specimens are then neutron irradiated in the Advanced Test Reactor to an average dose of 0.17 ± 0.02 displacements per atom at 322.4 ± 7.8 °C, representative of high-fluence end-of-life conditions. Specimens will undergo slow-strain-rate fracture testing over multiple temperatures to determine irradiation-induced ductile-to-brittle transition temperature shifts. Microstructural underpinnings of this irradiation embrittlement across different weld regions and materials, will also be ascertained. Specimens will be made available to researchers worldwide for future studies; this manuscript will serve as a permanent reference for material provenance.

English abstract

Preliminary experiments suggest that Cr-coated cladding can suppress CRUD relative to conventional Zr-alloy cladding. However, the underlying suppression mechanism remains unknown due to lack of understanding on the interfaces formed by NiFe2O4 and cladding surface oxide (ZrO2 or α-Cr2O3). 中文摘要

环形燃料可显著提高压水堆堆芯功率密度,对反应堆小型化设计和经济性的提高具有重要意义。相关方面的研究相对集中于组件的排布方式及少数几何尺寸的组合,而针对环形燃料元件的几何结构尺寸的量化研究相对较少,因此,量化各几何因素对环形燃料元件热工水力性能的影响有重要意义。本文以环形燃料单通道模型为研究对象,基于西屋公司设计的压水环形燃料元件参数进行热工水力设计。选取内外包壳内外径、芯块内外径等几何因素进行研究,并建立了4个评判指标,利用灰色关联分析和模糊数学理论,量化各几何因素对热工水力性能的影响。结果表明,在内外通道最小偏离泡核沸腾比(Minimum Departure from Nucleate Boiling Ratio,MDNBR)中各个几何因素的敏感性相当,但在绝热面位置和燃料芯块最高温度上差异较大,其中,燃料芯块本身的尺寸对绝热面位置表现出最高的敏感性,外包壳内外径对燃料芯块最高温度表现出最高的敏感性,该量化方法为压水堆环形燃料的设计和参数调整提供参考。

[Background]Annular fuel has the potential to significantly enhance the power density of Pressurized Water Reactor(PWR)cores.This characteristic holds great significance for the realization of the miniaturization design of reactors and the improvement of their economic efficiency.Previous study in this domain has primarily focused on the arrangement patterns of components and a limited number of geometric dimension combinations.Conversely,there has been relatively less research dedicated to the quantitative analysis of the refined geometric structure dimensions of fuel elements.[Purpose]This study aims to quantify the impact of various geometric factors on the thermohydraulic performance of annular fuel.[Methods]First of all,the single-channel model of annular fuel a valuable reference for the design and parameter adjustment of annular fuel in PWRs.

研究空白

为什么值得开展

可形成的论文贡献

  • 给出从原子界面指标到构件安全量的可复现参数传递方法。
  • 识别降低CRUD黏附与维持涂层结构完整性之间的设计窗口。
