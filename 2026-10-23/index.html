中文摘要

核能若与零液体排放海水淡化（ZLD）热耦合，可同时提供清洁电力、淡水并提取锂等矿物。ZLD消耗较多能量，而核能与淡化均为资本密集技术，耦合可能带来可靠性问题。本文评估不同维护策略，并考察用储热（TES）解耦、保持热独立性和降低停机的作用，使用EMRALD事件建模风险分析。研究设定下，对退化设备实施预防维护可使反应堆停机时间最多减少40%；用TES解耦动力循环与ZLD，最多可减少65%。收益取决于ZLD设备故障率，对本身可靠性较高的工艺热应用影响较小。储热设备需要具有高可靠性，否则其故障可能反使ZLD停机。研究展示EMRALD比较热电联供设计及维护策略可靠性的用途。

English abstract

Nuclear power has the potential to simultaneously address demand for clean electricity, freshwater scarcity, and demand for valuable minerals, such as lithium, if it is thermally integrated with zero liquid discharge desalination (ZLD). ZLD is an energy-intensive process that desalinates seawater, mining its minerals and extracting freshwater such that no liquid is discharged. However, such nuclear-thermal integration could present challenges for the reliability of the combined system, given both nuclear and desalination are capital-intensive technologies. In this paper, the reliability of such a system is assessed for different maintenance strategies. Additionally, the impact of using thermal energy storage (TES) to decouple designs to maintain thermal independence and reduce downtime is investigated. The system is modeled in Event Modeling Risk Assessment using Linked Diagrams (EMRALD). We determined that performing preventative maintenance on degrading system components can reduce reactor downtime by as much as 40% and that introducing TES to decouple the power cycle from the ZLD reduces reactor downtime by as much as 65%. The impact of decoupling on reducing reactor downtime was found to depend on the component failure rates of the ZLD system, with such a strategy being less impactful for a higher reliability process heat application. High component reliabilities in the TES were found to be necessary to prevent increased downtime in the ZLD system, as failure in the TES could otherwise drive the ZLD system offline. Seeing these changes in system performance, this research shows how EMRALD can be used to compare the reliabilities of different cogenerating plant designs and maintenance strategies.

中文摘要

过冷沸腾中的界面结构与温度场耦合演化难以预测，可靠验证需要两者的局部测量。本文建立竖直内加热环隙低压过冷沸腾实验数据库：外管内径30 mm、加热棒直径10 mm、有效加热长2.0 m。六组工况覆盖热流198–377 kW/m²、质量通量376–680 kg/(m²·s)、入口过冷度12.3–15.8 K和出口压力115–202 kPa。将高速可视化与既有光纤探针局部空泡份额，以及多个轴向位置的径向液温、加热面温度测量结合，系统表征不同工况下界面、液温及壁面热响应的轴向耦合演化。该综合数据库可作为低压过冷沸腾壁面闭合与界面输运模型的CFD验证基准。

English abstract

The coupled evolution of interfacial structure and thermal fields in subcooled boiling remains difficult to predict, and reliable model validation requires local measurements of both phase distribution and temperature. In this study, an extended experimental database was established for low-pressure subcooled boiling flow in a vertically oriented internally heated annulus. Water-steam subcooled boiling flow was generated between a 30 mm-inner-diameter outer tube and a 10 mm-diameter heated rod over an effective heating length of 2.0 m. Six test conditions were examined by varying wall heat flux, mass flux, inlet subcooling, and exit pressure over ranges of 198–377 kW/m2, 376–680 kg/m2s, 12.3–15.8 K, and 115–202 kPa, respectively. High-speed flow visualization was combined with previously measured local void fraction data obtained by optical fiber probes, together with measurements of radial fluid temperature profiles and heated surface temperatures at multiple axial locations. Based on these measurements, the coupled axial evolution of interfacial structure, fluid temperature distribution, and wall thermal response was systematically characterized under varying operating conditions. The resulting integrated dataset is expected to serve as a valuable benchmark for CFD-grade wall boiling closures and interfacial transport models for low-pressure subcooled boiling flows.

中文摘要

针对小尺度棒束通道两相流认识不足，本文用空气—水在3×3棒束中实验，结合高速摄影与自制双光纤探针，获取流型演化、局部相分布和信号时频特征。识别泡状、帽状泡、帽状湍动、搅混及细分散五类流型，并以组合判据建立流型图。总体转换顺序与既有竖直棒束判据一致，但个别边界存在差异；细分散边界的偏差随气体表观速度增大而减小。这些差异与棒束几何、气泡聚并破碎及局部扰动相关。空泡份额径向为中心峰型，平均气泡速度径向较均匀。泡状及细分散流呈宽频谱；帽状泡、帽状湍动和搅混流呈低频主峰，并有空间差异。

English abstract

To address the insufficient understanding of two-phase flow characteristics in the small-scale rod bundle channels, this study takes air-water two-phase flow as the research object and carries out a systematic experimental investigation in a 3×3 rod bundle channel. High-speed imaging is combined with a self-developed dual-fiber optical probe system. Flow pattern evolution, local phase distribution, and the time-domain and frequency-domain characteristics of probe signals are obtained under different operating conditions. Five typical flow patterns are identified from the optical probe signals, including bubbly flow, cap-bubbly flow, cap-turbulent flow, churn flow, and finely dispersed flow. A flow regime map is established using a combined identification criterion. Compared with existing transition criteria for vertical rod bundles, the general sequence of flow-regime evolution is consistent, while noticeable differences are observed in individual transition boundaries. The deviation of the finely dispersed-flow boundary from the existing criterion decreases as the gas superficial velocity increases. These differences are associated with rod-bundle geometry, bubble coalescence and breakup, and local disturbances. The local measurements show a core-peaked radial distribution of void fraction, while the radial profile of mean bubble velocity remains relatively uniform. Frequency-domain analysis of the probe signals reveals distinct spectral characteristics for different flow patterns. Bubbly and finely dispersed flows exhibit broadband spectra, whereas cap-bubbly, cap-turbulent, and churn flows show dominant low-frequency peaks with spatial variations.
