中文摘要

高成本CFD不便用大量随机采样传播输入不确定性。确定性采样DS用小集合编码均值、方差、协方差等矩。本文研究需要多少矩及如何仅凭传播矩恢复分位数，针对四个独立高斯输入，在具有解析矩和分位数的指数函数族上评价8–25样本集合，与数值积分及收敛随机抽样比较。定义承载95%输出方差的最低Hermite次数为有效阶数，发现其与最高混合交互权重决定DS精度。在满足所需阶次的范围内可精确恢复目标矩；超出时，同样8–25次评估在有效阶数4–5附近仍可把标准差误差控制在20%以内。随后以四个不确定边界及33次CFD研究Kozloduy-6 VVER-1000混合实验，截断多项式混沌估计的全堆有效阶数为1，处于已验证区域。各集合均可靠给出均值与标准差，但只有较丰富集合能提供适合四项Metalog拟合的峰度。

English abstract

Input uncertainty propagation through Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) simulations is limited by cost, making Monte-Carlo sampling impractical at reactor scale. Deterministic Sampling (DS) circumvents this by representing random variables with small ensembles built to encode chosen statistical moments (mean, variance, covariance …). Two questions are addressed: how many moments must be encoded, and how quantiles can be recovered from propagated moments alone. Ensembles of increasing complexity (8 to 25 samples) for four independent Gaussian inputs are assessed on an exponential family with analytical moments and quantiles, against numerical integration and converged Random Sampling. An effective order, the lowest Hermite degree carrying 95 % of the output variance, is introduced and shown to govern DS accuracy together with the highest mixed interaction weight. Below that order they are exact at a cost comparable to numerical integration and orders of magnitude below Random Sampling. Above it, the same 8 to 25 evaluations still recover the standard deviation to within 20 percent up to effective orders four to five, a fixed-budget robustness of direct industrial value. The methodology is then applied to a transient CFD simulation of the Kozloduy-6 VVER-1000 coolant mixing experiment, with four uncertain boundary conditions and 33 CFD runs. The effective order, estimated in situ from a truncated Polynomial Chaos Expansion, equals one over the whole core, inside the validated DS domain. All ensembles give reliable mean and standard deviation, but only the richer one yields a kurtosis usable for parametric fits such as the 4-term Metalog.

English abstract

Condensation of steam mixed with non-condensable gases (NCGs) is a key physical process governing containment depressurization and thermal removal during severe nuclear reactor accidents. Previous studies on steam condensation with NCGs outside tube bundles have mostly focused on bundle structures with a single length and pitch. This paper examines the effects of bundle length, pitch, inclination angle, arrangement, and tube rows number on steam-air condensation outside tube bundles via numerical simulation. The coupling effects between tube length and arrangement, as well as tube pitch and inclination angle, are also analyzed. Calculations indicate that for tubes of different lengths and arrangements, the condensation heat transfer coefficient (CHTC) first decreases and then stabilizes as the tube length increases. Furthermore, the CHTC of different tubes within the bundle increases with larger tube pitch. At a tube pitch of 1.5d, tube innermost tube exhibits the poorest heat transfer performance. At a tube pitch of 3d, its heat transfer capacity becomes 2.55 times that under the 1.5d condition. Inclination induces more obvious overlap effect with narrow tube pitch, at a pitch of 2d, and the overall CHTC of the bundle increases with a larger inclination angle. Additionally, the overall CHTC for a 1m tube bundle gradually increases with increased tube rows, the CHTC surpasses that of a single tube when the number of rows exceeds 10. For 3m and 5m tube bundles, it initially decreases and then tends to stabilize as the number of rows increases.

English abstract

Steam-air condensation is a significant heat transfer process within the thermal-hydraulics field. Previous research has predominantly focused on heat transfer tubes with large curvatures, resulting in experimental correlations that are largely limited to circular tubes and often neglect the effects of curvatures and inclinations on external condensation performance of tubes with limited curvatures or even flat plates. This study, conducted on the COAST (condensation and species transport) experimental facility, investigated steam condensation with non-condensable gases (NCGs) outside circular tubes/plates of different curvatures, covering a pressure range of 0.2∼1.6 MPa and an NCG concentration range of 7∼90%. The plate configurations included both upper-condensing plate and lower-condensing plate, with the influence of inclination angles also incorporated. The results show that the condensation heat transfer coefficient (hc) increases significantly with greater curvature, and the rate of this increase itself grows. The hc of the 100 mm tube shows an improvement of less than 10% compared to flat plate, whereas the 19 mm tube exhibits an average enhancement of approximately 50% compared to the 100 mm tube. Furthermore, the hc increases notably with decreasing inclination, although the magnitude of this improvement gradually diminishes. For the 19 mm tube, the hc of the horizontal tube is 18∼27% higher than that of the vertical tube. For the 100 mm tube, the improvement is only about 5∼15%. The influence of inclination angles on plate condensation differs from that on tubes. For upper-condensing plate, the hc is weaker in the horizontal orientation than in the vertical orientation. While that of the lower-condensing plate initially enlarges and then diminishes from horizontal to vertical orientation. Moreover, the hc of the lower-condensing plate consistently surpasses that of the upper plate. Finally, an experimental correlation applicable to heat transfer tubes and plates with different curvatures and inclination angles was developed. A comparison with existing correlations demonstrates that the proposed correlation has a predictive accuracy within ±20%.

中文摘要

当前反应堆热工水力多尺度耦合研究主要聚焦于多尺度耦合程序开发与验证,针对耦合程序不确定性的系统性研究仍较为匮乏。本研究基于preCICE开源框架,集成计算流体力学(Computational Fluid Dynamics,CFD)程序FLUENT、子通道程序SUBCHANFLOW以及DAKOTA不确定性量化模块,构建多尺度耦合不确定性分析程序。通过建立3×3棒束模型,开展稳态与瞬态条件下的数值验证,并进行不确定性量化与灵敏度分析。研究结果显示:1)稳态工况下耦合系统轴向温度分布与单体程序计算结果最大相对误差为1.65%,误差较小;2)瞬态验证中,入口流量正弦扰动工况下,出口流量波动在周期和相位上完全同步;3)不确定性量化表明冷却剂温度、包壳峰值温度等参数平均值位于95%置信区间内,标准差小,计算可信度高;4)灵敏度分析揭示出口压力和燃料棒热流密度对系统响应量影响较大,入口质量流量和包壳热导率分别对不同响应量产生显著的负向影响,研究结果验证了多尺度耦合系统在动态工况下的响应可靠性。
