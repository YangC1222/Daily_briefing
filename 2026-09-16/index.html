English abstract

The present paper examines thorium as a fertile material in combination with 233U or plutonium, accordingly two types of fuel (Th,Pu)N and (Th,233U)N in addition to the traditional MOX fuel are proposed as fuel used for a small modular fast reactor cooled by sodium (SMFR). Monte Carlo computer code is designed to optimize and analyze the characteristic of the reactor under different fuel types. Several parameters such as reactor multiplication factor, cycle length, discharge fuel burnup (Mwd/T) and number of neutrons released per fission are calculated for every fuel types. Isotopic transmutation through the entire fuel cycle is evaluated and analyzed. Safety and kinetic parameters at beginning (BOC) and end of cycle (EOC) for each fuel type are calculated. Power mapping distributions are calculated and analyzed through different core intervals. Control rods worth required to control the reactivity are determined at BOC and EOC for all fuel types. Multiplication factor for the present model was compared with developed homogeneous model for MOX fuel with differences of 203 pcm between the two models.

English abstract

Reactivity control with control rods is a crucial aspect of the safe operation of nuclear reactors. In this study, we compare the simulated reactivity results of a control rod withdrawal against experimental values obtained during a control rod worth calibration carried out in High Flux Reactor in Petten, The Netherlands. We evaluated the effect on determining the control rod worth caused by the differences between the ENDF/B-VIII.0 and JEFF-3.3 nuclear data libraries. The behavior of the studied transient is dominated by delayed neutrons, allowing us to attempt to evaluate the effect caused by the differences in the delayed neutron data between the two libraries. These simulations were performed with the Serpent 2 Monte Carlo code, with the reactivity calculated using two different methods: the traditional static calculation approach, and dynamic calculations explicitly simulating the rod movement. The estimated differential rod worth determined from both methods and with both libraries are comparable and using the libraries in their entirety has minimal effect on the accuracy of the calculations.

中文摘要

以可裂变材料水溶液为燃料的水溶液均相堆可用于医用同位素生产，但液位、功率分布和多相流波动会影响可靠性。本文针对一座 200 kW 水溶液均相堆，研究假设冷却盘管破裂后溶液上方形成中子反射层的事故情景。研究先确定表征运行稳定性的三个目标输出，再采用矩独立全局敏感性分析，评价 24 个不确定参数对事故瞬态的影响。三个指标分别为最大反应堆功率、最高平均温度和最高局部温度。基于模拟结果建立 24 个输入的敏感性排序，其中前十个参数贡献超过总敏感性的 93%。综合分析识别了控制水溶液均相堆稳定性的主导因素，为提高其运行稳定性和可靠性提供依据。

English abstract

The aqueous homogeneous reactor (AHR), which utilizes an aqueous solution of fissile materials as its fuel, is instrumental in the production of medical isotopes. However, the AHR encounters various operational instabilities, including fluctuations in the solution level, power distribution, and multi-phase flow, significantly affecting its reliability. Three target outputs indicative of the operational stability are identified at first. This analysis focuses on three critical stability indicators: maximum reactor power, maximum average temperature, and maximum local temperature. Based on the simulation results, a sensitivity ranking of the 24 input parameters was established, with the top ten parameters contributing over 93% of the total sensitivity. Comprehensive result analysis and synthesis identify the dominant factors governing AHR stability. The obtained findings provide actionable insights for improving the operational stability and reliability of AHRs.

展开中英文摘要

中文摘要

在动力堆燃料中引入钍以改善燃料和中子经济性已有长期研究，但在材料试验堆中的应用仍相对不足。为延长燃料循环而提高初始铀-235 装载量会带来经济和运行压力，本文试图通过钍减少高装载设计对可燃毒物的依赖。可燃毒物虽可抑制启动初期的过剩反应性，却损害中子经济性。研究用 OSCAR-5 对 SAFARI-1 多种钍-232 基板式燃料作无限晶格和全堆芯多循环平衡计算，比较均匀与微观非均匀构型，铀-235 装载量为 340–600 g，循环长度为 30–60 天，并检查停堆裕量和运行要求。结果表明，钍基燃料能控制较高装载量下的循环初期反应性，同时把循环延长至 45–60 天。全功率运行天数方面，非均匀设计优于均匀设计 2%–3%。在平衡状态下，循环末期最多 8.1% 的功率来自原位生成的铀-233，证实钍-232 转化。虽然易裂变核素增益有限，钍在改善经济性与替代可燃毒物方面的双重作用使其成为材料试验堆的潜在方案。

English abstract

The inclusion of thorium in power reactor fuels has long been studied for its potential to improve fuel and neutron economy. However, its application in research reactors, particularly material test reactors, remains underexplored. Rising commercial pressures and the need for higher initial 235U loadings to extend fuel cycle lengths motivate this investigation. This study aims to enhance fuel and neutron economy in the SAFARI-1 nuclear reactor by reducing reliance on burnable poisons in high-loading designs. While burnable poisons mitigate excess reactivity at start-up, they compromise neutron economy. This work evaluates several 232Th −based plate-type fuel designs for SAFARI-1 using the OSCAR-5 code system, through both infinite lattice and full-core multi-cycle equilibrium calculations. Homogeneous and micro-heterogeneous thorium configurations were modelled for 235U loadings ranging from 340 to 600 g and cycle lengths from 30 to 60 days. The designs were assessed for compliance with the shutdown margin and operational requirements. Results show that thorium-based fuels effectively control BOC reactivity for heavy loadings, while extending the cycle length to 45 – 60 days, offering improved flexibility and economic performance. Heterogeneous designs outperformed homogeneous ones by 2 – 3 % in full-power days. At equilibrium, up to 8.1 % of end-of-cycle power is produced from in situ 233U, confirming 232Th conversion. Although the fissile gain is modest, the dual role of thorium in enhancing the economy and replacing burnable poisons makes it a promising option for MTR-type reactors. These findings advance the feasibility of sustainable thorium–uranium fuel cycles in research reactors.

核心观点

  • 同一 3714.50 MW 压水堆模型在裂变源与燃料温度源两个边界下分别得到 35.80% 和 49.25% 火用效率。
  • 效率差异来自核能固有降级是否纳入边界，不能把数值差异直接解释为设备性能变化。

  • 使用概念压水堆和代表性平均芯块温度；摘要未说明空间温度分布、材料限制与不确定性传播。

  • 用燃料棒热工结构模型输出空间/时变温度分布，量化以单一 800 ℃ 替代真实温度场给火用归因带来的偏差。
⚑ 全文核对燃料平均温度定义、3714.50 MW 参考工况、蒸汽发生器压力、主回路热边界和 TESPy 节点数据。

专有名词解释

火用exergy

本文语境 用于区分不同边界中的损失。

对同一系统分别采用两种物理意义不同的能量输入边界。

本文语境 避免把裂变固有损失与设备损失混为一谈。

展开中英文摘要

中文摘要

核电厂传统火用分析依赖热力学边界的选择，但边界如何影响火用损失的解释和分配尚缺系统研究。本文提出双边界框架，使用 TESPy 非线性网络求解器耦合 CoolProp 对概念压水堆进行热力学和火用评价。模型先与参考压水堆的性能趋势比较验证，再在恒定热功率 3714.50 MW 下评价两种互补边界。第一种裂变边界用理论裂变伪温度作为火用源，火用效率等于热效率 35.80%，全厂火用损失为 2344.42 MW，其中包括核能固有降级和设备不可逆损失。第二种平均燃料温度边界用代表性芯块平均温度 800 ℃ 作为有限温热源，把裂变相关火用损失排除在热力学边界外；得到火用输入 2699.82 MW、火用效率 49.25%、卡诺效率 72.68%。作者还分析环境温度敏感性，并在蒸汽发生器压力 6.8–8.0 MPa、对应平均芯块温度约 799–806 ℃ 的范围做条件筛选局部敏感性分析。第一种方法度量核能转化为有用功的整体降级，第二种突出工程发电环节的不可逆损失与环境、燃料温度影响。双边界框架因此区分了核能固有不可逆性与可能通过工程改进降低的损失。

English abstract

Conventional exergy analyses of Nuclear Power Plants (NPPs) inherently depend on thermodynamic boundary selection, yet its influence on the interpretation and distribution of exergy destruction has not been systematically examined. This study proposes a Dual-Boundary Framework (DBF) for the thermodynamic and exergetic assessment of a conceptual Pressurized Water Reactor (PWR), implemented using the TESPy nonlinear network solver coupled with CoolProp. The model is validated against reference-PWR performance trends before evaluating two complementary exergy boundaries under constant thermal power of 3714.50 MW. The Fission-Based Boundary (Approach 1) employs the theoretical fission pseudo-temperature as the exergy source, yielding an exergetic efficiency equal to the thermal efficiency (35.80%) and total plant exergy destruction of 2344.42 MW, encompassing both intrinsic nuclear degradation and component-level irreversibilities. In contrast, the Average Fuel Temperature-Based Boundary (Approach 2) adopts representative average fuel pellet temperature (Tfp,avg = 800°C) as the finite-temperature heat source, excluding fission-related exergy destruction from the thermodynamic boundary. This yields an exergy input of 2699.82 MW, an exergetic efficiency of 49.25%, and a Carnot efficiency of 72.68%. An ambient-temperature (T0) sensitivity analysis and a criterion-filtered local sensitivity analysis over PSG = 6.8−8.0 MPa, corresponding to approximately Tfp,avg = 799−806°C, demonstrate complementary diagnostic responses of the two exergy boundaries. Approach 1 quantifies overall degradation of nuclear energy into useful work, whereas Approach 2 highlights engineering-domain power-conversion irreversibilities while revealing environmental and fuel-temperature effects on thermodynamic performance. The proposed DBF distinguishes intrinsic nuclear irreversibilities from potentially actionable engineering losses, providing a physically consistent framework for exergy assessment and thermodynamic evaluation of solid-fuel NPPs.

研究空白

建议工作

  1. 建立小片、标准片和管材统一的温度/应力/织构数据库。

研究空白

B2｜需系统几何

研究空白
