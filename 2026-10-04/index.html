中文摘要

本研究利用CFD考察了19棒绕丝式铅基快堆（LFR）燃料组件在正常运行与流道堵塞条件下的热工水力行为。数值模型采用SST k-ω湍流模型，并利用NACIE-UP ADP10实验进行验证；温度与压降对比确认了网格独立性。研究考虑了两种加热方式（均匀加热与中心加热）以及中心绕丝的四种旋转角度（顺时针和逆时针各60°、120°）。结果表明，绕丝旋转会改变冷却剂的径向流动路径并削弱近壁换热，造成局部温升，且中心加热下更加明显。随后，在中心加热条件下模拟了不同轴向位置的两种堵塞情况。虽然流动恢复后，堵塞对温度分布的影响可忽略，但在堵塞末端，峰值温度可能升高最多51 K，具体取决于转角和轴向位置，受扰动流动区域的范围也随之变化。这些发现强调，当绕丝转动与堵塞事故同时存在时，应更加关注局部热工水力安全。

English abstract

This study presents a CFD investigation of thermal-hydraulic behavior in a 19-pin wire-wrapped lead-based fast reactor (LFR) fuel assembly under normal operation and flow blockage conditions. The numerical model is validated against the NACIE-UP ADP10 experiment using the SST k-ω turbulence model, with temperature and pressure drop comparisons confirming grid independence. Two heating modes (uniform and central) and four rotation angles of the central wire wrap (60° and 120° clockwise and counterclockwise) are considered. Results show that wire rotation modifies radial coolant paths and degrades near-wall heat transfer, causing localized temperature rises that are more pronounced under central heating. Subsequently, two blockage cases are simulated under central heating at different axial positions. Although blockage effects on temperature distribution become negligible after flow recovery, at the blockage end, peak temperatures can increase by up to 51 K depending on rotation angle and axial location, and the extent of the disturbed flow region also varies. These findings underscore the need for enhanced attention to local thermal-hydraulic safety when rotated wire wraps and blockage accidents coexist.

本研究采用自旋极化DFT+U计算，考察Cr-U-O体系中CrUO₄与Cr₂UO₆化合物的热力学—力学性质以及辐照诱发缺陷演化。需要指出的是，本研究将这两种相视为彼此独立、假设性的化学实体。计算得到的晶格参数相对于实验和理论数据偏差为1%–2%，处于锕系氧化物模拟可接受的范围。两种化合物均具有明显的力学各向异性：正交晶系Cr₂UO₆对单轴压缩具有较强抵抗能力，而六方晶系CrUO₄的弹性刚度依赖取向。塑性指标（δ=B/C44）表明其具有良好延性；三维分析进一步证实了弹性模量、泊松比及硬度的各向异性。总态密度和分态密度揭示了Cr 3d、U 5f和O 2p轨道对成键的贡献。我们还计算了热力学参数和点缺陷能。与纯UO₂中以氧间隙原子占优势的情况不同，两种相中的铬间隙原子在能量上更有利。间隙缺陷显示负俘获能，其中铀空位具有最高的数值。本研究为理解CrUO₄与Cr₂UO₆自身的本征行为提供基础理论数据，并可能为先进掺铬UO₂核燃料体系的更广泛研究提供间接参考。

English abstract

In this study, spin-polarized DFT+U calculations were performed to investigate thermo-mechanical properties and irradiation-induced defect evolution of CrUO4 and Cr2UO6 compounds within the Cr-U-O system. It should be noted that these two phases are treated as independent, hypothetical chemical entities in the present work. The calculated lattice parameters deviate from experimental and theoretical data by 1-2%, an acceptable range for actinide oxide simulations. Both compounds exhibit distinct mechanical anisotropy: orthorhombic Cr2UO6 strongly resist uniaxial compression, while hexagonal CrUO4 has orientation-dependent elastic stiffness. Plasticity indices (δ = B/C44) confirm their good ductility, and 3D analyses further verify anisotropy in elastic moduli, Poisson’s ratio and hardness. Total and partial density of states reveal the bonding contributions of Cr 3d, U 5f and O 2p orbitals. We also calculated thermodynamic parameters and point defect energies. Chromium interstitials are energetically favorable in both phases, unlike oxygen interstitials dominant in pure UO2. Interstitial defects show negative trap energies, where uranium vacancies possess the highest values. This work offers fundamental theoretical data for understanding the intrinsic behaviors of CrUO4 and Cr2UO6 themselves, which may provide indirect reference for broader studies on advanced Cr-doped UO2 nuclear fuel systems.

中文摘要

气体原子的行为受到核反应堆设计人员的特别关注，因为它在决定裂变气体释放与燃料肿胀方面起关键作用。为燃料性能程序使用，已有多种UO₂裂变气体扩散和再溶解模型，包括通过实验数据拟合的经验模型与描述物理行为的机理模型。本研究建立了统一方法：对若干轻水堆整体辐照实验，评价经验及机理扩散、再溶解模型对BISON所计算裂变气体释放的影响。通过将BISON计算的释放量与辐照实验的最终裂变气体释放量比较，评估模型准确性。对各模型组合的Bland–Altman分析表明，在其他输入参数固定时，Matthews扩散与Setyawan再溶解组成的机理组合具有较小偏差，为−0.028% FGR；历史上采用的经验组合，即Turnbull-D1–D2–D3扩散与White再溶解，其偏差为−1.938% FGR。这些结果为扩散和再溶解模型选择提供了比较依据，并表明机理模型可能提高与整体辐照实验的一致性，同时为预测现有实验数据库以外的裂变气体释放提供更具物理基础的框架。

English abstract

Gas atom behavior is of particular interest to nuclear reactor designers as it plays a key role in determining fission gas release and fuel swelling. Various models have been developed for both the diffusivity and resolution of fission gas in UO2 for use in fuel performance codes, including both empirical models fitted to experimental data and mechanistic models that represent physical behaviors. This work establishes a consistent methodology for comparing empirical and mechanistic diffusivity and resolution models by evaluating their impact on calculated fission gas release in BISON for several integral light water reactor irradiation experiments. Model accuracies are assessed by comparing the BISON-calculated release with the final fission gas release from the irradiation experiments. Bland–Altman analyses of each combination of models indicate that the mechanistic combination of Matthews diffusivity and Setyawan resolution, with other input parameters fixed, has a lower bias of -0.028% FGR than the historically used empirical pairing, the Turnbull-D1–D2–D3 diffusivity with White resolution, which produces a bias of -1.938% FGR. These results provide a comparative basis for selecting diffusivity and resolution models and suggest that mechanistic models can improve agreement with integral irradiation experiments while offering a more physically based framework for predicting fission gas release beyond the existing experimental database.

中文摘要

现象识别与排序表（PIRT）是核安全分析的系统性工具，广泛用于识别事故场景中的关键物理现象，并评估其重要性与认识程度。传统PIRT具有结构化分析的优点，但高度依赖专家主观判断，影响排序过程的客观性与可重复性。本研究主要关注典型小型模块化压水堆（SMPWR）全厂断电（SBO）事故中的热工水力现象，提出融合响应面分析（RSA）的现象重要性排序方法。建立一维数值模拟节点模型，模拟完整SBO事故过程，并表征和识别热工水力现象。采用多参数RSA，量化不同现象对两个主要评价量——反应堆冷却剂系统（RCS）压力与堆芯液位——的影响。将SBO划分为三个阶段，系统识别压力容器（RPV）、堆芯、直流蒸汽发生器（OTSG）及非能动安全系统等关键系统与部件中的重要现象。RSA结果表明，OTSG管侧存量变化、自然循环（NC）和汽液相互作用等对事故进程具有显著影响。尤其是即使在主要由应急堆芯冷却系统（ECCS）控制的长期冷却阶段，OTSG管侧参数仍通过余热排出系统（DHRS）持续影响堆芯液位与RCS压力。这些参数对SMPWR安全具有突出作用。与仅依赖专家判断的传统PIRT相比，结合RSA的PIRT显著提高了热工水力现象排序的客观性与可靠性。本研究不仅提出详细的SMPWR全厂断电PIRT，也为设计优化与实验验证提供重要依据。该方法可进一步促进小型模块化反应堆核安全分析中PIRT制定过程的科学性与客观性。

English abstract

The Phenomena Identification and Ranking Table (PIRT), as a systematic tool in nuclear safety analysis, is widely used to identify key physical phenomena in accident scenarios and assess their importance and level of knowledge. Although the traditional PIRT method has the advantage of structured analysis, its strong reliance on experts' subjective judgments affects the objectivity and repeatability of ranking process. This study mainly focuses on thermal-hydraulic (T-H) phenomena occurring in a typical small modular pressurized water reactor (SMPWR) during station blackout (SBO) accident, and proposes a phenomena importance ranking method integrating response surface analysis (RSA). The one dimensional numerical simulation node model is established to simulate the entire SBO accident process, and to represent and identify the T-H phenomena. The multi-parameter RSA is conducted to quantify the impact of various T-H phenomena on two main figures of merit, namely the pressure of reactor coolant system (RCS) and the liquid level of core. The SBO accident is divided into three phases, and key phenomena in important systems and components such as reactor pressure vessel (RPV), core, once-through steam generator (OTSG), and passive safety systems are systematically identified. The results obtained by RSA indicate that phenomena such as tube-side inventory variation of OTSG, natural circulation (NC), and steam-liquid interaction have significant impacts on accident progress. Importantly, the tube-side parameters of OTSG continuously influence the core level and RCS pressure through the decay heat removal system (DHRS) even in the long-term cooling phase mainly dominated by emergency core cooling system (ECCS). The tube-side parameters of OTSG play prominent role in safety of SMPWRs. Compared with traditional PIRT method that solely relies on expert judgment, the PIRT in combination with RSA significantly enhances the objectivity and reliability of T-H phenomena ranking. Not only a detailed SBO PIRT for SMPWR is proposed in this investigation but also an important basis for design optimization and experimental verification are provided. This approach can be used to further promote the scientificity and objectivity of the PIRT development process in nuclear safety analysis for small modular reactors (SMRs).

中文摘要

球床堆（PBR）模拟通常采用均匀化的能谱分区，限制了功率分布、燃耗和温度等关键参数的准确性。为解决这一问题，扩展超高保真工具（HxF），通过耦合蒙特卡罗程序Serpent、多孔介质求解器GeN-Foam和离散元模型（DEM），纳入详细热反馈。研究开发了一种新的基于Voronoï的网格映射策略，使不同耦合求解器之间能够高效、准确地传递功率密度和温度场，并捕捉PBR堆芯固有的强空间非均匀性。方法应用于平衡状态的HTR-10反应堆。结果表明，所提方法能捕捉显著的堆内功率变化；与等温模型相比，逐球功率偏差可达±20%。此外，耦合模型给出了堆芯部件温度分布预测。该耦合框架实现球尺度上一致的多物理反馈，并表明基于Voronoï的耦合显著提高了PBR模拟保真度，为堆芯设计、安全分析和燃料管理提供可靠工具。

English abstract

The simulation of pebble bed reactors (PBRs) is often simplified using homogenized spectral zones, limiting the accuracy of key parameters such as power distribution, fuel burnup and temperatures. To address this, the hyper-fidelity tool (HxF) is extended to incorporate detailed thermal feedback by coupling the Monte Carlo code Serpent with the porous media solver GeN-Foam and a discrete element model (DEM). A novel Voronoï-based mesh mapping strategy is developed to enable efficient and accurate transfer of power density and temperature fields between the coupled solvers, capturing the strong spatial heterogeneities inherent to PBR cores. The methodology is applied to the HTR-10 reactor at equilibrium conditions. Results show that the proposed approach captures significant intra-core power variations, with pebble-wise powers deviating by up to ±20% compared to the isothermal model. In addition, the coupled model provides a prediction of the temperature distributions for the core components. The coupling framework enables consistent multi-physics feedback at the pebble scale and demonstrate that the proposed Voronoï-based coupling significantly enhances the fidelity of PBR simulations, providing a robust tool for core design, safety analysis, and fuel management.

溶液形式核燃料的反应性反馈特征分析

Reactivity Feedback Characteristics of Nuclear Fuel in Solution Form

科技视界 · 2026 · 刘佳艺 王帅 王云闯 周冰燕 陈长

2026年专题回溯补充；来源仅给年份/期次，不声称今日新发表。

核心观点

展开中英文摘要

中文摘要

溶液形式的核燃料,使燃料与慢化剂均匀混合,并且裂变中子会直接接触溶液,高能的裂变碎片、中子、γ射线将会引发显著的水和其他溶液成分的辐照分解现象,产生诸多辐照分解气体。本文针对溶液燃料的上述特点,以简化的圆柱形堆芯为对象,建模计算并分析了燃料溶液的慢化特性、温度反馈和气泡反馈等。研究结果表明:燃料溶液的水铀比所处的欠慢化或过慢化状态对温度反馈基本没有影响;温度反馈主要由溶液体积膨胀、气泡对泄漏的影响和多普勒效应贡献;气泡反馈体现为泄漏率变化造成的反馈,相同体积的气泡在中子通量密度较高的位置影响更大,而在相同位置时,含气量越高,影响越大;气泡半径尺寸在0.1~1.0 cm范围内变化时,非均匀效应对气泡反馈的影响较小,反馈大小主要受含气率和气泡位置的影响。

English abstract · 本报告由完整中文摘要翻译，非来源英文原文

Nuclear fuel in solution form uniformly mixes the fuel and moderator, and fission neutrons come into direct contact with the solution. High-energy fission fragments, neutrons and gamma rays induce substantial radiolysis of water and other solution constituents, producing various radiolytic gases. Considering these characteristics, this study models a simplified cylindrical core and calculates and analyzes the moderation characteristics, temperature feedback and bubble feedback of the fuel solution. The results indicate that whether the water-to-uranium ratio places the fuel solution in an under-moderated or over-moderated state has little influence on temperature feedback. Temperature feedback is mainly contributed by solution volume expansion, the effect of bubbles on leakage, and the Doppler effect. Bubble feedback manifests as feedback caused by changes in the leakage rate: bubbles of the same volume have a greater effect in regions of higher neutron flux, while at the same location a higher gas content produces a greater effect. When the bubble radius varies from 0.1 to 1.0 cm, heterogeneity has only a small effect on bubble feedback, whose magnitude mainly depends on gas volume fraction and bubble location.
