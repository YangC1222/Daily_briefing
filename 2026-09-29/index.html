国产新锆合金包壳管的内压蠕变性能

兵器材料科学与工程 · 2026 · 吴楠 高博 张鹏飞 俞海平 李小影

核心观点

  • 比较SZA-4、SZA-6与国产ZIRLO包壳管的内压蠕变；摘要列出343、385、450℃与80、125、150 MPa周向应力，试验持续200天。
  • 作者报告温度和应力增大时蠕变应变增加，同条件下SZA-4稳态蠕变速率最低。

创新点

  • 在同一试验体系中比较国产新锆合金的包壳管蠕变性能，为材料筛选提供数据；不能据摘要认定新的通用蠕变理论。

不足

  • 摘要的“分别”不能证明进行了全部九种温度—应力组合；需核对试验矩阵、样本重复和不确定度。
  • 未提供蠕变速率、应力指数及激活能具体值，也未说明辐照、氢含量、织构与尺寸；不能直接生成ANSYS材料卡或推广至堆内。

未来方向

  • 摘要写明研究目标与应用价值，未列出具体后续研究计划。
  • 获取原始应变—时间曲线，拟合温度和应力相关蠕变模型，在保留独立工况的条件下预测包壳半径与间隙演化。
  • 将材料参数误差传播到包壳接触压力与热阻；不同合金的试验不能无条件合并。
⚑ 已披露试验温度、周向应力和200天时长；核对管径/壁厚、内压换算、试验矩阵、蠕变曲线及拟合系数。

专有名词解释

稳态蠕变速率steady-state creep rate

材料在给定温度和应力下进入近似稳定蠕变阶段后的应变增长速率。

本文语境 用于比较三种合金；不等价于寿命或爆破压力。

展开中英文摘要

中文摘要

本文研究了国产新锆合金SZA-4和SZA-6合金包壳管的内压蠕变性能,并以国产ZIRLO合金为参比。试验在343,385,450℃下进行,周向应力分别为80,125,150 MPa,持续200天。结果表明:随着温度和应力的增大,内压蠕变应变增加,锆合金包壳管的抗内压蠕变性能降低。SZA-4合金的抗内压蠕变性能优于SZA-6和ZIRLO合金,其稳态蠕变速率在相同条件下最低。通过分析稳态蠕变速率、蠕变应力指数和蠕变激活能等指标,揭示了不同种类锆合金在不同试验条件下的内压蠕变机理。为自主化核燃料元件包壳材料的筛选与性能优化提供了参考。

English abstract

This article investigated the internal pressure creep properties of domestically produced new zirconium alloys SZA-4 and SZA-6 for cladding tubes,with domestic ZIRLO alloy as a reference.The experiment was conducted at 343,385,and 450℃,with circumferential stresses of 80,125,and 150 MPa,respectively,for 200 days.The results indicate that with the increase of temperature and stress,the internal pressure creep strain increases,and the resistance to internal pressure creep of zirconium alloy cladding tubes decreases.The resistance to internal pressure creep of SZA-4 alloy is superior to that of SZA-6 and ZIRLO alloys,and its steady-state creep rate is the lowest under the same conditions.By analyzing indicators such as steadystate creep rate,creep stress index,and creep activation energy,the internal pressure creep mechanism of different types of zirconium alloys under different experimental conditions was revealed.This provides a reference for the screening and performance optimization of autonomous nuclear fuel element cladding materials.

乏燃料棒锆合金包壳环向拉伸性能分析

原子能科学技术 · 2026 · 汤琪 王烨霆 张恺 郭一帆 钱进 王华才 梁政强

核心观点

  • 用商业压水堆乏燃料棒包壳做环向拉伸，对比堆内辐照样品与渗氢未辐照样品。
  • 本文条件下辐照提高强度但降低延性，高温促进塑性恢复；高氢未辐照样品断后伸长率在高温下超过40%、室温约20%。

创新点

  • 通过实际乏燃料样品与渗氢参照建立辐照和吸氢影响的试验对照，提供材料状态与环向力学响应证据。

不足

  • “本文条件下辐照影响更大”不能推广为氢化物不危险；取向、含氢量、温度和应力状态可改变结果。
  • 辐照与渗氢参照不是完备的辐照×氢含量析因试验；材料历史、织构和样品几何可能混杂。
  • 断后伸长率受试样与测量定义影响，不宜直接当作复杂组件的局部断裂应变限值。

未来方向

  • 摘要写明研究目标与应用价值，未列出具体后续研究计划。
  • 用可核材料状态建立各向异性弹塑性/延性退化模型，分别拟合辐照和吸氢试验；通过独立环向试验或爆破数据验证。
  • 在ANSYS包壳热结构模型中比较强度升高与延性降低对局部应变裕度的不同影响，避免只按屈服强度判定性能改善。
⚑ 摘要给出材料状态和伸长率线索；核对合金牌号、温度、含氢量/取向、辐照剂量、拉伸夹具、应力—应变曲线及重复次数。

专有名词解释

环向拉伸hoop tensile test

沿管周向评估力学性能的试验，夹具与试样形式影响应力和应变解释。

本文语境 用于比较包壳材料状态；需核对标距和局部受力。

氢化物hydride

氢与锆形成的相，其分布和取向可改变材料延性与断裂行为。

本文语境 本文对照下影响较小并非否定其他条件下的脆化风险。

展开中英文摘要

中文摘要

压水堆燃料棒堆内服役过程中,包壳锆合金经受长时间中子辐照和高温冷却水腐蚀,导致材料力学性能发生显著变化。为探究辐照和吸氢对锆合金包壳力学性能的影响,本文以某商业压水堆乏燃料棒锆合金包壳为研究对象,通过环向拉伸实验系统对比堆内辐照样品、渗氢未辐照样品的环向拉伸性能。环向拉伸实验结果显示:辐照显著提高堆内锆合金强度,但延性变差;高温下锆合金塑性得到恢复,表现出韧性断裂特征。吸氢对锆合金强度和塑性的影响相对较小,在高温下高氢含量渗氢拉伸实验样品断后伸长率保持在40%以上,在室温下断后伸长率降至20%,但未显现明显脆性断裂特征。断口初步观察显示,辐照拉伸实验样品室温下呈脆性断裂特征,高温下出现杯锥状韧性断裂;而高氢含量的渗氢拉伸实验样品即使在室温下也未有明显脆性断裂特征。在本文实验条件下,辐照作用主导堆内锆合金力学性能变化,其强化和脆化效应大于氢化物的影响,辐照是影响堆内锆合金包壳力学性能的主要因素。

English abstract

Zirconium alloys used as cladding in pressurized water reactor(PWR)fuel rods undergo significant mechanical properties changes due to prolonged neutron irradiation and high-temperature coolant corrosion during in-reactor service.This study examined the impacts of irradiation and hydrogen absorption on the mechanical properties of zirconium alloy cladding.Zirconium alloy cladding from spent fuel rods of a commercial pressurized water reactor served as the research subject.Hoop tensile tests were conducted in a hot cell facility to compare the mechanical properties of inreactor irradiated samples with those of out-of-reactor unirradiated but hydrogen-charged samples.The results demonstrate that irradiation substantially increases strength while reducing ductility,although elevated temperatures restore plasticity and promote ductile fracture modes.Hydrogen absorption exerts a comparatively limited influence:even at high hydrogen concentrations,specimens retain ductility elongations above 40%at elevated temperature and about 20%at room temperature,without evident brittle fracture.Fractographic observations confirm irradiation-induced brittle fracture at room temperature and ductile cup-cone fracture at elevated temperature,whereas hydrogen-charged specimens show no distinct brittle features.Under the present conditions,irradiation governs the mechanical property evolution of in-reactor zirconium alloys,with its strengthening and embrittlement effects exceeding those associated with hydride formation.

基于ICoCo规范的快堆燃料组件多物理场耦合性能分析方法

原子能科学技术 · 2026 · 杨广亮 刘振海 王浩煜 辛勇 李垣明 冯文培 董思凡 陈红丽

核心观点

  • 通过ICoCo将KMC-Fueltra燃料性能程序与KMC-Subtra子通道程序耦合，采用算符分离、区域重叠和MEDCoupling映射。
  • ORNL-19瞬态算例中数据传递前后，包壳外表面温度一致；冷却剂温度和压力的最大相对偏差分别为0.15%与1.4%。

创新点

  • 建立多根燃料棒并行耦合的半网格/全网格实现，并对关键传递量进行接口一致性核验。

不足

  • 传递前后一致属于接口验证，不能单独证明整体物理预测准确；还需实验或独立基准确认。
  • 最大值插值原则不自动保证热功率、质量或力的积分守恒，也不证明非线性反馈稳定。
  • 摘要未提供耦合时间步敏感性、迭代收敛或计算成本；英文摘要对接口及并行实现描述更多，不能补写进中文原摘要。

未来方向

  • 摘要写明研究目标与应用价值，未列出具体后续研究计划。
  • 在相同燃料棒基准上比较最大值保持与守恒映射，跟踪温度峰值、积分热量和包壳应力误差。
  • 在高保真耦合快照上建立POD代理，单独评估映射误差、时间分裂误差和降阶误差，采用残差或训练域检查触发回退。
⚑ 摘要明确ORNL-19算例及温度/压力边界传递；核对几何、热源、网格对应、时间步、插值定义、误差分母和验证数据。

专有名词解释

ICoCoInterface for Code Coupling

统一耦合程序调用和数据交换的接口规范。

本文语境 负责程序接口组织，本身不保证耦合守恒或物理准确。

算符分离operator splitting

把耦合演化分为依次求解的子问题。

本文语境 计算组织更方便，但要核对时间分裂和反馈误差。

展开中英文摘要

中文摘要

为了获得更加准确的燃料棒热-力边界,基于ICoCo规范将燃料性能分析程序KMC-Fueltra和子通道分析程序KMC-Subtra进行耦合,开发了快堆燃料组件多物理场耦合性能分析程序。采用C++语言编写的Supervisor程序作为驱动耦合的主程序,时间耦合采用算符分离方案,空间耦合采用区域重叠方案,建立了多根燃料棒并行计算的半网格法和全网格法。基于MEDCoupling库实现网格映射和数据插值,插值原则采用最大值原则。选取ORNL-19燃料组件的瞬态算例对包壳外表面温度、冷却剂温度、冷却剂压力等耦合参数在数据传递前、后的一致性进行验证。结果表明,包壳外表面温度完全一致,冷却剂温度的最大相对误差为0.15%,冷却剂压力的最大相对误差为1.4%,可以证明耦合程序的正确性。本文研究可为液态金属冷却快堆燃料棒的多物理场耦合服役性能分析提供借鉴和指导。

English abstract

The development of a multi-physics coupling program for the fast reactor fuel assembly was presented in this paper.The fuel performance analysis code KMC-Fueltra and the sub-channel analysis code KMC-Subtra were coupled based on the ICoCo interface to obtain more accurate thermomechanical boundary of fuel rods in the fuel performance analysis.KMC-Fueltra is a steady-state and transient performance analysis code developed for the fuel element in the liquid metal cooled fast reactor,aimed at predicting and evaluating the evolution of thermal,mechanical,and irradiation performance of the fuel element during service.KMC-Subtra is a sub-channel analysis code developed based on the thermal-hydraulic characteristics of lead-cooled fast reactors,used to calculate key parameters such as temperature and pressure of the coolant.ICoCo is a universal coupling interface that defines the essential functions required in a numerical simulation program.This allows the main code to arbitrarily call these functions when coupling with other codes,enabling operations such as time step advancement,mesh mapping,and data transfer.Numerical simulation programs must provide these functions to the ICoCo interface according to the evolution of the physical problem and specify the appropriate method to be called at specific times.The Supervisor code written in C++language was used as the main code to run the coupling program.KMC-Fueltra and KMC-Subtra were wrapped to different essential functions in the form of dynamic library or static library according to the ICoCo interface,which could be called by the Supervisor code.The operator separation scheme was used for time coupling,and the region overlap scheme was used for spatial coupling.The half-mesh method and full-mesh method,which could be chosen by the specific research problem and computer hardware condition,for parallel calculation of multiple fuel rods were established using OpenMPI library.The mesh mapping and data interpolation were implemented based on the MEDCoupling library,and the maximum value principle was used as the interpolation principle.The outer surface temperature of the cladding,the coolant temperature,and the coolant pressure were chosen as the coupling parameters between KMC-Fueltra and KMC-Subtra based on the physical equations.Verification of the data transfer interface of the coupling program was conducted with these three key parameters from the ORNL-19 fuel assembly benchmark case.The outer surface temperature of the cladding is completely consistent,the relative error of the coolant temperature is 0.15%,and the relative error of the coolant pressure is 1.4%,which can prove the accuracy of the coupling program.This study can provide reference and guidance for the multi-physics coupled service performance analysis of fuel rods in the liquid metal cooled fast reactor.

控制棒肿胀形貌及分布对落棒时间的影响分析研究

中国核电 · 2026 · 吕志鹏 刘维超 方润杰 李长征 李伟才 辜培勇

核心观点

  • 依据在运机组控制棒池边检查结果建立典型肿胀形貌与轴向分布模型，分析形貌特征参数对落棒时间的影响。
  • 重点对三角形肿胀的最大径向肿胀位置进行分析，以确定该形貌下保守落棒时间。

创新点

  • 把现场肿胀形貌的离散性纳入简化落棒分析，改进只用单一最大直径描述的思路。

不足

  • 最大概率轴向范围不等于最不利尾部；保守性结论只适用于作者检查的形貌和参数域。
  • 摘要没有给出具体尺寸、摩擦、流体模型、时间变化或独立落棒试验误差；不能据此判定某机组剩余寿命。

未来方向

  • 摘要写明研究目标与应用价值，未列出具体后续研究计划。
  • 在同一控制棒型式内，结合形貌测量误差、偏心和导向间隙，检验概率代表范围是否遗漏低概率但高落棒阻力的组合。
  • 比较最大直径、分段形貌和接触位置描述的预测能力；使用独立落棒试验而非同一模型结果判断保守包络。
⚑ 摘要有形貌与轴向范围参数线索；核对池边数据、特征参数、导向间隙、阻力模型和落棒时间验证。

专有名词解释

辐照肿胀irradiation swelling

辐照引起材料体积或形状变化的一类机制。

本文语境 吸收体膨胀可推动包壳外变形并影响间隙。

保守包络conservative envelope

在限定对象和不确定度域内覆盖不利响应的分析结果。

本文语境 最常见形貌不是天然的最坏情况。

展开中英文摘要

中文摘要

控制棒肿胀是影响控制棒使用寿命的关键影响因素,吸收体材料受辐照影响发生肿胀,导致控制棒包壳向外变形,当吸收体所受辐照达到一定程度后,使得控制棒发生肿胀,进而影响控制棒落棒时间,严重情况下会导致落棒时间超限,影响反应堆的安全运行。国内外的研究显示,控制棒的肿胀一般集中在吸收体的下端部区域,且控制棒的肿胀形貌具有一定的分散性。为了更好地研究不同肿胀形貌对落棒时间的影响,本文基于中广核在运机组控制棒池边检查结果,建立了几种典型肿胀形貌模型,并选取最大概率轴向肿胀范围,对不同类型肿胀形貌分别采用一系列特征参数,研究不同形貌下具体参数对落棒时间的影响规律。最后,通过控制棒三角形肿胀的最大径向肿胀位置进行特定分析以确定该形貌下保守的落棒时间。相关工作为获得契合机组运行实际的控制棒落棒时间的简化分析提供了依据。研究结果对制定控制棒运行策略、提升反应堆运行安全与经济性具有重要的指导意义。

English abstract

The swelling of control rods is a critical factor affecting their service life.The absorption material undergoes swelling due to irradiation,causing the control rod cladding to deform outward.When the irradiation of the absorption body reaches a certain level,it leads to swelling of the control rod,which in turn affects the rod drop time.In severe cases,this can result in the drop time exceeding limits,affecting the safe operation of the reactor.Both domestic and international studies have shown that control rod swelling generally concentrates in the lower end region of the absorption body and exhibits a certain degree of dispersion in its swelling morphology.To better study the effects of different swelling morphologies on the drop time,this paper is based on the pool-side inspection results of the control rod in an in-service unit of CGN,several typical swelling morphologies are given,and the maximum probability axial swelling range is selected.It employs a series of characteristic parameters for different types of swelling shapes to investigate the influence of specific parameters on the drop time under various shapes.Finally,a specific analysis is performed through the maximum radial swelling location of the triangular swelling of the control rod to determine the conservative falling rod time under this morphology.This work provides a basis for obtaining a simplified analysis of control rod drop times that aligns with the actual operation of the unit.The study results are of significant guiding importance for formulating control rod operation strategies and enhancing the safety and economy of reactor operation.
