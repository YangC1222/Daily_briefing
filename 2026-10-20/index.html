中文摘要

窄矩形通道两相流中的界面演化和流型转换受惯性、黏性、毛细力耦合作用影响，关系到紧凑换热。本文用高速摄影研究15×2 mm²和50×2 mm²竖直窄矩形通道内向上气液流，利用MATLAB图像处理识别流型并提取两相参数。识别四类流型，得到局部空泡份额分布和瞬时截面空泡份额的概率密度。空泡份额从壁面向中心持续增加；固定液速、增加气速时，概率密度由低空泡份额的尖峰向更高空泡份额的宽分布移动，主峰逐渐发展为双峰。提出修正漂移流模型预测时间平均截面空泡份额，对两通道与实验的误差均在10%以内。

English abstract

Two-phase flow in narrow rectangular channels is of continued interest in multiphase flow research because of its importance in compact heat transfer applications. Gas-liquid flow in these geometries exhibits complex interfacial behavior and flow-regime transitions driven by the interactions of inertial, viscous, and capillary forces, which strongly affect heat and mass transfer performance. In this study, visualization experiments using high-speed photography were conducted to investigate vertical upward gas–liquid two-phase flow in narrow rectangular channels with cross-sectional dimensions of 15 × 2 mm2 and 50 × 2 mm2. A MATLAB-based image-processing procedure was used to identify flow patterns and extract two-phase flow parameters. Four distinct flow patterns were identified, and the corresponding local void fraction distributions and probability density functions (PDFs) of instantaneous cross-sectional void fraction were obtained. The results showed that the void fraction increased consistently from the wall toward the channel center. At a fixed liquid flow velocity, increasing the gas flow velocity shifted the PDF from a sharp peak at low void fraction to a broader distribution at higher void fraction, with the dominant peak gradually evolving into a bimodal distribution. A modified drift-flux model was developed to predict the time-averaged cross-sectional void fraction, and the predicted values agreed well with the experimental data, with errors within 10 % for both channels.

中文摘要

水冷竖直管CHF准确预测关系到热工安全，许多关联式只适用于有限工况。本文以4384组实验，评估模糊c均值聚类（FCM）是否改善数据驱动CHF预测。输入为管径、加热长度、压力、质量通量、入口过冷度和出口含汽率。在固定70:30训练—测试划分上比较Elastic Net、K近邻、随机森林、AdaBoost及FCM辅助模型。独立模型用训练集五折交叉验证调参；FCM模型以内部分割联合优化聚类与回归。聚类输入不包含CHF，隶属度用于分解CHF目标。FCM-AdaBoost测试相对均方根误差rRMSE为18.1%，均方根相对误差RMSRE为11.7%，总体最优。FCM使RF的RMSRE从65.9%降至16.4%；相对独立AdaBoost从16.1%降至11.7%的改善较小。结果支持对本异质数据集采用模糊预聚类，但分区物理解释仍待研究。

English abstract

Accurate prediction of critical heat flux (CHF) in water-cooled vertical tubes is essential for thermal–hydraulic safety, yet many empirical and mechanistic methods are valid only within restricted operating ranges. This study evaluates whether fuzzy c-means (FCM) clustering improves data-driven CHF prediction using 4,384 experimental observations from vertical round tubes. Six predictors were considered: tube diameter, heated length, pressure, mass flux, inlet subcooling, and exit quality. Elastic Net, k-nearest neighbors, random forests (RF), AdaBoost, FCM-assisted RF, and FCM-assisted AdaBoost were evaluated using a common fixed 70:30 training-test partition. Hyperparameters of the standalone models were optimized by five-fold cross-validation within the training set, whereas the FCM-assisted models used an internal fitting-validation split for joint optimization of the clustering and regression components. FCM membership values were used to decompose the CHF target without including CHF in the clustering inputs. FCM-assisted AdaBoost achieved the best test performance, with a relative root-mean-square error (rRMSE) of 18.1 % and a root-mean-square relative error (RMSRE) of 11.7 %. FCM markedly reduced the RMSRE of RF, from 65.9 % to 16.4 %, whereas the improvement over standalone AdaBoost was smaller (RMSRE: 16.1 % to 11.7 %). These results demonstrate that fuzzy pre-clustering can improve predictive performance for the present heterogeneous CHF dataset, although the physical interpretation of the resulting fuzzy partitions requires further investigation.

展开中英文摘要

中文摘要

加热棒束瞬态耦合流量、温度及浮升力驱动的二次流，难以由固定全局降阶基底表示。POD对近线性强迫对流有效，但混合对流中横向结构随浮力状态变化，需要自适应基底。本文以规范一致的深度正交分解（DOD）建立棒束瞬态热工水力代理，重构三分量速度及温度场，并耦合具有数据标定系数的冷却剂停留时间热滞后模型及准稳态水力闭合。以处理后的稳态工况及瞬态CFD评价。与使用相同系数映射器的全局POD相比，DOD更准确地重构浮力敏感的横向速度。在均匀及偏置加热下，流量降低与升高瞬态中均能捕获主要轴向流动和温度演化。研究展示浮力自适应基底重构非线性横向结构的收益，为棒束实时数字孪生从等温水力拓展至受热浮力瞬态提供路径。

English abstract

Heated rod-bundle transients couple changes in flow rate, temperature, and buoyancy-driven secondary motion, making them difficult to represent with a fixed global reduced basis. Proper orthogonal decomposition (POD) works well for nearly linear forced-convection hydraulics, but the transverse structures that develop under mixed convection vary with the buoyancy state and require a basis that adapts to the operating condition. This study develops a buoyancy-adaptive reduced-order surrogate for transient rod-bundle thermal-hydraulics using gauge-consistent deep orthogonal decomposition (DOD). The surrogate reconstructs the three velocity components and the temperature field, and is coupled with a coolant-residence-time-based thermal-lag model with a data-calibrated prefactor and a quasi-steady hydraulic closure. It is evaluated against processed steady operating states and transient CFD simulations. Compared with a global POD using the same coefficient mapper, the DOD surrogate gives more accurate reconstructions of the buoyancy-sensitive transverse velocity components. Heated flow-decrease and flow-increase tests show that the surrogate captures the main axial-flow response and temperature evolution under both uniform and skewed heating conditions. These results demonstrate the benefit of buoyancy-adaptive reduced bases for reconstructing nonlinear transverse flow structures in heated rod-bundle transients and establish a practical route for extending real-time rod-bundle digital twins from isothermal hydraulics to heated buoyant transients.
