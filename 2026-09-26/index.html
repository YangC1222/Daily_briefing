06 跨堆型方法与共性问题乏燃料干式贮存多目标优化

GAMMA-PC 方法中用于干式贮存装载优化的动态功能更新

Updates to Dynamic Functions in the GAMMA-PC Methodology for Dry Storage Loading Optimization

Nuclear Technology · 2026-09-23 · Kristy Yancey Spencer, Peter Stefanovic

核心观点

  • 以乏燃料组件干式贮存装载为对象，联合优化罐数、初始热负荷和运输准备时间。
  • Comanche Peak 算例中，离散装载日程的优化表现与连续时间线相当或更好；分阶段搜索的解接近单次长搜索。

创新点

  • 在既有 GAMMA-PC 算法中加入可自定义装载日程，并以离散装载日期和分阶段优化测试该功能。

不足

  • 摘要未给出不同热负荷不确定性、燃料历史与安全约束下的鲁棒性；需要阅读全文核对热工约束如何进入目标函数。

未来方向

  • 将装载方案输出的衰变热分布映射到干式贮存罐温度和包壳最高温度模型，检验优化方案是否保持热安全裕量。
⚑ 建议下载全文核对衰变热、装载日程和罐体热限值的定义及 Comanche Peak 案例输入；它们可作为后续 ANSYS 热模型的候选边界，尚未验证。

中文摘要

本文介绍贪婪自适应多目标模因算法与部分聚类（GAMMA-PC）的更新。该进化算法用于在反应堆寿期内选择进入干式贮存的乏燃料组件，同时尽量减少贮存罐数量、平均初始热负荷和达到可运输状态所需的最长时间。美国能源部将该算法开发为 STANDARDS 乏燃料数据与分析工具的一部分。工具最近增加了用户自定义装载日程，研究据此比较默认连续时间线与便于排程的离散时间线。以 Comanche Peak 核电厂为例，离散方案设定了 2019 至 2025 年间的五个日期，此后每 24 个月装载一次。离散时间线下，GAMMA-PC 的表现与默认连续时间线相当或更好。研究还测试了分阶段优化：每阶段只运行总函数评估次数的五分之一，得到的解与单次长时间运行接近。这提示算法可能比原先预期更快接近帕累托前沿。更新增加了排程功能，并支持 GAMMA-PC 继续用于干式贮存装载与日程优化。

English abstract

This article presents updates to the Greedy Adaptive Multi-Objective Memetic Algorithm with Partial Clustering (GAMMA-PC), an evolutionary algorithm designed to select used nuclear fuel assemblies for dry storage over a reactor’s lifetime. The algorithm simultaneously minimizes the number of canisters, the average initial heat load, and the maximum time to transportation readiness. The U.S. Department of Energy developed GAMMA-PC for integration with the STANDARDS used nuclear fuel data and analysis tool. The tool was recently updated to enable user-defined loading schedules. This functionality was tested by comparing GAMMA-PC’s performance using the default continuous timeline versus the new scheduling-friendly timeline.Comanche Peak Nuclear Power Plant was chosen as a test case. For the discrete schedule, five dates were provided from 2019 to 2025 followed by a loading frequency of every 24 months. GAMMA-PC performed as well as or better than status quo (i.e. the default continuous timeline) when using the discrete timeline. A staggered optimization approach was also tested to simulate how a utility might use the software. Each stage of the optimization ran one-fifth of the number of function evaluations, resulting in a solution that was comparable to the single longer run. This suggests that GAMMA-PC might not need as long to converge toward the Pareto front as originally expected. These improvements add a new functionality and demonstrate the continued usefulness of GAMMA-PC in dry storage loading and scheduling optimization.

  • 将分层能量补偿滤片的输运模拟和多源实验表征结合，用于宽量程便携伽马剂量率监测器。

展开中英文摘要

中文摘要

在高辐射核设施中，准确测量伽马剂量率对辐射防护、环境监测和过程控制至关重要；测量系统还需具备灵活、便携和宽量程能力。本文开发并优化一种紧凑的光电二极管式宽动态范围伽马剂量率监测系统，面向高剂量率应用。研究重点是利用 FLUKA 辐射输运蒙特卡罗模拟设计能量补偿滤片，改善探测器的能量响应。未补偿探测器在低光子能量下因相互作用的能量依赖性出现明显过响应。为此，作者设计并优化铜与不锈钢分层构成的补偿滤片。实验采用 ISO 4037 的 80–250 kV 窄谱 X 射线束，以及 137Cs 和 60Co 伽马源表征。优化后的探测器在 100 keV 至 1.3 MeV 光子能量范围内响应近乎不随能量变化，实验与模拟预测吻合。利用 60Co 源开展至 10 000 R/h 的剂量率线性测量，探测器对参考值的偏差在 ±15% 以内。该系统可作为核燃料循环设施中传统电离室式宽量程剂量率监测器的一种紧凑、轻量、低功耗替代方案。

English abstract

Accurate gamma dose rate measurement is essential for radiation protection, environmental monitoring, and process control in nuclear facilities operating under high-radiation conditions. One of the key requirements of a measurement system is operational flexibility, portability, and the capability to perform measurements over a wide range. In response to these requirements, this study presents the development and optimization of a compact photodiode-based gamma dose rate monitoring system with a wide dynamic range for high dose-rate applications. The work primarily focused on improving the detector energy response through the design and optimization of an energy-compensation filter using Monte Carlo simulations performed with the FLUKA radiation transport code. The uncompensated detector exhibited significant over-response at lower photon energies due to the energy dependence of photon interactions within the detector material. To mitigate this effect, a graded compensation filter comprising copper and stainless-steel layers was designed and optimized. Experimental characterization was carried out using ISO 4037 narrow-spectrum X-ray beam qualities of 80–250 kV, along with 137Cs and 60Co gamma-ray sources. The optimized detector configuration demonstrated a nearly energy-independent response over the photon-energy range from 100 keV to 1.3 MeV. Experimental measurements showed good agreement with simulation predictions, validating the proposed compensation methodology. Dose rate linearity measurements performed up to 10 000 R/h using a 60Co source demonstrated linear detector response with deviations within ±15% of reference values. The developed system offers a compact, lightweight, and low-power alternative to conventional ionization chamber-based monitors for wide-range gamma dose-rate measurements in nuclear fuel cycle facilities.

English abstract

heat radiator fins in space heat-pipe reactors.Since space heat-pipe reactor design requires joining C/C composites to metal thermal components,brazing Cu to C/C composites has become a critical technical pathway for manufacturing high-performance fins.As the reinforcement phase and thermal conduction pathway in composites,fiber orientation significantly influences material properties,imparting pronounced anisotropy to characteristics such as thermal conductivity,coefficient of thermal expansion,and tensile strength.Thus,variations in fiber orientation within composites will inevitably affect joint performance.To address this objective,this study employed highly stable commercial AgCuTi filler metal to braze C/C composites with different fiber orientations to Cu metal.The microstructure,room temperature shear strength,fracture morphology,and thermal conduction properties of the brazed joints were systematically investigated.The results indicate that joints with fibers oriented perpendicular to the brazing surface(C/C⊥-Cu)exhibit the best performance.Under the condition of 880°C and without applied pressure,the room temperature shear strength reached 33.19 MPa(peak strength of 41.65 MPa),and the thermal diffusivity measured 154.539 mm2/s.In contrast,joints with fibers parallel to the brazing surface(C/C//-Cu)demonstrate inferior performance,with a significantly lower room temperature shear strength of only 14.71 MPa and a reduced thermal diffusivity of 84.581 mm2/s.Microstructural and fractographic analysis reveals that the brazed joints formed under this process can effectively absorb external loads due to the presence of highly plastic and dense Ag-based and Cu-based solid solutions.Concurrently,a continuous TiC layer formed on the C/C composite side ensures stable heterogeneous bonding between the metal and the C/C composite.The C/C⊥-Cu joint exhibits significantly superior strength to the C/C//-Cu joint.This difference stems from their distinct failure mechanisms:When subjected to shear stress perpendicular to the fiber orientation,the C/C⊥-Cu joint experiences crack propagation primarily within the TiC layer at the joint interface,as the fibers themselves possess higher shear resistance than the joint region.In contrast,the C/C//-Cu joint fails differently under stress parallel to the fiber direction.Cracks tend to initiate and propagate along interlaminar regions or between fiber bundles within the composite material.For 1D or 2D composites,interlaminar shear strength is typically lower than in-plane shear strength,making the C/C⊥-Cu joint more resistant to shear failure.Furthermore,braze filler infiltration provides additional pinning effects that enhance joint strength.The C/C⊥-Cu configuration facilitates better braze penetration,contributing to its higher shear strength.The C/C⊥-Cu joint exhibits both ideal mechanical properties and thermal conductivity,showing promise for use in brazing heat radiator fins for space heat-pipe reactors.

某高温气冷堆反应堆压力容器(Reactor Pressure Vessel,RPV)筒体锻件的低温冲击韧性未满足技术条件要求。采用扫描电子显微镜(SEM)和能谱分析(EDS)对冲击试样断口进行检测,结果表明:钢中非金属夹杂物是导致冲击韧性不合格的主要原因。文章进一步分析了非金属夹杂物的来源,并提出了相应的工艺改进措施。

English abstract

The low-temperature impact toughness of a cylindrical forging for the reactor pressure vessel(RPV)of a hightemperature gas-cooled reactor did not meet the specified technical requirements.Scanning electron microscopy(SEM)and energy dispersive X-ray spectroscopy(EDS)were employed to examine the fracture surface of the impact specimen.The results show that nonmetallic inclusions in the steel were the primary cause of the insufficient impact toughness.The sources of these inclusions are analyzed,and corresponding process improvement measures are proposed.

中文摘要

为评估西安脉冲堆燃料棒的运行安全性,本文基于MOOSE(multiphysics object-oriented simulation environment)平台,开展了燃料棒的三维热-力耦合分析。通过耦合温度场与位移场,重点研究了不同力学边界条件对燃料棒应力分布的影响。针对特定燃料棒(C10),模拟其在1 a稳态运行后叠加脉冲工况下的响应。模拟结果表明:稳态运行时燃料棒温度分布呈显著的非对称性;脉冲工况下,燃料芯块温度急剧升高,包壳与中心锆棒的温升则存在滞后。力学分析表明,燃料与包壳在稳态运行约3200 h发生显著接触,间隙压力和体积随之变化,且脉冲工况导致燃料棒整体应力水平升高。研究发现边界约束条件对燃料棒内部应力分布具有决定性影响,采用仅固定底部的约束可显著降低整体应力水平。本文为西安脉冲堆燃料棒的安全评估提供了三维热-力耦合分析依据。

English abstract

To evaluate the operational safety of fuel rods in the Xi'an Pulsed Reactor,a three-dimensional thermo-mechanical coupling characteristics of the fuel rods is analyzed using the multiphysics objectoriented simulation environment(MOOSE)platform.By coupling the temperature field and displacement field,this paper focused on the influence of different mechanical boundary conditions on the stress distribution of the fuel rod.For a specific fuel rod(C10),the response under pulsed conditions after one year of steady-state operation is simulated.The results indicate that during steady-state operation,the temperature distribution of fuel rods exhibits significant asymmetry.Under pulsed conditions,the fuel pellet temperature rises sharply,while the temperature rise of the cladding and the central zirconium rod exhibits a lag.Mechanical analysis shows that fuel-cladding mechanical interaction occurs after approximately 3200 hours of steady-state operation,leading to changes in gap pressure and volume,and the pulsed condition causes an increase in the overall stress level of the fuel rod.It is also found that boundary constraint conditions have a decisive influence on the internal stress distribution of the fuel rod,and adopting a constraint that fixes only the bottom can significantly reduce the overall stress level.This paper provides a basis for the 3D thermo-mechanical coupling analysis in the safety assessment of Xi'an Pulsed Reactor fuel rods.

摘要状态

本文针对新燃料厂房燃料棒贮存区的通风系统进行数值模拟研究,分析了不同工况下温度场分布特性及通风优化方案的可行性。通过建立包含燃料棒的三维流-固耦合模型,采用Fluent软件,模拟了正常工况和事故工况下的热流场分布。结果表明,常规下送上排通风方案在正常工况下可将z=4.1 m截面平均温度控制在49℃,事故工况下温度可控制在66.8℃,均满足工艺要求。对调送排风口位置的上送下排方案虽改善了区域温度差异,但顶部平均温度升高约3℃,超出正常工况限值,可能影响贮存区上方管线安全。研究进一步提出优化建议,包括调整送风面积、提高风速以增强顶部降温效果。
